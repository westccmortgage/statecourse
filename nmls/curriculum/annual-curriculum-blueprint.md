## 1. Architecture: a reusable core, not a universal eight-hour promise

The annual core categories are three Federal Law hours, two Ethics hours, and two Nontraditional Mortgage hours. Standard eight-hour courses add a general elective. State-specific requirements can replace that elective or require additional hours. Exact agency allocation is in [the 50-state/55-agency coverage matrix](../coverage-research.md), with DC separate. [NMLS approved-topic policy](https://mortgage.nationwidelicensingsystem.org/knowledge/Products/nmls/pubs/nmlsEdFuncSpec/education/funcSpec/maps/topics/approvedCourseTopics/approvedCourseTopicList.html).

Internal authoring blocks are not independently bankable courses. Whether to submit an eight-hour comprehensive course, a seven-hour core, separate electives, or combinations is a course-approval decision. Never split an approved eight-hour course into apparent seven-hour and one-hour credits without approval. Check previously completed course IDs and annual credit rules before recommending another bundle.

Planning workload from the verified agency-hour matrix:

- Seven shared core hours.
- Up to three distinct general-elective hours for the largest general-elective allocation.
- Twenty-nine state-specific authoring hours across 24 state-agency rows, including separate South Carolina agency approvals.
- That is 39 unique planned content hours, not 39 approved hours or 39 hours that every student must take.
- DC and Nevada's qualified-employee case are additional optional scope, not silently included in ordinary-MLO coverage.

Required course menus depend on agency, credit year, license/role, completion history, and delivery rules, not the mailing address alone. California DRE is not California DFPI; Texas SML is not Texas OCCC; Utah DFI is not Utah DRE.

### Time labels

All minute allocations below are proposed design budgets for instruction and embedded formative work, based on a 50-minute instructional-hour model. They are not NMLS-assigned per-topic minutes, measured completion times, or an approval determination. The annual background has an explicit NMLS two-to-three-minute instruction; we budget three. Other per-topic allocations are StateCourse proposals. Confirm the final delivery/timing and examination design against the [current Functional Specification](https://mortgage.nationwidelicensingsystem.org/knowledge/Products/nmls/pubs/nmlsEdFuncSpec/index.html) before submission.

Do not count orientation, idle time, repeated already-completed content, or arbitrary forced delay as substantive learning. The end-of-course assessment and any required validation are separate design items; this plan does not quietly count their time inside the instructional minimum.

### Controlling-authority crosswalk

- F01: [Reg B application/creditor definitions, §1002.2](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1002/2/); [notices, §1002.9](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1002/9/); [records, §1002.12](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1002/12/); [enforcement, §1002.16](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1002/16/); [TRID application/business-day definitions, §1026.2](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/2/); [initial LE, §1026.19(e)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/19/).
- F02: [§1026.19(e)(3)–(4) and official interpretations](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/19/). Teach the zero-tolerance rule, ten-percent aggregate bucket, other permitted variations, valid revision reasons, affected costs, and timing together. Do not label a fee by CD section alone without the required shopping/affiliation facts.
- F03: [§1026.19(f)(3)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/19/) and [§1026.38(f)(2)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/38/). Preserve the averaging exception's conditions; the general actual-charge rule is not a license to add undisclosed margin.
- Retention overlay: [§1026.25(c)(1)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/25/). Reg B's retention period is not the universal mortgage-record destruction policy.

### Source differences that must stay visible

The 2026 topic 5 heading cites §1026.19(a), but its finding concerns the Loan Estimate. F01 uses the applicable TRID provision, §1026.19(e), and notes the mismatch. The topic 7 prose also simplifies recording-fee tolerance; instruction must apply the ten-percent **aggregate** test in §1026.19(e)(3)(ii), not invent an individual ten-percent ceiling.

The NMLS findings select the curriculum; the operative regulation and official interpretation control the legal explanation. Human review must resolve any discrepancy before submission, not reproduce it for the sake of matching a heading.

## 6. Assessment and evidence plan

Every lesson has a traceability row: objective → source passage → original scenario → formative item → explanatory feedback → independent assessment item. A correct key alone is not evidence that an item discriminates between competent and incompetent performance.

| Detect a failed control | Receipt-versus-LOS exception report | KC12; final open response | Real representative-learner usability/timing evidence |

Before publication:

1. Confirm each reference is operative for the delivery date; retain source snapshots/version evidence and a change log.
2. Have a qualified human reviewer sign off on legal accuracy, scope, mathematics, answer keys, and state-specific nuances.
3. Conduct independent instructional/item review; improve weak distractors and avoid teaching the exact final-exam cases.
4. Run representative learners through the actual delivery mode. Record observed active time, comprehension, navigation problems, and required revisions. Do not use an AI run as those learner records.
5. Meet the NMLS course validation and assessment requirements for the submitted delivery mode. This artifact does not invent a validation sample or substitute formative questions for an approved final.
6. Verify accessible presentation, keyboard paths, captions/transcripts where used, state/resource attachments, and support procedures.
7. Bind the exact reviewed course version, provider/course approvals, delivery mode, year, credit categories, hours, and reporting process before enabling paid CE.
8. Verify credit banking, reconciliation, refunds, privacy, and customer support against the real operational integration.
