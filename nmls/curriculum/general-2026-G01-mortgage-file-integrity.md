## Purpose, applicability and non-duplication

An originator can transmit every requested attachment and still deliver an unreliable file. A processor may receive a payroll document without its relevant date. An underwriter may decide from a clear image that is no longer the operative version. A closing specialist may see a green status that predates a material update. The resulting problem is not simply missing paperwork. Different people are acting on different accounts of the same transaction.

This lesson develops a practical skill: move a residential mortgage file between authorized participants without losing the connection among facts, evidence, decisions and unresolved work. You will build a source map, distinguish a missing item from an unresolved decision, investigate changed facts without overwriting history, and prepare a closing handoff that another person can verify.

Assume an ordinary consumer-purpose, closed-end first-lien home-purchase mortgage subject to the applicable Regulation Z ability-to-repay and TRID requirements. The fictional creditor is a nonbank institution subject to the FTC Safeguards Rule. The case is not a reverse mortgage, HELOC, construction loan, business-purpose loan or state-specific program. Different coverage determinations require separate analysis.

The creditor's actual underwriting and release authorities are assigned by its procedures, subject to law and licensing requirements. Job titles alone do not establish that authority. Where this lesson says **case control**, it describes an original StateCourse training procedure, not a federally mandated form, software field, waiting period or approval hierarchy. Where an investor requirement appears, it is labelled separately from federal law.

G01 does not repeat F01's inquiry/application clocks, F02's tolerance calculations or F03's settlement-charge corrections. It does not provide a second hour for the same federal-law instruction. Those subjects are prerequisites and escalation references here. The substantive work is a new file-integrity case. Its final category and allocation need provider/course suitability review. NMLS recognizes a CE-elective category, but that does not approve this particular manuscript. [NMLS Functional Specification §1.3.2](https://mortgage.nationwidelicensingsystem.org/knowledge/Products/nmls/pubs/nmlsEdFuncSpec/education/funcSpec/maps/topics/nmlsFS_continuingEd.html).

### Learning objectives

After the lesson, the learner should be able to:

1. Trace a relied-upon mortgage-file fact to its source, relevant period, review and downstream use.
2. Separate absent information, conflicting information and an unmade decision.
3. Prepare an actionable changed-fact handoff without making an unauthorized credit decision.
4. Limit a transaction handoff to appropriate recipients and necessary information.
5. Reconcile source, decision, delivery and release versions before marking a handoff complete.
6. Explain which parts of the process are federal requirements, investor conditions, state-dependent matters or provider-selected controls.

## 1. G01-U01 — Reconstruct the file before trusting its status

### The Juniper Lane file

Jordan Vega is purchasing a single-family home for $410,000. The requested mortgage is $328,000, fixed rate for 30 years. Jordan is the only borrower. For this case, Stonebridge Funding is the creditor, and Harbor Desk Settlement is the settlement agent. All people, firms, records and events are fictional; dates below are relative workdays, not a legal calendar exercise.

Elena, the originator, has established the relationship with Jordan. Malik, the processor, gathers and organizes evidence. Priya, the underwriter, has authority under the creditor's procedures to determine whether underwriting conditions are satisfied. Theo, the closing specialist, coordinates the approved closing package. Theo does not acquire Priya's underwriting authority because the closing is approaching.

At 9:00 on workday 12, Malik receives a file bearing the status **Ready for closing review**. It contains the following entries:

| Record | What it says | What the entry does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Application, version 3 | Jordan works 40 hours weekly; reported gross monthly income is $6,400 | Whether those facts remain current |
| Payroll PDF, workday 4 receipt | Employer-produced statement for the preceding pay period | Whether a later payroll change exists |
| Underwriting decision, workday 8 | Conditional approval based on named income and asset documents | Whether conditions were subsequently cleared |
| Portal message, workday 11 at 16:20 | Jordan reports a temporary reduction in scheduled hours | Effect on qualifying income or eligibility |
| Bank upload, workday 11 | File named `statement-final.pdf` | Whether it is complete or the operative account statement |
| Closing-status note, workday 12 | “All documents uploaded” | Whether anyone reconciled the new facts |

There are at least four different kinds of information in this table: a consumer's statement, a source record, a professional decision and a workflow label. Confusing them makes the file look more complete than it is. A statement about work hours is relevant information, but not itself a new underwriting decision. A conditional approval is a decision on a stated record, not a promise that later facts are irrelevant. A status label is a summary, not the evidence that supports it.

### Use a source map, not a stack of filenames

The case control is a short source map for each material fact. Record the fact being used, document or communication ID, author or source, period covered, date received, whether it is complete, reviewer, conclusion and affected next step. A useful entry is: **Base income: employer payroll record P-04; preceding pay period; received workday 4; reviewed by Priya in decision UW-08; later hours-change message M-11 unresolved.**

An unhelpful entry is: **Income: verified.** It gives the next person no way to determine what was verified or what changed. The source map is deliberately selective: it follows facts used in the transaction rather than turning every email into a new underwriting condition.

The map also distinguishes three dates. The record's effective period describes the facts it concerns. The received time describes when the creditor had it. The review time describes when a person evaluated it. A payroll PDF received today can describe last month; an assessment completed yesterday can omit today's message. Do not move one date into another field to make the file appear current.

**Embedded exercise:** Write three separate sentences about the workday-11 hours message: one stating what is known, one stating what remains unknown, and one identifying the person who must decide the consequence. Avoid the words “approved,” “fraud” and “fine” unless evidence actually supports them. Retain your response for comparison with the private teaching notes.

## 2. G01-U02 — Missing evidence and conflicting evidence require different work

Malik opens `statement-final.pdf`. It contains pages 1 and 3 of a three-page bank statement. The ending balance is visible on page 1, but the missing page may explain a transfer used in the closing-funds analysis. Jordan's portal also contains a screenshot showing a newer balance. Neither the word “final” nor the newer timestamp resolves the absent page.

Classify the problem before sending a request. **Missing information** means a needed component is not in the record. **Conflicting information** means the available items disagree or do not fit the same explanation. **Pending decision** means sufficient material may be present, but the authorized reviewer has not yet reached or recorded a conclusion. A file can have all three at once.

For the bank record, the initial task is to obtain the missing page or other acceptable evidence under the creditor's procedures. If the new material contradicts the claimed source or availability of funds, the task becomes reconciliation. The originator should not mark the condition cleared merely because a new attachment arrived. Equally, a processor should not ask for the same missing page repeatedly after it has been received; the file may now be waiting for a decision, not the customer.

### Do not confuse the messenger with the source

For covered ability-to-repay determinations, Regulation Z requires verification of information relied upon using reasonably reliable records as specified in §1026.43(c)(3)–(4). The official interpretation permits appropriate third-party records to arrive from the consumer; a consumer-uploaded employer payroll statement is not automatically disqualified merely because the consumer transmitted it. Conversely, the consumer's own summary does not become an employer record by being uploaded to a lender portal. The rule also contains specific verification exceptions; it does not require every possible fact to be verified by an identical document. [§1026.43, including comments 43(c)(3)-2 and 43(c)(4)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/43/).

That distinction changes the request you send. Ask Jordan for the missing page of the identified statement, not for a vague “better document.” Explain the period and account reference using an appropriate masked identifier. Do not ask Jordan to recreate the bank's statement, paste information into an imitation template or edit an inconvenient entry. A reconstructed explanation can be labelled as an explanation; it should not be relabelled as the source record.

### Make the next handoff executable

Under this case control, each open item has an owner, an exact question, the evidence requested, the next review action and a due-for-follow-up point. The follow-up point is an internal work target, not a substitute for a disclosure deadline or a borrower commitment.

Compare two requests:

> **Unusable:** “Assets issue. Please resolve ASAP.”

> **Actionable:** “P-11 is missing page 2 of the statement covering the identified month. Malik will request the complete record through the approved portal. Priya will review whether it resolves the source-and-availability condition. Until that review is recorded, Theo will not treat the assets condition as cleared.”

The second message prevents a common ownership failure: the originator believes processing is deciding, processing believes underwriting already decided, and closing believes an upload means clearance.

**Embedded exercise:** A replacement upload arrives with all three pages, but page 1 displays a different statement period. Write the next question. A strong response identifies the mismatch and asks for the relevant period or an explanation; it does not assume the newest file should replace the earlier evidence.

## 3. G01-U03 — Changed facts need a decision trail

Jordan's workday-11 message states: “My scheduled hours are dropping from 40 to 32 for the next several weeks. My hourly rate is unchanged. I do not yet know when the old schedule returns.” Elena has already told the real estate agent that the loan is moving toward closing. She is tempted to leave the status alone until a later payroll record shows the effect.

The original decision and the later information answer different questions. The earlier review records what the underwriter determined from the information then evaluated. The message introduces a new fact that may affect that conclusion. Retaining the old decision is important. Treating it as though it already evaluated the new fact is the error.

The federal anchor is that the creditor's required reasonable, good-faith repayment determination is made at or before consummation. The case does not supply enough facts to approve or decline Jordan; it establishes that the changed information must reach the authorized decision process rather than being hidden behind an old status. [§1026.43(c)(1)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/43/).

### Use a change record with four parts

The provider-selected change record contains: **what changed; when it became known; which earlier conclusions may depend on it; and who must evaluate the consequence.** For Jordan, it points to M-11, payroll P-04 and decision UW-08. It does not recalculate qualifying income from a single text message, predict a denial or declare that the unchanged hourly rate cancels the reduced schedule.

Priya may need further evidence and may apply the creditor's applicable product and investor criteria. Elena can communicate the request and record the customer's response. Malik can reconcile the documents. Theo can update the release worklist. Those actions are coordinated without pretending that every participant has the same decision authority.

**Investor-specific comparison, not federal law:** Fannie Mae's Selling Guide B3-6-02 addresses discovery of additional debt or reduced income after underwriting through closing, with documentation, recalculation and applicable re-underwriting criteria. Its detailed tolerances and requirements govern eligibility for the relevant Fannie Mae delivery. They are not a universal federal rule for all mortgages. Before using that guide on an actual file, establish that the loan is subject to it and consult the relevant current provisions. This case does not teach a new DTI threshold or require a particular underwriting system for every lender. [Fannie Mae B3-6-02](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b3-6-02/debt-income-ratios).

### Correct the working record without rewriting history

Assume Priya requests an updated employer response and identifies the inputs requiring review. Malik must keep the original receipt and decision traceable while linking the replacement evidence. A note such as “Income updated” is too thin: it obscures whether the old and new numbers were compared and what conclusion followed.

A more useful entry reads: **UW-08 remains the earlier conditional decision. M-11 received at 16:20 on workday 11 reports reduced hours. Employer response E-13 and the identified current payroll evidence were routed to Priya. UW-13 records the subsequent evaluation. Closing checklist CL-12 is superseded by CL-13 only after its named conditions are resolved.** These IDs and steps are training controls, not mandated federal record labels.

Changes also need accurate language. A genuinely later event is not the same as a pre-existing fact the file missed. Regulation Z's subsequent-event provision recognizes that some later events can make earlier disclosures inaccurate without making the original disclosure a violation; it does not eliminate any applicable correction duties. Do not backdate a discovery, label every correction a changed circumstance or infer a new waiting period from the word “change” alone. [§1026.17(e), subject to the applicable §1026.19 rules](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/17/).

**Embedded exercise:** Draft an internal update and a separate customer update. The internal update should name the changed fact, evidence and review owner. The customer update should explain the next requested action without promising approval or blaming a colleague. Neither should pretend that an internal “pending review” label automatically changes any legal notification obligation.

## 4. G01-U04 — Share the work, not an unrestricted copy of the customer

Harbor Desk asks for the current approved loan terms and information needed to prepare its assigned closing items. At the same time, the seller's agent emails Elena: “Send me the whole file so I can explain the delay.” The two requests arise from the same purchase, but they do not have the same purpose or scope.

For this case, Elena does not send payroll, bank records and underwriting commentary to everyone copied on the purchase emails. She asks what the recipient is authorized to do and which information is necessary for that function. Where a party needs a status update rather than source documents, a carefully scoped status update can answer the operational question without broadcasting the contents of the file. This is a case control, not an assertion that all possible sharing scenarios have the same legal outcome.

Regulation P provides an exception to specified notice and opt-out requirements for certain disclosures necessary to process, administer or enforce a transaction the consumer requests or authorizes. That is not a blanket exception for every recipient with an interest in the sale, and it does not abolish all privacy obligations. State law can provide greater protection; applicability must be checked rather than assumed away. [§1016.14](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1016/14/); [§1016.17](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1016/17/).

The separate security question is how authorized information moves. For a covered institution, the FTC Safeguards Rule includes access limits tied to duties, information-security controls and oversight of service providers. Permission to perform work does not mean permission to expose unrelated customer records or bypass the institution's controls. This lesson assumes FTC coverage; the rule's definitions include mortgage brokers, while the applicable regulator and obligations must be determined for other institutions. [16 CFR §314.4(c)(1), (c)(3), (f)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-314/section-314.4); [16 CFR §314.2](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-314/section-314.2).

### An acknowledgement is part of the handoff

The sender's checklist may be complete while the recipient has not accepted the work. Under the case control, a handoff is not finished merely because an email left the outbox. The recipient confirms the file ID, current version, assigned responsibility, unresolved issues and the next response. If the designated closer is away, the sender uses the institution's documented alternate route; the work does not become ownerless.

Suppose Theo sends Harbor Desk package CL-13, but the recipient acknowledges CL-12. Theo should reconcile that discrepancy before treating the acknowledgement as acceptance. “Received” is not enough if the parties mean different packages. A shared transaction identifier helps, but it must be paired with the particular version and assigned work.

The same reasoning applies to outsourcing. A vendor can gather information or prepare assigned material. The firm's own responsibilities are not satisfied simply by writing “vendor handles it” in a note. A reliable handoff identifies what the vendor will do and what the creditor must still verify.

**Embedded exercise:** Prepare two brief responses: one to Harbor Desk identifying a permissible scoped package for its assigned task, and one declining to send the entire mortgage file to the seller's agent while offering an appropriate status update. Do not invent the customer's consent or claim a specific state-law exception that has not been checked.

## 5. G01-U05 — Close the evidence chain before releasing the package

On workday 14, the underwriter records the new decision after evaluating the requested information. For the exercise, assume that decision resolves the income and funds issues without changing the approved loan terms. This assumption supplies the next case stage; it is not a conclusion learners may infer from the reduced-hours message alone.

Theo now discovers two separate version problems. First, Harbor Desk is holding CL-12 rather than CL-13. Second, the appraisal vendor issued a revision correcting a legal-description typo after the earlier appraisal copy had already been sent to Jordan. The appraisal's estimated value and valuation methodology are unchanged. The record says “appraisal sent,” but does not identify the version.

Appraisal-copy obligations provide a concrete reason to track versions. For applications covered by §1002.14, when an applicant has already received one appraisal version and the creditor later receives a revision, the revision must also be provided. A clerical-only correction is not permission to omit it. The rule has specific timing and waiver provisions; the limited clerical-change treatment is not a blanket waiver. This exercise asks the learner to identify the unverified delivery task, not calculate a deadline from incomplete facts. [§1002.14(a)(1), comments 14(a)(1)-6 and -7](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1002/14/).

### Four independent checks

Use the case's four-check release method:

1. **Source check:** Does the handoff identify the operative evidence and unresolved revisions?
2. **Decision check:** Did the authorized reviewer evaluate the evidence now being relied upon?
3. **Delivery check:** Does the record identify what was provided, to whom, and the evidence supporting that conclusion?
4. **Release check:** Has the person with the relevant release authority confirmed the applicable conditions, rather than relying on a generic green flag?

These checks are not four new federal forms. They are an original control for keeping distinct work from being collapsed into “done.” A complete source file does not prove consumer delivery. A delivery acknowledgement does not prove underwriting approval. An underwriting decision does not prove that the settlement agent has the same closing package.

The Closing Disclosure work may be divided between the creditor and settlement agent. When the settlement agent provides the disclosure in the creditor's place, the creditor remains responsible for ensuring the applicable requirements are met; effective communication is necessary. Creating duplicative disclosures simply to demonstrate the creditor “also did it” is not the solution. Agree who prepares which portions, who provides the coordinated disclosure, and how the evidence returns to the file. [§1026.19(f)(1)(v) and comments 19(f)(1)(v)-3 and -4](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/19/).

The word **closing** should not conceal a state-law question. Regulation Z defines consummation by the consumer's contractual obligation on the credit transaction, with applicable state law determining when that obligation arises. A scheduled appointment, funding event and recording event are not automatically interchangeable. Theo must use the correct legal event in the applicable workflow rather than import a practice from another state. [§1026.2(a)(13) and comment 2(a)(13)-1](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/2/).

### Keep a usable record, not permanent duplicates of everything

After handoff, preserve the evidence required for the relevant obligations and applicable retention schedule. Do not delete source evidence solely because the item was marked cleared, or assume a vendor link will remain available. Regulation Z permits accurate reconstruction methods in appropriate circumstances and imposes distinct mortgage retention requirements; a single universal “keep everything for two years” rule is incorrect. [§1026.25(a), (c)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/25/).

This does not require endlessly retaining every convenient duplicate. The case control is an indexed record of operative documents, relevant superseded versions, decisions and necessary delivery evidence, maintained under the institution's lawful retention and disposal policy. Check that a future reviewer can retrieve the identified material. A link that works only in the sender's personal inbox is not a dependable institutional handoff.

**Embedded exercise:** CL-13 is now acknowledged, but the appraisal-revision delivery evidence is still missing. Write the unresolved task, its owner and the evidence required to close it. Do not mark the entire loan unlawful or ready solely on that one fact; identify the affected release check and route the question appropriately.

## 6. G01-U06 — Cold-file review: can the next person act safely?

Close your notes and consider this new file, not Jordan's. Ari Chen is applying for a $286,000 fixed-rate first mortgage to purchase a home. The file is assigned to you because the original originator is unexpectedly absent. You have these five entries:

- **A-02:** The application reports funds in one savings account.
- **B-07:** A complete bank statement supports the listed account at the time covered.
- **M-09:** Ari reports transferring some of those funds to a different account for the purchase.
- **UW-08:** The decision predates that transfer; no later review is recorded.
- **CL-10:** The closer's checklist says “assets clear,” with no linked source or decision.

Your task is to prepare a five-line handoff: known facts; unresolved question; evidence to obtain; authorized decision owner; and the condition for the next release step. Do not infer that the transferred funds disappeared, that they are automatically acceptable, or that the borrower made a false statement. The record does not establish any of those conclusions.

Before submitting, perform three checks on your own writing. Could another employee identify the exact material without calling you? Does each claimed conclusion identify who made it? Can the recipient distinguish a pending customer request from an internal review? If not, revise the handoff rather than adding more adjectives such as “urgent,” “verified” or “complete.”

Then test a counterfactual: the complete receiving-account evidence arrives and the authorized reviewer records acceptance. What changes? The open evidence request can close and the decision can be linked to the closing worklist. What does not change? The original transfer message remains part of the chronology, and the old decision does not acquire a false later date.

The professional habit is to hand over an explainable transaction, not a confident label. A dependable file lets a qualified successor continue the work without guessing which version is operative, which fact is disputed, who owns the decision or what remains to be done.
