## Learner reading

The 2026 required federal topics begin with examination experience. The Multi-State Mortgage Committee selected ten subjects from multistate examinations completed during the fourth quarter of 2023 and first quarter of 2024. NMLS identifies them for continuing education delivered during calendar 2026; they are not interchangeable with another year's required list. [S1]

The committee includes ten appointed state-regulator members and one CSBS member. State regulators license and supervise relevant financial institutions, including mortgage lenders, and examine loan files and corporate records for compliance and risk controls. An examination is not merely a test of whether a company owns a written policy. Individual originators can also be held accountable for violations identified in examinations. [S1]

Reported examination responses included correcting disclosures, returning borrower money, additional audits, staff training and documented changes to internal controls. These are examples of possible consequences, not a fixed penalty schedule or a promise that training alone resolves a violation. The appropriate response depends on the facts and governing authority. [S1]

The course uses those findings to practice identifying the applicable rule, reconstructing what occurred and testing a correction. A required-topic list does not itself approve this provider or this course. [S1; provider-status limitation]

### Apply the idea to a new fictional file

At fictional Alder Crossing Lending, a reviewer finds that a disclosure process failed. A manager's response is: “Everyone attended a meeting, so the problem is closed.” No one has checked whether affected consumers received appropriate corrections or whether a later sample follows the revised process.

Before moving on, identify one question about the affected files and one question about future performance. Useful questions include: “What evidence shows that the required corrective action was completed for each affected file?” and “What later sample would show whether the revised control actually works?” These are proposed review methods, not a regulator-prescribed sampling formula.

The next lesson applies this habit to application information, action notices and disclosure clocks. Do not assume that one definition or deadline governs every obligation in a mortgage file.
