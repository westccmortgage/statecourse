## Unit 3A Build a record that can be reviewed

### An application is information and a request

An application form organizes information, but a signature date does not necessarily mark the first legal event in a file. A consumer may provide material facts by telephone, through a portal, and by email on different days. The receiving team needs a reliable record of what arrived and when, including the consumer's request. A portal label such as inquiry or prequalification cannot settle every legal definition.

For transactions subject to the relevant integrated disclosure requirements, Regulation Z identifies six application elements: name, income, Social Security number to obtain a credit report, property address, estimated property value, and mortgage amount sought. The definition has a specific scope. A completed application under Regulation B is a different concept, linked to information the creditor regularly obtains and considers. Do not use the six elements as a universal definition for every mortgage law. [P3-01, P3-02]

At this stage, recognize the event and route it for the firm's deadline check. Do not delay the record until a pay statement, purchase contract, or signed form arrives. For a covered Loan Estimate, verification documents cannot be made a prerequisite to providing the estimate. Module PE05 addresses coverage, timing, notices, and the applicable business day rules. This unit does not ask you to calculate those deadlines. [P3-03]

### Keep four evidence states distinct

Requested means someone has asked for an item. Received means it is in the authorized system. Reviewed means an identified person has examined it for a stated purpose. Accepted means the authorized decision maker has determined that it satisfies the relevant requirement. A document can be received and still be incomplete, inconsistent, outdated, or waiting for review.

Use a short source map. For each material fact, record the source, period covered, receipt date, review status, and open question. Keep the original and identify later changes. An updated application should make the correction traceable rather than silently replacing the earlier account.

| Fact | Record and period | Current status | Next question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base pay | Employer statement P1 for September | Received; review pending | Does pay frequency match the application? |
| Closing funds | Bank statement B1 for August, pages 1 and 3 of 3 | Incomplete receipt | Obtain page 2 or an acceptable equivalent |
| Housing intent | Applicant message M1 | Applicant statement | Reconcile with the existing lease before deciding occupancy |

The person who sends a record and the person who created it can differ. An employer pay statement uploaded by the applicant can still be an employer record. An applicant's spreadsheet does not become a bank statement merely because it is in a lender portal. For covered ability to repay determinations, the verification rules require reasonably reliable records and contain specific requirements and exceptions. The method must fit the fact, the transaction, and the applicable program. [P3-04]

### Read a document before asking for another one

On a pay statement, locate the employee and employer identifiers, pay period, payment date, pay frequency, base earnings, variable earnings, year to date figures, and deductions. A two week pay period is not the same as twice monthly pay. A deduction explains why net pay differs from gross pay; it does not automatically determine how a liability is treated for underwriting.

On a bank statement, check the account holder, masked account reference, statement period, complete page count, opening and closing balances, and transactions relevant to the funds being used. An ending balance alone does not establish the source, availability, or permitted use of every dollar. A transfer between accounts is movement of the same money, not a second asset to add again.

Use secure approved channels and request only what the file reasonably needs. Do not put account numbers, identification numbers, or unredacted source records in general email threads or peer discussions. If a document is illegible or missing a page, identify the exact defect and period. Repeatedly requesting an already received item wastes the applicant's time and can hide a stalled review.

### Worked example W3A The missing page

Alex Chen reports a $6,000 monthly salary and $31,000 available for closing. The file contains an employer pay statement showing $3,000 gross per semimonthly pay period and a bank statement with a $31,000 ending balance. Page 2 of the three page statement is absent. The processor has marked both conditions complete because files were uploaded.

The pay figure is arithmetically consistent: $3,000 × 24 ÷ 12 = $6,000 monthly. That calculation does not by itself finish employment or income review. The asset condition remains unresolved because the record is incomplete. The accurate status is pay statement received and routed for review; bank statement incomplete. The next request is the missing page of the named statement or another acceptable record under the applicable process, not a newly typed summary of the account.

A useful request reads: Please send page 2 of the August statement for the account ending 4421 through the secure portal. The statement has three pages and we have pages 1 and 3. We need the complete record for review. This explains the request without claiming an outcome or accusing Alex of wrongdoing.

### Your case C3A The Cedar file

Mara Ortiz asks for a loan to buy 18 Cedar Lane. At 10:00 she supplies her name, income, property address, estimated value, and requested loan amount. At 14:00 she securely supplies the identifier needed to obtain a credit report. Assume these are the six elements for a transaction subject to the relevant integrated disclosure rule. At 16:00 she uploads an unsigned application and the first page of a two page pay statement. The portal still says lead. No one has yet reviewed income.

Complete a four line record: (1) the event to send for the disclosure deadline check, (2) the item received but incomplete, (3) the fact still awaiting a qualified review, and (4) one precise next request. Do not calculate a deadline or decide whether the loan qualifies. Then replace the label all documents verified with an accurate one sentence status.

## Unit 3B Analyze the borrower evidence

### Qualifying income needs a supported method

The application records what the applicant reports. Qualifying income is the amount accepted under the applicable underwriting method after the necessary analysis. Take home pay, gross wages, business revenue, taxable income, and qualifying income are different measures. They cannot be substituted simply because one is easier to find.

For a fixed salary, converting pay frequency is a useful starting calculation. Annual salary divided by 12 gives a monthly amount. Semimonthly pay has 24 periods per year; biweekly pay normally has 26. Hourly income also needs an appropriate hours assumption. Variable hours, bonuses, commission, overtime, seasonal work, and multiple jobs require the applicable history, trend, and continuance analysis. Do not multiply the best recent paycheck by the number of periods and call the result stable income. Fannie Mae's general guidance emphasizes a documented history and a reasonable expectation of continuation; its requirements are program guidance, not a universal rule for every lender. [P3-05]

For self employment, gross receipts belong to the business before operating expenses. Owner withdrawals are not automatically qualifying income. Tax returns, the ownership structure, current business records, and the program's permitted adjustments may matter. A permitted noncash expense adjustment does not mean every expense can be added back. Business funds withdrawn for closing can also affect business viability. The underwriter must use the applicable analysis rather than copying a bank deposit total into monthly income. [P3-06]

### Use consistent individual evidence

Do not reject income merely because it comes from part time work, retirement, or a prohibited basis. Regulation B requires an individual evaluation within its rules and permits consideration of the amount and probable continuance of income. It also limits requests about alimony, child support, and separate maintenance: applicants must be told they need not disclose that income if they do not want it considered. Relevant support payments the applicant is obligated to make are a separate liability question. PE05 and PE06 provide the full legal and ethics treatment. [P3-07, P3-08]

A defensible request describes the evidence needed for the actual source. It does not assume that a parent will stop working, a retiree cannot qualify, or two unrelated applicants should be treated less favorably. If a requested record creates a legal or accessibility question, obtain compliance guidance and an appropriate alternative rather than improvising a blanket rule.

### Assets and debts answer different questions

Funds to close pay transaction costs and the required borrower contribution. Reserves are eligible assets remaining after closing under the program's definition. A balance can cover one use or the other only once. A gift, a loan, and proceeds from selling property require different evidence and treatment. If repayment is expected, do not describe the funds as a gift. Source and documentation requirements depend on the program, transaction, and relevant amount; every deposit does not trigger the same national test.

For liabilities, read the application together with the credit report and other relevant evidence. Include obligations according to the governing method, including ones that may not appear on a report. Student loans, leases, co-signed debts, support obligations, debts paid by someone else, and debts proposed for payoff require specific treatment. Do not assume a zero reported payment means no qualifying payment, or that a debt disappears because the applicant plans to pay it. Current agency guidance is the source for its program, and lender overlays may add requirements. [P3-09]

A credit report is a record to evaluate, not proof that every entry is correct. If an applicant disputes a possible error, record the issue and follow the approved process. The consumer can dispute inaccurate information with the reporting company and the company that furnished it. Do not promise a score increase, an item deletion, or an approval after a dispute. Never suggest disputing accurate information merely to suppress a debt. PE05 covers permissible purpose and notice duties. [P3-10]

### Calculate DTI with the right inputs

For the exercises below, housing ratio = monthly qualifying housing expense ÷ monthly qualifying income × 100. Total DTI = housing expense plus all other included monthly obligations, divided by monthly qualifying income, multiplied by 100. Use the housing amount specified for qualification, not only principal and interest. Taxes, property insurance, mortgage insurance, association dues, and a simultaneous loan payment may affect that amount as applicable.

Keep full precision through the division, then round the displayed percentage to two decimals. State the denominator and every included payment. A higher denominator lowers the ratio when the numerator is unchanged; using smaller net pay would raise the ratio, not improve it. A good reasonableness check compares the result with a familiar fraction, such as one third or one half.

There is no universal DTI cutoff taught in this module. Program, loan features, underwriting method, compensating factors, and overlays affect evaluation. A ratio within a supplied practice threshold is only a result under that assumption. It is not a commitment to lend. A household budget also includes expenses such as food, transportation, child care, repairs, and savings that may not all appear in the qualifying DTI. The lender's limit and the household's comfortable payment can differ.

### Worked example W3B Salary and changed debt

For this exercise only, the authorized review accepts base pay of $3,000 semimonthly. No overtime is included. The qualifying housing expense is $1,850 principal and interest + $300 property taxes + $100 property insurance + $90 mortgage insurance + $60 association dues = $2,400 monthly. Other included payments are a $350 vehicle loan, $150 student loan payment stipulated by the reviewer, and $100 revolving minimums.

Income = $3,000 × 24 ÷ 12 = $6,000. Housing ratio = $2,400 ÷ $6,000 = 40.00%. Total DTI = ($2,400 + $350 + $150 + $100) ÷ $6,000 = 50.00%. The $150 student loan input is a case assumption, not a general student loan rule. No program approval threshold is supplied, so the result is a calculation for review.

Before closing, the applicant finances an appliance with a $120 monthly payment. Assuming that payment must be included and all other inputs stay the same, total DTI becomes $3,120 ÷ $6,000 = 52.00%. The new fact must be recorded and the affected decision rechecked. Keeping the old percentage because it was previously reviewed would misdescribe the current file.

### Worked example W3B2 A business deposit is not salary

In a fictional simplified exercise, a business has $144,000 annual revenue and $84,000 recognized expenses. The reviewer permits a $6,000 noncash adjustment and supplies no other adjustments. Adjusted annual income for this calculation is $144,000 − $84,000 + $6,000 = $66,000, or $5,500 monthly. Revenue divided by 12 would be $12,000 and would ignore operating expenses.

This is arithmetic practice, not a method for underwriting a real tax return. The permitted adjustment and accepted period are stipulated. If the current year shows a decline, the learner must request a program appropriate trend review rather than apply the old average automatically. The accepted result can change after that review.

### Your case C3B The River file

Assume the reviewer accepts $2,800 biweekly base pay, with 26 pay periods annually. Do not use the applicant's separate $900 monthly bonus because its acceptability remains unresolved. Qualifying housing expense is $2,250 principal and interest, $350 taxes, $125 property insurance, $75 mortgage insurance, and $100 association dues. Included debts are $420 vehicle, $180 student loan under the supplied program assumption, and $100 revolving payments. There are no other included obligations for this exercise.

Calculate monthly income, total housing expense, housing ratio, and total DTI. Show the numerator and denominator. Then calculate the total DTI if an additional $160 monthly debt is included. Finish with one sentence stating what your calculation does and does not decide.

The same file shows $28,000 of eligible liquid assets. Remaining cash required to close is $22,500; assume this is after any previously paid deposit and there are no other deductions. The practice program requires two months of the $2,900 housing amount in reserves. Calculate funds left and any reserve shortfall. The two month requirement is invented for this case, not an agency rule. Do not count the money paid at closing again as reserves.

### Optional self-review D3B

Then choose one changed fact, such as fewer work hours or a proposed debt payoff using closing funds: name the input that would need a new authorized review before recalculating. Do not invent an accepted income amount or treat the same funds as both a payoff and reserves. Keep a concise numerical response and a two sentence explanation. ## Unit 3C Explain decisions and resolve conditions

### Read the decision rather than trusting its label

Prequalification and preapproval labels vary among lenders. Find out what information was reviewed, by whom, for what amount and product, subject to which conditions, and for what stated validity period. A letter based on stated income has a different evidentiary basis from a documented conditional decision. Even a later approval can depend on current facts, remaining conditions, and authorized closing steps. Describe the actual record rather than promising funding from a label.

An automated underwriting system evaluates supplied data and returns findings. Fannie Mae's Desktop Underwriter and Freddie Mac's Loan Product Advisor are examples. The lender remains responsible for accurate inputs, required documentation, eligibility, and its credit decision. A favorable DU recommendation or LPA risk class does not itself clear all conditions or establish legal compliance. Do not increase income, hide a debt, or change occupancy to obtain a more favorable result. Record true changes and obtain any required reassessment. [P3-11, P3-12]

Manual underwriting also follows applicable rules and documented authority. It is not permission to disregard a program requirement. An exception to a lender policy requires the right authority and a recorded basis; an internal exception cannot waive applicable law or another party's binding requirement. Ask which requirement is involved before asking who can approve an exception.

### Occupancy and collateral need factual review

Record the applicant's actual plans for the property, current housing, and relevant supporting facts. A lease, proposed rental arrangement, second residence, or change in work location can create a question that needs clarification. It does not by itself prove fraud. Ask a neutral question, preserve the evidence, and send the inconsistency to the responsible reviewer. Never choose an occupancy label simply to reach a better price.

An appraisal supplies a professional opinion of value under its stated scope and assumptions. Its receipt does not establish that all collateral requirements are met. The lender may need to review value support, condition, property type, project eligibility, insurance, and title. Some eligible transactions use other authorized valuation routes; do not promise that every file receives an identical appraisal process.

For a suspected factual error, use the creditor's reconsideration process and provide relevant evidence without pressure for a target value. Valuation independence rules prohibit improper influence while allowing appropriate requests for correction or additional consideration. A contract price alone is not evidence that an appraiser must raise a conclusion. [P3-13]

### Worked example W3C A changed value

Assume a fictional purchase program uses the lower of price or accepted appraised value as the LTV denominator. The price is $400,000, the requested loan is $320,000, and the accepted value is $380,000. LTV = $320,000 ÷ $380,000 = 84.21%. Using price alone would give 80.00% and would ignore the stated rule.

If this exercise caps LTV at 80%, the maximum loan under that single assumption is $380,000 × 80% = $304,000. Keeping the $400,000 price requires a $96,000 difference between price and loan, before closing costs and other adjustments. That is $16,000 more than the original $80,000 difference. This does not establish available funds, eligibility, a right to cancel, or an obligation to renegotiate. The actual program and contract must be reviewed.

### Track each condition to its decision owner

A useful condition record names the question, required evidence, responsible person, status, and decision date. Separate evidence pending, review pending, and cleared. A processor can coordinate receipt without having authority to clear the credit question. A settlement agent can resolve a title requirement within its role without deciding the borrower's income. Roles and delegated authority vary by firm; verify them rather than assigning power from a job title.

### Your case C3C The Oak file

On workday 8, an authorized underwriter conditionally approves a fictional purchase using the income and assets then reviewed. On workday 10, the applicant reports reduced scheduled hours. On workday 11, a new bank statement is uploaded, and the processor marks uploads complete. The appraisal has a possible room count error. A sales partner asks for an unconditional approval letter today because the contract deadline is tomorrow.

Choose a current status: ready to fund, all conditions cleared, or changed facts and review outstanding. Prepare three short handoff lines: one for income, one for the bank record, and one for the appraisal concern. Each line needs the source fact, the open decision, and the responsible review function. Write a two sentence applicant update that avoids a funding promise. Do not decide contractual rights or send the applicant's records to the sales partner.

### Optional self-review D3C

Write which condition you would route first and why. Consider a different order and identify a possible consequence, such as a time sensitive disclosure review or a changed cash requirement. Challenge one status label or authority assumption in your own record: which source fact supports it, and who may decide it? Revise any unsupported statement. ## Key terms

**Qualifying income:** income accepted for a particular underwriting analysis after applying its rules. **DTI:** included monthly debt payments divided by accepted monthly income. **Reserve:** eligible assets remaining after closing under the applicable program definition. **Overlay:** an additional lender or investor requirement beyond a referenced program baseline. **Condition:** an unresolved requirement tied to an approval or later release. **AUS:** an automated underwriting system that produces findings from supplied data. **Source map:** a record connecting a material fact to evidence, review, and any later change.
