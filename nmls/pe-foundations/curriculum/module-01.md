- Trace a file through broker, lender, processor, underwriter, investor and servicer roles and identify their decision limits

## Unit 1A Follow the file and the authority

### A mortgage is both a transaction and an ongoing obligation

A household purchasing a home may need money before it can pay the seller. A residential mortgage loan provides credit secured by property. The borrower promises repayment; the security arrangement gives the creditor rights against the property subject to the contract and applicable law. The amount borrowed, payment schedule and security are related but distinct. PE02 will separate them in detail.

Origination is the work of bringing a proposed loan from the initial request toward a completed credit transaction. Servicing is the work of administering an existing loan. A borrower can encounter different firms at these stages without having taken out a new loan. The company that receives monthly payments may be different from the company that originally made the loan. [S1]

Start a file map with three questions: Who is the borrower dealing with now? What function is that person performing? What decision is that person actually authorized to make? A familiar company name or senior job title is not a substitute for the answer.

### Six roles you need before learning the detailed rules

| Role | Typical function | Boundary to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mortgage broker | Arranges financing between a borrower and a lender | In a brokerage transaction, the broker is not automatically the funding creditor or final credit decision maker |
| Lender or creditor | Extends the credit under the transaction documents | Identify the actual legal entity and applicable rules, not only a trade name |
| Processor | Collects, organizes and tracks information and conditions | Collecting evidence does not by itself confer authority to clear underwriting conditions or discuss terms |
| Underwriter | Evaluates the file against applicable credit requirements and assigned authority | A decision may be conditional, limited to specified facts, or subject to later review |
| Investor or loan owner | Owns or purchases mortgage assets, sometimes through a securitization structure | Ownership and payment administration are not necessarily performed by the same firm |
| Servicer | Collects payments and administers the account, including applicable escrow and borrower inquiries | Servicing authority does not mean the servicer originated or owns the loan |

These are working descriptions, not a universal job chart. A firm may perform several functions. A lender may delegate particular underwriting work. A person may hold both origination and management duties. A title such as loan officer, coordinator or mortgage consultant does not establish the person's license, employment relationship or delegated authority. Check the actual arrangement.

Other participants support the file. An appraiser develops an opinion of value within the assignment and applicable standards. A title or settlement professional investigates or coordinates title and closing matters within that professional's role. A real estate agent helps with the property transaction. A housing counselor can help a consumer understand options. None becomes the lender's underwriter merely because the closing is near.

### What changes between a broker and a lender

For this module, Cedar Desk Mortgage is a brokerage: it arranges loans but does not make the fictional loan in its own name. Harborline Lending is the proposed creditor. Cedar's originator explains the available terms and gathers information; Harborline makes the credit decision under its procedures. The borrower should not be told that Cedar is the bank or that Cedar can guarantee Harborline's decision.

A mortgage banking business may make loans in its own name and later sell them. It may use a warehouse credit facility to fund loans pending sale. In correspondent lending, the originating lender may sell a funded loan to another institution. Actual contracts, funding and delegation matter; these labels are not shortcuts to a legal classification. A broker's access to several lenders also does not establish that every lender or product in the market was considered.

The practical explanation is short and specific: “Our firm is acting as your mortgage broker on this file. Harborline is the proposed lender. We will submit the requested information and communicate the lender's decision and any conditions.” This wording describes the stipulated case. It does not promise approval or a particular rate.

### Worked file example

Sam Lee seeks financing for a home purchase. Cedar's originator Ana collects Sam's application and explains a proposed fixed rate product. Eli, the processor, requests a missing bank statement page. Harborline's underwriter Noor issues a conditional decision requiring review of complete asset records. Cedar's portal then displays “Documents uploaded.” A closing coordinator sees the portal and says the assets condition is cleared.

The last statement is unsupported. The upload establishes receipt of a file. It does not establish completeness, review or clearance. Noor has the stipulated authority to clear the underwriting condition. Eli should identify the received record and route it to Noor; the closing coordinator should rely on a documented decision, not the upload label. A useful entry is: “Statement pages received today; completeness check completed by Eli; asset condition awaiting Noor's review.” This fictional example practices the distinction between a source record, an operational status and an authorized decision.

Notice how little writing the correction requires. A reliable record names the evidence, the decision owner and the unresolved question. A long narrative saying “everything looks good” can be less useful than a single precise sentence.

### Practice FP-PE01-R1 Sort six actions

For each action, select the most directly responsible role in the stipulated workflow: broker originator, processor, underwriter, investor, servicer, or title and settlement specialist. Use each role once. Record only the role and one relevant fact.

1. Explain the brokerage's role and collect the borrower's requested financing information
2. Obtain the missing page of the identified statement through the approved channel
3. Determine whether the completed records satisfy Harborline's asset condition
4. Purchase the funded mortgage asset from Harborline
5. Investigate why a payment on the existing loan was credited to the wrong month
6. Investigate an unreleased lien identified in the title work

Now identify the unsupported conclusion in “An investor is interested, so the borrower is approved.” Select one: A the loan amount is necessarily too high; B investor interest is not the creditor's documented approval; C the investor must collect payments; D the broker must be a bank.

### Optional reflection FP-PE01-D1 A useful handoff

Optional self-review: Write two sentences naming the owner of the assets decision and the evidence needed before changing “pending” to “cleared.” Then change one fact: Sam reports spending part of those assets after the clearance.

Self-review checklist: Did you name Noor as the authorized reviewer in this case? Did you distinguish upload, completeness and review? Did you route the changed asset information back for a current decision rather than assuming yesterday's clearance still applies? A precise correction is more useful than “everything looks good.” No instructor response is supplied or implied.

### Check FP-PE01-K1

Noor approved the assets condition yesterday. Today Sam reports using part of those funds for a vehicle purchase. What is the best next action? A Keep yesterday's clearance because it already exists; B Treat the statement as proven fraud; C Document the changed fact and route its effect to the authorized reviewer; D Ask the settlement agent to change the bank statement. Select one and identify the changed fact.

## Unit 1B Understand SAFE without assuming a license

### The national foundation

SAFE is the Secure and Fair Enforcement for Mortgage Licensing Act. Its framework supports identification, accountability and minimum standards for mortgage loan originators. NMLS is the licensing and registry system through which relevant state licensing and federal registration are administered. It is not a lender, an underwriter or a guarantee of competence on every transaction.

Regulation H defines a residential mortgage loan for this purpose by its primarily personal, family or household use and its specified dwelling or residential real estate security. The definition also reaches residential land on which a dwelling is intended to be constructed. Do not decide coverage only from the property label or a borrower's unchecked business purpose box. Different statutes can use different definitions. [S2]

### Two pathways with different legal bases

Under Regulation G, an employee of a covered financial institution who acts as an MLO generally must register, obtain a unique identifier and maintain that registration. The rule has specified exceptions, including a limited de minimis exception; compliance must check their conditions. Covered institutions are defined in the rule and include specified banks and credit unions. Do not assume that every affiliate of a bank qualifies merely because of its name. [S3, S4]

State licensing generally applies to other covered origination activity under the applicable state framework. Regulation H states the federal minimum that state systems must meet, with defined exclusions and exceptions. A state can impose requirements that require a separate state analysis. A person changing employers or states must check the applicable pathway before acting; prior registration is not automatically a new state license. A limited temporary authority route may exist for eligible people under specific conditions, but it is not permission every applicant receives by filing. [S5, S6]

| Question | State licensing pathway | Federal registration pathway |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What must be identified first | State agency, activity and applicable license category | Covered institution, employee status and MLO activity |
| Where is the record administered | NMLS, with state agency licensing decisions | NMLS Federal Registry under Regulation G |
| What a public identifier establishes | The identity associated with a record, not current authority everywhere | The identity associated with a record, not a state license |
| What needs continuing attention | Current license status, required sponsorship and state conditions | Current employment, accurate registration and renewal obligations |

This comparison is an orientation, not a checklist for a personal application. An institution's compliance function must evaluate the actual facts. Never use a course exercise to make a declaration about your own eligibility.

### Activities matter more than titles

Regulation H's federal baseline includes, in a commercial context and habitually or repeatedly, taking residential applications and offering or negotiating terms for compensation or gain; it also addresses public representations that a person can or will perform those activities. Independent contractor processing or underwriting has its own licensing treatment. Exceptions for clerical work have conditions. “I am only an assistant” does not complete this analysis. State requirements must also be checked. [S5]

Clerical collection and distribution of information can differ from offering or negotiating terms. Under the Regulation H definitions, support communications to obtain needed information do not include counseling consumers about rates or terms. Compare “Please upload page two of this statement” with “Choose this loan; I can change its price for you.” The second is not merely document collection. [S2]

Do not memorize an unconditional rule that a processor is always exempt, or that a single word such as salary eliminates compensation. When the work approaches the boundary, record the activity, employment or contractor arrangement, compensation context, property and purpose, then route the question to the licensing and compliance owner. The purpose of this exercise is to recognize what must be checked, not to issue legal opinions from a job description.

### Education is one requirement among several

The federal minimum for state licensure includes at least 20 hours of NMLS reviewed and approved PE, including at least 3 hours of federal law, 3 hours of ethics covering fraud, consumer protection and fair lending, and 2 hours of nontraditional lending. Other minimum requirements include a licensing test, background and credit related review, financial responsibility and fitness, and specified financial protection requirements. A course completion alone does not grant a license. [S7]

The national SAFE MLO test includes Uniform State Content. Its coverage addresses common state regulatory concepts such as regulator authority, licensing, renewal, sponsorship and prohibited conduct. This does not mean that each state's substantive law is identical. The national test, an education course's own final, and a state license are distinct things. State specific education or other requirements may remain even after national PE and a passing national test. [S8, S9]

For example, a learner may have completed required national education but still need a state's specified education, an approved sponsorship arrangement or another application item. Another learner may have a long standing NMLS identifier but no authority for a proposed new state's activity. The correct response is a current checklist and status review, not a guess based on the identifier's age.

### Practice FP-PE01-R2 Read a fictional licensing record

Record A says: Morgan, NMLS identifier present; employed by a covered bank; current federal registration shown; no state license shown. Record B says: Taylor, NMLS identifier present; nonbank employer; state application submitted; state status pending; no temporary authority determination provided.

Select the best conclusion for each: 1 federal registration pathway may apply on the stipulated facts; 2 pending application alone does not establish authority to originate; 3 universal permission in all states; 4 approval of every loan submitted. Then select the missing fact that must be resolved before Taylor acts: A Taylor's favorite product; B the applicable agency's authorization and any actual temporary authority determination; C the property's paint color; D how many people work at Morgan's bank.

### Source lookup FP-PE01-L1

Use the current NMLS Completing Pre Licensure Education resource in S9. Locate the link for state specific education requirements, then locate the relevant agency's current licensing checklist through the NMLS State Resource Center. Choose one jurisdiction to research; this is an optional source-navigation exercise, not advice to apply there.

Save four fields: agency and license type; official source URL; source revision date if shown and today's access date; one requirement you would have to verify beyond approved national PE. If a page has moved or a revision date is absent, record that limitation and use the agency's current official site or NMLS State Resource Center to continue. Do not invent a date or use an unverified replacement. Do not invent a requirement or enter personal identifiers. Keep state details in this lookup record rather than turning them into a national rule. [S9, S10]

### Optional reflection FP-PE01-D2 Correct the claim

Optional reflection: A colleague says, “Once I pass the national test and have an NMLS number, I can originate anywhere.” Write a two-sentence correction and name a fact that could change the licensing pathway.

Self-review checklist: Separate the national test and identifier from current state authority or applicable federal registration. Check the jurisdiction, actual activity, employer and applicable conditions. A change from a covered bank to a nonbank employer is a reason to review the pathway. State-specific requirements do not disappear because test content is national. Use S3 through S10 to check your explanation.

## Unit 1C Connect the markets to the consumer

### Primary and secondary markets

The primary mortgage market is where a lender makes a mortgage loan to a borrower. The secondary market concerns existing mortgages and mortgage related securities. A lender can sell loans and use the proceeds to support further lending. Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac buy mortgages and may hold them or package them into mortgage backed securities. They are important secondary market participants; they are not the retail loan officer in this case. [S11]

Imagine Harborline lends a hypothetical $240,000. After funding, Harborline sells the mortgage asset. That sale can replenish funds available for new lending. Sam still has a repayment obligation under the loan terms. Ownership and servicing can move together or separately; a sale of the loan does not itself tell Sam where to send the next payment. Payment instructions and notices must be verified. This example explains the flow rather than describing an actual investment transaction.

Why does this matter before closing? A lender that intends to sell a loan may have to meet a purchaser's delivery standards. The lender may also impose its own requirements. Those conditions can affect documentation or available products. A lender holding a loan in its portfolio still operates under applicable law and its own risk policies. “Portfolio” does not mean that borrower protections disappear.

Do not treat product labels as interchangeable. Conventional means a loan is not government insured or guaranteed. Conforming refers to meeting specified purchase requirements for a conforming channel. Jumbo describes a loan amount beyond an applicable conforming limit, not a legal exemption. Qualified mortgage is a separate regulatory concept addressed later. This module does not supply current loan limits or determine a product's eligibility. A loan may need analysis under several labels at once.

### Law program rule and company policy

Before asking for an exception, identify which source created the requirement. A legal requirement, an investor delivery standard, an insurance or guaranty program condition, and a company's own rule can all apply to the same file. The person authorized to make an internal exception cannot waive a statute merely by approving a memo. A program exception also needs authority under that program.

Worked example: Harborline's fictional policy P4 requires an additional internal review of a particular document. The policy expressly allows its named credit manager to authorize an alternative review. Compliance has already confirmed, for this exercise only, that the proposed change does not conflict with law or another program. The originator may request that narrow policy decision. The request should name P4, the alternative and the decision owner. “Waive all conditions” goes beyond those facts. P4 is an invented company policy, not a federal requirement.

### Use the right source for the question

| Consumer or file question | Start with | What to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is this individual authorized for this activity here | Relevant state agency or federal registration record, then compliance review | Treating a number as a national license |
| Who can clear this income condition | The creditor's documented decision process | Treating a broker's optimistic update as clearance |
| Why did my payment or escrow amount change | Verified current servicer and the actual account records | Guessing that the original broker owns the loan |
| Who owns my mortgage now | Current servicing information and an ownership inquiry through a verified route | Confusing the payment address with proof of ownership |
| Which federal consumer rule applies | Current primary rule and the firm's compliance function | Assuming an investor guide replaces federal law |
| Is this lien valid or what does foreclosure permit | Title professional and qualified legal counsel in the jurisdiction | Importing another state's remedy |

CFPB materials explain how to identify a loan owner and how to raise a mortgage complaint. A consumer can use the CFPB complaint process or the relevant regulator's verified complaint channel. Which agency supervises an institution depends on its charter, activity and legal framework. State regulators and federal banking or credit union regulators may have different responsibilities. NMLS technical support is not the authority for approving a consumer's loan or resolving every legal dispute. [S12, S13]

When recording a complaint, preserve what the consumer said and the relevant dates. Do not promise the regulator's result, accuse someone of wrongdoing without evidence, or disclose a complete borrower file to an unverified recipient. Use the firm's approved channel and provide only information appropriate to the request. The detailed legal notice and complaint rules are taught in later modules.

### Practice FP-PE01-R3 The Oak Street handoff

Leah applied through a broker. The proposed lender issued a conditional approval. A processor has uploaded the requested records but no later underwriting decision is in the file. A sales message says, “Our investor has accepted your loan, so all approvals are finished.” Leah also asks whether a recent ownership notice means she should stop paying the current servicer.

Select the best next action for each issue:

1. Closing status: A mark complete; B obtain the authorized condition review; C delete the conditional approval; D assume investor acceptance is the underwriting decision
2. Payment destination: A stop payments immediately; B use an unverified text message's bank details; C verify current servicing instructions and the notice through established channels; D send payment to the broker
3. Source of a new documentation requirement: A assume it is federal law; B identify whether it comes from law, program, investor or company policy; C ask a peer to vote on it; D remove it because the borrower dislikes it

Create a three row decision record using the fields issue, evidence or missing fact, and responsible next step. Short phrases are sufficient. Keep the loan ownership issue separate from servicing instructions.

### Optional reflection FP-PE01-D3 Explain what the market does not prove

Optional reflection: Rewrite the sales message in the Oak Street case in two or three sentences. Explain one way a secondary-market requirement can affect a file.

Self-review checklist: Your correction should keep the underwriting condition unresolved until the authorized review is documented. Investor purchase or delivery standards may affect required documentation, but investor interest or loan sale alone does not clear every condition. Keep loan ownership separate from current servicing instructions, and identify the source of any new requirement before discussing an exception.

### Checks FP-PE01-K4 and FP-PE01-K5

FP-PE01-K4. A lender plans to keep a loan in its own portfolio. Which conclusion follows? A Federal law no longer applies; B the loan must be government insured; C the lender's holding strategy does not by itself remove legal requirements; D no underwriting is necessary.

FP-PE01-K5. A borrower wants to know who currently owns the loan. Which source is the best starting point? A A three year old real estate listing; B a verified inquiry to the current servicer using the ownership information process; C the newest social media comment; D the broker's company logo.
