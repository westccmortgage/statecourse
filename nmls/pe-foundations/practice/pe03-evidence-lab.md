## Document reading aids

### Read a pay record in this order

Identify the creator and the person it concerns, the pay period, payment date, stated pay frequency, current base and additional earnings, gross total, deductions, net amount, and year-to-date columns. Current and year-to-date columns measure different periods. A bank deposit is usually a net cash receipt; do not substitute it for gross qualifying income.

### Read a bank record in this order

Locate the holder, symbolic account label, statement period, page count, opening and closing balances, and transaction details. A missing page is a specific evidence gap even when the displayed totals balance. Add ending balances from the same date; do not then add the transfers already reflected in them.

### Four states answer four different questions

Requested: what was asked for? Received: what arrived and when? Reviewed: who examined it, and for what purpose? Accepted: who had authority to decide that a stated requirement was met? A completeness review can identify a missing page without accepting the assets. Track these events separately, not as interchangeable labels.

### Separate three kinds of statement

A source fact is a field in an exhibit. A derived result is arithmetic using identified fields. An accepted input is a case-specific decision expressly supplied by the fictional reviewer. The source notes below contain legal or program concepts; they do not turn a fictional reviewer choice into a rule.

## Primary source concepts and limits

**S01 · program guidance · B3-3.2-01.** Borrower-provided employment records must identify their third-party source and be complete and legible. A paystub needs enough information for the income calculation, including year-to-date earnings. A borrower upload does not change who originated the record. [Standards for Employment and Income Documentation](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b3-3.2-01/standards-employment-and-income-documentation)

Scope: Fannie Mae employment-income documentation; not a universal legal rule or a complete checklist. Verified 2026-10-01. Page revision date: 2026-03-04.

**S02 · program guidance · B3-3.3-01.** For fixed base pay, biweekly gross pay is converted using 26 payments divided by 12 months; twice-monthly pay uses two payments per month. The Guide also distinguishes fixed from variable base income and calls for consistency with year-to-date base earnings. This calculation alone does not establish eligibility. [Base Income](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b3-3.3-01/base-income)

Scope: Fannie Mae base-income treatment. Overtime is a separate income category; this lab supplies no overtime-eligibility method. Verified 2026-10-01. Page revision date: 2026-03-04.

**S03 · program guidance · B3-4.2-02.** Funds used for a down payment, closing costs, or reserves must be verified. A transfer whose source is identifiable on statements between verified accounts can be treated differently from an unexplained deposit. Indications of borrowed funds still require attention. [Depository Accounts](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b3-4.2-02/depository-accounts)

Scope: Fannie Mae depository assets. This lab does not teach large-deposit thresholds, all verification alternatives, or an account-exclusion rule. Verified 2026-10-01. Page revision date: 2022-12-14.

**S04 · federal regulation with covered-transaction scope · 12 CFR 1026.43(c)(3), (c)(4), and (c)(7).** For covered transactions, income or assets relied on must be verified with reasonably reliable third-party records, subject to the regulation. The official interpretation of paragraph (c)(7) does not prescribe one specific DTI limit. A computed ratio alone therefore does not decide this case. [Minimum standards for transactions secured by a dwelling](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/43/)

Scope: Covered ability-to-repay determinations; the regulation contains exceptions and requirements not reproduced here. Verified 2026-10-01.

The linked primary pages were read on October 1, 2026. Page revision dates differ from this verification date and from any applicable effective date. Check current law, program instructions, transaction coverage, and lender authority before using a real file. The concise concepts above support document-reading habits; they are not a whole underwriting policy.
