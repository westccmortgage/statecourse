Marketing and Licensing of NMLS Approved Courses Policy

This policy establishes the requirements governing the licensing, marketing, promotion, and distribution of NMLS-approved courses.

Purpose

This policy is intended to prevent fraud, eliminate student confusion, and ensure consistent and accurate representation of NMLS approved education.

Background and Reason for the Policy

The Secure and Fair Enforcement for Mortgage Licensing Act (SAFE Act) requires that all pre licensing (PE) and continuing education (CE) courses be reviewed and approved by the Nationwide Multistate Licensing System (NMLS). As such, any course taken by a state-licensed loan originator that has not been reviewed and approved by NMLS does not satisfy the PE or annual CE requirement as stipulated by the SAFE Act. To assist in the prevention of fraud and/or to eliminate confusion in the marketplace, this policy details the various policies that may be employed to promote, resell, and/or market NMLS approved courses. Additionally, this policy details when and how the NMLS name, the “NMLS Approved” phrase, and and/or the “NMLS Approved” logo may be used to promote and/or market courses.

Definitions

Affiliate
Any person or entity that has a relationship with an NMLS Approved Course Provider and engages in marketing, promotional, referral, or lead generation activities on behalf of that provider. Affiliates can be NMLS approved providers themselves and working with another approved course provider. Affiliates are also referred to as third parties.
Licensed Course
The originally approved NMLS course owned by the licensor. A licensee may offer the licensed course only after obtaining its own separate Course ID through NMLS course approval. Also referred to as a “parent” course.
Licensing Agreement
An agreement permitting the use of course content and/or technical capability (LMS and supporting technology) by an NMLS Approved Course Provider.
NMLS Approved Course (also referred to as “course”)
A course which has been submitted by an NMLS approved course provider and has been reviewed and approved by NMLS to satisfy the education requirements of the SAFE Act. Every NMLS approved course has a unique course ID number which is associated with only one NMLS approved course provider.
NMLS Approved Course Provider (also referred to as “provider”)
An organization which applied and has subsequently been approved by NMLS. Providers are responsible for obtaining and maintaining NMLS approval of PE and/or CE courses intended to satisfy the education requirements of the SAFE Act. Providers are responsible for course registration and delivery, charging fees, issuing refunds, issuing course completion certificates, performing credit banking, and retaining appropriate records.
Referral Marketing
A marketing arrangement between an NMLS approved course provider and an affiliate in which the affiliate refers a potential student to a course (or list of courses) owned and offered by an NMLS approved course provider.
Reseller
An entity that sells or accepts payment for a course on behalf of an NMLS approved provider. Reselling of NMLS approved courses is prohibited.

Policy

NMLS Approved Course Providers retain full responsibility for the delivery, administration, and marketing of their approved course.

NMLS approved providers may enter into licensing and referral marketing arrangements; however, such arrangements must not obscure the identity or responsibilities of the approved course provider. Under no circumstances may any entity other than the provider collect payment, process registrations, or present itself as an NMLS approved provider.

All marketing and communications must accurately represent the course provider, course information, and approval status. The use of NMLS names, designations, and trademarks must comply with this policy.

Course provider responsibilities, including but not limited to course delivery, student interaction, payment processing, and credit reporting, may not be delegated to any other entity.

NMLS approved course providers must abide by the Standards of Conduct for Approved Course Providers Per the SOC, NMLS reserves the right to review, monitor, and audit provider activities, including marketing practices, affiliate activities, and course delivery, to ensure compliance with this policy. Providers shall, upon request, provide documentation sufficient to demonstrate compliance.

Marketing Standards

All marketing must comply with the following requirements:
  1. All marketing must be truthful and be void of any misleading statements, promises, or guarantees that cannot be reasonably fulfilled.
  2. A course may not be promoted in such a way as to guarantee that an individual will pass the SAFE MLO Test or be issued a license after completing the course.
  3. Providers are expected to substantiate any claims made in marketing or promotional materials, including but not limited to pass rates or test performance.
  4. A course may not be promoted in a way that includes unrealistic or unsupported claims.
  5. All marketing is to be conducted in a manner that is free of disparaging and/or negative language of any individual, organization, entity, or federal/state licensing entity.
  6. Provider websites must, at a minimum, clearly post the following:
    • the organization’s business address,
    • phone number(s),
    • contact e-mail address,
    • terms and conditions,
    • privacy policy,
    • refund policy,
    • cancellation policy.
  7. Providers shall routinely monitor websites and marketing materials for, at a minimum:
    • Outdated information, requirements, laws, or test references,
    • Misuse of NMLS logos,
    • Broken links, obsolete outlines, inaccurate course descriptions,
    • Out-of-date course offerings,
    • Misleading affiliate redirection.
  8. All marketing used to promote an NMLS approved course, including websites, landing pages, ad banners, print marketing materials, direct mail, e-mail, brochures, etc., must, at a minimum, clearly display the following:
    • Provider name (as documented with NMLS),
    • Course name (as approved by NMLS),
    • NMLS course ID (as assigned by NMLS).
  9. Course providers may use the words “NMLS Approved” to market themselves only if they have received written approval from NMLS and continue to remain in an approved status by NMLS. Course providers that have had their approval status suspended may not promote themselves or their courses as NMLS approved.
  10. Courses may be promoted using the words “NMLS Approved” with the NMLS approved course ID logo only after the provider has received written approval for the course by NMLS, and so long as the course remains in an approved status.
  11. The term “accredited” is prohibited. NMLS only “approves” course providers and courses.
  12. Any term or terms other than “NMLS Approved” to market or promote an approval status is prohibited.
  13. Providers shall ensure that consumer data collected and retained is handled in accordance with applicable state and/or federal data protection and privacy laws.

Referral Marketing Agreement Standards

NMLS approved course providers may enter into referral marketing agreements with affiliates to acquire students. Such arrangements shall be governed by written agreements that clearly define roles, responsibilities, and compliance obligations. Providers must disclose referral marketing agreements to NMLS and are responsible for the actions of affiliates. Providers shall take prompt corrective action upon identification of noncompliant activity.

Course providers and their affiliates must adhere to the following practices:
  1. An affiliate website may host a link (text, banner, image, etc.) directing users to the provider’s website where the course is described and available for registration.
  2. Providers must ensure affiliate content clearly discloses:
    1. Provider’s full name (as approved by NMLS),
    2. Course name and course ID.
  3. Providers are responsible for monitoring affiliates and ensuring marketing does not mislead users into believing the affiliate is approved, endorsed by, or affiliated with NMLS.
  4. Affiliates may not perform any marketing activity that creates the appearance that they are the approved course provider and/or that it is selling, hosting, or doing anything else that may lead a prospective student to believe that they are the approved course provider and/or the owner of the approved course.
  5. Affiliates may not take any registrations and/or collect any fees on behalf of the approved course provider.
  6. No entity can display or use the NMLS Approved Course ID logo. This logo is reserved for exclusive use by the approved course provider.
  7. An entity, whether an approved course provider or not, cannot sell (accept payment for) a course on behalf of an NMLS approved course provider.
  8. All payments must be made directly to the NMLS Approved Course Provider.
  9. Payment redirection, co-branded checkout pages, or any mechanism that obscures or confuses the identity of the entity receiving payment is prohibited.
  10. Affiliates may not be compensated in a manner that incentivizes misleading marketing practices or creates the appearance that the third party is the course provider.
  11. In accordance with the Standard of Conduct for NMLS Approved Course providers, NMLS will hold the provider responsible for any misleading or deceptive marketing practices, including those practices of its affiliates.

Licensing of Course Content Standards

Providers may license course content and/or technical capability (LMS and supporting technology) from other approved providers or from third parties subject to the following:
  1. The receiving provider must be NMLS approved and must submit the course for independent approval and will receive a unique course ID.
  2. The receiving provider must demonstrate the capability to deliver the course in the approved format as part of acquiring course approval.
  3. The receiving provider must disclose and maintain documented proof of authorization for the use of licensed materials and/or technology.
  4. The provider delivering the course is responsible for instruction, payments, refunds, support, reporting and retention of course records.
  5. The provider delivering the course must always remain clearly identifiable to students.
  6. Licensed courses must comply with all NMLS requirements.
  7. The provider offering a licensed CE course must retain the course title as was initially approved by NMLS. This will serve as an obvious indicator on the individual’s education record if the individual is compliant or not with the SAFE Act's Successive Year Rule

Trademark Protection and Enforcement

The Nationwide Multistate Licensing System, Nationwide Mortgage Licensing System, Nationwide Mortgage Licensing System & Registry, NMLS, NMLS-R, NMLS Approved, NMLS Reviewed, and the NMLS Approved course logo are all registered trademarks of the Conference of State Bank Supervisors (CSBS) and the State Regulatory Registry, LLC. NMLS names, logos, and trademarks may only be used as authorized. They must not be used in company names, URLs, or branding in a manner that implies ownership, endorsement, or affiliation beyond what is permitted. CSBS will enforce and defend any use of its trademarks which is inconsistent with this policy.

Verification and Consumer Protection

NMLS maintains an NMLS Approved Course Provider List on the NMLS Resource Center website which contains the providers that have at least one course approved by NMLS. Industry professionals are encouraged to check this list prior to enrolling in any PE or CE course.

Compliance and Enforcement

Failure to comply with this policy may result in corrective action, up to and including suspension or revocation of approval in accordance with the Administrative Action Procedures for SAFE Act Education Requirements NMLS Approved Course Providers.

Corrective actions may include, but are not limited to:
  • Written warnings or required remediation
  • Mandatory removal or correction of noncompliant marketing or practices
  • Suspension of course or provider approval
  • Revocation of approval

NMLS may establish timelines for remediation and may take immediate action where student harm or significant risk is identified.

Providers shall cooperate with all NMLS compliance reviews, investigations, and audits. Failure to cooperate may result in additional enforcement action.

Providers may be required to demonstrate ongoing compliance as a condition of maintaining approval.
