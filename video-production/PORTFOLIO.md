The first coverage target is **two high-value instructional videos per built state**, with California
and Texas retaining their existing larger sets. This is a starting coverage target, not a promise to
force two decorative videos into every course. A video is selected only when it materially improves
a document walkthrough, relationship, decision sequence, calculation or act of professional judgment.

## Non-negotiable release gate

No instructional video becomes active until all of the following exist and pass review:

1. final MP4;
2. poster image;
3. shipping WebVTT captions timed from the final render, never provisional timings;
4. separate plain-text transcript;
5. complete text equivalent in the lesson;
6. factual/source review;
7. visual and document-fidelity review;
8. logo-safe responsive placement review;
9. accessibility and keyboard review; and
10. exact non-assessment instruction-screen placement with optional, non-blocking playback and no autoplay.

The machine-readable controlling ledger is `video-production/portfolio.json`. Tests reconcile its
14 states and 30 courses to the public catalog so this document cannot silently overstate coverage.
