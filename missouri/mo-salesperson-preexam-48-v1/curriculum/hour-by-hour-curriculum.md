# Missouri Salesperson Pre-Exam 48h v1.0 — Hour-by-Hour Curriculum

Regulatory status: **Development; not approved; no enrollment or credit.**

This file contains 48 auditable instructional units. Each unit has its own objectives, reading, state layer, applied work, knowledge check, and remediation.

## MO48-01 — Property ownership and land use (7 hours)

Authority: Current testing-service outline required by 20 CSR 2250-6.060(1)

## Hour 001 — Real versus personal property

Module: MO48-01 · Required engagement: 60 minutes · Content layer: NC-OWN

### Learning objectives

By the end of this hour, the student can:

1. explain real versus personal property using accurate professional vocabulary;
2. apply real versus personal property to a state-specific transaction fact pattern; and
3. identify the controlling source, required record, and point for professional referral.

### Instructional reading

**Concept and professional purpose.** Real versus personal property is studied as part of Property ownership and land use. The student begins by identifying the people, property interests, documents, money, deadlines, and public-protection purpose involved. The lesson distinguishes a fact from an assumption, a legal requirement from a provider practice, and a licensee's task from work that must be referred to an attorney, lender, appraiser, surveyor, inspector, tax professional, or other qualified specialist.

**Transaction application.** The student applies the concept to a realistic file. The file is read chronologically: source document, consumer communication, decision point, required disclosure or agreement, performance, and retained evidence. A compliant response identifies controlling authority, verifies material facts from an appropriate source, communicates within the licensee's role, documents the action, and escalates unresolved legal or technical issues. Marketing language, form selection, compensation, and technology do not override the state rule.

**State-law layer.** Missouri layer: apply Current testing-service outline required by 20 CSR 2250-6.060(1), Chapter 339 RSMo, 20 CSR 2250, Commission-prescribed forms, and the currently approved testing-service outline where applicable. The 48-hour and 24-hour records remain separate. Source dates and form editions are checked at controlled release. If a cited source changes, the lesson is held from new enrollment until the course owner completes impact review and records a new content version.

**National-core relationship.** This unit may reuse the concepts in NC-OWN, but its state examples, terminology, forms, citations, assessment record, and approval mapping are versioned independently for Missouri.

### Applied activity

Analyze a Missouri transaction fact pattern involving **real versus personal property**. Build a five-entry audit note: (1) known fact, (2) missing fact, (3) controlling source, (4) permitted licensee action, and (5) referral or follow-up. Explain why payment status, course progress, and regulatory credit are separate records.

### Knowledge check and remediation

2. What fact must be verified before the licensee communicates or acts?
3. What evidence belongs in the transaction or course audit record?

If any response is incorrect, the LMS assigns the source excerpt, the worked scenario, and a new parallel check. The original answer is not exposed as a substitute for remediation. Hour 1 is complete only after the tracked instructional sequence and required check are complete.
