# Missouri Salesperson Pre-Examination Course — Module MO-09

## Hours 45–48 · Missouri licence law, the Commission, and professional conduct

**Clock hours:** 4. A Missouri clock hour is **60 minutes containing at least 50 minutes of actual
instruction** and no more than 10 minutes of recess — 20 CSR 2250-7.070(2).
**Content basis:** a StateCourse organisation for Commission review. **20 CSR 2250-6.060(1) requires
the 48-hour course to be developed from the testing service's detailed content outline, which this

**Regulatory status:** Development. Not approved. No Missouri pre-license credit is available.

## Learning objectives

1. Describe the Missouri Real Estate Commission's place in the regulatory structure and the sources of
   Missouri real estate law.
2. State the licensing requirements of § 339.040, RSMo, including the six-month education window.
3. Describe the education a Missouri salesperson must complete and how the two courses differ.
4. Describe how a complaint proceeds and what a licensee does when one arrives.
5. State the trust fund principle and route every specific to the operative chapter.
6. Explain what this school may and may not say about itself, and why that tells you how to evaluate
   any provider.
7. State what a Missouri salesperson carries out of 48 hours, and what they do not.

## Hour 45 — The regulatory structure and the sources of Missouri law

### 45.1 Where the rules come from

Four layers, and knowing which one you are reading is the beginning of research competence:

**Statute** — **Chapter 339, RSMo**, enacted by the General Assembly. This is the top of the
hierarchy. Sections 339.010 through 339.180 govern licensing and practice; §§ 339.710 through 339.860
are the brokerage relationships provisions Module MO-03 covered in detail.

**Regulation** — **Title 20, Division 2250** of the Code of State Regulations, promulgated by the
Missouri Real Estate Commission under the authority §§ 339.045, 339.090 and 339.120 give it. Chapter 6
is educational requirements; Chapter 7 is schools. A regulation cannot exceed the statute that
authorises it.

**Commission forms, letters and determinations** — the broker disclosure form, the certificate of
course completion, course approval letters. **These are operative documents**, and using a superseded
version of a prescribed form is a real problem.

**Case law** — the courts' interpretation of all of the above.

**Where to find them.** The statutes at the Revisor of Statutes; the regulations in the Code of State
Regulations published by the Secretary of State; the forms and approved-provider lists on the
Commission's own pages. **Read the section, not a summary of it.** A summary — including this course —
is a navigation aid. The operative text is the operative text.

### 45.2 The Commission

The Missouri Real Estate Commission sits within the Department of Commerce and Insurance, Division
2250. It licenses brokers and salespersons, accredits schools, approves courses, investigates
complaints, and brings disciplinary proceedings.

**Note where a disciplinary case actually goes.** Under 20 CSR 2250-7.090(5) the Commission **causes a
complaint to be filed with the Administrative Hearing Commission** — a separate body — when it believes
there is a probability that a school or a person acting on its behalf has committed one of the listed
acts. Missouri splits the accusing function from the adjudicating function, which is worth
understanding: the Commission does not simply decide cases it brings.

## Hour 46 — Licensing

### 46.1 The qualification requirements

Section 339.040, RSMo, read on 2026-08-20 in its text effective 28 August 2020:

- **.1** Licences are granted only to persons who present satisfactory proof to the Commission that
  they are **competent to transact the business** in such a manner as to safeguard the interest of the
  public.
- **.2** The Commission holds oral or written examinations at such times and places as it may
  determine.
- **.3** Each applicant is **at least eighteen** and pays the examination fee.
- **.6** Each application for a salesperson licence includes **a certificate from a school accredited
  under § 339.045 that the applicant has, within six months prior to the date of application,
  successfully completed the prescribed salesperson curriculum** — with a Commission discretion to
  waive all or part on proof of other educational background or experience acceptable to it.

### 46.2 The six-month window, again

It was stated in Module MO-01 Hour 5 and it is stated again here because it is the single provision
students most often learn about too late.

**Six months from successful completion of the education to the date of application.** Not to the date
of the examination. Not to the date you decide to look for a broker. To the **date of application**.

Plan the sequence backwards: finish the education, sit the examination promptly, secure a broker
association, and apply — all inside six months. Students who finish the education and then spend a
year deciding have to do the education again.

### 46.3 The education

Two courses, and they are different in kind:

**The 48-hour Salesperson Pre-Examination Course** — this course. Its content, under 20 CSR
2250-6.060(1), is developed from **the detailed content outline supplied by the professional testing
service** contracted to administer the examination, based on that service's most recent job analysis.
Its purpose is the body of knowledge the examination tests.

**The 24-hour Missouri Real Estate Practice Course** — the MREP. Its content, under 20 CSR
2250-6.060(2), is **sixteen topics the Commission itself lists**, and they are overwhelmingly about
Missouri practice: the broker disclosure form, listing, buyer representation, dual agency and
transaction brokerage, termination, compensation, cooperating with other licensees, obligations to
clients and customers, pricing, the appraiser's role, financing, closing costs, contracts, earnest
money, avoiding misrepresentation, and types of construction.

**Section 339.040.8 is a different thing entirely and must not be confused with either.** It requires
every active licensee to be able to show **twelve hours** of approved instruction in the preceding two
years. That is **continuing education**, after licensure, and it has nothing to do with the pre-licence
requirement.

### 46.4 What this school must tell you before you are contractually obligated

**20 CSR 2250-7.010(1)(D)**: the school shall advise all students, **prior to contractual obligation**,
that **certain criminal convictions may prevent the student from receiving or retaining a real estate
licence.**

That notice is given at enrolment and it is repeated here because it is the one that costs the most
when it arrives late. If it may apply to you, get the question answered **before** you pay for
education, not after you have finished it.

**20 CSR 2250-7.010(1)(E)**: the school shall enter into a **contract** with each student, identifying
the tuition, the refund policy for dismissal or voluntary or hardship withdrawal, any additional fee
for supplies or materials, **the standard for issuance of a certificate of satisfactory completion**,
and other material matters. Read that contract. It is where the completion standard you will be held
to is written down.

## Hour 47 — Conduct, complaints, and trust funds

### 47.1 The conduct standard, restated

Module MO-03 set out the statutory duties in full. Restated here as the working standard a licensee
carries into practice:

- **Honesty and reasonable skill and care**, to the client, always — § 339.730.1(2).
- **Adverse material facts disclosed in writing**, to clients and to customers alike —
  §§ 339.730.1(3)(c), 339.730.3, 339.740.3, 339.755(2)(d).
- **Confidentiality maintained**, including after the relationship ends — §§ 339.730.2, 339.790.2.
- **Accounting in a timely manner** for all money and property received — § 339.730.1(4).
- **Written agreements** for every brokerage relationship — § 339.780.
- **The broker disclosure form** at first substantial contact in a residential transaction —
  § 339.770.1.
- **Expert advice recommended** on every material matter outside the licence — § 339.730.1(3)(d).
- **Fair housing and civil rights compliance** as a statutory licence duty — § 339.730.1(6).

**This course states no ground for discipline from § 339.100**, because that section was not read from
primary text for this project. What it states is that the section exists, that it is the operative list
of grounds for disciplining a licensee, and that it is read directly by every licensee before they
practise — not summarised from a course.

### 47.2 What happens when a complaint arrives

The Commission may investigate on its own motion or on a written complaint. For schools,
20 CSR 2250-7.090 sets out the mechanism explicitly — investigatory hearings, visits with or without
prior notice, surveys of participants and instructors — and the same investigative posture applies to
licensees under the statute.

**What a licensee does when a complaint arrives:**

1. **Tell your designated broker immediately.** Not eventually.
2. **Preserve everything.** Every file, email, text message and note. Altering or destroying a record
   after a complaint converts a defensible matter into an indefensible one.
3. **Get counsel.** Before responding.
4. **Respond within the time given.** Ignoring a regulator's letter is its own violation.

And the reason your files matter: **you write every file as though someone else will read it, because
someone may.** That is not defensiveness. It is the ordinary standard of a regulated profession.

### 47.3 Trust funds

The principle, and it does not bend:

**Money that belongs to someone else — earnest money, security deposits, rents collected for an owner
— is trust money from the moment it comes into your hands.** It goes to the firm for handling under the
firm's trust account procedures. It never touches an operating account. It is never held uncashed in a
drawer as a favour. It is never released on a party's request when its disposition is disputed.

Section 339.730.1(4) puts the statutory duty plainly: **to account in a timely manner for all money and
property received.**

**This course states no Missouri trust account requirement, threshold, deadline or exception**, because
§ 339.105 and the relevant chapters of 20 CSR 2250 were not read from primary text for this project.
**Read them, and read your firm's policy, before you accept your first cheque.** Trust account failures
are the fastest route out of this profession, and they are almost never theft — they are convenience,
helpfulness and sloppiness, which look identical on an audit.

### 47.4 Errors, omissions, and the honest response

Every licensee will eventually make a mistake — a missed deadline, a figure transposed, a disclosure
that went out late. The professional response is the same every time: **tell your broker, tell the
affected party, correct it in writing, and document what happened.**

The alternative — quietly fixing it and hoping — converts an error into a concealment, and concealment
is what turns a survivable problem into a licence problem.

## Hour 48 — The school rules, and what you carry forward

### 48.1 What this school may never do, and why you should know

These are rules **on the school**, not on you. Knowing them lets you evaluate every education provider
you will ever deal with:

- **20 CSR 2250-7.010(1)(L)4** — no school shall permit anyone associated with it to make **any
  warranties or guarantees that a student will pass the real estate licence examination** by taking
  its courses. A provider advertising a guaranteed pass is telling you something about itself.
- **20 CSR 2250-7.010(1)(L)2** — no school shall advertise or imply that it is **recommended, endorsed
  or approved by the Commission**, though an accredited school may state which courses the Commission
  has approved it to teach. "The Commission approved this course" can be true; "the Commission endorses
  this school" cannot.
- **20 CSR 2250-7.010(1)(L)3** — no school shall permit anyone associated with it to **provide any
  misleading information to the public or prospective students.**
- **20 CSR 2250-7.010(1)(L)6** — **no course shall be announced or advertised until it is approved by
  the Commission, in writing, for credit.**
- **20 CSR 2250-7.090(5)(K)** — a school may face a complaint for **awarding credit to any student who
  has either not taken the course or who has failed to satisfactorily complete it.**

That last one is why this course's completion tracking is what it is. Credit is awarded by the school's
record of what you actually completed, and by nothing else — not by a payment, not by a request, not by
a plausible explanation.

### 48.2 What Missouri measures about this course

**20 CSR 2250-7.080(4)** requires that by **31 July and 31 January** a distance provider report
statistical information on **completion times** for the preceding period. And: **if the statistics
indicate that at least fifty percent of students took less time to complete the course than the course
submission identified**, the school has **ten days** either to withdraw the course or to file an amended
outline.

Read that as a student and it means something reassuring: **Missouri checks whether the hours are
real.** A provider whose 48-hour course is completed in eleven hours by half its students is required to
withdraw it or fix it. That provision is the strongest anti-inflation rule this project has read in any
state, and it is the reason the hour figures in this course are the hours the material actually takes.

### 48.3 The final examination

**20 CSR 2250-7.080(1)(B)** requires, for a distance-delivered pre-examination course, **two final
examination forms — a primary and an alternate — of at least fifty multiple-choice items each, with no
duplication of items between them**, together with a key showing correct responses. The examinations
must reflect sound test development practices and must **measure knowledge of the subject matter of the
entire course as set out in the learning objectives.**

**20 CSR 2250-7.080(1)(E)** sets the passing standard: within **ten days** following administration of
the final examination, the school delivers **to the student who achieves a score of at least
seventy-five percent (75%)** a certificate of course completion on a Commission-prescribed form.

**That 75 percent is the Commission's figure**, from the rule, and it is the standard for the final
examination in this course. Distinguish it from the 80 percent in-module engagement threshold and the
100 percent applied threshold, which are **StateCourse standards** the rule does not prescribe.

**20 CSR 2250-7.080(1)(D)** requires the school to state **the methods it will use to assure
examinations are maintained securely**, and **7.080(3)** requires a **course introduction statement**
setting out the dates during which the course is approved, the terms and conditions of the final
examination, and **a list of the specific learning objectives referenced in the final examination.**

### 48.4 What you carry out of 48 hours — and what you do not

**What you carry:**

- A working knowledge of property, interests, contracts, finance, valuation, title and closing.
- The Missouri brokerage relationships framework — client and customer, the six duties, adverse
  material facts, the five things a dual agent may not say, the written agreement requirements, the
  broker disclosure form trigger.
- The federal fair housing framework and the practices it forbids, including the ones nobody intends.
- The arithmetic.
- **And, most durably: a reliable sense of which questions are yours and which are routed** — to your
  designated broker, to counsel, to the lender, to the title company, to the municipality, to a
  qualified professional, to the Commission.

**What you do not carry:**

- The right to give legal, tax, engineering, environmental or appraisal advice.
- The specifics of anything this course could not read from primary text. Every one of those gaps is
  named in `missouri/regulatory/primary-text-readings.md`, and each must be read in current text before
  it is relied on. The list is not short and it is honest.
- A licence. This course is one requirement among several.

### 48.5 The status of this course, stated one last time

This is a **version 1.0 submission draft**. It is **not approved**. **No Missouri pre-license credit is
available for it.** Under 20 CSR 2250-7.010(1)(L)6 it cannot even be advertised for credit until the

it has been reconciled against the testing service's detailed content outline, which this project has

Those are not disclaimers. They are the actual state of this document, and a school that would not tell
you them is not a school to buy education from.
