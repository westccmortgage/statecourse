## Form A · Item 001

**Objective:** MO48-01 — Severance of personal property

A landowner cuts a standing tree into logs for delivery under a separate sale. Which change in classification has occurred?

A. The logs have become a recorded easement.
B. Severance has converted the timber from part of the real estate into personal property.
C. The cutting creates a new life estate.
D. The logs remain permanently attached to the land after delivery.

**Key:** B

**Rationale:** Standing timber is part of the land; cutting and removing it is severance. The resulting movable logs are personal property, not an interest in land.
