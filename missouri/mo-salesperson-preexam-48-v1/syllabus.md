# Missouri Salesperson Pre-Exam 48h v1.0 — Syllabus

**Regulator:** Missouri Real Estate Commission  
**Hours:** 48  
**Delivery:** Online / correspondence / home study distance delivery — approval pending  
**Status:** Development; not submitted or approved; enrollment disabled  
**Instructor:** Unassigned. School must document subject-matter preparation, training, and practical experience; response to distance-student inquiries by end of next regular business day.

## Outcomes

Students who complete the approved version will be able to explain the required real-estate concepts, apply Missouri law and forms to entry-level transaction scenarios, perform required calculations, recognize when referral is necessary, and document decisions consistent with consumer protection.

## Completion

Complete all modules and pass a secure final form at 75% or better.

## Assessment

Module knowledge checks and quizzes diagnose mastery and trigger remediation. Final structure: 2 secure form(s), 50 multiple-choice items per form, 75% passing standard. Primary and alternate forms; no duplicated items between forms.

## Integrity and access

The authenticated student must complete the state-configured identity, timing, module, assessment and final controls. Payment grants only the purchased course after a verified webhook; it never establishes completion or regulatory credit.
