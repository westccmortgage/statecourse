# Missouri Official Source Register

Last verified: 2026-08-16. Sources are rechecked at submission, approval, every controlled release, and before enrollment after a law/rule/form change.

1. [MREC Approved Education Providers](https://pr.mo.gov/realestate-approved-education-providers.asp) — Confirms classroom and online/correspondence/home-study categories for both salesperson courses.
2. [20 CSR 2250 Chapter 6](https://www.sos.mo.gov/cmsimages/adrules/csr/current/20csr/20c2250-6.pdf) — 48-hour vendor-outline rule and the complete 24-hour MREP topic list.
3. [20 CSR 2250 Chapter 7](https://www.sos.mo.gov/cmsimages/adrules/csr/current/20csr/20c2250-7.pdf) — School accreditation, instructors, distance delivery, finals, pass score, records, reports, renewal.
4. [MREC Education Provider Applications](https://pr.mo.gov/realestate-education-provider-applications.asp) — Current school accreditation, course application, pre-exam/MREP attachment, attendance and renewal forms.
5. [MREC Exam Information](https://pr.mo.gov/realestate-exam-information.asp) — Current contracted testing-service handbook entry point.

## Source-control rule

- Official regulator, legislature/secretary-of-state rule text, and current regulator forms control.
- Third-party summaries may aid navigation but cannot create a requirement.
- A missing or ambiguous requirement becomes a submission question or release gate; it is not inferred from another state.
