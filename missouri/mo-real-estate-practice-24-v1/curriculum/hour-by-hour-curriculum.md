## MO24-01 — Broker Disclosure Form (1 hours)

Authority: 20 CSR 2250-6.060(2)(A)

## Hour 001 — Purpose, timing, and use of the Broker Disclosure Form

Module: MO24-01 · Required engagement: 60 minutes · Content layer: NC-AGY

### Learning objectives

By the end of this hour, the student can:

1. explain purpose, timing, and use of the broker disclosure form using accurate professional vocabulary;
2. apply purpose, timing, and use of the broker disclosure form to a state-specific transaction fact pattern; and
3. identify the controlling source, required record, and point for professional referral.

### Instructional reading

**Concept and professional purpose.** Purpose, timing, and use of the Broker Disclosure Form is studied as part of Broker Disclosure Form. The student begins by identifying the people, property interests, documents, money, deadlines, and public-protection purpose involved. The lesson distinguishes a fact from an assumption, a legal requirement from a provider practice, and a licensee's task from work that must be referred to an attorney, lender, appraiser, surveyor, inspector, tax professional, or other qualified specialist.

**Transaction application.** The student applies the concept to a realistic file. The file is read chronologically: source document, consumer communication, decision point, required disclosure or agreement, performance, and retained evidence. A compliant response identifies controlling authority, verifies material facts from an appropriate source, communicates within the licensee's role, documents the action, and escalates unresolved legal or technical issues. Marketing language, form selection, compensation, and technology do not override the state rule.

**State-law layer.** Missouri layer: apply 20 CSR 2250-6.060(2)(A), Chapter 339 RSMo, 20 CSR 2250, Commission-prescribed forms, and the currently approved testing-service outline where applicable. The 48-hour and 24-hour records remain separate. Source dates and form editions are checked at controlled release. If a cited source changes, the lesson is held from new enrollment until the course owner completes impact review and records a new content version.

**National-core relationship.** This unit may reuse the concepts in NC-AGY, but its state examples, terminology, forms, citations, assessment record, and approval mapping are versioned independently for Missouri.

### Applied activity

Analyze a Missouri transaction fact pattern involving **purpose, timing, and use of the broker disclosure form**. Build a five-entry audit note: (1) known fact, (2) missing fact, (3) controlling source, (4) permitted licensee action, and (5) referral or follow-up. Explain why payment status, course progress, and regulatory credit are separate records.

A compliant response identifies controlling authority, verifies material facts from an appropriate source, communicates within the licensee's role, documents the action, and escalates unresolved legal or technical issues. Marketing language, form selection, compensation, and technology do not override the state rule.

**State-law layer.** Missouri layer: apply 20 CSR 2250-6.060(2)(P), Chapter 339 RSMo, 20 CSR 2250, Commission-prescribed forms, and the currently approved testing-service outline where applicable. The 48-hour and 24-hour records remain separate. Source dates and form editions are checked at controlled release. If a cited source changes, the lesson is held from new enrollment until the course owner completes impact review and records a new content version.

**National-core relationship.** This unit may reuse the concepts in NC-OWN, but its state examples, terminology, forms, citations, assessment record, and approval mapping are versioned independently for Missouri.

### Applied activity

Analyze a Missouri transaction fact pattern involving **construction systems and vocabulary**. Build a five-entry audit note: (1) known fact, (2) missing fact, (3) controlling source, (4) permitted licensee action, and (5) referral or follow-up. Explain why payment status, course progress, and regulatory credit are separate records.

### Knowledge check and remediation

1. Which source controls this issue in Missouri, and what is the effective-date check?
2. What fact must be verified before the licensee communicates or acts?
3. What evidence belongs in the transaction or course audit record?

If any response is incorrect, the LMS assigns the source excerpt, the worked scenario, and a new parallel check. The original answer is not exposed as a substitute for remediation. Hour 24 is complete only after the tracked instructional sequence and required check are complete.
