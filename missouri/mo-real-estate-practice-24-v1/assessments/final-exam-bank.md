## Form A · Item 001

**Objective:** MO24-01 — Purpose, timing, and use of the Broker Disclosure Form

A residential prospect has no brokerage agreement. At the first substantial contact, an agent promises to supply the broker disclosure form with the closing papers. What is the timing defect?

A. The form may wait until the buyer chooses a specific property because a general representation discussion does not start the statutory disclosure clock.
B. The written current form is due at the earliest practicable opportunity during or following that contact.
C. The form is due only after a purchase contract is signed.
D. There is no defect if the prospect eventually receives it.

**Key:** B

**Rationale:** Section 339.770.1 anchors disclosure to first substantial contact, not closing; postponing it until the transaction ends defeats its purpose.

**Curriculum reference:** `missouri/mo-real-estate-practice-24-v1/curriculum/module-01.md` § 1.1.

**Authority:** [339.770 RSMo](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=339.770).

A prospect says an oral explanation of brokerage roles was helpful. No written current broker disclosure form was supplied despite the statutory trigger. Which step remains necessary?

A. Wait until the prospect files a complaint.
B. Assume the oral explanation satisfies every form requirement.
C. Have another prospect sign an acknowledgment instead.
D. Provide the current prescribed form in writing.

**Key:** D

**Rationale:** A helpful explanation complements, but does not replace, the required written copy under 339.770. Documentation should reflect what was actually supplied.

**Curriculum reference:** `missouri/mo-real-estate-practice-24-v1/curriculum/module-01.md` § 1.6.

**Authority:** [339.770 RSMo](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=339.770).

## Form B · Item 049

**Objective:** MO24-02 — Types of seller agency agreements

An owner signs an exclusive right-to-sell listing with no reserved-buyer exception. The owner later procures a purchaser independently. Under that stated structure, what compensation issue remains?

A. The listing firm’s agreed compensation may still be due.
B. Owner procurement necessarily converts the listing into an open listing.
C. The agent becomes the owner of the sale proceeds.
D. The purchaser automatically owes double the stated fee.

**Key:** A

**Rationale:** An exclusive right-to-sell ordinarily differs from exclusive agency by not reserving an owner-procured-sale exception. The actual signed terms determine the compensation obligation.

**Curriculum reference:** `missouri/mo-real-estate-practice-24-v1/curriculum/module-02.md` § 5.1.

## Form B · Item 050

**Objective:** MO24-02 — Elements of seller agency agreements

Before a listing is signed, the parties disagree over whether the broker may offer subagency. Which completion procedure is appropriate?

A. Leave the field blank and assume every other broker is a subagent.
B. Let the first buyer unilaterally choose the seller’s agency terms.
C. Resolve and record the election in the written seller agency agreement.
D. Replace the election with a promise about mortgage approval.

**Key:** C

**Rationale:** Section 339.780.2 requires the agreement to specify whether subagency may be offered. An unresolved blank should not be treated as informed agreement to a particular choice.

**Curriculum reference:** `missouri/mo-real-estate-practice-24-v1/curriculum/module-02.md` § 6.2.

**Authority:** [339.780 RSMo (effective August 28, 2025)](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=339.780).
