# StateCourse institutional experience

## Reference study

StateCourse uses leading continuing-education institutions as information-architecture references, not as templates or brand sources:

- UCLA Extension: direct program discovery, credential pathways, course facts, and career-centered outcomes.
- Harvard Division of Continuing Education: institutional confidence, restrained hierarchy, school/program orientation, and clear academic navigation.
- UC San Diego Extended Studies: practical program taxonomy, audience pathways, instructor visibility, and student services.
- UT Austin University Extension: prominent course catalog access, self-paced delivery explanation, and compact registration facts.
- UCR University Extension: certificate/program discovery, outcome-led summaries, and transparent next steps.
- University of San Diego Professional & Continuing Education: professional audience language, course pathways, and registration support.

No reference institution's marks, copy, layouts, or distinctive visual assets are reproduced.

## StateCourse principles

1. Institutional identity precedes technology.
2. A learner chooses profession, state, license path, approved program, then course.
3. Every course page opens with the academic record: title, hours, delivery, instructor, regulator, approval state, and enrollment availability.
4. Learning outcomes and curriculum precede sales language.
5. Official approval status is never implied by visual polish.
6. The student reader behaves like a digital textbook: chapters, objectives, definitions, law notes, examples, cases, questions, remediation, and clear continuation.
7. Payment, identity, study time, assessment, regulatory credit, and certificate issuance remain separate system controls.

## Visual system

- Warm paper and off-white fields create an editorial reading surface.
- Deep navy and charcoal carry institutional authority.
- Burgundy and muted brass are restrained functional accents.
- Georgia is the bundled-safe editorial heading face; readable system sans-serif is used for body and interface text.
- Thin rules, squared panels, folio numbers, and modest spacing replace gradients, glow, glass effects, and oversized product cards.

## Page hierarchy

National catalog → profession → state → license path → course record → syllabus/outcomes → curriculum → exam/completion → compliance/approval → enrollment disclosures → payment.

Virginia and Maine share this institutional shell but never share regulatory claims, timing rules, assessments, approval records, policy versions, or certificate language.
