# New York real estate salesperson practice baseline

The New York Department of State lists a minimum age of 18, approved 77-hour qualifying education, a separate DOS exam, sponsorship by a New York licensed broker and the application and identity requirements. DOS identifies the 77-hour curriculum as the basis for the multiple-choice examination. The school’s proctored qualifying-course exam is separate. StateCourse’s free practice consists of original study questions. It offers no qualifying education, certificate or credit. [DOS licensing and exam](https://dos.ny.gov/real-estate-agent) · [DOS FAQs](https://dos.ny.gov/real-estate-salesperson-frequently-asked-questions)
