## New York

**Entry licence and hours.**
- The DOS adopted rule text (effective 12/21/2022) amends 19 NYCRR 176.3(a) to **77 hours of instruction plus a 3-hour final examination, 80 in total**. The 176.3(a) subject table is also amended; for example, Human Rights and Fair Housing goes from 4 to 6 hours.
- DOS's "Real Estate Agent" page says: "Have successfully completed 77 hours of approved qualifying education."
- **Justia's 176.3 still prints the old 75 + 3 = 78** and notes that the amended version is "not yet available". Do not use the Justia text of 176.3.

**Delivery.**
- **176.22** defines distance education as any educational process "based on the geographical separation of instructor and learner". Providers must be evaluated and approved under 176.23–176.25.
- **176.23** requirements for a distance course:
  - the material is divided into major units, and each unit into instructional modules;
  - a **time-default mechanism for inactivity**, so that no credit accrues while the student is not actively participating;
  - a **signed and dated statement from each student that he or she personally completed each module**;
  - participation and completion records kept for **3 years**;
  - an instructor approved under 176.11 available during reasonable business hours;
  - **(g) a proctored final examination at a location within New York State approved by the department**.

**Third-party certification.**
- Form **DOS-1503-f-a (Rev. 12/21)**, *Real Estate Qualifying Course Approval Application*, Section 10 checklist, requires for distance learning:
  - "letter from an outside organization certifying the design and delivery format";
  - a "distance learning affidavit";
  - "New York State in person proctored final examination location(s)".
- Exam locations for distance learning "MUST BE NEW YORK STATE LOCATION".
- The form does not name ARELLO or IDECC. However, an outside certifying letter is mandatory, so the owner decision puts New York in skip.

**Approval route.**
- **Approved entities (176.1):** accredited colleges and universities, public and private vocational schools, real estate boards, and real estate professional societies and organizations.
- **Affiliation bar.** A course may not be "affiliated with or controlled by" a real estate broker, salesperson, appraiser, firm, company or franchise, or by a subsidiary of one. The form also bars a real estate licensee from signing certificates. **StateCourse's ownership by a licensed broker would need to be cleared against 176.1 before any New York filing.**
- **Application (176.24):** filed 60 days before the course is offered. It must include the final exams with answer keys and references, the means of monitoring and verifying active participation, the hardware and software required, a technical-support plan, and a module-level outline.
- **Fees (176.21, form):**
  - $300 annual registration, for the year running Sept 1 to Aug 31;
  - $150 for each additional location;
  - $25 one-time fee per instructor.
- **Instructors (176.11):** each instructor is certified by DOS under a 100-point scoring system covering experience, teaching, coursework and degrees.

**Engine rules.**
- Hours are counted as **50 minutes**, with a mandatory 10-minute break (176.5).
- Attendance: absence above **10%** loses credit, and a student who has not met attendance may not sit the final (176.6).
- A failed final means a failed course (176.9(b)). The pass mark is set by the school and stated on the form; the rule sets none.
- The course, **including passing the final**, must be completed **within 12 months** of starting (176.25(a)).
- The completer list goes to DOS **within 15 days** (176.20(b), 176.25(b)).
- The certificate must carry the entity's name and code number, course title and hours, and a statement of satisfactory attendance. It must also carry the signature and the school's official seal (176.25(c)).
- Records are kept **3 years** (176.9(a), 176.23(e)).

## New Jersey

**Entry licence and hours.** Salesperson: **75 hours**, with a prescribed topic table that includes 3 hours of quizzes and final exam (N.J.A.C. 11:5-2.1). It must be completed at a school licensed by the Commission under N.J.S.A. 45:15-10.4. Breaks may not exceed 5 minutes of each course hour.

**Delivery.**
- **DOBI Bulletin No. 22-10 (11/30/2022):** pre-licensure courses may be offered online on a permanent basis, effective 8/29/2023.
- Two online forms are recognised:
  - live-remote, at a scheduled time with a live instructor;
  - on-demand, available at any time.
- **"Courses delivered by on-demand instruction must also receive Distance Education Certification"** from ARELLO.
- N.J.A.C. 11:5-2.1(f) and (g) and the instructor rule 11:5-2.2(k) still apply.
- Online schools must file a technical-support policy, a final-exam policy and a satisfactory-completion policy. Policy changes must be disclosed within 10 business days.

**Verdict.** Asynchronous delivery requires ARELLO, so New Jersey is a skip. Live-remote delivery would avoid the certification, but it is synchronous and outside the StateCourse model.

## Ohio

**Entry licence and hours.**
- ORC 4735.09(F)(6), effective 9/30/2025, requires completion "at an institution of higher education" of the following, "by either classroom instruction or distance education":
  - (a) 40 hours of real estate practice;
  - (b) 40 hours covering Ohio real estate law, civil rights law, housing-discrimination case law, desegregation, and methods of eliminating the effects of prior discrimination;
  - (c) 10 hours of appraisal;
  - (d) 10 hours of finance.
- **Total: 100.** The same split appears in the 4/9/2025 version.
- Division (I): an applicant not licensed within the prior 4 years must have completed the instruction within the prior **10 years**.

**Delivery.** ORC 4735.01(DD) defines "distance education" as instruction "accomplished through use of interactive, electronic media and where the teacher and student are separated by distance or time, or both". **Asynchronous delivery is therefore within the statute.**

**Provider route: the gate.**
- ORC 4735.01(D) limits "institution of higher education" to four types:
  1. a state institution;
  2. a nonprofit holding a Chapter 1713 certificate of authorization;
  3. a private institution exempt under Chapter 3332 per ORC 3333.046;
  4. an institution registered with the **State Board of Career Colleges and Schools** under Chapter 3332 and approved to offer degree or certificate programs under ORC 3332.05.
- An "eligible course" must be applicable toward a degree or certificate at the institution (4735.01(CC)).
- **ORC 4735.011:** if an institution is approved under 3332.05 "to offer only certificate programs through distance education, the design and delivery method of a course offered by the institution must be certified by [ARELLO], the international distance education certification center, or another certifying body recognized by the superintendent".
- For StateCourse, a for-profit, online-only school, route 4 is the only one available, and it is the route the statute gates on certification. Hence **skip**.
- The one way around it is a content or licensing partnership with an Ohio college. That would not be StateCourse operating as the school.

**Not used.** OAC 1301:5-7-08 (distance education, effective 2/10/2019) was read. It governs **continuing education** only and is not authority for pre-license courses.

## Kentucky

**Entry licence and hours.**
- 201 KAR 11:210 §1(1): a prospective sales associate completes "six (6) academic credit hours, or its equivalent, of prelicensing education". The provider must be an approved pre-license education provider or an accredited institution as defined in KRS 324.010(8).
- 201 KAR 11:011 defines an academic credit hour as one college semester hour **or "sixteen (16) fifty (50) minute hours of actual classroom attendance"**. Six credit hours is therefore **96 hours**.
- No completion window is stated.

**Delivery.**
- 201 KAR 11:011 defines a "distance education course" as including "a pre-license course" taught with teacher and student in separate locations, using "internet-based training, computer-based training (CBT), satellite transmission, or teleconferencing".
- 201 KAR 11:170 §9 governs distance courses:
  - a classroom-approved course needs separate distance approval (§9(2));
  - the application must include the course itself or a link to it, plus the final-exam bank and key (§9(3)).
- **ARELLO appears nowhere** in 11:170 or on KREC Form 102.

**Approval route.**
- **School approval** is on KREC Form 101 and renews annually (11:170 §2).
- The school application includes a **Kentucky Commission on Proprietary Education licence "if applicable"**. This is a possible second regulator, and its reach to an out-of-state online school was not read.
- **Course approval** is on KREC Form 102 (rev. 7/2019):
  - filed at least **90 days** before the first offering;
  - $75 initial fee, $25 renewal;
  - approval lasts 1 year;
  - a full re-application is due every 5 years (11:170 §5).
- The form treats any course with "ANY distance component" as a distance course. The applicant must explain medium, proctoring, remediation, hardware and software, technical support, instructor availability and **student identity**, and must attach the proctor certification document.
- **Instructors:** KREC Form 100, approved annually, with mandatory educator training within 12 months (11:170 §10).

**Content (method question 2).**
- §4: instruction on all topics except Kentucky-specific law "shall be from a text approved by the commission". StateCourse would either have its own text approved or teach from an approved text.
- §4 lists 20 required topics.
- §4 also requires a **scenario-based practicum or project**, completed individually, using at least three commission-approved forms, graded pass/fail.

**Engine rules.**
- **Course structure:** units, modules or chapters, with a **quiz at the end of each unit**. Students may not skip ahead to quizzes, and **time spent is tracked** (§9(4)).
- **Credit-time cap:** a pre-license course may run no more than **9 hours per calendar day** (§4).
- **Final exam content and pass mark:** **100 multiple-choice items** for a 6-credit-hour course, or 50 for a 3-credit-hour course. Pass mark **75%** (§9(6)).
- **Item bank:** must hold **40% more items** than the exam, so that retakes get unique questions (§9(6)(b)1).
- **Attempts:** a maximum of **3 attempts**, then the course must be retaken. A retake comes no earlier than the **fourth day** after a failure. After a second failure, the school documents deficiencies and offers additional training (§4).
- **Monitoring:** the exam is **monitored** by the instructor or a designee who is not a relative or business associate. A "monitor from a qualified online test monitoring service" is allowed (§9(6)(b)2–3).
- **Monitor duties:**
  - verify identity;
  - observe the student;
  - allow no aids and only a non-programmable calculator;
  - ensure devices are put away;
  - **certify to the provider in a notarized statement** (§9(6)(b)4).

  How a remote proctoring vendor meets the notarised-statement requirement needs confirming with KREC.
- **Affidavit:** the student signs an affidavit, which may be electronic, before credit is given (§9(7)).
- **Completion date:** the later of passing the exam and submitting the affidavit and evaluation (§9(10)).
- **Records:** kept **3 years**, including courses, hours, final grades and test scores, with the certificate held in the student record (§13).

**Currency risk.** 201 KAR 11:011 and 11:210 (and 11:170, which carries the same 12/16/2019 effective date) show a **7-year expiration of 12/16/2026**. The commission must re-promulgate or renew them, and the text may change. **Re-read all three after 12/16/2026, before filing.** The LRC pages read are labelled "Alternate View" (pre-engrossed text). The engrossed 11:170 (LRC doc 1964) was cross-checked for §9 and matches.

## Tennessee

**Entry licence and hours.**
- 1260-05-.03: affiliate broker applicants complete "a sixty (60) hour course in basic principles".
- Classroom hour = 50 minutes. Attendance minimum is 80%, or 100% for courses of 8 hours or less. Courses over 8 hours need a comprehensive final with a **70%** pass mark.
- The Department's FAQ: 60 hours before the exam, and the **30-hour Course for New Affiliates "prior to their license being issued"** (for licences after 12/31/2004).
- Total education before licensure: **90 hours**.

**Delivery and certification: 1260-05-.18 (new rule, effective 11/23/2023).**
- (2)(a): "A distance education course using computer-based or online materials or formats may be submitted if the course is certified by the Association of Real Estate License Law Officials (ARELLO) or another certifying body at the discretion of the Commission".
- (2)(b): the requirement "does not apply to a synchronous course ... but does apply to an asynchronous course".
- (6): approval "will be automatically withdrawn should certification by the respective certifying body be discontinued for any reason".
- (7): a distance version of basic principles must include at least one interaction with a Commission-approved instructor for every 5 hours of credit.
- Synchronous courses carry their own conditions:
  - 100% attendance;
  - camera on, with photo-ID check;
  - a proctor;
  - a 4-year retention of the recording.
- **Skip:** asynchronous delivery requires third-party certification. Synchronous delivery avoids the certification but is live.

## West Virginia

**Entry licence and hours.**
- W. Va. Code 30-40-14(b): "90 clock-hours, equivalent to six college semester credit hours, in a course or courses approved by the commission".
- 30-40-14(c): completed within the **3 years** before application.
- 174CSR3 §3.4.1 splits the 90 hours into 30 principles and practice, 20 law, 20 finance and 20 appraisal.

**Delivery and certification: 174CSR3, effective 4/10/2024, sunset 8/1/2029.**
- §2.6 defines distance education as instruction not in "a traditional classroom setting".
- §3.6.1: courses must be well-structured and allow monitoring.
- **§3.6.3: course design and delivery "shall be certified by the Association of Real Estate License Law Officials or by another institution" whose standards the Commission deems equivalent.**
- **§3.8: distance instructors "shall obtain Distance Education Instructor Certification from IDECC".**
- Two separate mandatory third-party gates, so **skip**.

**Other rules, recorded for completeness.**
- Providers are accredited post-secondary or proprietary education institutions, approved by and registered with the Commission, and renewed annually. Fees are in Series 2.
- Out-of-state providers must register with the WV Secretary of State (§3.3).
- Final-exam pass mark is **70%**, and pre-license students need 70% in each subject area (§3.6.5, §3.4.3).
- Certificates certify **90% attendance** (§3.4.4).
- Records are kept **5 years** (§3.4.5).

## Cross-batch notes

- **Third-party certification again decides most of the outcome.**
  - New Jersey (bulletin) and West Virginia (rule) name ARELLO directly. West Virginia adds an IDECC instructor certification.
  - Tennessee (rule) names ARELLO "or another certifying body".
