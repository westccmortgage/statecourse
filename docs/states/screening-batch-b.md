## South Carolina

- **Entry licence:** "associate" (S.C. Code 40-57-30(5); the 2024 amendments replaced "salesperson").
- **Hours:** S.C. Code 40-57-320(A)(1)(a) — "sixty hours of classroom instruction in fundamentals of real estate principles and practices" and "thirty hours of classroom instruction in advanced real estate principles within five years before the application"; the exam may be taken before the 30 advanced hours. Reg. 105-6 requires prelicense courses to be taught in distinct units meeting those minimums.
- **Delivery:** Reg. 105-8(A) — prelicense and CE may be taught in person or by distance learning ("where the licensed instructor and student are physically apart"). Synchronous and asynchronous distance courses require Commission-approved instructors and ARELLO or IDECC certification; design must be competency-based with active participation, not passive reading/watching. Recorded video used as a supplement is not distance learning. Reg. 105-6: a distance course application "must include a copy of ARELLO or IDECC certification". (Reg. 105-6 cites S.C. Code 40-57-340(F) for this; the current 40-57-340 has no subsection (F) and the statute nowhere mentions distance learning, ARELLO or IDECC — the requirement stands on the regulation.)
- **Approval:** Reg. 105-4 — a provider may be "any school, organization, association, institution, or instructor", must be approved before instruction; reg. 105-5 — separate provider and course approval applications; approvals renew biennially (even years). Reg. 105-12 fees: provider $200 / renewal $100; course $100 / renewal $50; instructor $100 / renewal $50; late renewal $50. No bond found in 105-4 to 105-12. Reg. 105-10: Commission-approved instructors (broker licence, related degree or accepted experience, plus teaching competence, e.g. REEA/IDECC instructor certification); biennial renewal and workshop.
- **Engine-relevant:** proctored final, minimum passing score 70%; exams administered/monitored only by an approved instructor, school administrator or qualified person; re-exams at least 80% new items; two failures require retaking the course (105-6). Course day 2–8 hours; "strict attendance", no partial credit; recorded make-up only if under 20% missed (105-6). Signed, dated completion certificate (105-6). Enrolment agreement signed (or affirmatively accepted electronically) before class, with prescribed contents (105-7). Records (enrolment agreements, advertising, rosters, attendance) kept five years (105-9). Advertising must carry provider and course approval numbers (105-8(C)).
- **Not checked:** whether the S.C. Commission on Higher Education separately licenses an out-of-state online provider. Moot while the state is skipped.
- **Sources (read 2026-10-29):**
  - S.C. Code Title 40 Ch. 57 — https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t40c057.php
  - S.C. Code of Regulations Chapter 105 — https://www.scstatehouse.gov/coderegs/Chapter%20105.pdf

## Alabama

- **Entry licence:** salesperson. 60 clock hours, completed within one year of course start or no credit; max 8 hours' credit per day (790-X-1-.06(5)(c)); pass the licence exam within six months of completing the course (.06 summary).
- **Delivery:** .06(5)(f) — "At least 80% of the course hours in the prelicense and post license courses shall be presented by live instruction", unless an ARELLO-certified distance education course. .21(4) — a primary provider's distance courses "shall be certified by ARELLO or IDECC before being submitted to the Commission for approval"; .21(7) defines asynchronous courses and (7)(b) limits secondary providers to asynchronous courses a primary provider has had ARELLO/IDECC-certified. College-credit courses are exempt. Distance instructors must hold IDECC Distance Education Instructor certification (.21(3)).
- **2028 change:** Act 2025-379 (HB 225), effective 1 Oct 2028, has the Commission certify **synchronous** distance prelicense courses itself (fee up to $400) and states it "may accept but may not require" third-party certification of a synchronous distance prelicense course. Nothing read changes the asynchronous rule. The Act also restructures entry education to a 60-hour salesperson basics course (12-month window, exam within 180 days) plus a 60-hour professional-development course within 90 days of the exam. Read in the Hollis substitute to HB225 (15 Apr 2025) and AREC's Act 2025-379 summary; the enrolled act text was not read.
- **Approval:** proprietary schools need a school licence, $250/year initial, $250 two-year renewal, surety bond verification with the application, a branch licence for each location, a named administrator (.10). Instructors: current broker licence, 70-point evaluation, orientation score 70+, written approval before teaching; $50/year initial, $100 two-year renewal (.07, .10). Course schedule/location filed five business days before start; student credit reported within three business days of completion (.06(3)).
- **Engine-relevant:** incremental assessments plus final exam; minimum course grade 70% (.06(5)(g),(i)); distance prelicense students must pass a **proctored** final at 70%+ (.21(9)); 90% attendance (.06(5)(h)); records (attendance, answer sheets, credit submissions) kept four years (.06(5)(m)).
- **Sources (read 2026-10-29):**
  - Rule 790-X-1-.06 — https://admincode.legislature.state.al.us/api/rule/790-X-1-.06
  - Chapter 790-X-1 (rules .07, .10, .12, .21) — https://admincode.legislature.state.al.us/api/chapter/790-X-1
  - HB225 Hollis substitute — https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/files/pdf/SearchableInstruments/2025RS/CXYP6QW-1.pdf
  - AREC Act 2025-379 summary — https://arec.alabama.gov/docs/ACT%202025-379_HB%20225%20Summary.pdf

## Louisiana

- **Entry licence:** salesperson. 90 hours — §5331(A)(1) "Real Estate 101-salesperson 90-hour course" (principles/practices, Louisiana licence law, Commission rules, law of agency, civil law). LREC page: "No substitution will be allowed for the 30-hour portion of the 90-hour requirement."
- **Delivery:** §5333(B) — distance education includes "interactive Internet-based instruction". §5333(D)(1) content approval by the Commission first; (D)(2) then ARELLO delivery-method certification before any offering, and "Loss of ARELLO certification ... shall automatically suspend commission approval"; (D)(3) only in-curriculum college credit courses are exempt. LREC salesperson page: courses must be "Internet-based and completed through a Louisiana state certified real estate school" and approved by ARELLO for delivery standards.
- **Approval:** a pre-licensing school needs a Real Estate School Certificate of Authority (§5303; fees per R.S. 37:1443, not read); $10,000 surety bond from an insurer authorised in Louisiana (§5305); certificate valid one calendar year, expires 31 Dec (§5307, amended LR 49:656, Apr. 2023); designated director (§5311); Commission-certified pre-licence instructors (§5319). No express Louisiana-office requirement seen.
- **Engine-relevant:** distance final exams are four-option multiple choice (§5333(E)); a signed, dated statement that the student personally completed course and exam (§5333(E)(3)); no prelicense question count, pass mark or proctoring stated in Chapter 53. Completion requires written assignments and passing the exam (§5333(F)). Student records kept five years, indexed, including hours, dates, test results, method of completion and contract (§5315). Certificates issued immediately, showing delivery method (§5335). Attendance verification reports within 30 days of completion (§5337).
- **Sources (read 2026-10-29):**
  - LAC 46:LXVII Chapter 53 — https://lrec.gov/chapter-53
  - LREC salesperson pre-licensing education — https://lrec.gov/become-licensed/salesperson/pre-licensing-education
  - LREC education provider — https://lrec.gov/become-licensed/education-provider
  - Not read (permission request timed out): full LAC Part LXVII https://www.doa.la.gov/media/mtuakm0q/46v67.docx; LREC school application page https://lrec.gov/become-licensed/education-provider/school. Chapter 53 as published by LREC carries no "current through" date; the latest amendment shown is LR 49:656 (Apr. 2023).

## Arkansas

- **Entry licence:** salesperson. 60 hours (AREC At a Glance Guides, 2025: "Complete 60 hours of real estate coursework (Pre-License Education)"; AREC Education page: the salesperson 60-hour certificate has no expiration). 18-hour post-licence course within six months of licensure.
- **Delivery:** AREC Education page: "All distance education courses must be ARELLO® certified." The page cites A.C.A. §17-42-303 and Commission Regulations 4.1(d), 4.1(a)(2), 4.1(b)(2).
- **Approval:** per the Education page, courses must be conducted by an accredited post-secondary school, a school or organisation licensed by the Commission, or one approved by an equivalent licensing jurisdiction. No fee, bond or form found on the regulator pages read.
- **Open within Arkansas (does not change the verdict):** the current rule text was not read. Older versions on LII (Reg. 4.1, 1997 and 2007) say "actual classroom attendance ... or correspondence courses" and refer to the State Board of Private Career Education, so a second regulator may exist; the current Code of Arkansas Rules page timed out. Engine rules (exam, attendance, retention) not established.

## Minnesota

- **Entry licence:** salesperson. Minn. Stat. 82.60 subd. 1 — prelicense education "must consist of Course I, Course II, and Course III"; subds. 2–4 set the topic hours (30 each; Course III a 30-hour course chosen from listed subjects, combination of up to three subjects at 10+ hours each).
- **Delivery:** Minn. Stat. 45.305 ("Prelicense education courses offered over the Internet") subd. 2 — real estate Internet courses "must be approved by either IDECC or the Association of Real Estate License Law Officials" before Commerce approval; subd. 3 exempts only academic-credit courses at approved/accredited institutions. The Commerce course application offers "On-Demand (not live)" and "Interactive Internet" delivery types, but they sit under 45.305.
- **Approval:** Commerce (Department of Commerce) approves provider and course; course fee $300 per 30-hour course; provider application/fee also required (amount not on the form); application at least 30 days before first offering; instructor qualifications by degree/experience; coordinator certifies compliance with chs. 45 and 82 (course application form).
- **Engine-relevant (45.305 subd. 4–5):** seat-time technology; identity authentication; 24/7 availability; commissioner viewing access; automatic bookmarking; questions at end of each unit that must be answered before proceeding, with feedback; comprehensive final; review allowed except during the final; end-of-course evaluation; final exam **proctored**, proctor certifies the student took it, paper or encrypted online, no content review once begun. Form: "A closed book, proctored, end of course exam is ALWAYS required for Pre-License Education"; proctor verifies identity and processes an affidavit. Completion credits uploaded to the Pearson VUE portal (Minn. Stat. 45.43, per form). No pass mark, question count or record retention stated in what was read.

## North Dakota

- **Entry licence:** salesperson. 90 hours "in courses of study approved by the commission" — NDCC 43-23-08(4) as amended by 2019 HB 1281 (raised from 45 to 90 hours, effective 1 Jan 2021; bill shows passed and governor signed). The current codified 43-23-08 was not read.
- **Delivery:** NDAC 70-02-02-18 — "Online-only asynchronous prelicensure courses must be certified" by ARELLO before commission approval; must be completed within one year of enrolment. The course application asks for date, time and place of any synchronous hours (70-02-02-02), so other formats exist, but a fully asynchronous course needs ARELLO.
- **Approval:** application at least 45 days before first course, nonrefundable fee not to exceed $200, with outline/hours, instructor résumés, tuition (70-02-02-02); re-application every three years (70-02-02-05); instructors must have "the necessary specialized preparation, training, and experience" (70-02-02-03).
- **Engine-relevant:** 50-minute course hour (70-02-02-10); each course includes examinations, the school sets weighting and passing criteria, copies furnished to the commission (70-02-02-12); certificate showing school, course title and hours (70-02-02-13). No proctoring, retention or identity rule in this chapter.

## District of Columbia

- **Delivery:** **open.** Neither DLCP page says anything about delivery format. That shows a distance route has been approved; it does not show whether it may be asynchronous or whether ARELLO/IDECC is required.
- **Approval:** DCREC (under DLCP). No provider/course application form is posted on the pages read. Whether the DC Higher Education Licensure Commission also licenses an out-of-state online provider has not been checked.
- **What must be read to close it:** 17 DCMR Chapter 26 (especially the salesperson licensure and education-provider sections) and D.C. Code Title 47 ch. 28 subch. I-B. Specifically: (1) does the rule say classroom or allow distance; (2) is ARELLO/IDECC certification mandatory for distance; (3) are any hours required to be live; (4) provider/course application, fees, exam and record rules.
