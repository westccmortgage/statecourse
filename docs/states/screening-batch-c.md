## Per-state detail

### Nevada — Build, with CPE licence and a proctoring question

**Hours.** NRS 645.343(1): an original salesperson applicant must furnish proof of "not less than 120 hours of instruction" in principles, practices, procedures, law and ethics, including "Not less than 15 hours of instruction in the preparation of contracts" and "Not less than 15 hours of instruction on agency"; subsection (1) does not use the word "classroom". The course may be from NSHE, another accredited college or university, or a Commission-approved school. Last amended 2019.
**Discrepancy to note:** the Division's Course Manual (4th ed., 2019) still gives the salesperson course as **90** hours (C1-4). The statute controls; NAC 645.435 ("Course required to obtain original license as salesperson") could not be read to confirm it has been conformed.

**Delivery.** NAC 645.021 defines distance education (video, computer, television, mail correspondence, internet or other electronic means; instructor and student separated by space, time or both). NAC 645.443 (approval of distance education course), as amended by R199-22 §14 effective 7/1/2025: active participation through substantial interaction with the instructor, other students or a computer program; testing and remediation to ensure mastery; completion time for an average student within approved hours and a system confirming the student performed the tasks; technical support; an approved instructor reasonably available, a reply within **2 business days** being timely; an orientation package (fees, refunds, objectives, completion requirements, hardware/software); procedures ensuring the student did the work, including the sponsor's ability to contact the student by phone or email and a **signed student statement of personal completion**, retained by the sponsor. Factors the Division considers include a written examination proctored by an acceptable person **or "a secure electronic method acceptable to the Division"**, and accreditation of course design by a Division-approved distance-education accreditor (nine listed criteria: mission, design/update, interactivity, mastery, support, medium, time study, per-module objective/method/assessment/remediation, syllabus). R199-22 lowers the minimum from 3 hours to 1 and appears to strike the continuous audio/visual communication language. ARELLO is not mentioned in the rule.
Course Manual (2019), B4-1/B4-2: ARELLO certification is "encouraged"; without it the sponsor completes the Division's Distance Education Questionnaire with every DE application.

**Approval route.** School approval NAC 645.403–.407, annual renewal. NAC 645.404 and the Course Manual: a sponsor that is not NSHE and not a "school" as defined in NAC 645.400 must show licensure by the **Nevada Commission on Postsecondary Education** (copy of CPE licence or conditional grant with the application). Forms 560 (salesperson course), 635 (each instructor). Instructors need Division written approval (NAC 645.426: e.g. bachelor's + 2 years full-time experience; or 75 teaching hours in 3 years + 3 years; or 6 years experience). Guest lecturers capped at 9 hours per course (NAC 645.425). Advertising must state Division approval. Fees not stated in what was read.

**Engine rules.**
- At least **two examinations** per course; an hour of instruction is **50 minutes**; partial completion does not count (NAC 645.410). Course Manual C1-5: minimum **75%** for the course before a certificate issues.
- NAC 645.412 (students taking courses "by correspondence"): closed-book final **supervised by a proctor at a location listed in the school's application**, plus two progress exams/quizzes, identity proof before each exam, minimum and maximum completion time. **Unresolved:** whether an internet course is "correspondence" for 645.412 or is governed only by 645.443's "secure electronic method". Course Manual B4-3/B4-4: online testing needs an Application for Secure Electronic Testing; a notarised Certificate of Test Proctor where the sponsor requires a proctored final. Plan for a proctored or Division-approved secure electronic final.
- Records: attendance and certification records kept **7 years** after enrolment (NAC 645.404).

### Utah — Build as interactive distance education; outline to obtain

**Hours.** Utah Code 61-2f-203(1)(d)(i) (eff. 5/6/2026): division-approved program of "not less than 120 hours"; an hour is 50 minutes of each 60; **no more than 8 hours per individual per day**. R162-2f-202a(1)(c)(i): 120 hours; (3) both exam parts within 12 months of completing education, application within 90 days of passing.

**Delivery.** R162-2f-102(15) distance education (interactive methods incl. interactive computer software, internet-based instruction, other interactive online courses; teacher and student separated by distance and sometimes time); excludes home study and correspondence. R162-2f-102(13) correspondence course: self-paced, not distance or traditional, primarily student-initiated, instructor interaction lacking in substance or irregular. R162-2f-206b(2): to certify a prelicensing course for distance education, file **no later than 60 days** before start: the (1) items, delivery methods, media, Division access in the same delivery, "specific and regularly scheduled interactive events", how learning objectives are measured "at regular intervals", how and when certified prelicensing instructors answer questions, director attestation on technology, complaint process. R162-2f-206b(3)(d): classroom facility "unless approved for distance education". **Design implication:** an asynchronous course qualifies only if built with regular interactive checkpoints and a documented instructor-answer channel; whether "regularly scheduled" can be satisfied by in-course computer interactions (rather than calendar events) is the one interpretive point to confirm with the Division.

**Approval route.** School: name approval, then certification filed at least **90 days** before teaching (R162-2f-206a); Pre-License School Application rev. 01/23/2023, **$100** non-refundable; certification lasts **24 months**; material changes within 15 days. Course: R162-2f-206b(1) outline with class periods per subject, **3–5 learning objectives per 3 class hours**, the commission-approved outline reference for each topic, number of quizzes/exams, grading, **at least two final exams with answer key**, failure procedure, texts. Checklist (5/14/2014): for real-estate distance education, instructions for accessing the course (IDECC only for appraisal). Instructors: R162-2f-206d — 5 years licensee experience or equivalent, 12 months teaching or Division instructor workshop, **80%** on a subject exam, renew every 24 months. No bond or in-state facility requirement found in the rules or forms read. Second regulator (Utah postsecondary proprietary school registration) **not checked**.

**Engine rules.** Max **8 credit hours per student per day** (statute and R162-2f-206b(3), 401d(2)); no credit to a student who misses more than **10%** of class time or fails the final (R162-2f-401d(2)); testing limited to **10%** of required class time (206b(3)); no challenge exams; records (registration, attendance, exam results) kept **3 years** after enrolment (401d(1)(f)); completion uploaded within **10 days** (401d(1)(j)); guest lecturers max 20%. No final-exam pass mark found in the rule. **Question 2 ("is the content mine?"):** the course must follow a commission-approved outline that the school must give its instructors (401d(1)); it was not found published — obtain it before writing.

### Oregon — ARELLO-only, skip

OAR 863-022-0010: 150 clock-hours in seven courses (table above); final exam question counts per course — Law, Practice, Finance 60 each; Contracts, Agency 30 each; Property Management 20; Brokerage 40; pass **75%** for private career schools. OAR 863-022-0005: clock-hour is 60 minutes excluding breaks; "distance education" is not defined there. OAR 863-022-0030(3): online application with (a) HECC private career school licence, **(b) ARELLO certification documentation if delivered through distance education**, (c) vendor authorization, (d) materials incl. Agency topic checklist, final exams and separate answer keys, instructor forms, (e) Oregon SOS registration; no application fee; approvals expire every **June 30**. REA Broker Education page: only Oregon community colleges, colleges, universities or HECC-licensed private career schools may offer the courses.

### Hawaii — Build

**Hours and delivery.** HAR §16-99-36: minimum 60 class hours conforming to the Commission's salesperson curriculum. §16-99-52.1 (eff. 5/21/01, am. 12/19/16): independent study courses conform to Commission curricula and must reasonably require **at least 60 hours** (salesperson); the registered school must be available for questions, provide reasonable oversight that the enrolled student does the work, obtain the signed statement "I certify that I have personally completed each assigned module of instruction", and certify completion only after all modules and a passed final examination. §16-99-61: certificate on 80% attendance plus final (classroom) or completion of an independent study course; valid **2 years** from the class completion date. HRS §467-9.5 requires a Commission-approved prelicensing course without prescribing a delivery format; §467-25.5 provides school and instructor registration.

**Approval route.** Register as a real estate school (HAR ch. 99 subch. 5) at least **90 days** before opening; registration expires each even-numbered year (re-register by Nov 30). Form SCHOOLREG_2410 fees: $50 application; $850 registration for one curriculum ($1,260 both); Compliance Resolution Fund $63 (even years) / $126 (odd years). Bond required for live-classroom courses (minimum $2,000 under §16-99-53(c)(8)); **not required for students enrolled only in an electronically delivered independent study course** (§16-99-53(c)(8)(C)). Principal designated; instructors certified biennially per course, initial exam **85%** (§16-99-58). Online course submitted on thumb drive or screenshots; school must describe identity verification and a cheating/identity-misrepresentation policy. No in-state presence requirement found. Second regulator (Hawaii postsecondary authorization) **not checked**.

**Engine rules.** Per-module signed personal-completion attestation; final exam required (no numeric pass mark found in the rules read); records **3 years** (§16-99-62(c)); completion lists to the Commission within **10 days** (§16-99-62). **Question 2:** content is prescribed by the Commission's salesperson curriculum, which is published (cca.hawaii.gov) — public, like Alaska.

### Connecticut — Open, no Build path found

DCP "Instructions for Submission of Courses" (undated): P&P at least 60 hours of classroom instruction, final exam not counted; asynchronous online courses must include an **ARELLO certificate** and expire on the ARELLO expiration date; certificate to student within 7 days; completions to PSI within 30 days; in-classroom schedules 10 days ahead (RCSA 20-314a-9); no courses on brokerage premises. DCP "Instructions for Real Estate Course Providers" (changes eff. April 1, 2024): P&P "at least 60 hours of classroom instruction"; ARELLO named only for asynchronous CE; $100 biennial school registration fee. DCP salesperson application page: 60-hour P&P course approved in Connecticut. **Unresolved:** the statute (CGS 20-514) and RCSA 20-314a — both unreachable — decide whether "classroom" is a statutory term (Not viable) or whether ARELLO-certified asynchronous courses are permitted (ARELLO-only — skip). Neither is Build.

### Rhode Island — Not viable

230-RICR-30-20-2 (eff. 02/21/2022): §2.7(A) salesperson 45 classroom hours, hours older than 2 years do not count; §2.2(A)(4)–(5) classroom = in-person or live-virtual real-time interactive; clock hour ≥ 50 minutes; §2.7(D) live-virtual pre-licensing must stay live and interactive, on-demand/pre-recorded prohibited (short videos allowed if most is real-time), identity verified before each course, attendance each session, students on video throughout, records 3 years; §2.29 school permit annual (expires Aug 31), course approval 3 years, instructor 5 years licensed experience.

### Delaware — ARELLO-only, skip

24 DE Admin. Code 2925: §5.4.6 99 hours (3/36/33/24/3); §5.8 hour = 50 minutes instruction + 10 minutes break; §4.1 classroom or distance, asynchronous or synchronous; §5.6 all 99 hours may be distance; §4.3.1.1 asynchronous courses must hold ARELLO or IDECC certification (or successor) before submission and keep it; §4.3.1.4 software shuts down after 10 minutes of inactivity; §4.3.1.7 asynchronous exams at least 5 multiple-choice questions per credit hour, 80% pass; §4.3.1.9 IDECC Distance Education Instructor Certification; §5.19 70% on each of sales, law, math exams; course applications 60 days ahead (§8.1), approvals 2 calendar years (§9.5); classroom schools need Delaware DOE facility approval (§§5.15, 9.8). Note: Delaware moved to a new Regulations Management System on 19 May 2025; the text read is the archive copy (page updated 6/5/2024), so a post-2024 amendment is not excluded.
