# Arizona Salesperson Pre-License 90h v1.0 — Course Syllabus

**Regulator:** Arizona Department of Real Estate  
**Hours:** 90  
**Status:** Development; not approved; enrollment disabled  
**Instructor:** Unassigned; state approval/qualification and course assignment required

## Learning outcomes

Students completing an approved release will explain the prescribed subjects, apply Arizona law/forms to entry-level facts, perform required calculations, recognize professional-scope limits, and produce an auditable transaction response.

## Delivery and completion

Synchronous or asynchronous distance learning under ADRE SPS 2025.01 and the applicable course application. The exact course configuration controls active time, sequence, units, interactions, assessment, remediation, identity, inactivity, final eligibility, completion period, and proctoring. Payment never determines completion or credit.

## Assessment

Formative checks and module quizzes trigger objective-specific remediation. Draft final configuration: 1 form(s), 90 items/form, passing standard: 75%. ADRE SPS 2025.01 compliant proctoring; final not on a student-supplied device. Any item count not prescribed by the regulator is a StateCourse submission proposal.
