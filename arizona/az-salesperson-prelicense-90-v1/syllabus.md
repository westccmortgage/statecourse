# Arizona Salesperson Pre-License 90h v1.0 — Course Syllabus

**Regulator:** Arizona Department of Real Estate  
**Hours:** 90  
**Status:** Development; not approved; enrollment disabled  
**Instructor:** Unassigned; state approval/qualification and course assignment required

## Learning outcomes

Students completing an approved release will explain the prescribed subjects, apply Arizona law/forms to entry-level facts, perform required calculations, recognize professional-scope limits, and produce an auditable transaction response.

## Course organization

- Hours 01–63: StateCourse National Core submitted as part of this Arizona course version.
- Hours 64–90: 27-hour Arizona-specific layer mapped to the ADRE outline effective January 1, 2026.
- Separate Arizona Contract Writing 6h v1.0: independent course/certificate and not included in these 90 hours.

## Delivery and completion

Proposed asynchronous distance learning under ADRE SPS 2025.01 and the current prelicense application. Each unit is 50 active instructional minutes. After 15 minutes of inactivity, the learner is logged out, returns to the unit start, and re-verifies identity. The exact approved configuration controls time, sequence, interactivity, assessment, remediation, identity, final eligibility and proctoring. Payment never determines completion or credit.

## Assessment

Each unit contains scenario interactivity and a scored quiz requiring 75% before progression. Incorrect responses trigger alternate instruction and a new scored item. The comprehensive school final requires 75%, uses a secure bank of at least twice the final-form length, is delivered in a lockdown environment, and is excluded from the 90 hours. Final-form length remains a provider design decision pending SME and ADRE review; it is not invented as a regulator requirement. Proctor method and device controls remain a release gate.

## Development status

The 90-hour source/objective/activity blueprint is complete. Student lesson manuscripts, verified citations, media, item bank, instructor guide and accessibility review are still in development. This syllabus cannot be used for enrollment or credit.
