## Per-hour completion design

Each hour is a single 50-minute distance module. A student cannot advance until the module's active instructional time, required content, scenario interactivity, 75% quiz, any remediation, identity controls and compliance checks are satisfied. After 15 minutes of inactivity, the session is logged out; the student returns to the unit start and re-verifies identity. Passive open-page time never accrues.
