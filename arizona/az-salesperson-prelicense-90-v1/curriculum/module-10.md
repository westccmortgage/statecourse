# Arizona Real Estate Salesperson Prelicense — Module AZ-10

## Arizona real estate licensing and the Department

**Course:** Real Estate Salesperson Prelicense · **Instructional hours: 7.0** of 90.0
**Arizona-specific:** ★ **YES** — this module is part of the **27-hour Arizona-specific course** that
**A.R.S. § 32-2124(B)** and **A.A.C. R4-28-401(A)(2)** provide **may not be waived.**
**Course version:** 1.0 submission draft
**Regulatory status:** Development. Not approved. No Arizona prelicensure credit is available.

> ### The 27 hours that cannot be waived
>
> **A.R.S. § 32-2124(B)**, closing sentence: *"the commissioner may waive all or a portion of the
> prelicensure course requirement, **other than the TWENTY-SEVEN-HOUR ARIZONA-SPECIFIC COURSE**, for an
> applicant who holds a current real estate license in another state."*
>
> **A.A.C. R4-28-401(A)(2)**: an applicant may, *"**except for the 27-hour Arizona-specific course**, apply
> for and be granted a waiver of the prelicensure courses."*
>
> **Modules AZ-10, AZ-11, AZ-12 and AZ-13 are that 27 hours** — 7 + 7 + 7 + 6. An experienced licensee
> moving to Arizona from another state may be relieved of the other 63; **they may not be relieved of
> these.**
>
> **A submission gate.** **Neither the statute nor the rule states what the 27 hours must contain**, and
> **the Department's prescribed course content — which R4-28-401(D) says the Department will provide on
> request — was NOT obtained by this project.** These four modules are built from **A.R.S. Title 32,
> Chapter 20 and A.A.C. R4-28**, read on 2026-08-20. **Conforming them to the Department's own content is a
> submission gate.**

## Learning objectives

1. State the education, age and character requirements for an Arizona salesperson's licence.
2. State the ten-year limit on course completion and the exception to it.
3. State the passing score and the proctoring requirement.
4. State what the state examination must establish, under A.R.S. § 32-2124(E).
5. State what a certified real estate school and an approved course must do.
6. State the licensing consequence of passing an examination.

## A. What A.R.S. § 32-2124 requires of you

### A.1 The education — subsection (B)

> *"All applicants ... for a real estate salesperson's license shall show evidence satisfactory to the
> commissioner that they have **completed a real estate salesperson's course that is prescribed and approved
> by the commissioner** and that is **at least ninety classroom hours, or the equivalent**, of instruction
> **in a real estate school certified by the commissioner** and have **satisfactorily passed an examination
> on the course**."*

**Four conditions in one sentence.** The course must be **prescribed and approved by the commissioner**; it
must be **at least ninety classroom hours or the equivalent**; it must be delivered by **a school the
commissioner has certified**; and the applicant must have **passed an examination on it.**

### A.2 Delivery — the same subsection

> *"An applicant may complete the ... course ... through a **live classroom course or a distance learning
> course** if ... offered by a real estate school that is **certified by the commissioner**. **The applicant
> must complete a proctored examination on the live classroom course or the distance learning course IN
> PERSON.** An applicant may complete the required course or instructional segments in **any combination of
> in-person or synchronous remote delivery methods**."*

**Read the three sentences together, because they are not saying the same thing.**

**Instruction** may be **live classroom or distance learning**, and may be taken in **any combination of
in-person or synchronous remote** delivery. Arizona is permissive about how you learn.

**The examination is not.** *"**The applicant must complete a proctored examination ... IN PERSON.**"* However
the course was delivered, **the examination is proctored and it is taken in person.**

### A.3 The ten-year limit

> *"**The real estate salesperson's course completion or its equivalent may not be more than ten years before
> the date of application** unless, at the time of application, the commissioner **determines in the
> commissioner's discretion** that the applicant has **work experience in a real estate-related field and
> education that together are equivalent** to the prelicensure education requirement."*

**Ten years, measured to the DATE OF APPLICATION.** And the exception is **discretionary** — the
commissioner *"determines in the commissioner's discretion"* — and requires **work experience AND
education together**, not either alone.

### A.4 The passing score — A.A.C. R4-28-401(A)(1)

> *"Complete the required **90-hour prelicensure education course** and **satisfactorily pass an examination
> on the course with a score of AT LEAST 75 PERCENT** as prescribed in A.R.S. § 32-2124."*

**Seventy-five percent is the Department's figure.** The in-module checks throughout this course are **not**
that examination.

### A.5 Age, and the felony bar

> **§ 32-2124(D):** *"Before receiving any license provided for by this chapter, an applicant must be **at
> least eighteen years of age**."*
>
> **§ 32-2124(M):** *"The commissioner **shall not issue a license** to a person who has been **convicted of
> a felony offense** and who is **currently incarcerated** for the conviction, **paroled or under community
> supervision and under the supervision of a parole or community supervision officer** or who is **on
> probation** as a result of the conviction."*

**Note what (M) is and is not.** It is a **bar while the person is in custody or under supervision** for a
felony conviction. It is **not** a general statement of how a criminal record affects licensure — that is
governed by **A.R.S. § 32-2153**, which **this project did not read**, and by the Department's own process.
**A licensee asked about a criminal record routes the question to the Department.**

### A.6 The six additional hours — subsection (L)

> *"An applicant for an original real estate salesperson's license, **after completing the requirements of
> subsection B of this section**, shall provide certification to the department **at the time of
> application** evidencing completion of **six hours of instruction in real estate contract law and contract
> writing**. **This instruction shall include participation by the applicant in drafting contracts to
> purchase real property, listing agreements and lease agreements.**"*

**Ninety plus six is ninety-six.** The six are **separate and additional**, proved **at the time of
application**, and they require **participation in drafting** three named document types. The companion
course delivers them.

## B. What the state examination must establish — § 32-2124(E)

> *"The commissioner shall ascertain by a written, electronic or other examination method that an applicant
> for a real estate license has:*
>
> **1.** *An **appropriate knowledge of the English language**, including reading, writing and spelling, and
> of **arithmetical computations common to real estate practices**.*
>
> **2.** *At a minimum, an **understanding of the general purpose and legal effect of any real estate
> practices, principles and related forms**, including **agency contracts, real estate contracts, deposit
> receipts, deeds, mortgages, deeds of trust, security agreements, bills of sale, land contracts of sale and
> property management**, and of any other areas that the commissioner deems necessary and proper.*
>
> **3.** *A **thorough understanding of the obligations between principal and agent**, the **principles of
> real estate and business opportunity practice**, the **applicable canons of business ethics**, **this
> chapter and rules adopted pursuant to this chapter**.*
>
> **4.** *An **appropriate knowledge of other real estate practices and principles** as determined by the
> commissioner."*

### B.1 This is the closest thing to a content outline this project obtained

**And it is not the Department's prescribed content**, which was not obtained. It is, however, what the
statute says the examination must establish, and it is worth reading as a map of this course:

| § 32-2124(E) requires | Delivered in |
|---|---|
| Arithmetical computations common to real estate practices | **AZ-09**, and throughout |
| Purpose and legal effect of **agency contracts** | **AZ-05**, **AZ-11** |
| Purpose and legal effect of **real estate contracts and deposit receipts** | **AZ-04**, **AZ-13**, and the companion contract writing course |
| **Deeds, mortgages, deeds of trust, security agreements, bills of sale, land contracts of sale** | **AZ-01**, **AZ-03**, **AZ-06** |
| **Property management** | **AZ-09** |
| **Obligations between principal and agent** — *thorough* | **AZ-05**, **AZ-11** |
| **Canons of business ethics** | **AZ-11**, **AZ-12** |
| **This chapter and rules adopted pursuant to it** | **AZ-10** through **AZ-13** |

**Note the word "thorough" in (3).** Compare § 32-2124(F) and (G), which require only a **"general
understanding"** of the principal-agent obligations for cemetery and membership camping applicants. **The
real estate standard is higher**, and Module AZ-11 is written to it.

### B.2 The examination itself — A.A.C. R4-28-403

> **(A)** *The Department shall hold, or contract for, **at least one state licensing examination each
> week**.*
>
> **(B)** *A state licence examination **shall not be returned to the applicant**. The applicant shall be
> **notified in person** of the results by the words **"passed" or "did not pass."** The results notification
> for an applicant who did not pass **shall also show the score for the examination and the relative score
> for each content area.***
>
> **(C)** *"**Qualifying to take or passing a license examination does not constitute a waiver of the
> Commissioner's right to deny issuance of a license** if grounds exist pursuant to A.R.S. § 32-2153 or any
> other applicable statute."*

**Subsection (C) is the one to hold.** **Passing the examination is not a licence.** The Commissioner may
still deny issuance on grounds in § 32-2153 — a section **this project did not read**, and from which **no
ground, penalty or procedure is stated in this build.**

**And (B)'s failing-candidate provision is a genuine benefit**: a candidate who did not pass receives **the
score and the relative score for each content area**, which tells them where to study.

**§ 32-2124(I)** adds that *"the examination for a broker's license shall be **more exacting and stringent
and of a broader scope** than the examination for a salesperson's license."*

## C. Schools, courses and instructors — the framework this course sits in

### C.1 A.R.S. § 32-2135(A)

> *"before offering a course of study towards completion of the education requirement ... a school shall
> **obtain from the commissioner a certificate of approval or renewal to operate a school for a period of at
> least four years**. A school shall also **obtain a certificate of course approval for each course offered
> for credit** ... **Each school is responsible for the content of any course it offers and for the
> professional administration and teaching of the course.** **Prelicensure education live classroom courses,
> prelicensure education distance learning courses, continuing education live classroom courses and distance
> learning continuing education courses are subject to approval** pursuant to this section."*

**Two separate approvals: the SCHOOL and each COURSE.** And the sentence in the middle is the one that
matters most to a student: **the school is responsible for the content.**

**§ 32-2135(B):** *"Each approved school shall **issue a certificate of real estate course attendance** to
each person who completes an approved prelicensure or continuing education course."*

### C.2 What the Department may withdraw approval for — § 32-2135(C)

Among the grounds: **improperly certifying student attendance or performance**; ***"teaching information or
using course materials that have not been approved by the commissioner"***; **filing false or misleading
applications, reports or documentation**; and ***"teaching course content that is not current or that has
substantially changed from the course as approved."***

**"Not current" is an obligation that runs continuously**, not at approval. And **R4-28-404(H)(3)** requires
a school to **obtain approval BEFORE changing course content or course length**, or offering a new course.

### C.3 What a course application must contain — A.A.C. R4-28-404(B)

Among the requirements:

> **4.** *"A **detailed outline of course material content** that clearly lists the subject matter to be
> covered";*
> **5.** *"The number of credit hours requested. **The time allocated by a school for examination shall not
> be included in calculating credit hours if the examination is used for overall evaluation**";*
> **7.** *"A **definition of segments** if the course is to be offered in part and in its entirety";*
> **8.** *"If any **third party recorded material** will be used as instructional aids, **they will not
> exceed 20 percent of instructional time**."*

**Two constraints on this build follow directly.**

**Examination time is not instruction.** Where the examination is used for overall evaluation, **its time
does not count towards the 90 hours.** So the ninety hours are ninety hours of **instruction**, and the
proctored examination sits outside them.

**Third party recorded material may not exceed 20 percent of instructional time.** For a 90-hour course
that is **18 hours** at most.

### C.4 School records and your enrolment agreement

**R4-28-404(D)** — the school shall maintain a record **for five years** of each student: the **name**, the
**dates of attendance**, the **title of each course**, the **course number, category and credit hours
awarded**, and **the original signature roster, if live in person instruction.**

**R4-28-404(E)** — the enrolment agreement must include, ***"in bold type and capital letters"***: the
**course or course segment title within a curriculum**; the **total credit hours applicable for licensure or
renewal**; the **cost of each course**; a **statement of the refund policy**; a **statement related to
license pre-determination petitions**; and a **statement of any job placement service.**

**Note the pre-determination petition item.** A person with a concern about whether their history will bar
licensure may petition the Department **before** paying for a course, and **the enrolment agreement must
tell them so, in bold capitals.** **This project did not read the statute governing that petition**, and no
procedure, deadline or fee for it is stated here.

**R4-28-404(F)** — a school may advertise **job placement services** only if referrals result from **direct
contact between the school placement service and prospective employers**, with **documented evidence
maintained.** *"The Department does not consider lists of employers given to graduates to be a placement
service."*

### C.5 Instructor approval and school advertising

**R4-28-404(C)** — any person wishing to instruct an approved course shall submit **detailed information of
professional qualifications, demonstrating an ability to instruct, subject matter expertise**, or other
applicable information.

**R4-28-502(D)** — *"A **school** shall include its **name, school number, phone and email contact
information, and the name of the school administrator** in all advertising of Department-approved courses.
The school owner, director, or administrator shall **supervise all advertising**. The school owner shall
ensure that the school's advertising is **accurate**."*

**§ 32-2135(D)** — a school **shall not** offer a course for credit that is not approved (*"except that the
school **may advertise a course as pending approval** before its approval"*), or **promote or advertise the
school using false or misleading statistics or testimonials or any other form of deceptive advertisement.**
