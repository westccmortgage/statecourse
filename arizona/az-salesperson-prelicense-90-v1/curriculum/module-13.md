2. State the delivery duty in R4-28-802(A).
3. State the offer-submission duty in R4-28-802(B), including what does **not** release the licensee from it.

7. State the six-month inspection right for an uninspected lot or parcel.
8. State how rescission notices are delivered and deemed received.

## A. Compensation sharing: A.A.C. R4-28-701

> *"A real estate broker shall **disclose to all the parties in a transaction, in writing at least THREE
> CALENDAR DAYS BEFORE CLOSING, the name of each employing broker who represents a party to the transaction
> and who will receive compensation from the transaction.**"*

Break the sentence into its five requirements:

| Element | The rule |
|---|---|
| **Who discloses** | A **real estate broker** |
| **To whom** | **All the parties** in the transaction |
| **Form** | **In writing** |
| **When** | **At least three calendar days before closing** |
| **Content** | The **name of each employing broker** who **represents a party** and **will receive compensation** from the transaction |

**"Calendar days,"** not business days. The rule says calendar and this module states nothing else.

**"At least three ... before closing"** is a floor, not a target. A disclosure delivered two days before
closing is late, and lateness cannot be cured at the closing table.

**The content is narrower than licensees assume.** The rule requires **names of employing brokers** meeting
**both** conditions — representing a party **and** receiving compensation. **The rule as read does not
require the amount**, and **this module states no amount requirement**, because none appears in the text
read.

**Where it sits against Module AZ-11.** R4-28-1101(F) requires **prior written consent of all parties**
before a licensee accepts compensation from, or represents, more than one party; R4-28-1101(G) requires
**prior written acknowledgement** for compensation for related goods or services. **R4-28-701 is a third,
separate obligation** with its own deadline. **Satisfying one does not satisfy another**, and a licensee who
treats them as one document has missed two rules.

## B. Conveyance documents: A.A.C. R4-28-802

### B.1 Delivery of signed documents — subsection (A)

> **(A)**: *"Upon execution of any transaction document a licensee shall **expeditiously deliver a legible
> copy of the signed document and final agreement to each party signing the document.**"*

Four elements: **upon execution**, **expeditiously**, a **legible copy**, to **each party signing.**

**"Legible"** is in the rule. A copy that cannot be read is not delivery.

**"Each party signing the document."** Not the client only.

**"Expeditiously"** is the same word R4-28-1101(C) uses for performance generally, and the two work
together: a licensee who does not deliver has both failed (A) here and delayed performance under
R4-28-1101(C) — which, recall, reaches delay **through neglect** as well as intentional delay.

### B.2 Submitting offers — subsection (B)

This is the longest operative sentence in the module and the most consequential. Read it whole.

> **(B)**: *"During the term of a listing agreement, a licensee shall **promptly submit to the licensee's
> client all offers to purchase or lease** the listed property. **Upon receiving permission from the seller
> or lessor**, the licensee ... **may disclose to all offerors or their agents the existence and terms of
> all additional offers**. The licensee shall submit to the client **all offers made prior to closing and
> IS NOT RELEASED FROM THIS DUTY BY THE CLIENT'S ACCEPTANCE OF AN OFFER** unless the client instructs the
> licensee **in writing** to cease submitting offers or unless otherwise provided in the listing agreement,
> lease, or purchase contract. The licensee **may voluntarily submit offers ... regardless of any
> limitations contained in the listing agreement and may submit offers after the listing agreement is
> terminated.**"*

| The rule says | What follows from it |
|---|---|
| **Promptly submit all offers** during the listing term | No screening by price, terms, or the licensee's view of seriousness |
| Disclosure of the **existence and terms of additional offers** requires **permission from the seller or lessor** | The licensee does not decide this; the client does. Without permission, no disclosure. |
| **All offers made prior to closing**, and **acceptance of an offer does NOT release the duty** | The duty continues **after** the property is under contract |
| Released only by a **written** instruction from the client to cease, **or** as otherwise provided in the listing agreement, lease, or purchase contract | An oral "stop sending them" does **not** release the duty |
| The licensee **may voluntarily** submit offers regardless of limitations in the listing agreement, **and after the listing agreement is terminated** | A permission, not a duty — it survives limitations and termination |

**The sentence licensees get wrong:** *"is not released from this duty by the client's acceptance of an
offer."* Under contract is not the end of the duty. It continues **until closing** unless one of the two
stated releases applies, and the first of them must be **in writing.**

**And note the direction of the last clause.** The rule permits a licensee to submit offers **despite**
listing-agreement limitations and **after** termination. It converts what a licensee might fear is a breach
of the listing agreement into something the rule expressly allows.

### B.3 Retention — subsection (C)

> **(C)**: The broker shall retain **copies of all receipts and disbursements**, **copies of the executed and
> delivered escrow closing statements**, and **any transaction documents signed by parties or any
> disclosures made as part of the transaction** — **in addition to A.R.S. §§ 32-2151.01 and 32-2174.**

Three categories, stated as **additions** to two statutes.

**The rule points to statutes this project did not read.** R4-28-802(C) says its list is *"in addition to"*
**A.R.S. §§ 32-2151.01 and 32-2174**, and **neither statute was read by this project.** **No retention
period appears anywhere in this module** for that reason — the periods live in those statutes, and this
course states nothing it has not read. **Obtaining them is a submission gate**, and it is recorded as one in
`arizona/regulatory/primary-text-readings.md`.

Compare Module AZ-10, which does state a **five-year** school record requirement — that figure comes from
**A.A.C. R4-28-404**, which *was* read. **The distinction between what has been read and what has not is
maintained item by item throughout this course.**

## C. Contract disclosures: A.A.C. R4-28-803

### C.1 The public report legend

Where a **public report** is required, the agreement must carry, **in bold or larger print above the
signature portion**:

> ***"THE DEVELOPER SHALL GIVE A PROSPECTIVE PURCHASER A COPY OF THE PUBLIC REPORT AND AN OPPORTUNITY TO
> READ AND REVIEW IT BEFORE THE PROSPECTIVE PURCHASER SIGNS THIS DOCUMENT."***

Three placement requirements travel with the words: **bold or larger print**, **above the signature
portion**, and **in the agreement itself.**

**A boundary this module holds.** **When a public report is required, what one contains, who issues it, and
the subdivision and unsubdivided-land statutes behind it were NOT read by this project.** This module states
**only that R4-28-803 requires this legend where a public report is required** — it states **no rule about
when that is.** A licensee working in subdivided lots, unsubdivided land, or time-shares takes that question
to the designated broker and to the Department.

### C.2 Where the earnest money goes — subsection (C)

> **(C)**: *"The contract **shall indicate where the earnest money or down payment, if any, will be
> deposited** and shall include the **name of the title company, the name of the broker's trust account, or
> other depository.**"*

**The contract must say where the money goes, by name.** Three named possibilities: the **title company**,
the **broker's trust account**, or **other depository.**

### C.3 Money paid directly to the seller — subsection (D)

> **(D)**: Where money is paid **directly to the seller and not placed in a neutral escrow depository**, the
> document must **conspicuously disclose** it and **the purchaser shall sign or initial the provision**, and
> a bold or large-print warning must appear in the **public report, purchase contract and agreement of
> sale**:
>
> ***"PROSPECTIVE PURCHASERS ARE ADVISED THAT EARNEST MONEY DEPOSITS, DOWN PAYMENTS, AND OTHER ADVANCED
> MONEY WILL NOT BE PLACED IN A NEUTRAL ESCROW. THIS MONEY WILL BE PAID DIRECTLY TO THE SELLER AND MAY BE
> USED BY THE SELLER. THIS MEANS THE PURCHASER ASSUMES A RISK OF LOSING THE MONEY IF THE SELLER IS UNABLE
> OR UNWILLING TO PERFORM UNDER THE TERMS OF THE PURCHASE CONTRACT."***

**Three requirements, all of which must be met:**

1. **Conspicuous disclosure** in the document;
2. The **purchaser signs or initials the provision** — the rule requires the purchaser's own mark on it,
   not merely a signature on the contract as a whole; and
3. The **bold or large-print warning** in the **public report, the purchase contract, and the agreement of
   sale.**

**Read what the warning actually says.** *"May be used by the seller"* and *"assumes a risk of losing the
money."* The Department wrote a warning that names the risk in plain words rather than describing the
arrangement neutrally, and a licensee should not soften it in explanation.

## D. Rescission: A.A.C. R4-28-804

**Two different periods, and a licensee must not confuse them.** They are the two figures most likely to be
transposed under examination pressure, so learn them by the transaction type they attach to.

| Transaction | Deadline | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **An unimproved subdivided lot, or any unsubdivided land** | **By midnight of the SEVENTH calendar day** following the day the purchaser or lessee executed the agreement | **R4-28-804(A)** |
| **A time-share interval** | **By midnight of the TENTH calendar day** following the day the purchaser or lessee executed the agreement | **R4-28-804(B)** |

### D.1 The unimproved-lot and unsubdivided-land legend — subsection (A)

The agreement must contain, **in bold or larger print above the signature portion**, substantially:

> ***"HAS THE LEGAL RIGHT TO RESCIND (CANCEL) THIS AGREEMENT WITHOUT CAUSE OR REASON OF ANY KIND ... BY
> MIDNIGHT OF THE SEVENTH CALENDAR DAY FOLLOWING THE DAY THE PURCHASER OR LESSEE EXECUTED THE AGREEMENT. IF
> THE PURCHASER OR LESSEE DOES NOT INSPECT THE LOT OR PARCEL BEFORE THE EXECUTION OF THE AGREEMENT, THE
> PURCHASER OR LESSEE SHALL HAVE SIX MONTHS TO INSPECT ... AND AT THE TIME OF INSPECTION SHALL HAVE THE
> RIGHT TO UNILATERALLY RESCIND THE AGREEMENT."***

Four things in one legend:

1. **"Without cause or reason of any kind."** The purchaser owes no explanation, and the licensee may not
   ask for one as a condition.
2. **"By midnight of the seventh calendar day following the day"** of execution. The count starts the day
   **after** execution, and the deadline is **midnight**, not the close of business.
3. **The six-month inspection right**, which attaches only where the purchaser or lessee **did not inspect
   the lot or parcel before execution.**
4. **At the time of inspection, the right to UNILATERALLY rescind.** A separate right from the seven-day
   right, triggered by a different fact, exercisable at a different time.

**The six-month right is the one that surprises people.** An uninspected lot carries a rescission right that
does not expire with the seventh day. **Both rights are in the same legend and both must be in the
agreement.**

### D.2 Time-share intervals — subsection (B)

> **(B)**: Rescission ***"by midnight of the TENTH calendar day following the day the purchaser or lessee
> executed the agreement."***

**Ten, not seven**, and the same midnight-and-following-day counting.

### D.3 How the opportunity is given and how notice works — subsection (C)

> **(C)**: An opportunity to exercise rescission ***"shall be provided by conspicuously disclosing the
> complete and current name, address, telephone number, and email address of the seller ON THE FACE of all
> agreements and contracts."*** Notices of rescission **must be in writing** and are **deemed delivered and
> received when hand delivered, sent via email, sent by courier service, or certified mail** — and the
> **seller must issue a receipt if hand delivered.**

**Four items of seller contact detail, complete and current, on the FACE of the agreement:** **name**,
**address**, **telephone number**, **email address.** Buried contact details do not satisfy (C) — the rule
says *"conspicuously"* and *"on the face."*

**Notice must be in writing**, and the rule states four methods that are **deemed delivered and received**:

| Method | Deemed delivered and received |
|---|---|
| **Hand delivery** | Yes — **and the seller must issue a receipt** |
| **Email** | Yes, **when sent** |
| **Courier service** | Yes, **when sent** |
| **Certified mail** | Yes, **when sent** |

**"When ... sent"** is what makes this rule work for the purchaser: a notice emailed on the seventh day is
deemed received then, whatever happens afterwards. **The only method carrying an extra obligation on the
seller is hand delivery**, which requires a **receipt.**

## E. The whole module as one page of deadlines

Every deadline this module states, in one place, each with the rule that states it. **No period in this
table is stated anywhere else in this course, and none of them is a StateCourse figure.**

| Period | Applies to | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Three calendar days before closing** | Written compensation-sharing disclosure to all parties | **R4-28-701** |
| **Expeditiously, upon execution** | Delivery of a legible copy of a signed document to each signing party | **R4-28-802(A)** |
| **Promptly, and until closing** | Submission of all offers to the client | **R4-28-802(B)** |
| **Midnight of the seventh calendar day** after execution | Rescission — unimproved subdivided lot or unsubdivided land | **R4-28-804(A)** |
| **Six months** to inspect, with a right to rescind at inspection | Purchaser or lessee who did **not** inspect the lot or parcel before execution | **R4-28-804(A)** |
| **Midnight of the tenth calendar day** after execution | Rescission — time-share interval | **R4-28-804(B)** |

**And one period this module deliberately does not state:** the **retention period** for transaction
records. **R4-28-802(C)** points to **A.R.S. §§ 32-2151.01 and 32-2174**; **neither was read**; **no number
appears.**

## F. Where the 27 hours end

Modules AZ-10 through AZ-13 are the **27-hour Arizona-specific course** that **A.R.S. § 32-2124(B)** and
**A.A.C. R4-28-401(A)(2)** provide **may not be waived** for a licensee coming from another state. Reading
the four together, the reason is visible:

| Module | What is genuinely Arizona's own |
|---|---|
| **AZ-10** | 90 hours, **proctored in person**, a **ten-year** completion window, **75 percent**, weekly examinations, and **passing is not a licence** |
| **AZ-11** | A **fiduciary** standard where neighbouring states legislated one away; disclosure of **either party's inability to perform**; an affirmative duty to **obtain** information |
| **AZ-12** | A **progressive disciplinary policy** required by rule; **"owner/agent"**; **43,560 square feet**; **artificial intelligence** named as an advertising medium; **no scrolling** |
| **AZ-13** | **Three calendar days**; a duty to submit offers that **survives acceptance**; **seven days**, **ten days**, and **six months** |

**An experienced out-of-state licensee who skipped these 27 hours would carry the wrong rule into an
Arizona transaction in every one of those rows.** That is what the statute's closing sentence protects
against, and it is why this course labels each of the four modules on its first page.
