## Item record requirements

Every item contains:

- immutable item ID and version;
- course/objective and official-source mapping;
- cognitive level and difficulty target;
- scenario/stem, options, keyed response and rationale;
- source quotation locator kept in the secure authoring record;
- legal/SME/editorial review names and dates;
- bias, accessibility, duplication and psychometric review;
- exposure status, retirement reason and change history.

## Release gate

No final can be activated until the approved instructor/SME validates the completed bank, the form generator passes distribution and uniqueness tests, the proctor/device workflow is accepted, and a secure production copy is separated from the public repository/build artifacts.
