**Objective:** AZCW-01 — Parties, property, price, and acceptance

A. Use another state's customary form without checking current Arizona authority.
B. Verify material facts, check the controlling current source/form, act within the broker's role, communicate and document, then refer unresolved specialized issues.
C. Treat payment or LMS completion as permission to give legal, tax, lending, appraisal, inspection, or engineering advice.
D. Omit the issue unless the transaction fails.

**Key:** B

**Rationale:** The approved-course method requires current state authority, verified facts, professional scope, communication, retained evidence, and referral.
