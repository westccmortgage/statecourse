## 1. The education requirement — and why Arizona is 96 hours, not 90

### A.R.S. § 32-2124(B) — the 90-hour prelicensure course

> *"All applicants other than renewal applicants ... for a real estate salesperson's license shall show
> evidence satisfactory to the commissioner that they have **completed a real estate salesperson's course
> that is prescribed and approved by the commissioner** and that is **at least ninety classroom hours, or
> the equivalent**, of instruction **in a real estate school certified by the commissioner** and have
> **satisfactorily passed an examination on the course**. An applicant may complete the ... course ...
> through a **live classroom course or a distance learning course** if ... offered by a real estate school
> that is certified by the commissioner. **The applicant must complete a proctored examination on the live
> classroom course or the distance learning course IN PERSON.** An applicant may complete the required
> course or instructional segments in **any combination of in-person or synchronous remote delivery
> methods**. **The real estate salesperson's course completion or its equivalent may not be more than ten
> years before the date of application** unless, at the time of application, the commissioner determines
> ... that the applicant has work experience in a real estate-related field and education that together are
> equivalent ... **the commissioner may waive all or a portion of the prelicensure course requirement,
> other than the TWENTY-SEVEN-HOUR ARIZONA-SPECIFIC COURSE, for an applicant who holds a current real
> estate license in another state.**"*

### A.R.S. § 32-2124(L) — the SIX ADDITIONAL HOURS, and what they must contain

> *"An applicant for an original real estate salesperson's license, **after completing the requirements of
> subsection B of this section**, shall provide certification to the department **at the time of
> application** evidencing completion of **SIX HOURS OF INSTRUCTION IN REAL ESTATE CONTRACT LAW AND
> CONTRACT WRITING**. **This instruction shall include PARTICIPATION BY THE APPLICANT IN DRAFTING
> CONTRACTS TO PURCHASE REAL PROPERTY, LISTING AGREEMENTS AND LEASE AGREEMENTS.**"*

### What those two subsections fix

**Ninety hours, plus six. Ninety-six in total**, and the six are a **separate, additional** requirement
proved **at the time of application** — not a segment of the ninety. Subsection (L) says so in its own
opening words: *"after completing the requirements of subsection B."*

**And the six hours are not a lecture.** The statute requires **participation by the applicant in
drafting** — and it names **three document types**: **contracts to purchase real property**, **listing
agreements**, and **lease agreements**. A course that reads contract law to a student for six hours has
not satisfied § 32-2124(L).

**Twenty-seven of the ninety are an Arizona-specific course**, named by the statute itself in the waiver
clause and again in the rule below. **The statute does not say what those 27 hours must contain**, and the
Department's prescribed course content was **not obtained** — see § 5.

### A.A.C. R4-28-401(A) — the passing score

> *"Any individual applying for a real estate license shall either:*
> **1.** *Complete the required **90-hour prelicensure education course** and **satisfactorily pass an
> examination on the course with a score of AT LEAST 75 PERCENT** as prescribed in A.R.S. § 32-2124; or*
> **2.** *Except for the **27-hour Arizona-specific course**, apply for and be granted a waiver of the
> prelicensure courses."*

**Seventy-five percent is the Department's figure**, stated in the rule. The **27-hour Arizona-specific
course cannot be waived** — the rule says so, and § 32-2124(B) says so.

### A.A.C. R4-28-401(D) — the content the Department holds

> *"**The Department shall provide a copy of the prelicensure course content to any person requesting
> it.**"*

**The Department has prescribed course content, and this project did not obtain it.** § 32-2124(B) requires
a course *"prescribed and approved by the commissioner."* **Obtaining that content and conforming this
build to it is a submission gate.**

### Other statutory qualifications

- **§ 32-2124(D):** *"Before receiving any license provided for by this chapter, an applicant must be at
  least **eighteen years of age**."*
- **§ 32-2124(M):** the commissioner **shall not** issue a licence to a person **convicted of a felony
  offense** who is **currently incarcerated**, **paroled**, **under community supervision** and supervised
  by an officer, **or on probation** as a result of the conviction.
- **§ 32-2124(H):** a renewal applicant need not re-examine if the application is made **within twelve
  months after the licence expires** and the licence is not cancelled, terminated or suspended.
- **§ 32-2124(I):** *"The examination for a broker's license shall be **more exacting and stringent and of
  a broader scope** than the examination for a salesperson's license."*

### § 32-2124(E) — what the state examination must establish

> *"The commissioner shall ascertain ... that an applicant for a real estate license has:*
> **1.** *"An **appropriate knowledge of the English language**, including reading, writing and spelling,
> and of **arithmetical computations common to real estate practices**.*
> **2.** *"At a minimum, an **understanding of the general purpose and legal effect of any real estate
> practices, principles and related forms**, including **agency contracts, real estate contracts, deposit
> receipts, deeds, mortgages, deeds of trust, security agreements, bills of sale, land contracts of sale
> and property management**, and of any other areas that the commissioner deems necessary and proper.*
> **3.** *"A **thorough understanding of the obligations between principal and agent**, the **principles of
> real estate and business opportunity practice**, the **applicable canons of business ethics**, **this
> chapter and rules adopted pursuant to this chapter**.*
> **4.** *"An appropriate knowledge of other real estate practices and principles as determined by the
> commissioner."*

**That paragraph is the closest thing to a content outline this project obtained**, and the curriculum in
this build is organised so every one of its four items is covered. **It is not a substitute for the
Department's prescribed content.**

## 2. Schools, courses and instructors

### A.R.S. § 32-2135(A)

> *"before offering a course of study towards completion of the education requirement for real estate
> licensure or renewal of licensure, a school shall **obtain from the commissioner a certificate of
> approval or renewal to operate a school for a period of at least four years**. A school shall also
> **obtain a certificate of course approval for each course offered for credit** that is not currently
> approved for another school. **Each school is responsible for the content of any course it offers and
> for the professional administration and teaching of the course.** **Prelicensure education live
> classroom courses, prelicensure education distance learning courses, continuing education live classroom
> courses and distance learning continuing education courses are subject to approval** pursuant to this
> section."*

### § 32-2135(B) — the certificate of attendance

> *"Each approved school shall **issue a certificate of real estate course attendance** to each person who
> completes an approved prelicensure or continuing education course."*

### § 32-2135(C) and (D) — grounds for withdrawal, and prohibitions

The commissioner may withdraw or deny certification for, among other things: **improperly certifying
student attendance or performance**; ***"teaching information or using course materials that have not been
approved by the commissioner"***; **filing false or misleading applications or reports**; and ***"teaching
course content that is not current or that has substantially changed from the course as approved."***

A school **shall not** *"offer a course of study for credit that is not approved by the department, except
that the school **may advertise a course as pending approval before its approval**"*, or *"promote or
advertise the school using **false or misleading statistics or testimonials** or any other form of
deceptive advertisement."*

### A.A.C. R4-28-404 — what a school and a course must do

**Certificate of School Approval** is required of any school offering a course of study for original or
renewal licensure, except an accredited community college or university.

**Certificate of Course Approval** is required **for each course**, and the application must include:

> *"**4.** A **detailed outline of course material content** that clearly lists the subject matter to be
> covered; **5.** The number of credit hours requested. **The time allocated by a school for examination
> shall not be included in calculating credit hours if the examination is used for overall evaluation.**
> ... **7.** A **definition of segments** if the course is to be offered in part and in its entirety;
> **8.** If any **third party recorded material** will be used as instructional aids, **they will not
> exceed 20 percent of instructional time**."*

**Two build constraints follow directly.** **Examination time does not count towards credit hours** where
the examination is used for overall evaluation — so the 90 hours are 90 hours of instruction. And **third
party recorded material may not exceed 20 percent of instructional time.**

**Records — R4-28-404(D):** the school shall maintain a record **for five years** of each student,
including the **name**, **dates of attendance**, **title of each course**, **course number, category and
credit hours awarded**, and **the original signature roster, if live in person instruction.**

**Enrolment agreement — R4-28-404(E):** the agreement or application to enrol must include, ***"in bold
type and capital letters"***: the **course or course segment title within a curriculum**; the **total
credit hours applicable for licensure or renewal**; the **cost of each course**; a **statement of the
refund policy**; a **statement related to license pre-determination petitions**; and a **statement of any
job placement service.**

**Changes — R4-28-404(H)(3):** a school must **obtain approval from the Commissioner BEFORE** changing a
business name, establishing a school location, ***changing the course content***, ***changing the course
length***, or **offering a new course.**

### A.A.C. R4-28-403 — the state examination

> **(A)** *The Department shall hold, or contract for, **at least one state licensing examination each
> week**.*
> **(B)** *A state licence examination **shall not be returned to the applicant**. The applicant shall be
> **notified in person** of the results by the words **"passed" or "did not pass."** The results
> notification for an applicant who did not pass **shall also show the score for the examination and the
> relative score for each content area.***
> **(C)** *"**Qualifying to take or passing a license examination does not constitute a waiver of the
> Commissioner's right to deny issuance of a license** if grounds exist pursuant to A.R.S. § 32-2153 or
> any other applicable statute."*

## 3. Licensee duties — A.A.C. R4-28-1101

**Arizona is a FIDUCIARY state, and the rule says so in its first sentence.** This is a real difference
from several other states in this project: Oklahoma abrogated common-law fiduciary duties by statute, and
Washington provides that its duties are *"statutory duties and not fiduciary duties."* **Arizona does the
opposite.**

> **(A)** *"**A licensee owes a FIDUCIARY DUTY to the client and shall protect and promote the client's
> interests. The licensee shall also DEAL FAIRLY with all other parties to a transaction.**"*

### (B) — the disclosure duty, owed to ALL other parties, in writing

> *"A licensee participating in a real estate transaction shall **disclose in writing to all other parties
> any information the licensee possesses that materially or adversely affects the consideration to be paid
> by any party** to the transaction, **including**:*
> **1.** *"Any information that the **seller or lessor is or may be unable to perform**;*
> **2.** *"Any information that the **buyer or lessee is, or may be, unable to perform**;*
> **3.** *"Any **known adverse material fact concerning the property or material defect existing in the
> property** being transferred; and*
> **4.** *"The **existence of a lien or encumbrance** on the property being transferred."*

**Note items 1 and 2.** Arizona's disclosure duty expressly reaches **a party's inability to perform** —
on **either** side. That is unusual, and it is written as an example of information *"materially or
adversely affect[ing] the consideration to be paid."*

### (C) and (D) — performance

> **(C)** *"A licensee shall **expeditiously perform all acts required by the holding of a license**. A
> licensee **shall not delay performance, either intentionally or through neglect**. If a licensee is
> unable to perform any act as required by rule or statute, **they must expeditiously notify their
> designated broker of their inability to perform**."*
>
> **(D)** *"A licensee **shall not allow a controversy with another licensee to jeopardize, delay, or
> interfere with the initiation, processing, or finalizing of a transaction on behalf of a client**."* —
> though this does not oblige a licensee to alter compensation terms or relinquish the right to maintain
> an action.

### (E) — conflicts, disclosed in writing BEFORE any binding agreement

> *"A licensee shall not act directly or indirectly in a transaction **without informing the other parties
> in the transaction, in writing and before the parties enter any binding agreement**, of a present or
> prospective interest or conflict in the transaction, **including that the**:*
> **1.** *"**Licensee has a license and is acting as a principal**;*
> **2.** *"**Purchaser or seller is a member of licensee's or designated broker's immediate family**;*
> **3.** *"Purchaser or seller **has ownership or is the employee of the licensee's employing broker**; or*
> **4.** *"**Licensee, or a member of the licensee's immediate family, has a financial interest in the
> transaction** other than the licensee's receipt of compensation for the real estate services."*

### (F) and (G) — compensation

> **(F)** *"A licensee shall **not accept compensation from or represent more than one party to a
> transaction without the PRIOR WRITTEN CONSENT of all parties**."*
>
> **(G)** *"A licensee shall **not accept any compensation, including rebate or other consideration,
> directly or indirectly, for any goods or services provided to a person if the goods or services are
> related to or result from a real estate transaction, without that person's PRIOR WRITTEN ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
> of the compensation**. **This prohibition does not apply to compensation paid to a broker by a broker who
> represents a party in the transaction.**"*

**(G) is broad and it is the one licensees breach without noticing.** Any rebate or consideration for any
goods or services related to a transaction needs the person's **prior written acknowledgement** — with a
single carve-out for **broker-to-broker** compensation.

### (H) and (I) — competence and information

> **(H)** *"The services that a licensee provides to a client shall **conform to the standards of practice
> and competence recognized in the professional community** for the specific real estate discipline ... A
> licensee **shall not undertake to provide professional services concerning a type of property or service
> that is outside the licensee's field of competence without engaging the assistance of a person who is
> competent** to provide those services, **unless the licensee's lack of expertise is first disclosed to the
> client in writing and the client subsequently employs the licensee**."*
>
> **(I)** *"A licensee shall **exercise reasonable care in ensuring that the licensee obtains information
> material to a client's interests and relevant to the contemplated transaction and accurately communicates
> the information to the client**. A licensee shall **take reasonable steps to assist a client in confirming
> the accuracy of information relevant to the transaction**."*

**(I) is an affirmative duty to OBTAIN information**, not merely to pass on what arrives. Compare
Washington, where the statute expressly removes any duty to investigate. **Arizona's rule runs the other
way**, and a build that treated the two states alike would misstate both.

### (J) and (K) — possession

> **(J)** *"A licensee shall not: **1.** Permit or facilitate **access to or occupancy in a person's real
> property without prior written authorization from the owner**; or **2.** **Deliver possession prior to
> closing unless expressly instructed to do so by the owner** of the property or property interest being
> transferred."*
>
> **(K)** *"A licensee shall **recommend to a client that the client seek appropriate counsel from
> insurance, legal, tax, and accounting professionals regarding the risks of pre-possession or
> post-possession of a property**."*

## 4. Other rules read

### A.A.C. R4-28-1102 — Property Negotiations

> **(A)** *"Real estate licensees **may not contact a principal represented by another licensee** unless
> the principal's **Designated Broker, broker representative with delegated authority, and the licensee are
> unavailable for 24 hours**. A principal may **waive or alter this requirement by issuing written
> instructions**."*
>
> **(B)** *"For a buyer and unless the buyer waives this requirement in writing, **negotiation must be
> conducted exclusively through the principal's broker or the broker's representative** unless the
> designated broker, a broker with delegated authority, and the buyer's licensee with an agency
> relationship are **unavailable for 24 hours**."*

### A.A.C. R4-28-1103 — Broker Supervision and Control

> **(A)** *An employing broker and a designated broker shall exercise **reasonable supervision and
> control**, which **includes the establishment and enforcement of WRITTEN policies, procedures, and
> systems** to review and manage **transactions of all licensees**, **use of disclosure forms and
> contracts**, **filing, storing and maintaining documents**, **handling of trust funds**, **use of
> unlicensed assistants**, and **advertising**; to **oversee delegation of authority**; to **familiarize
> licensees with federal, state and local law**; and to **review and inspect** documents that may have a
> material effect on a party's rights or obligations, and **advertising and marketing by licensees in the
> broker's employ.***
>
> **(B)** *A designated broker shall establish a system for monitoring compliance that ***"must include a
> PROGRESSIVE DISCIPLINARY POLICY for managing violations of the employing broker's policies which would
> also represent a violation of any statutory requirement"*** — and **failure to enforce it is itself a
> violation.***
>
> **(D)** *"**An employing broker is responsible for the acts of all licensees and other employees acting**
> ..."*

### A.A.C. R4-28-701 — Compensation Sharing Disclosure

> *"A real estate broker shall **disclose to all the parties in a transaction, IN WRITING AT LEAST THREE
> CALENDAR DAYS BEFORE CLOSING, the name of each employing broker who represents a party to the transaction
> and who will receive compensation from the transaction**."*

### A.A.C. R4-28-802 — Conveyance Documents

> **(A)** *"Upon execution of any transaction document a licensee shall **expeditiously deliver a legible
> copy of the signed document and final agreement to each party signing the document**."*
>
> **(B)** *"During the term of a listing agreement, a licensee shall **promptly submit to the licensee's
> client all offers to purchase or lease** the listed property. **Upon receiving permission from the seller
> or lessor**, the licensee ... **may disclose to all offerors or their agents the existence and terms of
> all additional offers**. **The licensee shall submit to the client all offers made prior to closing and
> IS NOT RELEASED FROM THIS DUTY BY THE CLIENT'S ACCEPTANCE OF AN OFFER unless the client instructs the
> licensee IN WRITING to cease submitting offers** or unless otherwise provided in the listing agreement,
> lease, or purchase contract. The licensee **may voluntarily submit offers ... regardless of any
> limitations contained in the listing agreement and may submit offers after the listing agreement is
> terminated**."*
>
> **(C)** *The broker shall retain **copies of all receipts and disbursements**, **copies of the executed
> and delivered escrow closing statements**, and **any transaction documents signed by parties or any
> disclosures made as part of the transaction** — in addition to A.R.S. §§ 32-2151.01 and 32-2174.*

### A.A.C. R4-28-804 — Rescission of Contract

**Two different periods, and a broker must not confuse them.**

> **(A)** *For **an unimproved subdivided lot, or any unsubdivided land**, the agreement must contain, in
> bold or larger print above the signature portion, substantially: the purchaser or lessee **"HAS THE LEGAL
> RIGHT TO RESCIND (CANCEL) THIS AGREEMENT WITHOUT CAUSE OR REASON OF ANY KIND ... BY MIDNIGHT OF THE
> SEVENTH CALENDAR DAY FOLLOWING THE DAY THE PURCHASER OR LESSEE EXECUTED THE AGREEMENT. IF THE PURCHASER
> OR LESSEE DOES NOT INSPECT THE LOT OR PARCEL BEFORE THE EXECUTION OF THE AGREEMENT, THE PURCHASER OR
> LESSEE SHALL HAVE SIX MONTHS TO INSPECT ... AND AT THE TIME OF INSPECTION SHALL HAVE THE RIGHT TO
> UNILATERALLY RESCIND THE AGREEMENT."***
>
> **(B)** *For a **time-share interval**: rescission **"BY MIDNIGHT OF THE TENTH CALENDAR DAY FOLLOWING THE
> DAY THE PURCHASER OR LESSEE EXECUTED THE AGREEMENT."***
>
> **(C)** *An opportunity to exercise rescission **"shall be provided by conspicuously disclosing the
> complete and current name, address, telephone number, and email address of the seller ON THE FACE of all
> agreements and contracts."*** Notices of rescission **must be in writing** and are **deemed delivered and
> received when hand delivered, sent via email, sent by courier service, or certified mail** — and the
> **seller must issue a receipt if hand delivered.***

### A.A.C. R4-28-803 — Contract Disclosures

Where a **public report** is required, the agreement must carry, in bold or larger print above the
signature portion: ***"THE DEVELOPER SHALL GIVE A PROSPECTIVE PURCHASER A COPY OF THE PUBLIC REPORT AND AN
OPPORTUNITY TO READ AND REVIEW IT BEFORE THE PROSPECTIVE PURCHASER SIGNS THIS DOCUMENT."***

**(C)** *"The contract **shall indicate where the earnest money or down payment, if any, will be
deposited** and shall include the **name of the title company, the name of the broker's trust account, or
other depository**."*

**(D)** Where money is paid **directly to the seller and not placed in a neutral escrow depository**, the
document must **conspicuously disclose** it and **the purchaser shall sign or initial the provision**, and
a bold or large-print warning must appear in the public report, purchase contract and agreement of sale:
***"PROSPECTIVE PURCHASERS ARE ADVISED THAT EARNEST MONEY DEPOSITS, DOWN PAYMENTS, AND OTHER ADVANCED MONEY
WILL NOT BE PLACED IN A NEUTRAL ESCROW. THIS MONEY WILL BE PAID DIRECTLY TO THE SELLER AND MAY BE USED BY
THE SELLER. THIS MEANS THE PURCHASER ASSUMES A RISK OF LOSING THE MONEY IF THE SELLER IS UNABLE OR
UNWILLING TO PERFORM UNDER THE TERMS OF THE PURCHASE CONTRACT."***

### A.A.C. R4-28-502 — Advertising by a Licensee

> **(A)** *A licensee **shall not advertise property in a manner that implies that no salesperson or broker
> is taking part** in the offer.*
> **(B)** *A licensee advertising **their own or another licensee's property** shall **disclose they are
> licensed** and, as the property owner, **place the words "owner/agent" in the advertisement**.*
> **(C)** *All advertising shall contain **accurate claims and representations** and **fully state factual
> material**; a licensee **shall not misrepresent the facts or create misleading or ambiguous
> impressions**.*
> **(D)** *A **school** shall include its **name, school number, phone and email contact information, and
> the name of the school administrator** in all advertising of Department-approved courses.*
> **(E)** *All advertising shall **identify in a clear and prominent manner the employing broker's legal
> name or the dba name(s)** on the employing broker's licence certificate.*
> **(G)** *"**The designated broker is responsible for the advertising of all real estate activity.**"*
> **(H)** *"A licensee shall **not use the term 'acre,' either alone or modified, unless referring to an
> area of land representing at least 43,560 square feet**."*
> **(I)** *Before placing a sign or publishing to an electronic medium, a licensee shall **secure the
> written consent of the property owner**, and the sign or publication **shall be promptly removed upon
> request of the property owner**.*
> **(J)** *"The use of an **electronic medium, such as the Internet, ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE, or web site
> technology**, that **targets residents of this State** with the offering of a property interest or real
> estate brokerage services **constitutes the dissemination of advertising** as defined in A.R.S. §
> 32-2101(2). All advertising using an electronic medium must comply with Subsection E ... and **if
> advertising online, all requirements of advertising set forth above must be satisfied on the
> advertisement WITHOUT THE NEED TO SCROLL**."*

### A.A.C. R4-28-503 — Promotional Activities

A licensee **shall not** describe a premium offered at no cost or reduced cost as an ***"award"*** or
***"prize"*** or a similar term, and shall **clearly disclose in writing the terms, costs, conditions,
restrictions, and expiration date** of a premium offer **before the person participates**. Unless otherwise
provided by law, a person **shall not solicit, sell, or offer to sell an interest in a development by
conducting a lottery contest, drawing, or game of chance.**

## 5. What was NOT read, and is therefore stated NOWHERE in this build

-502, -503, -701, -802, -803, -804, -1101, -1102 and -1103** are transcribed above.

these:**

- **The Department's prescribed prelicensure course content**, which **R4-28-401(D)** says the Department
  will provide on request and which **A.R.S. § 32-2124(B)** requires a course to follow. **Submission
  gate.**
- **Any Department outline for the 27-hour Arizona-specific course.** The statute and the rule both name it
  and **neither states its content.** **Submission gate.**
- **Any Department content outline or candidate handbook for the state licensing examination**, and any
  figure for the examination's **item count, passing score or time limit.**
- **A.R.S. § 32-2151 and § 32-2151.01** — Arizona's **trust account** and **record retention** provisions —
  in their own text, and **§ 32-2174**. They are cited by R4-28-802(C), which was read; **the sections
  themselves were not.**
- **A.R.S. § 32-2153** — grounds for denial, suspension and revocation — in its own text.
- **The remainder of A.R.S. Title 32, Chapter 20**, including the **subdivision and public report**
  provisions, the **time-share** provisions, and **§ 32-2101**'s definitions.
- **A.R.S. Title 33** — Arizona's property, landlord-tenant, condominium, planned community, deed of trust
  and lien statutes — including the **Arizona Residential Landlord and Tenant Act.**
- **Arizona's Residential Seller Disclosure practice and any standard disclosure form**, and **the Arizona
  Association of REALTORS® Residential Resale Real Estate Purchase Contract** or any other standard form.
- **Arizona's groundwater, water adequacy and assured water supply statutes**, and the **Affidavit of
  Disclosure** required for certain unsubdivided land sales.
- **Arizona's fair housing statutes** and the **federal Fair Housing Act.**
- **Arizona's transfer, property tax and income tax statutes**, and any **rate.**
- Federal law in its own text: **TILA/Regulation Z, RESPA/Regulation X, TRID, ECOA**, and the **federal
  lead-based paint regulations.**

provision, no water adequacy rule, no transfer or property tax rate, no licensing examination score, and no
penalty amount.** Where one is needed, the curriculum names the source and routes there.
