# Arizona Official Source Register

Last verified: **2026-08-16**. Official regulator, legislature/rule publisher, and current regulator forms control. Missing or ambiguous requirements remain filing questions; another state's rule is never substituted.

1. [ADRE Salesperson License](https://azre.gov/about/services/salesperson-license) — 90-hour prelicense certificate and separate 6-hour Contract Writing certificate.
2. [ADRE Education Forms and Resources](https://azre.gov/divisions/education/education-forms-resources) — Current school, instructor, prelicense-course, and Contract Writing applications.
3. [ADRE School Approval Application (09/2025)](https://azre.gov/sites/default/files/2025-09/School%20Approval%20Application%20Form.pdf) — School type, owner, administrator, instructor, entity, and distance-school exhibits.
4. [ADRE Instructor Application (12/2025)](https://azre.gov/sites/default/files/2025-12/Instructor%20Application%20Form.pdf) — Fingerprint, workshop, subject qualification, résumé, and disclosure requirements.
5. [ADRE Prelicense Course Application (09/2025)](https://azre.gov/sites/default/files/2025-09/Course%20Application%20Form%20-%20Pre-Licensing.pdf) — Outline, enrollment agreement, distance-review access, IP, identity, support, and clock-hour attestations.
6. [ADRE Contract Writing Course Application (01/2026)](https://azre.gov/sites/default/files/2026-01/Course%20Application%20Form%20-%20Continuing%20Education%20or%206-Hour%20Contract%20Writing.pdf) — Separate six-hour Contract Writing course record and delivery application.
7. [ADRE SPS 2025.01](https://azre.gov/sites/default/files/2025-07/S.P.S.%202025.01.pdf) — Distance identity, inactivity, timing, interactivity, quizzes, finals, proctoring, and certificate controls.
8. [A.R.S. § 32-2124](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/32/02124.htm) — Salesperson education, Arizona-specific instruction, course examination, and certificate rules.
9. [ADRE Fees](https://azre.gov/all-adre-resources/fees) — Current public fee schedule; education-application fees are not listed and must be confirmed at filing.

## Release control

All sources are checked again on filing day, on regulator correction, before each controlled release, and before enrollment after a legal/form change. A snapshot, checksum, effective date, reviewer, and impact decision belong in the regulatory-document record.
