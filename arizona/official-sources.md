## Interpretation controls

- The ADRE state-specific outline does not itself assign StateCourse's hour-by-hour delivery allocation. The 27-hour allocation in the StateCourse outline is a **provider proposal** mapped to every ADRE section and must be accepted as part of course approval.
- The six-hour Contract Writing requirement is a separate course/certificate under A.R.S. § 32-2124(L). It is never counted inside the 90-hour course record.
- SPS 2025.01 describes ADRE's current distance-learning approach and expressly identifies itself as advisory. StateCourse treats its controls as the submission baseline while separately preserving the underlying statute/rule citations.
- No school, instructor, course, delivery method, certificate, or enrollment is represented as approved until ADRE issues the corresponding approval and an administrator records it.

## Filing-day evidence

The compliance administrator stores a dated PDF snapshot, URL, checksum, form revision, effective date, reviewer, and impact decision for every source above. Any unresolved change places the affected course version in regulatory hold.
