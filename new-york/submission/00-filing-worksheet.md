## First resolve the course-provider independence question

This is a potential eligibility conflict, not an adjudication that DOS has rejected StateCourse or a conclusion that the California license automatically resolves the question. Selecting a different instructor does not establish organizational independence. Do not substitute an undisclosed nominee or omit actual control facts. ## Course and distance-learning attachments

The retained application is DOS-1503-f-a, revision 12/21, currently supplied by DOS; a current link does not remove the need to check the operative rule and form at filing. Page 1 requires applications 60 days before the proposed course and exam forms/keys with question-by-question source page and curriculum location. Sections 176.2 and 176.24 supply the course/distance application requirements. Do not schedule a launch by treating submission as approval.

Build the complete 19-subject manuscript and major-unit/module outline; the public practice collection cannot replace it. Attach the final examination and answer key, actual source/reference pages, subject categories, grading standard, distributed materials and books. Freeze real pagination before completing page-linked keys.

Under section 176.23, the distance design needs major units and modules, an inactivity mechanism, each student's signed and dated personal-completion statement, three-year participation/completion records available to DOS, an approved instructor available in reasonable business hours, and a proctored final at a DOS-approved New York State location. Section 176.24 also calls for ongoing participation verification, student hardware/software and technical-support descriptions. Section 176.25 requires completion, including the school final, within 12 months of starting.

The application additionally asks for an outside organization's design/delivery certification letter and a distance-learning affidavit. Collect actual accepted evidence; do not print a self-created certification letter as though an outside evaluator issued it. Page 2 explicitly requires the distance final-exam location to be in New York State. A home address in another state or a generic remote-proctor plan does not satisfy that printed field.

Fees printed in the current form and rule must be checked for the filing date; no amount is paid, stored as a purchase instruction or used to bypass the no-spending boundary. Approval identifiers, official seal and approved-course completion language belong only in authorized completion artifacts after actual approval and student completion.
