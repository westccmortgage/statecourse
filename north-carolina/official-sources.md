# North Carolina Official Source Register

Last verified: **2026-08-16**. Official regulator, legislature/rule publisher, and current regulator forms control. Missing or ambiguous requirements remain filing questions; another state's rule is never substituted.

1. [21 NCAC 58H — current compiled rules](https://reports.oah.state.nc.us/ncac/title%2021%20-%20occupational%20licensing%20boards%20and%20commissions/chapter%2058%20-%20real%20estate%20commission/chapter%2058%20rules.html) — Provider, instructor, course, asynchronous delivery, completion, examination, reporting, records, fees, and renewals.
2. [N.C.G.S. Chapter 93A, Article 3](https://www.ncleg.gov/enactedlegislation/statutes/html/bychapter/chapter_93a.html) — Education Provider certification and statutory authority.
3. [NCREC 2026 Rule Changes](https://bulletins.ncrec.gov/rule-changes/) — July 1, 2026 synchronous-camera attendance amendment.
4. [NCREC Prelicensing Rosters](https://bulletins.ncrec.gov/lets-talk-prelicensing-rosters/) — Seven-day roster fields and reporting responsibility.
5. [NCREC Policies and Procedures Disclosure](https://bulletins.ncrec.gov/did-you-know-education-providers-must-publish-a-policies-and-procedures-disclosure-ppd/) — Required provider/student disclosure contents and signed receipt.
6. [NCREC Broker Prelicensing Course Syllabus](https://www.ncrec.gov/Education/Prelicensing) — Current prescribed 75-hour Broker Prelicensing syllabus and provider resources; controlled snapshot required at filing.

## Release control

All sources are checked again on filing day, on regulator correction, before each controlled release, and before enrollment after a legal/form change. A snapshot, checksum, effective date, reviewer, and impact decision belong in the regulatory-document record.
