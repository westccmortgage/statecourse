## Verification conclusions

- North Carolina has one Broker Prelicensing course of at least 75 hours.
- Prelicensing may be delivered in person, synchronously, self-paced by distance, or blended only after NCREC approves the specific delivery method.
- For self-paced distance, the Commission reviews the full course and a time outline measured at 225 words per minute plus actual audio/video duration; the course must be reviewable in its entirety.
- A distance student completes every unit and assessment, passes a comprehensive closed-book proctored final at 75 percent or higher, and completes the course within 180 days of enrollment.
- The current public rules and student syllabus reviewed here do not fix a universal production-final item count, bank ratio, retake count, or vendor. Those remain written-confirmation/application gates.

All sources must be checked again on filing day, on regulator correction, before each controlled release, and before enrollment after a law, rule, syllabus, or form change. The regulatory-document record stores a snapshot, checksum, effective date, reviewer, and impact decision.
