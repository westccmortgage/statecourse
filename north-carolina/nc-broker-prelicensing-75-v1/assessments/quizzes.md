# North Carolina Broker Prelicensing — Formative Assessment Blueprint

**Development draft — item writing and North Carolina SME review incomplete.**

Each of the 72 instructional units will contain at least one scored mastery check. Major modules also require a cumulative quiz or applied assignment. Formative assessments do not replace the comprehensive proctored end-of-course examination.

## Required assessment evidence

For every scored item, store:

- course and content version;
- NCREC syllabus section, topic, and instructional level;
- learning objective and cognitive demand;
- item stem, answer options, keyed answer, rationale, and citations;
- first response, score, timestamp, attempt number, and time spent;
- remediation trigger, alternate explanation and alternate item; and
- instructor-support flag when repeated difficulty meets provider policy.

## Remediation rule

An incorrect required check opens an alternate explanation and new example, then an alternate question. The unit remains incomplete until the alternate question is answered correctly or the approved provider policy routes the student to instructor intervention. All attempts remain in the audit record.
