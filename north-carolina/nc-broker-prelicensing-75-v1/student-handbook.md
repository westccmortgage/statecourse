# North Carolina Broker Prelicensing 75h — Student Handbook and Required Disclosures Draft

## Public and enrollment status

This v1.0 course is **in development and not approved**. Enrollment, payment, regulated study time, credit, final eligibility, and certificates are disabled. Final disclosures, refund/cancellation terms, price, instructor, approval number, effective dates, complaint contacts, and access period require approved versions before checkout.

## How an approved online course will work

The student completes the approved identity step, receives and signs the current Policies and Procedures Disclosure before payment, and accesses only the purchased course after verified payment. Every self-paced unit and assessment must be completed within 180 days of enrollment. StateCourse's proposed active-engagement controls exclude inactivity, hidden-tab time, disconnection and concurrent sessions; NCREC approval of the submitted delivery method remains required. The final is comprehensive, closed-book, proctored, identity-secured, and requires at least 75 percent.

## Support, integrity, and records

Students receive instructor, technical, accessibility, complaint, cancellation, and refund contacts in the approved PPD. Sharing credentials, using unauthorized assistance, or defeating identity/exam controls is prohibited and documented. A reviewer bypass never creates student credit. The education director reports a qualifying completion within seven calendar days and StateCourse retains prescribed provider records for three years.
