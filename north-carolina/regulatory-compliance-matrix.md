## Release blockers

1. certified Education Provider and named education director;
2. NCREC-approved instructor assignment;
3. complete 72-hour student manuscripts and authorized North Carolina form resources;
4. full formative item/remediation set;
5. unique secure comprehensive final and approved alternate/retake method;
6. accepted self-paced distance course, time study, identity/attendance, reviewer and proctor procedure;
7. final PPD/refund/technology/support policies; and
8. regulator-issued provider/course/instructor numbers and effective/expiration dates.
