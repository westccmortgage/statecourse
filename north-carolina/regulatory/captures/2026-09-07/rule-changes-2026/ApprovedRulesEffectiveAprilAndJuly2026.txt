RULEMAKING NOTICE
The North Carolina Real Estate Commission and Rules Review Commission have approved rule
changes that will go into effect as noted in the history note of the rule text.
21 NCAC 58A .0302, .0503, and .1803 rule changes and the adoption of a new rule,
21 NCAC 58A .0513 go into effect on April 1, 2026.
21 NCAC 58G .0103 and 58H .0101 and .0210 rule changes go into effect on July 1, 2026.
21 NCAC 58A .0112 rule changes are subject to legislative review and, if approved,
will become effective upon legislative approval pursuant to G.S. 150B-21.3(b1). As noted in the
Commission’s August 2025 Bulletin, the current rule provisions subject to change will not be
enforced.
Changes to the rules are reflected by underscoring text added and striking through deleted text.
Any change occurring after the initial publication of the proposed rule text is highlighted in
yellow. A redline copy of the rule text can be found on the Commission’s homepage under
‘License Law & Rule Changes.’

21 NCAC 58A .0513 LICENSE CANCELLATION 3
(a)  A broker seeking to voluntarily cancel their real estate license shall file a license cancellation notice by logging 4
into the broker’s online portal located on the Commission’s website. 5
(b) The license cancellation shall be granted if the broker’s license does not have a pending complaint or disciplinary 6
action with the Commission. If the license cancellation is granted, the broker’s license shall be permanently cancelled 7
effective as of the date the notice was filed pursuant to Paragraph (a) of this Rule. 8
(c)  A license cancelled pursuant to this Rule shall be ineligible for reinstatement. 9

21 NCAC 58G .0103 DEFINITIONS 3
The following definitions apply throughout this Chapter and to all forms prescribed pursuant to this Chapter: 4
(1) "Branch Office" means any office in addition to the principal office of a broker that is operated in 5
connection with the broker's real estate business. 6
(2) "BIC" means a broker-in-charge pursuant to G.S. 93A-2(a1). 7
(3) "BIC Eligible" means a broker's license status who has satisfied the broker- in-charge qualification 8
requirements and filed application pursuant to G.S. 93A-4.2 and 21 NCAC 58A .0110. 9
(4) "Commission" means the North Carolina Real Estate Commission. 10
(5) "Commission's website" means www.ncrec.gov. 11
(6) "Day" means calendar day unless the rule expressly states otherwise. The first day counted is the 12
day following the act, event, or transaction that triggered the tolling of the designated time period. 13
(7) "Fee" means a payment made to the Commission by a bank check, certified check, money order, 14
debit card, credit card, or other electronic means and is nonrefundable once the payment has been 15
processed. 16
(8) "Firm" means a partnership, corporation, limited liability company, association, or other business 17
entity, except for a sole proprietorship. 18
(9) "Form" means an original form template provided by the Commission and completed by the 19
submitting party. 20
(10) "Office" means any place of business where acts are performed for which a real estate license is 21
required or where monies received by a broker acting in a fiduciary capacity are handled or records 22
for such trust monies are maintained. 23
(11) "Principal Office" means the office so physical address designated in the Commission's records by 24
the qualifying broker of a licensed firm or the broker-in-charge of a sole proprietorship. 25

21 NCAC 58H .0101 DEFINITIONS 7
The following definitions apply throughout this Subchapter and to all forms prescribed pursuant to this Chapter: 8
(1) "Assessment" means a quiz or evaluation that tests a student's mastery of the learning objective. 9
(2) "Blended learning" means any combination of distance education, synchronous distance learning, 10
and in-person methods of instruction. 11
(3) "Branch location" means any location in addition to the principal address of an education provider 12
that offers Prelicensing or Postlicensing Courses. 13
(4) "Continuing education" means a continuing education elective or Update Course. 14
(5) "Distance education" means a method of instruction accomplished through the use of media 15
whereby teacher and student are separated by distance and time. 16
(6) "End-of-course examination" means an examination administered at the conclusion of a course that 17
tests students' knowledge and mastery of all course subjects mandated by the Commission 18
prescribed course syllabus. 19
(7) "Instructional hour" means 50 minutes of instruction and 10 minutes of break time. 20
(8) "Instructor development program" means courses of instruction designed to assist real estate 21
instructors in the performance of Prelicensing, Postlicensing, or continuing education instructor 22
duties or in the development of teaching skills. 23
(9) "Learning objective" means a statement of what a student will be able to do after completing a unit 24
or course. A learning objective shall be structured in accordance with Bloom's Taxonomy. 25
(10) "License Examination Performance Record" means the percentage of an instructor's or education 26
provider's students who, within 30 days of completing a Prelicensing course pursuant to 21 NCAC 27
58H .0207(a), take and pass the license examination, as defined in 21 NCAC 58A .0402, on their 28
first attempt. 29
(11) "Postlicensing course" means any one of the courses comprising the 90 hour Postlicensing education 30
program pursuant to G.S. 93A-4(a1) and 21 NCAC 58A .1902. 31
(12) "Prelicensing course" means a single course consisting of at least 75 hours of instruction on subjects 32
prescribed by the Commission pursuant to G.S. 93A-4(a). 33
(13) "Public education provider" means any proprietary business or trade school licensed by the State 34
Board of Community Colleges under G.S. 115D -90 or approved by the Board of Governors of the 35
University of North Carolina that conducts approved real estate courses. 36
