This is a sample of a completed Loan Estimate for a fixed rate loan. This loan is for the purchase of property at a sale price
of $180,000 and has a loan amount of $162,000, a 30-year loan term, a fixed interest rate of 3.875 percent, and a
prepayment penalty equal to 2.00 percent of the outstanding principal balance of the loan for the first two years after
consummation of the transaction. The consumer has elected to lock the interest rate. The creditor requires an escrow
account and that the consumer pay for private mortgage insurance.
