## The structural fact: North Carolina prescribes the course

This is the opposite of Wisconsin and Oklahoma, and it changes what a StateCourse build may claim.

The Syllabus states: **"The Prelicensing Course must be taught as prescribed by the Commission in this
Syllabus."** It then prescribes, in the Commission's own words:

- **sixteen numbered sections**, each with an assigned **Instructional Level** and an assigned number
  of **licence examination items**;
- a **recommended hour allocation** across twelve major subject areas, totalling 75;
- **Instructional Levels** that education providers and instructors are **"REQUIRED to comply with"**;
- **"Required Activities"** that **"must be completed as part of the course"**, distinguished from
  "Suggested Activities" which are not required;
- **Instructor Notes** that education providers and instructors are **"REQUIRED to comply with"**;
- the **end-of-course examination standard**; and
- named **resources** providers must supply or review.

**So for North Carolina the topic list, the depth of coverage, the required activities, the hour
allocation and the passing standard are ALL the Commission's.** Almost nothing structural is left to
this school, and a StateCourse document that described any of it as a "StateCourse proposal" would be
misdescribing the position. What remains this school's is the writing itself, the in-module engagement
items, and the order of presentation — which the Syllabus expressly leaves open.

## Course hours and delivery

> **Course Hours and Delivery Method:** The Prelicensing Course must consist of a **minimum of 75
> instructional hours**, although approved Education Providers may, at their option, schedule the
> course for more than 75 hours. The course may be offered via **in-person, synchronous distance,
> self-paced distance, or blended** delivery. **Pre-approval is required for each type of course
> delivery.**

> **Requirements for Offering the Course:** This course may only be offered by **Commission-certified
> Education Providers (EPs)** that have received course approval for each specific course delivery
> method. EPs may only use **instructors approved by the North Carolina Real Estate Commission**.

StateCourse's delivery would be **self-paced distance**, which requires its own pre-approval.

| Real Estate Financing | 7.0 |
| Real Property Valuation | 6.0 |
| Fair Housing/Anti-discrimination | 1.5 |
| Landlord/Tenant & Property Management | 3.5 |
| **End-of-course Examination** | **3.0** |
| **TOTAL INSTRUCTIONAL HOURS** | **75** |

**Two things to read carefully here.**

**First, the twelve instructional subject areas total 72.0 hours, and the end-of-course examination's
3.0 hours are counted INSIDE the 75.** North Carolina therefore differs from Maine, where 3,000
instructional minutes sit alongside 300 assessment minutes, and from Wisconsin and Oklahoma, where the
hour figure is instruction and the examination sits outside it. **A build that delivered 75 hours of
instruction plus a separate examination would be delivering 78, not 75.**

**Second, the table is headed "RECOMMENDED".** The Syllabus's own note explains what is not timed:

> **Note:** There is no separate timing for coverage of **Real Estate License Law and Commission Rules,
> Agent Safety, and Real Estate Mathematics** because all of them should be **appropriately scattered
> throughout the course.**

So the allocation is the Commission's recommendation and the 75-hour minimum is a requirement. This
build follows the recommended allocation exactly and says so, rather than proposing its own.

## The three Instructional Levels — transcribed

> **Education providers and instructors are REQUIRED to comply with the assigned Instructional Levels
> when teaching this course.**

Each is defined by a **Competency** — what students should be able to do — and an **Instruction** —
the prescribed depth of coverage and instructional methods. They are based on Bloom's Taxonomy.

**Level 1 — Recall.** *Competency:* students should be able to recall facts and basic concepts and to
explain ideas or concepts; learning objectives may include **define, duplicate, classify, explain,
describe**. *Instruction:* the instructor should review and discuss basic definitions, facts, concepts
and procedures. **In-depth instruction is not required.**

**Level 2 — Application.** *Competency:* students should be able to **use information in new
situations and draw connections among ideas**; objectives may include **implement, solve, demonstrate,
interpret, differentiate, relate, compare, contrast**. *Instruction:* review and discuss the topic **in
moderate depth** sufficient to illustrate and enhance understanding of facts, principles and procedures
**and their relevance to brokerage practice**.

**Level 3 — Analysis.** *Competency:* students should be able to **justify a stand or decision and
produce new or original work**; objectives may include **defend, judge, critique, weigh, design,
assemble, develop, formulate**. *Instruction:* review and discuss the topic **in substantial depth**,
using examples, **and requiring students to complete practical work assignments** demonstrating both
understanding and the ability to apply knowledge **to common fact situations that will be encountered
in real estate practice**.

Each major topic carries an assigned Level, and a subtopic needing significantly greater or lesser
emphasis carries its own Level applying to that subtopic only.

**The Levels are the reason a North Carolina build cannot simply write to a uniform density.** A Level
1 topic is over-taught by treating it in substantial depth; a Level 3 topic is under-taught by
defining terms. **This build carries the assigned Level on every module and writes to it.**

## End-of-course examination and course completion standards — transcribed

> For successful completion of the course, students must
> 1) **satisfactorily complete any required activities and homework exercises**,
> 2) **meet attendance requirements**, and
> 3) **pass the end-of-course examination.**

> EPs are required to utilize end-of-course examinations in accordance with **Commission Rule 58H
> .0207**. End-of-course exams must be **comprehensive, closed-book, and proctored. The minimum passing
> grade is 75%.** EPs, at their option, **may require a higher passing grade** and/or other
> assessments. **EPs and instructors shall safeguard the integrity and confidentiality of examinations
> at all times.**

**Three separate completion conditions, and the first of them is not an examination at all.** The
Required Activities are a completion condition in their own right, which means a build must track them
as completion state rather than treating them as optional enrichment.

**And the examination must be proctored.** That is a delivery requirement a self-paced distance
provider must actually satisfy.

Regarding homework: **"instructional time should only be used for the introduction, and review upon
completion, of the assignment(s)."** So homework does not consume the 75 hours.

## Required Activities and Instructor Notes — transcribed

> **EPs and instructors are REQUIRED to comply with the Instructor Notes and conduct "Required
> Activities" when teaching this course. "Suggested Activities" are offered to spur the educator's
> creative ideas and are not required to be implemented.**

And from the Syllabus's own legend:

> **Notes** contain **testable points** and important information about testing parameters.
> *Italicized terms* are basic terms for which **students should know the definitions**.
> **Required Activities must be completed** as part of the course; additional activities may be
> required by the Education Provider for successful completion of the course.
> **Math calculations should be addressed throughout the course** as part of each appropriate topic.
> Law & rule references are included where relevant with hyperlinks. **Students will be tested on
> concepts, not on law or rule numbers.**

That last sentence is worth keeping in view when writing assessment items.

## Course materials the Commission requires

**Commission Rule 58H .0205(a)**, quoted in the Syllabus:

> "All courses shall have course materials that **cover current North Carolina real estate related
> laws, rules, and practices**. The nature and depth of subject matter coverage shall be **consistent
> with the competency and instructional levels prescribed by the syllabus** for the course for which
> approval is sought."

Education Providers must provide and/or review the resources referenced throughout the Syllabus,
including but not limited to:

1. **North Carolina Real Estate License Law and Commission Rules**, including the Comments on the Law
   and Rules — "LLCR" in the Syllabus.
2. **Residential Square Footage Guidelines.**
3. **North Carolina Real Estate Agent Safety Guide** brochure.
4. **NC REALTORS® Offer to Purchase and Contract — Standard Form 2-T**, available to approved
   instructors and education directors via ShareFile.
5. **Real Estate Licensing in North Carolina** — "RELINC".
6. Various course topics and sections from Update Courses.
7. Various disclosure forms and publications.

The Syllabus also names the **North Carolina Real Estate Manual** ("Manual") as a major resource.

The standalone LLCR publication, Manual and form/publication resources in this list remain open
course-material dependencies. The statutes and current rule text were inspected separately during
Phase 0; they are not a substitute for the required student publications or licensed forms.

## Instructor conduct — Commission Rule 58H .0304, as quoted in the Syllabus

Instructors shall ensure classes run **at the scheduled time and for the full amount of time scheduled
or required**, and shall conduct courses in accordance with the rules and any applicable syllabus,
instructor guide or course plan. Eight demonstrable abilities are listed, including stating student
learning objectives at the beginning of the course, using a variety of instructional techniques both
teacher-centred and student-centred, using assessment tools including **quizzes and midterm
examinations for Prelicensing and Postlicensing courses**, **avoiding criticism of any other person,
agency, or organization**, identifying key concepts and correcting student misconceptions, and
maintaining control of the class.

And:

> **Instructors shall not obtain, use, or attempt to obtain or use, in any manner or form, North
> Carolina real estate license examination questions.**

**That prohibition governs how this project's assessment items may be written.** Every item in this
build is authored from the Syllabus's own stated topics and from general pre-license knowledge, and no
item is derived from, modelled on, or checked against any actual licence examination question.

## Real estate mathematics

> Instruction on relevant mathematical functions is to be provided **under the appropriate subject
> areas rather than as a separate arithmetic course section.** To assist instructors, however, **all
> required real estate mathematics instruction is listed in Section XVI** of the Outline.
> **Consideration was given to the amount of mathematics involved in each subject area when
> establishing the recommended number of instructional hours** for subject areas.

> Instruction in basic mathematics concepts such as decimals, fractions, percentages, multiplication,
> division, etc. **is not included in this course syllabus.**

Section XVI is a **reference list**, not a teaching block, and it carries **7 National examination
items**. This build integrates the mathematics and does not allocate separate hours to Section XVI —
which is what the Syllabus directs and what its hour table already assumes.

## Presentation order

> Educators are **NOT required to teach topics in the order of this syllabus.** … Educators are
> expected to deliver the content in an order that is **logical and conducive to retention**.

So the module sequence is this school's, while the content and depth are the Commission's.

## The three-year clock, and the criminal record report

From Section I, quoted from the Syllabus's own note:

> **The Prelicensing Course is valid for licensing purposes for 3 years from the course completion
> date on the certificate received after passing the end-of-course exam.**

Note the measuring date: **the course completion date on the certificate**, which issues **after
passing the end-of-course examination** — not the date the last module was finished.

Also from Section I:

- Students are encouraged to **wait to order their criminal record report** until at least halfway
  through the course, **because the report is only valid for 6 months**; reports covering multiple
  names, or residency in multiple states or countries, take longer.
- **An individual with a criminal history record may petition the Commission for a
  predetermination** of whether that history will likely disqualify them from obtaining a licence.
- A student **can begin the online licence application but cannot complete it** until the Education
  Provider reports their passing of the Prelicensing Course to the Commission.
- The application fee stated in the Syllabus is **$105**.

**Fees change. This build states the $105 figure only as the figure appearing in the March 2024
Syllabus as amended May 2026, and routes the current fee to the Commission.**

## Agency disclosure — Section VIII, read because it is the heaviest state topic

Section VIII carries **16 state examination items** and is **Level 3**. Read from the Syllabus:

**The Commission's mandatory Working with Real Estate Agents (WWREA) Disclosure** — Commission Rule
58A .0104(c):

- **Applies to all residential and commercial sales transactions; NOT required for property management
  or lease transactions.**
- Provided at **First Substantial Contact**: **in person**, provide and review **immediately**; **by
  phone or electronic means**, at the earliest opportunity after first substantial contact and **no
  later than 3 days after**, transmitting a copy to the consumer.
- **Review the agency options at the earliest practicable opportunity after receipt**, and determine
  whether the broker will act as agent of the buyer or the seller.
- **Mandatory review of the disclosure form.** The Syllabus's own note: **"Inclusion of the WWREA
  Disclosure form in a broker's email signature or sending by an electronic signature program alone
  does not comply with the review component of disclosure requirements."**
- **Completion and retention of the disclosure form for 3 years — even if agency is not reached** —
  Commission Rule 58A .0108.
- The Commission's **Questions & Answers on: Working with Real Estate Agents** is a companion piece
  brokers should strongly consider giving consumers.

The Syllabus states the **primary purposes** of agency disclosure as ensuring the consumer understands
**as soon as possible that there is no confidentiality between the broker and the consumer until an
agency relationship is established**, and introducing the consumer to the available agency options.

A **Required Activity** attaches: *"Provide examples of when first substantial contact occurs in
various situations, including, but not limited to, contact in-person, by phone, by electronic means
such as email or social media, and at open houses."*

Statutory and rule references named: **NCGS 93A**; **NCGS 93A-6(a)(4) and (6)**; **Commission Rules 58A
.0104(c)–(i), (m), (o) and (p)**; **58A .0104(e)** for disclosure of agency status by a seller agent or
subagent to a prospective buyer; **58A .0108** for retention.

## Source boundary — current disposition

### Original curriculum gaps — current Phase 0 disposition

Named explicitly, because an unnamed gap becomes an invented fact. **The North Carolina gap list is
longer than any other state's in this project, because the Commission's syllabus is a map to source
documents rather than a substitute for them.**

- **21 NCAC Chapter 58, Subchapters A and H:** Phase 0 inspected the current official indexed OAH
  text for the controlling applicant, provider, instructor, course, distance, reporting, records and
  renewal rules. Modules NC-01 and NC-02 were reconciled on 2026-09-07 to their applicable applicant,
  other-jurisdiction, supervision, BIC, and compensation rules. The remaining modules still need
  topic-level reconciliation, and the official PDF bytes still need a filing-date integrity lock.
- **NCGS Chapter 93A:** Phase 0 inspected the current official General Assembly text for §§ 93A-4 and
  Article 3. Module NC-01 was reconciled to § 93A-4 on 2026-09-07; the other topic-level curriculum
  reconciliations remain open.
- **North Carolina Real Estate License Law and Commission Rules (LLCR)**, including the Comments.
- **Real Estate Licensing in North Carolina (RELINC):** the April 2026 official PDF was captured,
  text-extracted, and used to reconcile Module NC-01 on 2026-09-07. Its live identity and fees must
  still be rechecked before filing and enrollment.
- **The North Carolina Real Estate Manual.**
- **The Working with Real Estate Agents (WWREA) Disclosure form itself**, and the **Questions & Answers
  on: Working with Real Estate Agents.** Their existence, their trigger and their retention period ARE
  stated because the Syllabus states them; **no field, wording or layout of either is described.**
- **NC REALTORS® Offer to Purchase and Contract — Standard Form 2-T**, and every other standard form.
  The Syllabus devotes Section XI to the Offer to Purchase and Contract. **This project has not read
  Form 2-T and no document in this build reproduces, describes or paraphrases any provision of it.**
  This is a submission gate: a course covering Section XI must be reconciled against the current form.
- **Residential Square Footage Guidelines**, cited in Section XV. No measurement standard from it is
  stated.
- **The Real Estate Agent Safety Guide.**
- **The Residential Property and Owners' Association Disclosure Statement (RPOADS)**, the **Tenant
  Security Deposit Act**, the **Vacation Rental Act**, the **Connor Act**, the **Marketable Title Act**,
  the **Timeshare Act**, the **Unfair and Deceptive Trade Practices Act**, and North Carolina's
  **statute of frauds**, **onsite septic**, **street disclosure**, **subdivision** and **lead-based
  paint** provisions — all named in Sections XIV and XV. **Every one is named by the Syllabus and none
  was read.** No requirement, deadline, form, exemption or figure from any of them appears.
- **The full text of Sections II through VII and IX through XVI of the Syllabus** was extracted and is
  available, but this readings file transcribes in full only the front matter, the hour table, the
  section and item table, the Instructional Levels, the completion standards and Section VIII. The
  remaining sections are worked from directly during curriculum authoring, and the curriculum's own
  instructor source notes record what each module used.
