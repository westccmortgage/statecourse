# North Carolina product roadmap

## Portfolio sequencing decision

As directed on 2026-09-07, StateCourse will add no further states after North Carolina. The next
portfolio workstream is video production and integration for every state already built, following
the established California lesson-video pattern. North Carolina's videos must be tracked by exact
duration and content hash so they can contribute to the Rule 58H .0401 timing ledger only after the
student-visible media actually exists and is reviewed.

## Market signal

NCREC's August 19, 2026 detailed count supports a large addressable profession: 77,507 active full
brokers plus 4,080 active provisional brokers = **81,587 active individuals**. That is not an applicant
or enrollment count, so no revenue forecast should use it as traffic.

## Build order

1. **75-hour Broker Prelicensing, self-paced distance.** Largest new-entrant product, but the most
   expensive content/proctoring submission; current build is far below measured hours.
2. **Three 30-hour Postlicensing courses:** 301 Broker Relationships & Responsibilities; 302 Contracts
   and Closing; 303 NC Law, Rules, and Legal Concepts. Provisional brokers generally need all 90
   hours within 18 months. Each course has its own approval/material/current-manual obligations and
   $10 completing-licensee remittance.
3. **Four-hour CE electives, self-paced distance.** Annual repeat market and shorter product; finish
   within 30 days, observe the June 11-30 blackout, scheduling/reporting rules and $10 completion fee.
4. **Update course only as a live operation.** GENUP/BICUP are Commission-created and limited to
   in-person or synchronous distance; visible-camera and annual materials/instructor-seminar rules
   make this a different operating model, not a copy of the self-paced engine.

The 12-hour BIC course is a Commission course and is not listed as a StateCourse-owned product.

## Go/no-go economics

Before expansion, obtain the live original course-review fee, criminal-report/Pearson/proctor/vendor
costs, content-rights cost, support staffing, registered-agent/foreign-LLC costs, payment/refund loss,
and annual renewal/remittance costs. Build revenue scenarios from verified NCREC completions or
applicant volumes, not the total licensee population.
