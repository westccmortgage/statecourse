# Recordkeeping and regulator reporting SOP

**Owner:** education director  
**Retention floor:** three years unless another applicable rule/law/hold requires longer.

## Completion report — within seven calendar days

For every completing student, validate and submit: legal name; email and telephone; unique ID for
prelicense; broker license number if applicable; completion date; provider name/number; course number;
instructor name/number. Reconcile the portal receipt to the LMS/certificate record. Exceptions remain
open until corrected; a certificate does not shift reporting responsibility to the student.

## Monthly pre/post summary — by the fifth day

For the previous month, report instructors, courses, tuition-paying students not refunded, students
meeting all requirements, and students meeting attendance/unit requirements but not the exam grade.
A zero-activity month is handled according to the live portal's current instruction. Preserve the
submission/receipt and source report.

## Retained record classes

1. enrollment and attendance/progress;
2. each student's end-of-course exam, grade and graded answer sheet;
3. master final exam/key with course title, dates and instructor;
4. required instructor evaluations;
5. advertisements;
6. ARELLO/IDECC certification, if any;
7. bulletins, catalogues, PPD and official publications;
8. course schedules;
9. student course materials and versions;
10. signed PPD/material certifications;
11. written endorsement consent; and
12. operational evidence needed to reproduce identity, proctoring, support, incident, refund, payment,
    completion and report decisions.

## Integrity controls

Records are timestamped, access-controlled, encrypted, backed up, recoverable, searchable, exportable,
and subject to a litigation/regulator hold. Corrections append author/reason/time; they do not erase
the original. Quarterly samples reconcile LMS completion, certificate, regulator report and fee.
Provider representatives give authorized NCREC staff access during an investigation or application
process.

## Annual calendar

- June 1: statutory target for provider renewal application under G.S. 93A-35(b).
- June 10: end of annual CE completion season.
- June 11-30: no CE offering, conducting, or completion.
- June 30: provider and course approvals expire unless renewed.
- within ten days: instructor employment notice; education-director change; cheating report; ≥50%
  ownership transfer where the applicable rule requires it.
- within 30 days: initial education-director video.
- within 45 days before provider expiry: annual education-director video.
