## Outcome

The previous Module NC-15 was not reliable enough to teach or submit. It contained an obsolete UCC
fixture citation, treated required disclosure and measurement artifacts as unavailable, and repeated
the false proposition that square footage was not a material fact. It also routed substantial
prescribed content to future research instead of delivering the instruction.

The corrected module now teaches and tests the current authority boundaries for marketable title,
Torrens registration, planned communities, fixtures, owner disclosures, State Fair Housing,
timeshares, unfair or deceptive practices, residential measurement, subdivision streets, building
codes and commercial broker liens. The RPOADS/MOG and Residential Square Footage Guidelines
activities are delivered from retained current NCREC materials. The exact prescribed measurement
exercise resolves to **2,292 square feet** and shows the arithmetic.

This is a content correction, not an approval claim. It does not cure the course-wide timing,
source-capture, LMS, final-exam, form-rights or regulator-approval blockers.

## Legal and instructional corrections

| Area | Correction now carried by the module |
|---|---|
| Fixtures | Replaced obsolete G.S. 25-9-313 with current G.S. 25-9-334; added realty-priority baseline, statutory exceptions, qualifying PMSI fixture filing and the 20-day period. |
| Marketable title and Torrens | Added Chapter 47B's 30-year framework, exceptions and preservation route; kept Chapter 43 registration/removal as a court-and-attorney process rather than a broker title opinion. |
| Planned communities | Separated physical townhouse form from the declaration and statutory scope that determine whether Chapter 47F governs. |
| Owner disclosures | Reconciled Chapter 47E coverage, exemption categories, delivery, earliest-of cancellation cutoffs, owner completion and the distinct RPOADS/MOG response structures. |
| Fair housing | Added Chapter 41A protected bases, state-specific exemptions, the advertising boundary, one-year Commission complaint period and conditional right-to-sue workflow; kept federal and license-law layers separate. |
| Sexual demands in housing | Added the exact G.S. 14-395.1 offense boundary and Class 2 classification without confusing it with the civil or disciplinary route. |
| Timeshares | Added the nonwaivable five-day later-of cancellation rule, refund/closing/escrow controls, three-year records and program-broker duties. |
| Unfair practices | Replaced automatic-liability language with G.S. 75-1.1's actual scope and professional-services exclusion and G.S. 75-16's assessed-damages trebling rule. |
| Square footage | Removed the false “not material” statement. Current NCREC guidance treats heated living area as material, requires current-listing verification, identifies unreliable carry-forward sources and supplies heat, finish, access, ceiling, measurement and record criteria. |
| Streets | Distinguished an offer of public dedication on a plat from government acceptance for maintenance and required verification of actual street status. |
| Building codes | Kept the syllabus's R-13/R-30/R-19 figures only as a memory aid, not a universal current compliance table; recorded the current 2018-code status and delayed 2024 implementation. |
| Commercial broker liens | Added written owner agreement, performance/earned-compensation, notice, service, filing and enforcement thresholds under Chapter 44A Part 4. |
