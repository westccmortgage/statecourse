## Instructional disposition

- **WWREA:** the disclosure form and Q&A are taught together, but the lesson states that signing or
  receiving the disclosure does not itself hire a firm or create agency. Seller agency, buyer agency,
  dual agency, designated dual agency and an unrepresented party are compared from the retained
  publications and current Rule 58A .0104 boundary.
- **Subdivisions:** the lesson reviews HOA obligations, preliminary/final plats, road maintenance and
  common areas, then gives current statutes priority. Chapter 47F controls present planned-community
  scope and remedies; G.S. 160D-807 controls the preliminary/final-plat contract sequence; G.S.
  136-102.6 controls subdivision-street disclosure.
- **Release gate:** the authentic approved-plat activity remains outstanding. Neither publication is
  used as a substitute for an actual plat, licensed survey or project-specific legal determination.
