# North Carolina filing index — pre-filing draft

**StateCourse status:** Phase 0 complete; provider not certified; course not approved; instructor not
approved; enrollment closed; no NC credit available.

## Correct filing order

1. **Resolve entity authority.** Ask NC Secretary of State/counsel whether StateCourse LLC's exact
   distance-education operations require foreign-LLC authority under G.S. 57D-7-01. If required,
   file [Form L-09](https://www.sosnc.gov/webfiles/documents/forms/Business_Registration/limited_liability_companies/application_for_certificate_of_authority.pdf),
   $250, with a California certificate of existence authenticated and less than six months old,
   registered office/agent, principal officials, and signature. Record the NC SOS ID.
2. **Resolve the individual license path.** Obtain certified California broker history and NCREC's
   written answer on comparable status; apply under Rule 58A .0511(b)(1), pay $105, supply the
   designated criminal report, pass the NC State exam, and wait for the issued status.
3. **Resolve instructor eligibility.** Confirm 60 college semester hours and one Rule
   .0302(b)(5) branch/equivalency. After a nonprovisional NC broker license is recorded and active,
   complete the instructor exam, then the NIS within the required sequence/window, file the
   nonresident consent, and obtain NCREC instructor approval.
4. **Complete provider packet.** Finalize the provider datasheet and PPD; identify the education
   director and officials; attach entity/economic/operations evidence and foreign-entity consent;
   submit through the [Education Provider Portal](https://educprovider.ncrec.gov/) with $200 per
   proposed provider location. Do not treat submission as certification.
5. **Receive provider certification and number.** Only then populate the course application with
   the provider number. Record issue/expiry dates and conditions.
6. **Complete the course build.** Close all Required Activity/source gaps, expand student-visible
   instructional content to demonstrate 72 non-exam instructional hours (3,600 actual minutes under
   .0101(7)) at 225 words/minute plus media, with separate breaks/exam ledgers. The two-form protected
   comprehensive final is authored; obtain substantive signoff, implement identity/closed-book
   proctoring and protected attempt storage, add reviewer access, and verify every distance control
   in production.
7. **Refresh sources.** Capture the exact current statutes/rules/forms/syllabus, hash every retained
   file, compare relevant rendered pages against digital text (OCR only if missing/disputed), and
   run the source-lock validator. Reconcile any changed
   form or rule before signing.
8. **Submit the course application.** Use the live REC 7.33 workflow at the
   [official portal](https://educprovider.ncrec.gov/EducationCourseApplication/Index). Attach the full
   reviewable course, course guide, all student materials, timing outline, attendance evidence,
   hardware/software/support specifications, material-possession verification, prospective
   instructors, ownership permission, and proctoring description. Confirm the live original-review
   fee; it was not exposed in the reviewed static rule text.
9. **Receive written course approval.** Record course number, delivery method, effective/expiration
   dates, conditions, and approved instructor. Approval for another delivery method does not clear
   self-paced distance.
10. **Release audit.** Test identity/proctoring, sequential unit assessments, 180-day shutoff,
    completion/report fields, monthly summary process, record retention, PPD-before-payment, support,
    accessibility, security, backups, and regulator reviewer access. Only a signed release decision
    may open enrollment and payment.
