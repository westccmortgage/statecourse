## Outcome

The former Module NC-14 was not submission-ready. It correctly refused to invent law, but it left
required Level 2 content as routes to unread statutes: security-deposit amounts, permitted uses and
accounting; protected retaliation activity; statutory duties; vacation-rental coverage, transfer,
eviction and evacuation; and operational property-management controls.

The corrected module now teaches and tests those rules from the current official text. It contains
4,438 whitespace-counted words, one integrated application lab, decision sequences, deposit
calculations, and the course engine's eleven items. Section XIV prescribes no separate Required
Activity, so no proprietary form or constructed substitute was added.

## Corrections made

1. Removed the false current statement that Chapter 42, Chapter 42A, and Rule .0116 had not been read.
2. Replaced the broad “transient occupancy” shortcut with each Act's actual scope and exclusions.
3. Stated the managing-agent definition while preserving G.S. 42-44(c1)'s limit against automatic
   personal-liability overstatement.
4. Added the enumerated landlord and tenant duties, nonwaiver structure, and G.S. 42-44(c)'s required
   judicial determination before rent withholding.
5. Separated Article 2A's self-help prohibition, Article 3 summary ejectment, and Article 7's
   criminal-activity civil route.
6. Replaced the categorical expired-lease retaliation answer with Article 4A's five protected acts,
   12-month defense, and six statutory landlord grounds.
7. Added the security-deposit maxima: two weeks' rent, one and one-half months' rent, and two months'
   rent; listed permitted uses; and added the 30-day ordinary/interim and 60-day final accounting rules.
8. Separated an owner-landlord's statutory bond alternative from a broker-manager's Commission trust-
   account duty and retained the 30-day depository notice.
9. Distinguished a statutory pet fee from prohibited pet treatment of service and other qualifying
   assistance animals, without immunizing actual animal damage.
10. Added Chapter 42A's written-agreement and fund controls, including three banking days, 50% ordinary
    pre-occupancy disbursement ceiling, no deposit bond, and 45-day deposit accounting.
11. Added the voluntary-transfer sequence: 180-day booking protection and applicable 10-, 20-, and
    30-day information, notice, and fund-transfer duties.
12. Distinguished the under-90-day vacation-rental definition from the 30-day-or-less expedited-
    possession threshold and added the four-hour notice and 12-to-48-hour hearing window.
13. Added habitability/substitute/refund, broker responsibilities, evacuation refund/insurance, and
    written-from-formation property-management controls.
