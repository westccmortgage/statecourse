## Provider evidence packet

- California formation/status record and recent authenticated certificate of existence.
- NC L-09 certificate of authority and SOS ID, if the exact operations require it.
- registered-agent acceptance and registered office, if L-09 is filed.
- organization chart and ownership ledger.
- education director résumé, license record, appointment, duties acknowledgment, and contact data.
- officials/instructors biographies and credentials.
- financial-capacity evidence sufficient to show the school can meet commitments to students.
- accessibility, information-security, continuity, complaint, refund, records, and exam-security
  policies.
- final PPD and all public descriptions/advertising samples.
- course inventory and delivery methods.
- signed Consent to Service of Process and Pleadings for the foreign entity.

## Education director operating commitments

The designated director owns course/instructor compliance, completion certificates, regulator
fees/rosters/reports, instructor notification within ten days of employment, exam integrity,
regulator liaison and access. The director views NCREC's education-director video within 30 days of
initial designation and annually within the 45 days before provider expiration, reports a director
change within ten days, and reports cheating in writing within ten days.
