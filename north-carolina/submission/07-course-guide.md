# Course guide — North Carolina Broker Prelicensing

**Version:** 1.0 submission draft  
**Delivery:** self-paced distance  
**Regulatory status:** not approved; no credit; enrollment closed.

## Purpose and outcomes

The course prepares an entry-level candidate to apply North Carolina license law, rules and brokerage
practice at the competency levels prescribed by the March 2024 syllabus amended May 2026. A successful
student should be able to recall Level 1 content, apply Level 2 concepts to practice situations, and
analyze/defend Level 3 decisions without using actual license-exam items.

## Delivery and assessment

Each module begins with its section/level objectives, provides instruction and required activities,
then requires an applied item and engagement assessment before progression. Remediation follows an
unsuccessful attempt. All units/assessments, required activities/homework, the applicable attendance
record and a comprehensive closed-book proctored final at 75% or higher are completion conditions.
Homework time is not counted as instructional time.

## Required-material warning

The course is not filing-ready until the current official/copyright-authorized versions of Form 2-T
and addenda/guidance, WWREA resources, RPOADS/MOG, Residential Square Footage Guidelines, NC Real
Estate Manual where applicable, statute/rule readings, and every other prescribed Required Activity
resource are lawfully obtained and reconciled. Module 12's settlement artifacts and Module 13's
completed URAR/reconciliation artifacts are retained with all-page review evidence; placeholders do
not satisfy any remaining Required Activity.
