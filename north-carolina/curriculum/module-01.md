## Learning objectives

At Level 1 — recall, define, classify, explain, describe:

1. Describe the sequence of steps required to obtain a North Carolina broker licence.
2. Describe how the Commission considers an applicant's character, and the routes available.
3. State how long the Prelicensing Course remains valid for licensing purposes, and from what date.
4. State how long a criminal record report remains valid and why its timing matters.
5. Describe the licence examination's format at the level the Syllabus states it.
6. Explain what a provisional broker is and what the provisional status requires.
7. Describe the Commission and the general licensing categories.

## A. Licensing Process

### A.1 The sequence

North Carolina's path to a broker licence runs in a fixed order, and the order matters because
several of the steps carry their own clocks.

1. **Prelicensing education** — this course, a minimum of 75 instructional hours, taken from a
   **Commission-certified Education Provider** whose course has been approved **for the specific
   delivery method** being used.
2. **Application** — made online to the Commission.
3. **Consideration of the applicant's character.**
4. **Licence examination.**
5. **Licence issuance**, initially on **provisional** status.

### A.2 Prelicensing education, and the clock it starts

**The Prelicensing Course is valid for licensing purposes for 3 years from the course completion date
on the certificate** received after passing the end-of-course examination.

Read the measuring date carefully. It is **the course completion date on the certificate**, and the
certificate issues **after** the end-of-course examination is passed. It is not the date the last
module was finished and it is not the date of application.

Note also the reporting step, because it constrains the application: **a student can begin the online
licence application but cannot complete it until the Education Provider reports their passing of the
Prelicensing Course to the Commission.**

### A.3 Application

The application is made online. The Syllabus states an application fee of **$105**, paid when the
application is submitted with all its required parts — proof of course completion, the criminal record
report, and the completed online application.

**Fees change.** The figure above is the figure appearing in the Commission's Syllabus, March 2024
Edition as amended May 2026. **Confirm the current fee with the Commission before relying on it.**

### A.4 Consideration of the applicant's character

Two things a student needs to know early, and this course states them in Section I rather than
leaving them to be discovered.

**The Criminal Record Report (CRR).** Students are encouraged to **wait to order the report until they
are successfully through at least half of the Prelicensing course, because the report is only valid
for 6 months.** Reports covering **multiple names, or residency in multiple states or other
countries, take longer to obtain**, and that lead time has to be planned for.

The two facts together produce the practical rule: **order the report late enough that it does not
expire before the application is complete, and early enough that a long search does not delay it.**
Roughly halfway through the course is the Commission's own suggestion.

**Petition for Pre-Determination.** **An individual with a criminal history record may petition the
Commission for a predetermination of whether that history will likely disqualify the individual from
obtaining a licence.**

That route exists and it is worth using **before** paying for education rather than afterwards.
Detailed information is on the Commission's website under Licensing / Apply for a License.

**This course states no offence, category, period or disqualification rule**, because NCGS Chapter 93A
and 21 NCAC Chapter 58 were not read from primary text for this project. What is stated is that the
Commission considers character, that a criminal record report is required and expires in six months,
and that a predetermination petition is available. **Every specific goes to the Commission.**

### A.5 The licence examination

The Syllabus covers the examination's **format** and the process of **applying for and scheduling**
it, and it directs students to the Commission's own videos on both.

What this course states, because the Syllabus states it:

- The examination is administered by the Commission's contracted testing service.
- The candidate is emailed a **Notice** once the application is complete and approved.
- The examination has a **national section** and a **state section**. The Commission's Syllabus assigns
  every one of its sixteen sections to one category or the other, and the totals are **80 national
  items and 60 state items — 140 in all.**

**This course states no examination fee, no scheduling deadline, no retake rule and no scoring
method**, because none was read from primary text for this project. All are routed to the Commission
and to the testing service's current candidate information.

**And one rule that governs how this course itself was written:** Commission Rule 58H .0304(b), as
quoted in the Syllabus — **"Instructors shall not obtain, use, or attempt to obtain or use, in any
manner or form, North Carolina real estate license examination questions."** Every assessment item in
this course is authored from the Syllabus's stated topics and from the general body of pre-license
knowledge. **No item is derived from, modelled on, or checked against any actual licence examination
question.**

## B. Licensing Qualifications

### B.1 What the Commission is looking for

The Commission licenses persons it is satisfied are qualified to act as brokers **in a manner that
protects and serves the public interest** — which is the Syllabus's own statement of what this whole
course is for.

**This course states no age requirement, residency requirement, citizenship requirement or education
waiver ground**, because NCGS 93A and 21 NCAC 58A were not read from primary text for this project.
*(21 NCAC 58A .0304 concerns waiver of the 75-hour prelicensing education requirement and was
identified but not obtained.)* **Route every qualification question to the Commission.**

## C. The Commission

The **North Carolina Real Estate Commission** licenses brokers, certifies Education Providers,
approves courses and instructors, publishes the Syllabus this course is built to, and administers the
licence examination through a contracted testing service.

Two of its publications are named by the Syllabus as required resources and are worth obtaining on day
one:

- **North Carolina Real Estate License Law and Commission Rules**, including the **Comments on the Law
  and Rules** — the Syllabus calls it **LLCR**.
- **Real Estate Licensing in North Carolina** — **RELINC**.

Both are available for free download or as printed booklets from the Commission's website.

**Neither was obtained for this project.** This course therefore states no provision of the License Law
or the Commission Rules **except where the Syllabus itself quotes one**, and every quoted passage is
identified as such. **Read the LLCR.** It is free, it is short, and it is the operative text this whole
course is a map to.

## D. General Licensing

### D.1 Provisional broker, and what "provisional" means

A North Carolina licence issues initially on **provisional** status. A **provisional broker** may not
practise independently: they must be **supervised by a broker-in-charge**, and they must complete
**postlicensing education** to remove the provisional status.

**This course states no postlicensing hour requirement and no deadline for completing it**, because the
governing statute and rules were not read from primary text for this project. Module NC-02 takes up
supervision itself, which the Syllabus places in Section II at **Level 3** — the highest level of
instruction it assigns.

**What a student should take from Section I is the shape:** the licence you are working toward is a
**provisional** one, it comes with a supervision requirement and a further education requirement, and
**this 75-hour course is the first step of a longer sequence rather than the whole of it.**

### D.2 The firm licence

Real estate brokerage in North Carolina is conducted through licensed firms as well as licensed
individuals, and Module NC-02 covers the consequence that matters most to a new licensee: **who may
lawfully pay you.**

## Module NC-01 engagement check

Eleven items: one applied scenario, five engagement items and five remediation items. The applied item
must be answered correctly; the engagement and remediation sets are passed at 80 percent.

**Both thresholds are StateCourse standards.** The Commission's Syllabus prescribes completion
conditions — required activities and homework, attendance, and the end-of-course examination — but no
in-module score. **The 75 percent that IS the Commission's applies to the end-of-course examination**,
which under Commission Rule 58H .0207 must be **comprehensive, closed-book and proctored**, with a
**minimum passing grade of 75%** that a provider may raise but not lower.
