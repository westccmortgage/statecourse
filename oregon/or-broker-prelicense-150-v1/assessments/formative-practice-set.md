## Real Estate Law

1. A buyer wants to rely on an old marketing statement that a parcel permits short-term rentals. What is the best response?  
A Treat the old listing as authorization. B Verify current local rules and applicable recorded restrictions through authoritative sources. C Ask a neighbor. D Promise it is allowed.  
**Answer B.** An old ad is not authoritative evidence of current land-use permission.

2. A fence appears to cross a boundary line. What evidence should be sought?  
A Social-media photos only. B A qualified survey and relevant title/record review. C Seller's estimate only. D Appraisal.  
**Answer B.** Survey and record evidence are needed; a licensee should not determine the legal boundary.

3. A buyer asks the licensee to interpret a deed restriction's enforceability.  
A Give a definitive legal opinion. B Explain the limit of the licensee's role and refer to counsel/title professional. C Ignore it. D Guarantee it is void.  
**Answer B.** Legal enforceability requires appropriate professional advice.

4. A property is subject to a utility easement. The buyer should understand that the easement:  
A Automatically transfers title to the utility. B May give a defined use right subject to its terms. C Removes all zoning rules. D Guarantees utilities are installed.  
**Answer B.** The instrument defines the right and limits.

5. Which is the strongest support for a parcel's legal description?  
A Old listing. B Deed and current title/record documents, reviewed by appropriate professionals. C Seller's memory. D Mailing address.  
**Answer B.** Use authoritative land records and professional review.

## Oregon Real Estate Practice

6. Seller requests “perfect for families” in an advertisement. Best revision?  
A Keep it. B Use objective property facts such as bedroom count, square footage if verified, and nearby amenities. C Replace with “ideal for children.” D Say “adults only.”  
**Answer B.** Describe housing features, not preferred occupants.

7. Seller provides an old square-footage figure with no supporting document. The licensee should:  
A Publish as verified. B Confirm its source and disclose uncertainty as required before using it. C Add 10%. D Remove all property details.  
**Answer B.** Verify material factual claims.

8. Seller says roof repairs are complete but has no receipt. Best practice?  
A State “new roof.” B Ask for records and accurately distinguish seller statement from verified documentation. C Guarantee the repair. D Delete the history.  
**Answer B.** Keep claims tied to evidence.

9. Before taking a listing to market, confirm:  
A Authority to market and support for stated material facts. B Buyer financing. C Guaranteed appraisal. D Future sale date.  
**Answer A.** Confirm authorization and factual accuracy.

10. A brokerage file is missing a signed authorization.  
A Recreate a signature. B Pause the affected action, inform the supervising broker, and obtain accurate documentation. C Mark complete. D Use another client's form.  
**Answer B.** Never fabricate records.

## Real Estate Finance

11. A client asks whether a lender will approve a loan based on a prequalification conversation.  
A Guarantee approval. B Explain lender makes the decision and conditions may change; refer questions to lender. C Promise a rate. D Tell client to waive financing.  
**Answer B.** A licensee cannot guarantee underwriting.

12. Which item is a contract term rather than a lender underwriting decision?  
A Closing date stated in the signed agreement. B Final debt-to-income approval. C Appraisal condition. D Underwriting exception.  
**Answer A.** It is a negotiated contract term; lender decisions remain with the lender.

13. A comparison uses two fictional loan estimates but one omits taxes and insurance. Best action?  
A Compare payments as complete. B Mark the comparison incomplete and request comparable figures. C Assume both are equal. D Remove the contingency.  
**Answer B.** Explain the missing inputs and avoid misleading comparisons.

14. Who can confirm current loan conditions and eligibility?  
A Listing agent. B Lender or mortgage professional authorized to discuss the file. C Appraiser. D Title company.  
**Answer B.** Qualification and loan terms are lender matters.

15. A client asks to change a financing deadline because the lender is delayed. The licensee should:  
A Extend it orally. B Promptly inform client and broker and seek properly documented agreement from the parties. C Backdate. D Assume automatic extension.  
**Answer B.** Changes need authorized documentation.

## Contracts

16. Parties verbally agree to move a deadline in a signed contract. Until appropriate written change is completed, the agent should:  
A Treat the new date as binding. B Track the written deadline and seek broker guidance/documentation. C Edit the contract unilaterally. D Ignore all deadlines.  
**Answer B.** Do not assume an oral discussion changed the signed document.

17. A contract contains an ambiguous term. Best next step?  
A Guess intent. B Flag it for principal-broker review and appropriate legal guidance. C Delete it. D Ask escrow to decide.  
**Answer B.** Avoid making legal interpretations beyond scope.

18. A contingency date is approaching. The agent should:  
A Remind client and document instructions. B Wait. C Remove the contingency. D Notify only the other party.  
**Answer A.** Prompt communication supports informed client action.

19. A party asks the agent to sign an amendment for them.  
A Sign if verbally authorized. B Do not sign for the party; follow authorized signature process. C Copy their previous signature. D Ask another agent to sign.  
**Answer B.** Do not forge or substitute signatures.

20. Which file entry best supports a contract change?  
A Agent's memory. B Fully executed written amendment retained with dated communication record. C Undated note. D Calendar reminder alone.  
**Answer B.** Preserve the signed instrument and timeline.

## Agency Law

21. A licensee has a relationship with a seller and a buyer asks for confidential strategy about that property. First action?  
A Share seller's confidential information. B Identify the relationships, disclose as required, and consult the supervising broker. C Promise secrecy and proceed. D Delete the seller file.  
**Answer B.** Recognize conflict and protect confidentiality.

22. A client asks to keep a material fact secret when disclosure is required.  
A Agree. B Explain limits, consult broker, and follow applicable disclosure duties. C Tell only one bidder. D Avoid written records.  
**Answer B.** Confidentiality does not authorize concealment of legally required facts.

23. A person asks the agent to negotiate before the parties' relationship is clear.  
A Explain representation options and required disclosures before acting as an advocate. B Assume representation. C Refuse all service. D Promise a result.  
**Answer A.** Clarify relationship and duties.

24. A licensee discovers a personal conflict after entering a transaction.  
A Continue silently. B Promptly disclose and seek broker guidance on compliant handling. C Ask client to keep quiet. D Destroy messages.  
**Answer B.** Timely disclosure and supervision are appropriate.

25. Which record is most useful for agency disclosure?  
A Undated memory. B Dated disclosure acknowledgment and related communication retained in file. C Informal conversation only. D Listing photo.  
**Answer B.** Contemporaneous documentation is useful.

## Property Management

26. Tenant reports water intrusion. Manager should first:  
A Diagnose the cause. B Log time and details, promptly route under policy and law, and arrange qualified assessment as appropriate. C Wait a month. D Tell tenant to stop reporting.  
**Answer B.** Document and escalate promptly without diagnosing.

27. Owner asks manager to delay an urgent repair without inspection.  
A Comply without question. B Explain the report, escalate under applicable requirements/policy, and document instructions. C Delete the request. D Promise no harm.  
**Answer B.** Preserve the issue and use proper escalation.

28. A tenant requests repair by text. Best recordkeeping response?  
A Keep no record. B Enter dated request and all follow-up in the approved management log. C Forward publicly. D Rewrite the date.  
**Answer B.** Maintain accurate, secure records.

29. A smoke alarm chirps and tenant reports it. Manager should:  
A Ignore. B Log and route promptly under maintenance and safety procedures. C Diagnose wiring. D Tell tenant to disable it.  
**Answer B.** Follow safety procedures and document.

30. A property manager is unsure what a statutory notice requires.  
A Invent a deadline. B Check current official law and consult principal broker or counsel. C Use a notice from another state. D Rely on memory.  
**Answer B.** Verify current jurisdiction-specific requirements.

## Real Estate Brokerage

31. A deposit is entered in the ledger one day later than the bank record. What should staff do?  
A Change the bank record date. B Preserve actual records and promptly escalate/reconcile the exception. C Backdate the receipt. D Ignore the mismatch.  
**Answer B.** Never alter history; investigate and document.

32. A staff member proposes changing a date to make records match.  
A Approve. B Refuse alteration, preserve evidence, and notify the responsible broker. C Delete the deposit entry. D Ask the client to sign a blank form.  
**Answer B.** Records must remain accurate.

33. A trust-fund reconciliation identifies unexplained variance.  
A Use operating funds to hide it. B Immediately notify the responsible principal broker and follow controls for investigation. C Wait for month end. D Change the client ledger.  
**Answer B.** Escalate discrepancies promptly.

34. An office advertises a property without confirming authority.  
A Publish anyway. B Stop and confirm written authorization and required disclosures. C Rely on a text from a third party. D Use a competitor's photos.  
**Answer B.** Verify authority before advertising.

35. Which is a sound corrective action after a file-control failure?  
A Blame a staff member and stop reviewing. B Document the cause, correct the file transparently, coach staff, and schedule follow-up testing. C Delete records. D Ignore if closed.  
**Answer B.** Correct, document, and check recurrence.
