## 1. The education requirement — and the number this project had to correct

### 49 Pa. Code § 35.272 — Examination for salesperson's license

> **(a)** An individual who wants to take the salesperson's examination for the purpose of obtaining a
> standard salesperson's license shall:
>
> **(1)** Be **18 years of age or older**.
> **(2)** Have successfully completed **four credits, or 60 hours of instruction, in basic real estate
> courses** as determined by the Commission under subsection (b).
> **(3)** Submit a completed examination application to the Commission or its designee with the
> examination fee.

> **(b)** The Commission will apply the following standards in determining whether an examination
> candidate has met the education requirement of subsection (a)(2):
>
> **(1)** A candidate who has obtained one of the following degrees will be **deemed to have met the
> education requirement**: a **bachelor's degree with a major in real estate** from an accredited
> college; a **bachelor's degree** from an accredited college **having completed coursework equivalent
> to a major in real estate**; or a **juris doctor degree from an accredited law school**.
>
> **(2)** Credits will be allowed for ***Real Estate Fundamentals* and *Real Estate Practice*** and all
> acceptable basic real estate courses when offered by: an accredited college; **a real estate
> education provider who has met the approval requirements in § 35.341**; or a real estate education
> provider outside this Commonwealth approved by the licensing authority of its own jurisdiction, whose
> transcript or certificate states that approval.
>
> **(3)** **Courses shall have been completed within 10 years prior to the date of successful
> completion of the licensing examination.**

> **(c)** A licensee converting a reciprocal license to a standard salesperson's license is exempt from
> subsections (a) and (b) and is only required to pass the state portion of the examination.

### 49 Pa. Code § 35.201 — the definition that makes the arithmetic work

> ***"Credit — A period of 15 hours of instruction."***

### 49 Pa. Code § 35.358(b)(1)

> ***"A prelicensure course must be assigned one credit for every 15 clock hours of instruction."***

### The finding, stated plainly

**The regulation's minimum is 4 credits = 60 hours of instruction.** It is *not* 75.

**The two courses the Commission names by title in § 35.272(b)(2) — *Real Estate Fundamentals* and
*Real Estate Practice* — are named without hours.** The regulation this project read **does not state
how many hours or credits either course carries**, and **does not state a 30/45 split.**

The **75-hour** figure in general circulation is the two named courses as providers commonly deliver
them: **Real Estate Fundamentals at 30 hours (2 credits) and Real Estate Practice at 45 hours (3
credits) = 5 credits / 75 hours**, which **exceeds** the regulatory minimum of 4 credits / 60 hours.

**This project has NOT obtained any Commission document assigning hours to either named course.**
Accordingly this build:

- states the **60-hour / 4-credit minimum as the regulation's own figure**, transcribed above;
- builds **75 hours** across the two named courses **as a StateCourse proposal**, which satisfies the
  minimum with margin; and
- records the **30/45 split as a StateCourse decision requiring Commission confirmation**, not as a
  regulatory requirement.

**Obtaining the Commission's own hour assignment for *Real Estate Fundamentals* and *Real Estate
Practice* is a submission gate.**

## 2. What the Commission prescribes about course content — and the outline this project does NOT have

### 49 Pa. Code § 35.358 — Administration of curriculum

> **(a)** Real estate education providers shall observe the following standards in the administration
> of prelicensure and continuing education curriculum:
>
> **(1)** *"Instructor-led learning **may not exceed 7 1/2 clock hours of instruction per day**. For
> purposes of this section, a **clock hour is defined as a 60-minute period comprising 50 minutes of
> instruction and a 10-minute break**. **A student may not be required to attend class for more than 90
> consecutive minutes without a break.**"*
>
> **(2)** *"The substantive content of the course, as evidenced by the course outline, text and other
> instructional materials, shall adequately reflect the stated purpose of the course, as evidenced by
> the course title and course description. **Instruction in a Commission required course shall conform
> to the content or the outline developed by the Commission for the course.**"*
>
> **(3)** *"Unless the course is taught by means of distance education, **a student shall be physically
> present during at least 80% of the classroom instruction for a prelicensure course** and during at
> least 90% of the classroom instruction for a continuing education course, **to receive credit**. The
> real estate education provider shall be responsible for **verifying student attendance**."*
>
> **(4)** *"**Courses delivered by distance education**, in addition to meeting the content requirements
> in § 35.384, **must have the delivery method approved by the Association of Real Estate License Law
> Officials or another certifying body with similar approval standards approved by the Commission.**"*

> **(b)** In addition:
>
> **(1)** *"A prelicensure course must be assigned **one credit for every 15 clock hours of
> instruction**."*
> **(2)** *"**A prelicensure course must be graded by proctored examination**, except when a student's
> handicap or disability would make grading by examination impractical."*

### Two hard gates this creates for an online Pennsylvania course

1. **§ 35.358(a)(2) — the Commission develops content outlines for Commission-required courses, and
   instruction "shall conform" to them.** **This project has NOT obtained any Commission-developed
   content outline for *Real Estate Fundamentals* or *Real Estate Practice*.** The curriculum in this
   build is written from **49 Pa. Code Chapter 35 itself** and must be **conformed to the Commission's
   outline before submission.** **Submission gate.**

2. **§ 35.358(a)(4) — a distance education delivery method must be approved by ARELLO or another
   certifying body approved by the Commission.** **No such approval is held.** **Submission gate.**

**§ 35.358(b)(2) — the course must be graded by PROCTORED examination.** The engine treats the final
examination as proctored and separate from in-module checks.

**Note on the attendance standard.** § 35.358(a)(3)'s 80 percent applies *"unless the course is taught
by means of distance education."* This build does **not** claim an equivalent distance standard, because
the regulation states none in the text read; the engine records attendance as a completion input to be
set by the provider under whatever standard the Commission approves.

## 3. Records, transcripts and retention — education provider

### § 35.359(a) — Course documentation

> *"**Within 30 days after a course has been taught**, a real estate education provider shall provide
> each student in the course with an **official course transcript** that contains the information in
> § 35.360(a)(5) and **is signed by the director**."*

### § 35.360(a)(5) — what the academic transcript must contain

> The provider's name and **Commission approval number**; the location at which the course was taught;
> the name of the student; the course title; **the date that the student completed the course**; **the
> number of hours of the course**; the student's final grade **if an examination is required**; the date
> the transcript was issued; and *"**the fact that the course will be accepted by the Commission towards
> fulfillment of the education requirement** for either the real estate broker's examination or real
> estate salesperson's examination."*

### § 35.360(e) — Retention

> **(1)** *"Continuing education records must be maintained for **4 years**."*
> **(2)** *"**All other records must be retained for 10 years.**"*

### § 35.357 — Student enrollment agreements

Each student must enter into an enrollment agreement that **itemizes tuition and other fees**, states
the **refund policy**, and **contains the Bureau's toll-free telephone number, (800) 822-2113**, which
the student may call to obtain information about **filing a complaint against the provider**.

### § 35.356 — Tuition

> *"A real estate education provider shall charge tuition that **bears a reasonable relationship to the
> quality and quantity of instructional services rendered**."*

## 4. Duties owed to every consumer — § 35.292

**This is the spine of Pennsylvania practice and the section every other duty section builds on.**

> **(a)** *"The following duties are **owed to all consumers of real estate services and may not be
> waived**:*
>
> **(1)** *Exercise **reasonable professional skill and care**.*
> **(2)** ***Deal honestly and in good faith.***
> **(3)** *Present, **in a reasonably practicable period of time, all offers, counteroffers, notices and
> communications to and from the parties in writing**, unless the property is subject to an existing
> contract and the seller/landlord has agreed in a written waiver.*
> **(4)** *Comply with **68 Pa.C.S. Chapter 73 (Real Estate Seller Disclosure Law)**.*
> **(5)** *Account for escrow and deposit funds as required by section 604(a)(5) of the act and
> §§ 35.321—35.328.*
> **(6)** *Provide consumers with the information in **section 608 of the act at the initial
> interview**.*
> **(7)** *Disclose, in a reasonably practicable period of time, **all conflicts of interest and
> financial interests** as required in § 35.283.*
> **(8)** ***Advise the consumer to seek expert advice on matters about the transaction that are beyond
> the licensee's expertise.***
> **(9)** *Ensure that all services are provided in a **reasonable, professional and competent
> manner**.*
> **(10)** *Keep the consumer **informed about the transaction and the tasks to be completed**.*
> **(11)** ***Provide assistance with document preparation.***
> **(12)** ***Advise the consumer about compliance with laws pertaining to real estate transactions
> without rendering legal advice.***"

And the three limits, transcribed:

> **(b)** *"A licensee is **not required to conduct an independent inspection of the property**."*
> **(c)** *"A licensee is **not required to independently verify the accuracy or completeness of any
> representation made by the consumer** to a transaction which the licensee **reasonably believes to be
> accurate and reliable**."*
> **(d)** *"A licensee is **not liable for the acts of a consumer** unless the consumer is acting at the
> express direction of the licensee or as a result of a representation by a licensee reasonably relied
> on by the consumer."*

**Note the pairing of (11) and (12).** Pennsylvania expressly directs the licensee to **provide
assistance with document preparation** and to **advise about compliance with laws** — and in the same
breath **without rendering legal advice.** That is a narrower line than most states draw, and it is
drawn inside a single sentence.

## 5. Permitted relationships — §§ 35.311 to 35.316

**Pennsylvania is a statutory-relationship state with FIVE named relationships, one of which is not an
agency at all.**

### § 35.311 — Generally

> **(b)** *"A broker **may not extend or delegate the broker's agency relationship without the written
> consent of the principal**."*
> **(c)** *"**Compensation paid by a broker to another broker** who assists in the marketing and
> sale/lease of a consumer's property **does not create an agency relationship** between the consumer
> and that other broker."*
> **(d)** *A licensee in an agency relationship **may not knowingly, during or following the termination
> of an agency relationship, reveal or use confidential information of the principal**, except where:
> **(1)** the principal consented; **(2)** disclosure is to another licensee or third party acting solely
> on behalf of the principal; **(3)** disclosure is required under subpoena or court order; **(4)**
> disclosure is **necessary to prevent the principal from committing a crime**; or **(5)** the
> information is used by the licensee **to defend in a legal proceeding against an accusation of
> wrongdoing**.*

### § 35.312 — Seller's agent

Additional duties beyond § 35.292: **loyalty**; **confidentiality, *except that a licensee has a duty to
reveal known material defects about the property***; **a continuous and good faith effort to find a
buyer/tenant** except where the property is under an existing agreement; and **disclosure to other
parties that the licensee has been engaged as a seller's agent.**

> **(b)** *"A licensee **does not breach a duty** to a seller/landlord by **showing alternative
> properties** to a prospective buyer/tenant **or listing competing properties**."*
> **(c)** *"A seller's agent **may compensate other brokers as subagents if the seller/landlord agrees in
> writing**. **Subagents have the same duties and obligations to the seller/landlord as the seller's
> agent.**"*
> **(d)** *"A seller's agent may also **compensate a buyer's agent and a transaction licensee who do NOT
> have the same duties and obligations** to the seller/landlord."*
> **(e)** *"Upon entering into a written agreement with the seller/landlord, **each licensee employed by
> the broker will act as a seller's agent** unless a licensee has been named a designated agent."*

### § 35.313 — Buyer's agent

Additional duties: **loyalty**; **confidentiality** *(with no material-defect exception stated, unlike
the seller's agent)*; **continuous and good faith effort to find a property**; **disclosure to other
parties.**

> **(c)** ***"A buyer's agent represents the interests of the buyer/tenant even if paid by the
> seller/landlord."***

### § 35.314 — Dual agent

> **(a)** *"A licensee **may act as a dual agent if both parties consent in writing**."*
>
> Additional duties: **(1)** *"Taking **no action that is adverse or detrimental to either party's
> interest**"*; **(2)** unless otherwise agreed in writing, continuous and good faith effort for both;
> **(3)** *"Confidentiality, **except that a licensee is required to disclose known material defects
> about the property**."*

### § 35.315 — Designated agent

> **(a)** *"A broker, **with the written consent of the principal**, may designate one or more licensees
> to act **exclusively** as the agent of the seller/landlord, and designate one or more licensees to act
> **exclusively** as the agent of the buyer/tenant **in the same transaction**."*
> **(c)** *"Regardless of when the designation takes place, the broker and the designated agents shall
> use **reasonable care to ensure that confidential information is not disclosed or used**."*
> **(d)** *"**The licensees employed by the broker who are not designated have no agency relationship
> with either party in the transaction.**"*
> **(e)(3)** *Each designated agent must **disclose to the principal prior to writing or presenting an
> offer to purchase that the other party is represented by a designated agent also employed by the
> broker**.*
> **(e)(4)** ***"Confirm that the broker is a dual agent in the transaction."***
> **(f)** *The employing broker, **as a dual agent**, must **take reasonable care to protect confidential
> information** disclosed to the designated licensees and **direct and supervise their business
> activities while taking no action adverse or detrimental to either party**.*

### § 35.316 — Transaction licensee — **not an agent**

> *"In addition to the duties required in § 35.292, a transaction licensee shall **advise the consumer**
> that the licensee:*
>
> **(1)** ***"Is not acting as an agent or advocate for the consumer and should not be provided with
> confidential information."***
> **(2)** *Owes the additional duty of **limited confidentiality** in that the following **may not be
> disclosed**:*
> - *"The **seller/landlord will accept a price less than the asking/listing price**."*
> - *"The **buyer/tenant will pay a price greater than the price submitted in a written offer**."*
> - *"The **seller/landlord or buyer/tenant will agree to financing terms other than those offered**."*

**Three items, and only three.** *Limited* confidentiality is a defined list, not a general duty.

## 6. Disclosure of business relationships — §§ 35.284, 35.336 to 35.339

### § 35.284 — timing

> **(a)(1)** *A licensee shall provide the **disclosure summary in § 35.336** to consumers seeking to
> purchase or sell real estate **at the initial interview if the interview occurs in person**.*
> **(a)(2)** *If the initial interview **does not occur in person**, the licensee shall provide the
> **oral disclosure in § 35.339 at the initial interview** and the **written disclosure statement in
> § 35.336** no later than the earlier of the stated points.*
> **(b)(2)** *A licensee **working on behalf of the owner** in a lease shall provide the disclosure
> summary in **§ 35.337**.*
> **(c)** *A licensee shall provide the disclosure summary in **§ 35.338** to consumers seeking to
> purchase **time-share estates** at the initial interview.*
> **(d)** *"A licensee shall **provide a copy of the signed disclosure** to the consumers and retain the
> signed acknowledgment under § 35.286. **If a consumer refuses to sign the acknowledgment, the refusal
> shall be noted on the acknowledgment.**"*

**Four different disclosure summaries for four different situations**, and the rule sets each out in
full text. **This project read that §§ 35.336 to 35.339 exist and set out the summaries verbatim; it
did NOT transcribe their full text into this build, and no document here reproduces any of them.**
Delivering the correct summary, in the Commission's own words, is a **submission gate.**

## 7. Escrow — §§ 35.321 to 35.328

- **§ 35.321(a):** a broker shall deposit money received belonging to another **into an escrow account
  in a Federally or State-insured bank or depository**, held pending consummation or a prior
  termination not involving a dispute. *"If a broker is a partnership, association or corporation, its
  **broker of record shall be responsible for ensuring that the escrow duty is performed**."*
- **§ 35.321(b):** *"A broker is **not required to hold in escrow rents** that he receives as a property
  manager for a lessor. A broker shall deposit rents received into a **rental management account that is
  separate from the broker's escrow and general business accounts**."*
- **§ 35.321(e):** for a **security deposit**, the duty to pay over **arises when the tenancy ends.**
- **§ 35.322 — Nonwaiver:** *"A broker's escrow duty **may not be waived or altered by an agreement**
  between the parties to the transaction, between the broker and the parties, or between the broker and
  other brokers."*
- **§ 35.323 — cobrokerage:** if the deposit is tendered **to the listing broker**, the **listing broker**
  holds it. If tendered to the selling broker **with prior notice** that it will go to the listing
  broker, the **listing broker** holds it, and the buyer must **acknowledge that prior notice in writing
  before signing the agreement of sale**, covering four stated points. **Without that prior notice, the
  selling broker assumes the escrow duty.**
- **§ 35.324(a) — deadline:** *"a broker shall deposit money belonging to another into an escrow account
  **by the end of the next business day following its receipt** in the real estate office where the
  escrow records are maintained."*
- **§ 35.324(b) — the one exception:** where the money was tendered **by check under an offer**, the
  broker may, **with the written permission of both buyer and seller (or lessee and lessor)**, hold it
  pending acceptance — and must then deposit it **within 1 business day of acceptance.**
- **§ 35.325(a):** the escrow account shall be **in a Federally- or State-insured bank or recognized
  depository**, **designate the broker as trustee**, **provide for withdrawal of funds without prior
  notice**, and be **used exclusively for escrow purposes.**
- **§ 35.325(c):** if money is expected to be held **more than 6 months**, the broker is **encouraged**
  to use an **interest-bearing** account; interest is held and disbursed **pro rata in the same manner as
  the principal** unless the parties direct otherwise, and *"**a broker may not claim the interest earned
  on an escrow account**"* except as a lessor under section 511.2 of the Landlord and Tenant Act.
- **§ 35.326:** **no commingling and no misappropriation** — except that a broker **may** deposit
  business or personal funds to **cover bank service charges or maintain a required minimum balance.**
- **§ 35.327 — disputes:** the broker **shall retain the money in escrow until the dispute is
  resolved**; if resolution appears remote without legal action, the broker may, **following 30 days'
  notice to the parties**, **petition the county court to interplead the rival claimants.**
- **§ 35.328 — escrow records:** six items — from whom received; to whom the money belongs; for whose
  account deposited; **date received**; **date deposited**; **date withdrawn.**

## 8. Real estate documents — §§ 35.281, 35.331 to 35.334, 35.340

### § 35.281(a) — writing

> *"**All contracts, commitments and agreements** between a broker, or a licensee employed by the
> broker, and a principal or a consumer **who is required to pay a fee, commission or other valuable
> consideration shall be in writing** and contain the information specified in § 35.331."*

### § 35.331(a) — what every such written agreement must contain

Eight items, including:

> **(1)** Notification that a **Real Estate Recovery Fund** exists to reimburse a person who has
> obtained a **final civil judgment** against a licensee **owing to fraud, misrepresentation or deceit**
> and **who has been unable to collect after exhausting legal and equitable remedies**; details by
> calling the Commission at **(717) 783-3658**.
> **(2)** Notification that payments received by the broker on account of a sale **shall be held in an
> escrow account**.
> **(3)** ***Notification that the broker's commission AND the duration of the agreement have been
> determined as a result of negotiations*** between the broker or a licensee employed by the broker and
> the seller/landlord or buyer/tenant.
> **(4)** A description of the services to be provided and the fees to be charged.
> **(5)** Notification about the **possibility that the broker or any licensee employed by the broker may
> provide services to more than one party in a single transaction**, with an explanation of the duties
> owed to the other party and the fees the broker may receive.
> **(6)** Notification of the licensee's **continuing duty to disclose in a reasonably practicable period
> of time any conflict of interest.**
> **(7)** In a seller/landlord agreement — a statement on **cooperation with subagents and buyer agents**,
> a disclosure that **a buyer agent, even if compensated by the listing broker or seller/landlord, will
> represent the interests of the buyer/tenant**, and a disclosure of **any potential for the broker to act
> as a dual agent.**
> **(8)** In a buyer/tenant agreement — an explanation that the broker **may be compensated based upon a
> percentage of the purchase price**, the broker's **policies regarding cooperation with listing brokers
> willing to pay buyer's brokers**, the same representation disclosure, and the same dual agency
> disclosure.

### § 35.332 — Exclusive listing agreements

An exclusive listing agreement is either **the exclusive agency of the broker** or the **exclusive
right-to-sell / right-to-lease**, and must contain the **price**, the **commission or other
compensation**, the **duration**, and — in an exclusive right-to-sell or right-to-lease — **a statement
in bold face type that the broker earns a commission on a sale or lease during the listing period *by
whomever made, including the owner*.**

> **(c)** It **may not contain**:
> **(1)** ***A listing period exceeding 1 year.***
> **(2)** ***An automatic renewal clause.***
> **(3)** *A cancellation notice to terminate the agreement at the end of the listing period set forth in
> the agreement.*
> **(4)** *Authority of the broker to **execute a signed agreement of sale or lease for the owner**.*
> **(5)** *An **option by the broker to purchase** the listed property.*
> **(6)** *Authority of the broker to **confess judgment** against the owner or lessor.*

### § 35.333 — Agreements of sale

Eleven required contents, including the **zoning classification of the property**, except where the
property is zoned solely or primarily for single-family dwellings — *"together with a statement that
**the failure of the agreement of sale to contain the zoning classification of the property shall render
the agreement voidable at the option of the buyer** and, if voided, **deposits tendered by the buyer
shall be returned to the buyer without a requirement of court action**."*

Also required: a statement identifying **the capacity in which the broker or licensee is involved and
whether services have been provided to another party**; the **escrow provision**; the **Real Estate
Recovery Fund statement in the words the rule prints**; and *"**A statement that access to a public road
may require issuance of a highway occupancy permit from the Department of Transportation.**"*

> **(a)(11)** For a **time share or campground membership**, a **right-of-cancellation statement in bold
> face type of at least 10 point size immediately above the purchaser's signature line**, in
> substantially the form the rule prints — *"you may cancel this purchase at any time prior to midnight
> of the **fifth day** following the date of this transaction"*, by **certified return receipt mail or
> any other bona fide means of delivery that provides a receipt.**

> **(b)** A **mortgage-contingent** agreement must contain: **the type of mortgage; the mortgage
> principal; the maximum interest rate; the minimum term; the deadline for the buyer to obtain the
> mortgage; and the nature and extent of assistance the broker will render** in obtaining it.

> **(c)** **Printed in bold face if made part of an agreement of sale:** a provision **relieving the
> seller from responsibility for defects** or requiring a release to that effect at settlement; and a
> provision **reserving to the builder-seller the right to change or depart from the building
> specifications.**

### § 35.334 — Statements of estimated cost and return

> **(a)** ***"Before an agreement of sale is executed, the brokers involved in the transaction shall
> provide each party with a written estimate of reasonably foreseeable expenses associated with the sale
> that the party may be expected to pay"*** — including **the broker's commission; mortgage payments and
> financing costs; taxes and assessments; and settlement expenses.**
> **(b)** *"The estimates shall be **as accurate as may be reasonably expected of a person having
> knowledge of, and experience in, real estate sales**."*
> **(c)** The rule then prints an **exemplary Statement of Estimated Costs to Buyer at Settlement**.

### § 35.340 — Comparative market analysis

> *"A comparative market analysis shall contain the following statement **printed conspicuously and
> without change on the first page**:*
>
> ***"This analysis has not been performed in accordance with the Uniform Standards of Professional
> Appraisal Practice which requires valuers to act as unbiased, disinterested third parties with
> impartiality, objectivity and independence and without accommodation of personal interest. It is not to
> be construed as an appraisal and may not be used as such for any purpose."***

## 9. Advertising and solicitation — §§ 35.301 to 35.308

- **§ 35.301(a):** a licensee **may not advertise** the sale or lease of real estate, or solicit
  prospective buyers or tenants, **without the authority of the seller or lessor or their agent.**
- **§ 35.302 — Harassment:** a licensee may not solicit *"by personal contact, telephone, mail or
  advertising — the sale or other disposition of real estate **with such frequency as to amount to clear
  harassment of the owner**."*
- **§ 35.303 — Panic selling:** *"'panic selling' is **frequent efforts to sell residential real estate
  in a particular neighborhood because of fear of declining real estate values when the fear is not based
  on facts relating to the intrinsic value of the real estate itself**."* Proof of **systematic
  solicitation of sales listings** may be **sufficient, but not conclusive**, evidence of an attempt.
- **§ 35.304:** a licensee selling or leasing **his own real estate** shall **disclose that he is a real
  estate licensee in advertisements** — *"This requirement does not apply if the property is listed with
  a real estate company."*
- **§ 35.305(a):** companies shall advertise **only under the business name designated on their
  license.** **(c)** *"An advertisement by an associate broker, salesperson, cemetery associate broker or
  cemetery salesperson shall contain **the business name and telephone number of the employing broker**.
  **The names and telephone numbers shall be of equal size.**"*
- **§ 35.306:** advertisements employing **lotteries, contests, prizes, certificates, gifts or free
  lots** must state a **description**, the **prerequisites**, any **limitation on number**, the **fair
  market value** (in print, in the same size type as the description), and **the odds of winning** (same
  size type, immediately adjacent to the description).
- **§ 35.307:** *"sales volume" or "production"* shall refer **only to closed transactions**, a closed
  transaction being *"either a listing sold or a sale made after a fully executed deed is delivered."*
  A claim about **market position** must **identify the municipality that the market comprises.** A claim
  about **number of offices** shall refer only to offices **issued branch office licenses.**

## 10. Other standards of conduct

- **§ 35.282 — Misleading advice, assurances and representations.**
- **§ 35.283 — Disclosure of interest.**
- **§ 35.284a — Disclosures required by the Real Estate Seller Disclosure Law:** the licensee shall
  **provide sellers with a property disclosure statement that meets or exceeds the disclosures set forth
  in § 35.335a.**
- **§ 35.285 — Disclosure of real estate affiliations.**
- **§ 35.286 — Retention and production of records:** *"A broker or cemetery broker shall **retain records
  pertaining to a real estate transaction for at least 3 years following consummation**"* — **except** four
  categories retained for **6 months**, including *"the acknowledgement portion of the consumer notice
  applicable to the sale or purchase of real estate in § 35.336 **when there is not a real estate
  transaction**."*
- **§ 35.287 — Supervised property management assistance by salespersons:** a salesperson may assist in
  property management **if supervised and controlled by the employing broker**, and *"**may not
  independently negotiate the terms of a lease nor execute a lease on behalf of the lessor**."*
- **§ 35.288 — Duties when selling or leasing own real estate:** the licensee **shall disclose his
  licensed status to a prospective buyer or lessee before the buyer or lessee enters into an agreement of
  sale or lease.**
- **§ 35.290 — Reporting of crimes and disciplinary actions:** a licensee shall notify the Commission of
  **conviction of, or a plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, a felony or misdemeanor within 30 days of
  the verdict or plea**, and of **disciplinary action by another jurisdiction's licensing authority within
  30 days of receiving notice.**
- **§ 35.335a — Seller property disclosure statement:** the rule prints the **minimum disclosures** a
  seller's property disclosure statement must contain, opening with: *"**A seller must disclose to a buyer
  all known material defects about property being sold that are not readily observable.**"* And its
  definition: *"**A material defect is a problem with the property or any portion of it that would have a
  significant adverse impact on the value of the residential real property or that involves an
  unreasonable risk to people on the land. The fact that a structural element, system or subsystem is
  near, at or beyond the end of its normal useful life is not by itself a material defect.**"*

## 11. Broker price opinions — Subchapter J, added 26 December 2025

**New Subchapter J (§§ 35.501–35.503) was added December 26, 2025, effective December 27, 2025, at
55 Pa.B. 8747.** It is current law and it matters to a salesperson.

### § 35.501 — what a BPO must contain

A BPO must carry, **conspicuously and without change**, the same **USPAP disclaimer** § 35.340 requires
on a CMA, and must be **signed manually or electronically** by its preparer and contain nine items,
including **the basis for the conclusion as to price, including applicable market data, cost data or
capitalization computation**; **each assumption or limiting condition**; **each existing or contemplated
interest of the licensee**; **the effective date**; and **the real estate license number of the preparer
and, if applicable, the reviewing broker.**

> **(c)** *"A **fee or valuable consideration** for a broker price opinion, if any, **shall be paid
> directly to the employing broker**."*
> **(d)** ***"A salesperson may not prepare a broker price opinion unless the salesperson has held an
> active license for the 3 years immediately preceding the effective date of the broker price opinion"***
> and has satisfied the § 35.503 education requirements.
> **(e)** *A BPO prepared by a salesperson **shall be signed by the salesperson and reviewed and signed by
> the employing broker or a designated associate broker**.*

### § 35.502 — permitted and prohibited uses

> **(a)** **May be prepared only for use in conjunction with:** *"A property owned by a lender after an
> unsuccessful sale at a foreclosure auction"*; *"A modification of a first or junior mortgage or equity
> line of credit"*; *"A short sale of a property"*; *"An evaluation or monitoring of a portfolio of
> properties."*
>
> **(b)** **May NOT be prepared for use:** *"As the basis to determine the value of a parcel of real
> property for a **mortgage loan origination**, including a first or junior mortgage, refinancing or
> equity line of credit"*; or in connection with **an eminent domain proceeding; a Federal, State or local
> tax appeal; a bankruptcy or insolvency proceeding; an action or proceeding involving divorce or
> equitable distribution of property; any other action or proceeding before a court of record; or the
> distribution of a decedent's estate.**

### § 35.503 — education

A **Commission-approved initial education course** in the preparation of BPOs is required **before
preparing one**, and **before a broker or associate broker may sign one prepared by a salesperson.**
**At least 3 hours** of Commission-approved BPO continuing education is required **during the current or
immediately preceding 2-year license period.** The Commission has approved four broker prelicensure
courses as satisfying the initial requirement: **valuation of residential property; valuation of
income-producing property; basic appraisal principles; basic appraisal procedures.**

## 12. Continuing education — § 35.384

> **(a)** *"a licensee shall complete **14 hours of continuing education** in acceptable courses in a
> **minimum of 2-hour increments**."*
> **(b)** *"during the **first biennial period** that continuing education is required, a **new licensee
> shall complete the Commission-developed 14-hour required course for new licensees**."*

The rule then lists **25 acceptable course subjects** and, in **(d)**, unacceptable ones — *"mechanical
office and business skills; for example, typing, speed writing, preparation of advertising copy,
development of sales promotional devices, word processing, calculator and computer operation and office
management and related internal operations procedures that do not have a bearing on the public
interest."*

**This is recorded here because a prelicensing student needs to know it exists**, not because this
course delivers it.
