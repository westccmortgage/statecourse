# Pennsylvania Practice — Formative Assessment Development Blueprint

**CONFIDENTIAL development record — no production-ready quiz bank exists yet.**

Each of the 45 units requires a versioned scored progress check tied to its measurable objective. Each module also requires a cumulative assessment. A repeated generic stem is prohibited.

## Item-development requirements

- use the unit title, objective, authority and applied activity from `curriculum/course-blueprint.mjs`;
- include agency, form-selection, chronology, source-selection, calculation and transaction-scenario items;
- provide direct feedback only after submission;
- record item/version, response, score, timestamp, attempt and remediation path;
- assign an alternate explanation, different example and parallel scored item after missed mastery;
- flag repeated objective failure for instructor support;
- complete Pennsylvania legal/current-source, SME, editorial, accessibility and duplication review.

Formative checks are a proposed engagement and mastery control. Numeric mastery and attempt rules remain approval gates and do not replace the required proctored final.
