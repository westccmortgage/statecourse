## Controlling specification

- A prelicense course must be graded by a proctored examination under 49 Pa. Code § 35.358(b)(2).
- The examination and answer key must accompany the distance-course application.
- Items must map to the Commission Real Estate Practice outline and approved objectives.
- The official materials reviewed do not state a numeric item count, passing score, attempt limit or alternate-form rule. These remain written Commission-confirmation and approval gates; StateCourse will not invent them.
- Final-examination time is outside the 45 instructional clock hours in this development plan.

## Release gates

1. Complete lesson manuscripts and the objective crosswalk.
2. Confirm exam length, pass score, attempts and form rules with the Commission.
3. Write a unique, blueprint-balanced secure pool with replacement items; no template duplication.
4. Complete Pennsylvania legal/current-source, instructor/SME, bias/accessibility, editorial and psychometric review.
5. Produce a proctored form, key, protocol, integrity log and version manifest.
6. Obtain provider, instructor, course and distance approvals before administration for credit.
