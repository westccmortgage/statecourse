# Pennsylvania Real Estate Practice — Module PA-P-09

## Valuation products, property management, records and risk management

**Course:** Real Estate Practice · **Instructional hours: 5.0** of 45.0 — the final module of the course
**Course version:** 1.0 submission draft
**Regulatory status:** Development. Not approved. No Pennsylvania prelicensing credit is available.

## Learning objectives

1. State what § 35.340 requires on every comparative market analysis.
2. State what a broker price opinion must contain, who may prepare one, and who must sign it.
3. State the four permitted uses of a BPO and the seven situations in which one may not be used.
4. State the BPO education requirements.
5. State what a salesperson may and may not do in property management.
6. State the retention periods in § 35.286 and § 35.328.
7. State the reporting obligations in § 35.290 and the continuing education requirement in § 35.384.

## A. Three different opinions of value, and Pennsylvania regulates all three

| | Who produces it | What Pennsylvania requires |
|---|---|---|
| **Appraisal** | A licensed or certified **appraiser**, under professional standards | Governed by appraiser licensing law, **not read for this project** |
| **Comparative market analysis (CMA)** | A **licensee** | **§ 35.340** — the USPAP disclaimer, **conspicuously and without change, on the first page** |
| **Broker price opinion (BPO)** | A **broker, associate broker or qualified salesperson** | **Subchapter J, §§ 35.501—35.503** — content, signature, permitted uses, and education |

### A.1 § 35.340 — the CMA disclaimer

> *"A comparative market analysis shall contain the following statement **printed conspicuously and without
> change on the first page**:*
>
> ***"This analysis has not been performed in accordance with the Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal
> Practice which requires valuers to act as unbiased, disinterested third parties with impartiality,
> objectivity and independence and without accommodation of personal interest. It is not to be construed as an
> appraisal and may not be used as such for any purpose."***

**Four requirements: the statement, conspicuously, without change, on the first page.** Not paraphrased, not
in a footer, not on page three. A CMA that omits it does not comply, however good the analysis inside it is.

## B. Broker price opinions — Subchapter J

**Subchapter J was added December 26, 2025, effective December 27, 2025, at 55 Pa.B. 8747.** It is recent, and
it is current law.

### B.1 § 35.501 — what a BPO must contain, and who may make one

**The same USPAP disclaimer § 35.340 requires on a CMA** must appear on a BPO, *"displayed conspicuously and
without change."*

A BPO must be **signed manually or electronically by the person who prepared it** and contain **nine** items:

> **(1)** *"An identification of the **intended users and intended uses** of the broker price opinion, if
> known."*
> **(2)** *"A brief **description of the subject property**."*
> **(3)** *"A brief **description of the property interest to be priced**."*
> **(4)** *"The **basis for the conclusion as to the price**, including applicable **market data, cost data or
> capitalization computation**."*
> **(5)** *"**Each assumption or limiting condition.**"*
> **(6)** *"**Each existing or contemplated interest of the licensee** who prepared the broker price
> opinion."*
> **(7)** *"The **effective date** of the broker price opinion."*
> **(8)** *"The **date that the document is signed**."*
> **(9)** *"The **real estate license number of the preparer** and, if applicable, **the reviewing broker or
> associate broker**."*

**Note (7) and (8) are two different dates**, and the rule wants both. The **effective date** is the date as of
which the opinion speaks; the **signature date** is when it was signed. They are often not the same day.

**Note (6) — each existing or contemplated interest.** *Contemplated.* A licensee thinking about making an offer
on the property discloses that.

**Compensation — § 35.501(c):** *"A **fee or valuable consideration** for a broker price opinion, if any, **shall
be paid directly to the employing broker**."* Not to the salesperson.

**Who may prepare one — § 35.501(d):**

> *"**A salesperson may not prepare a broker price opinion unless the salesperson has held an active license for
> the 3 years immediately preceding the effective date of the broker price opinion** and has satisfied the
> educational requirements in § 35.503."*

**Three years of active licensure, measured to the BPO's effective date** — and the education as well. **Both
conditions.**

**And § 35.501(e):** a BPO prepared by a salesperson *"**shall be signed by the salesperson and reviewed and
signed by the employing broker or a designated associate broker**."*

**So a salesperson's BPO carries two signatures**, and the second one is a review, not a formality.

### B.2 § 35.502 — the four permitted uses

> **(a)** *A BPO **may be prepared ... only for use in conjunction with** any of the following:*
> **(1)** *"A property **owned by a lender after an unsuccessful sale at a foreclosure auction**."*
> **(2)** *"A **modification of a first or junior mortgage or equity line of credit**."*
> **(3)** *"A **short sale** of a property."*
> **(4)** *"An **evaluation or monitoring of a portfolio of properties**."*

**"Only."** Four uses, and the list is closed.

### B.3 § 35.502(b) — where a BPO may NOT be used

> **(1)** *"**As the basis to determine the value of a parcel of real property for a mortgage loan
> origination**, including a first or junior mortgage, refinancing or equity line of credit."*
> **(2)** *In connection with:* **an eminent domain proceeding**; **a Federal, State or local tax appeal**; **a
> bankruptcy or insolvency proceeding**; **an action or proceeding involving divorce or equitable distribution
> of property**; ***"any other action or proceeding before a court of record"***; **the distribution of a
> decedent's estate.**

### B.4 Reading (a)(2) against (b)(1) — the distinction that matters

**A loan MODIFICATION is a permitted use. A loan ORIGINATION is prohibited.**

Both involve a lender and a mortgage, and a licensee who does not hold the distinction will get it wrong. The
line is whether **new credit is being extended on the strength of the value.** Modifying an existing loan
adjusts terms on a debt that already exists; originating one puts new money out against the property. **The
prohibition names refinancing and equity lines of credit expressly**, so a licensee cannot reason their way
around it.

**And (b)(2)'s catch-all is very wide:** *"any other action or proceeding **before a court of record**."*
Litigation of any kind takes a BPO off the table. Divorce, estate distribution, tax appeals and bankruptcy are
named because they are where the requests actually come from.

### B.5 § 35.503 — the education

- **Initial education before preparing one.** A **Commission-approved initial education course** in the
  preparation of BPOs.
- **Initial education before signing one.** A broker or associate broker **may not sign** a salesperson's BPO
  unless they too have completed it.
- **Continuing education.** *"at least **3 hours** of Commission-approved continuing education in broker price
  opinion topics **during the current or immediately preceding 2-year license period**."*
- **Records.** The licensee **shall retain the transcript or certificate of instruction** for all required BPO
  courses and **provide a copy to the Commission upon request.**

**Four broker prelicensure courses are Commission-approved for the initial requirement:** *"valuation of
residential property; valuation of income-producing property; basic appraisal principles; basic appraisal
procedures."*

**Other courses qualify** if **3 or more hours** in length and covering four stated topic areas — valuation
definitions including **BPO, CMA and AVM**; the **permitted and non-permitted uses**; the **qualifications and
limitations** on preparation, signature and fees; and the **basics of preparation**, including sources,
analysis, and **liability issues arising from broker price opinions.**

## C. Property management — § 35.287 and § 35.321(b)

> **§ 35.287:** *"A salesperson **may assist in property management if the salesperson's work is supervised and
> controlled by the employing broker**. The salesperson **may not independently negotiate the terms of a lease
> nor execute a lease on behalf of the lessor**."*

**Two prohibitions, both containing a word that carries the meaning.**

**"Independently negotiate."** A salesperson may participate in a negotiation the broker supervises and
controls. What they may not do is negotiate terms **on their own authority.**

**"Execute a lease on behalf of the lessor."** Signing for the owner is the broker's, not the salesperson's.

**And the money — § 35.321(b):** rents are **not** escrow, but they are **not the broker's** either. They go
into a **rental management account separate from both the escrow account and the general business account.**
**Security deposits are different again**: they **are** escrow, and under **§ 35.321(e)** the duty to pay over
**arises when the tenancy ends.**

**Three accounts.** Escrow, rental management, general business. Module PA-P-06 has the detail.

**Pennsylvania's Landlord and Tenant Act was not read for this project.** **No security deposit limit, notice
period, eviction procedure or lessor interest entitlement is stated anywhere in this build.**

## D. Records — the periods, in one place

| Record | Period | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Records pertaining to a real estate transaction | **3 years following consummation** | § 35.286(a) |
| Acknowledgement of the § 35.336 consumer notice **where there is no transaction** | **6 months** | § 35.286(a)(1) |
| Acknowledgement of the § 35.338 time-share notice | **6 months** | § 35.286(a)(2) |
| Acknowledgement of the § 35.336 / § 35.337 lease notices | **6 months** | § 35.286(a)(3) |
| Written disclosure establishing a **subagent or transaction licensee** relationship | **6 months** | § 35.286(a)(4) |
| **Escrow records** — three names and three dates | Kept and **produced on written request or office inspection** | § 35.328 |
| Education provider **continuing education** records | **4 years** | § 35.360(e)(1) |
| Education provider **all other** records | **10 years** | § 35.360(e)(2) |
| A licensee's own **BPO course transcripts** | Retained; **copy to the Commission on request** | § 35.503(d) |

**Production is not optional.** § 35.286(b) and § 35.328 both require production **upon written request** or
**pursuant to an office inspection under § 35.246**, and § 35.325(d) requires the broker to hand the Commission
**a letter authorising the bank to release the escrow account's records.**

## E. Reporting and continuing obligations

### E.1 § 35.290 — what you must tell the Commission

> **(a)** *"A licensee shall notify the Commission of being **convicted of, or pleading guilty or nolo
> contendere to, a felony or misdemeanor, within 30 days of the verdict or plea**."*
> **(b)** *"A licensee shall notify the Commission of **disciplinary action taken against him by the real estate
> licensing authority of another jurisdiction within 30 days of receiving notice** of the disciplinary
> action."*

**Both 30 days.** Both self-reporting. **(a)'s trigger is the verdict or the plea** — not the sentence, not the
end of appeals — and a **nolo contendere** plea counts.

### E.2 § 35.291 — posting a suspension

A **broker** whose licence is suspended **returns the licence** and **posts the Commission's notice prominently
on or near the public entrance** of the main office and every branch. *"Failure to post the notice constitutes
grounds for further disciplinary action."*

### E.3 § 35.384 — continuing education, once you are licensed

> **(a)** *"a licensee shall complete **14 hours of continuing education** in acceptable courses in a **minimum
> of 2-hour increments**."*
> **(b)** *"during the **first biennial period** that continuing education is required, a **new licensee shall
> complete the Commission-developed 14-hour required course for new licensees** in satisfaction of the
> continuing education requirement."*

**A new licensee does not choose their first 14 hours.** The Commission has developed the course and it is
required.

The rule then lists **25 acceptable subjects** — from real estate ethics and laws affecting real estate through
agency relationships, landlord-tenant law, environmental issues, antitrust, and use of technology in delivering
real estate services — and, in **(d)**, what is **not** acceptable: *"mechanical office and business skills; for
example, typing, speed writing, preparation of advertising copy, development of sales promotional devices, word
processing, calculator and computer operation and office management and related internal operations procedures
**that do not have a bearing on the public interest**."*

**The test in (d) is the public interest.** Courses that make you a better salesperson are not thereby
continuing education; courses that make you a safer one are.

## F. Risk management — the whole of both courses in one page

Nine habits, each traceable to a rule you have now read.

1. **Disclose the relationship at the initial interview**, in the right form, and **note a refusal to sign.**
   *(§ 35.284)*
2. **Put it in writing** — every commitment where compensation may be owed, with all eight § 35.331 contents.
   *(§ 35.281, § 35.331)*
3. **Present every offer**, in writing, in a reasonably practicable time. *(§ 35.292(a)(3))*
4. **Deposit money belonging to another by the end of the next business day**, into an account that is insured,
   names you as trustee, allows withdrawal without notice, and is used exclusively for escrow. *(§ 35.324,
   § 35.325)*
5. **Never mix and never use.** *(§ 35.326)*
6. **On a dispute, hold.** *(§ 35.327)*
7. **Reveal known material defects** — the exception written inside the confidentiality duty itself.
   *(§ 35.312(a)(2), § 35.314(b)(3))*
8. **Route what is beyond you**, because it is a duty and not a courtesy. *(§ 35.292(a)(8))*
9. **Keep the record** — three years, six months, or the escrow record's three names and three dates.
   *(§ 35.286, § 35.328)*

**And the one that holds the rest together — § 35.292(a)(2): deal honestly and in good faith.** Owed to
everyone. Not waivable. Every violation in these two courses is, underneath, a failure of that sentence.
