# Pennsylvania Real Estate Fundamentals 30h v1.0 — Course Syllabus

**Regulator:** Pennsylvania Real Estate Commission  
**Hours:** 30  
**Status:** Development; not approved; enrollment disabled  
**Instructor:** Unassigned; state approval/qualification and course assignment required

## Learning outcomes

Students completing an approved release will explain the prescribed subjects, apply Pennsylvania law/forms to entry-level facts, perform required calculations, recognize professional-scope limits, and produce an auditable transaction response.

## Delivery and completion

Distance delivery method approved by ARELLO/IDECC or another Commission-accepted body; prelicense final proctored. The exact course configuration controls active time, sequence, units, interactions, assessment, remediation, identity, inactivity, final eligibility, completion period, and proctoring. Payment never determines completion or credit.

## Assessment

Formative checks and module quizzes trigger objective-specific remediation. Draft final configuration: 1 form(s), 75 items/form, passing standard: Provider proposal withheld until regulator-approved standard is recorded. Proctored prelicense final under 49 Pa. Code § 35.358. Any item count not prescribed by the regulator is a StateCourse submission proposal.
