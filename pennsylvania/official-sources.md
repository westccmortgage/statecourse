## Verified controls

- salesperson education is 75 hours: Fundamentals 30h plus Practice 45h;
- one credit equals 15 instructional hours; an instructional hour is at least 50 minutes;
- required-course instruction must conform to the Commission-developed outline;
- distance delivery requires ARELLO or another Commission-approved comparable certifier;
- every prelicense course is graded by a proctored examination;
- an official director-signed prelicense transcript is due within 30 days after the course is taught;
- prelicense attendance and scholastic records are retained 10 years;
- annual provider renewal is due no later than October 31; current form lists $375 provider, $15 instructor and $15 per-course renewal fees.

## Filing-day control

Forms and fees are rechecked on filing day. Each source snapshot receives retrieval date, checksum, reviewer and impact decision in the regulatory-document register.
