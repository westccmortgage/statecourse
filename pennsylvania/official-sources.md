# Pennsylvania Official Source Register

Last verified: **2026-08-16**. Official regulator, legislature/rule publisher, and current regulator forms control. Missing or ambiguous requirements remain filing questions; another state's rule is never substituted.

1. [Pennsylvania Commission — Education Information](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dos/department-and-offices/bpoa/boards-commissions/real-estate-commission/education-information) — Approved-provider requirement, forms, and separate 30/45-hour course outlines.
2. [Initial Education Provider Application (04/2024)](https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/dos/department-and-offices/bpoa/real-estate/App%20-%20Approval%20Initial%20Provider.pdf) — Provider, director, office, bond, inspection, agreement, refund, and fee requirements.
3. [Education Provider Owner Approval](https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/dos/department-and-offices/bpoa/real-estate/App%20-%20Approval%20Owner.pdf) — Owner approval, character, résumé, references, background check, and fee.
4. [Education Provider Director Approval](https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/dos/department-and-offices/bpoa/real-estate/App%20-%20Approval%20Director.pdf) — Director qualifications, résumé, background check, and fee.
5. [Education Provider Surety Bond](https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/dos/department-and-offices/bpoa/real-estate/Document%20-%20Surety%20Bond.pdf) — $10,000 bond instrument.
6. [Instructor Approval Application](https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/dos/department-and-offices/bpoa/real-estate/App%20-%20Approval%20Instructor.pdf) — Instructor qualification certification and fee.
7. [Distance Education Course Application](https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/dos/department-and-offices/bpoa/real-estate/App%20-%20Approval%20Course%20Distance%20Ed.pdf) — Timed outline, materials, final/key, ARELLO/IDECC delivery approval, reviewer access, and proctored prelicense final.
8. [Fundamentals Commission Outline](https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/dos/department-and-offices/bpoa/real-estate/Outline%20-%20RE%20Fundamentals.pdf) — Controlling 30-hour Fundamentals outline.
9. [Practice Commission Outline](https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/dos/department-and-offices/bpoa/real-estate/Outline%20-%20RE%20Practice.pdf) — Controlling 45-hour Practice outline.
10. [49 Pa. Code Chapter 35](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=%2Fsecure%2Fpacode%2Fdata%2F049%2Fchapter35%2Fchap35toc.html) — Provider, course, distance, clock hour, proctoring, certificate/transcript, and record rules.

## Release control

All sources are checked again on filing day, on regulator correction, before each controlled release, and before enrollment after a legal/form change. A snapshot, checksum, effective date, reviewer, and impact decision belong in the regulatory-document record.
