Last verified: 2026-08-16. Sources are rechecked at submission, approval, every controlled release, and before enrollment after a law/rule/form change.

1. [OREC Code and Rules (Nov. 1, 2025)](https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/orec/documents/meetings/Nov%201%202025%20Oklahoma%20Real%20Estate%20Commission%20Code%20and%20Rules%20Book.pdf) — Current course content, applications, instructor, final, distance, records, and renewal rules.

3. [Become an Instructor](https://oklahoma.gov/orec/education/become-an-instructor.html) — Current public instructor qualification summary and 80% state/national exam requirement.
4. [OREC Licensing FAQ](https://www.oklahoma.gov/orec/licensing/application-and-exam-faqs.html) — 90-hour course and three-year validity for licensing application.

## Source-control rule

- Official regulator, legislature/secretary-of-state rule text, and current regulator forms control.
- Third-party summaries may aid navigation but cannot create a requirement.
- A missing or ambiguous requirement becomes a submission question or release gate; it is not inferred from another state.
