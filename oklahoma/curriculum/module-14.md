## Overview

The regulatory structure a licensee practises inside — the Commission, the Code, the Rules — and the
standards of conduct enforced through it. The module works through the sources of Oklahoma real estate
law, the licensing path in detail, § 858-312's twenty-eight grounds for discipline, the complaint and
investigation process, and the federal regimes that overlay all of it.

## Learning objectives

1. Name the four layers of Oklahoma real estate law and say which controls.
2. Describe the Commission and the licence categories.
3. State the licensing requirements of § 858-302 including the three-year education clock.
4. State the state examination passing score and the validity of results and applications.
5. Distinguish the three Oklahoma passing percentages and their examinations.
6. Describe the postlicense requirement and the two-part series.
7. Name the § 858-312 grounds that most commonly arise and explain each.
8. Describe how a complaint proceeds and what a licensee does when one arrives.
9. State the fifteen-day written response requirement and the thirty-day felony notification.
10. State the penalties available under § 858-401.
11. Describe the federal regimes that overlay Oklahoma practice.
12. Describe antitrust exposure and the practical rule that prevents it.
13. Describe the do-not-call and email marketing regimes at the level of principle.

## Hour 78 — The sources of Oklahoma law, and the Commission

### 78.1 Four layers, and which controls

**Statute** — **Title 59 of the Oklahoma Statutes, the Oklahoma Real Estate License Code**, enacted by the
Legislature. The top of the hierarchy.

**Rule** — **Oklahoma Administrative Code Title 605**, promulgated by the Oklahoma Real Estate Commission.
**A rule cannot exceed the statute that authorises it.**

**Commission forms, orders and determinations** — course approval letters, prescribed forms, the deed
theft notification form once its rules issue. **These are operative documents**, and using a superseded
version of a prescribed form is a real problem.

**Case law** — the courts' interpretation of all of the above.

**Where to find them:** the Commission publishes the *Oklahoma Real Estate License Code and Rules* as a
single volume, which is what this course was built from. **Read the section, not a summary of it.** A
summary — including this course — is a navigation aid; the operative text is the operative text.

### 78.2 The Commission

The **Oklahoma Real Estate Commission** consists of **seven members**. It is, by statute, **the sole
governmental entity — state, county or municipal — with authority to regulate and issue real estate
licences in Oklahoma.**

Composition, as read: **five** members licensed real estate brokers with at least **five years' active
experience** as brokers before appointment and engaged full time in the brokerage business; **one** lay
person not in the real estate business; and **one** active representative of a school of real estate
located within Oklahoma. Members must be U.S. citizens and Oklahoma residents for at least **three years**
before appointment.

The Commission licenses, approves education providers, courses and instructors, investigates complaints
and imposes sanctions.

### 78.3 The licence categories

**Provisional sales associate** — the entry licence, obtained after the 90-hour Basic Course and the
examination. **Sales associate** — obtained after the provisional licence and the postlicense education
§ 858-302 requires. **Broker associate** and **broker** — requiring the 90-hour **Advanced Course**, active
licensure experience, and for a broker, documentation of transactions and the Broker in Charge course.

## Hour 79 — Licensing

### 79.1 The requirements — 59 O.S. § 858-302

- **Eighteen years of age or older.**
- **Evidence of successful completion of ninety (90) clock hours** of basic real estate instruction in a
  Commission-approved course of study.
- **Application** on Commission forms with the fee and all required information and documents.
- **Appear in person for an examination** in the form and on the subjects the Commission prescribes.
- On passing, final approval and payment of the licence fee **plus the Oklahoma Real Estate Education and
  Recovery Fund fee**, the licence issues.

**605:10-3-2** adds what the application contains, including **evidence of successful completion of the
course requirement**, a **sworn statement** as to accuracy, documentation verifying citizenship or
qualified alien status, and **submission to a national criminal history record check** — which is valid
for **six months** from issuance.

### 79.2 The three-year education clock

**§ 858-302(A):**

> The education required in this subsection **shall only be valid for a period of three (3) years from
> the date the school certified successful completion of the course**; thereafter, the applicant shall be
> required to successfully complete an additional ninety (90) clock hours or its equivalent in basic real
> estate instruction.

**Three years, from the date the SCHOOL CERTIFIED completion.** Not from the day you finished the last
module; not from your application date. **Plan around it from the day you enrol.**

### 79.3 The examination, and three percentages

**605:10-3-5(f):**

> A score of **seventy-five percent (75%)** or more shall be considered a passing grade on the **broker**
> examination. A score of **seventy percent (70%)** or more shall be considered a passing grade on the
> **provisional sales associate/sales associate** examination.

**605:10-3-5(g)** — passing examination results are **valid for one (1) year.**
**605:10-3-5(e)** — the original examination application is **valid for one (1) year** from filing.
**605:10-3-3(a)(1)** — an approved licence application is **valid for one (1) year.**

**And the three percentages, one final time:**

| Percentage | Examination | Rule |
|---:|---|---|
| **75%** | **this course's** end-of-course final examination | 605:10-5-3(f) |
| **70%** | the **state licensing** examination for provisional sales associate / sales associate | 605:10-3-5(f) |
| **80%** | an **instructor** applicant's qualifying examination | 605:10-5-1(f) |

**A document that names a percentage without naming its examination has misstated the regulator.**

**605:10-3-5(d)** — an applicant who fails may **review their missed questions at the end of the
examination** and may challenge the validity of a question. **No notes, pencils or electronic devices**
during the review, and nothing leaves the examination area.

### 79.4 Part I of II

**605:10-5-3(j)** requires each entity to promote the Basic Course as **Part I of a two part series**, with
the **Provisional Postlicense Course of Real Estate as Part II.** Section 858-302 requires that
postlicense education **after the provisional licence issues**, before a full sales associate licence.

**Ninety hours is not the whole of your education**, and any provider that lets you think otherwise has
misled you.

### 79.5 What the school must tell you before you enrol

**605:10-5-1(m)** requires the notice about **felony crimes referenced in 59 O.S. §§ 858-301.1 and
858-4000.1**, including that **the Commission will allow the applicant to seek preapproval prior to
enrolling in a pre-license course** — and the notice about eligibility for applicants who are not
permanent U.S. citizens.

**Use the preapproval route if it may apply to you**, before you pay for ninety hours. **This course states
no offence, period or eligibility rule from those sections**, because they were not read from primary
text for this project.

## Hour 80 — § 858-312: the grounds for discipline

### 80.1 The provision

The Commission **may upon its own motion, and SHALL upon written complaint filed by any person**,
investigate the business transactions of any licensee, and may impose sanctions as provided in § 858-208
on a showing of cause. **Twenty-eight grounds are listed.**

### 80.2 The ones that arise most, grouped

**Honesty and representations**

- **(2)** substantial misrepresentations or false promises in the conduct of business, or through
  licensees, or **advertising**, intended to influence, persuade or induce.
- **(8)** **any other conduct which constitutes untrustworthy, improper, fraudulent, or dishonest
  dealings** — the catch-all, and it is broad.
- **(10)** **guaranteeing future profits** which may result from the resale of real estate.
- **(28)** soliciting, selling or offering real property **by means of a deceptive practice.**
- **(25)** being **unworthy or incompetent** to act as a broker in a manner as to endanger the public.

**Money**

- **(6)** failing within a reasonable time to **account for or remit** monies, documents or other property
  belonging to others.
- **(16)** **commingling** the money or property of others with the licensee's own, unless held in an
  escrow account containing **only** the money or property of others.
- **(22)** offering, loaning, paying, **or making to appear to have been paid**, a down payment or earnest
  money deposit for a purchaser or seller.
- **(4)** an associate accepting compensation **except from the broker with whom the associate is
  associated.**
- **(21)** paying any part of a fee or commission **to any person not licensed**; and **(14)** paying
  compensation to a person performing licensee services without a licence.
- **(18)** a broker's **failure, after receipt of a commission, to account to and pay a licensee their
  earned share.**

**Advertising and property**

- **(20)** advertising to buy, sell, rent or exchange real estate **without disclosing in writing that the
  licensee is a real estate licensee.**
- **(11)** advertising, offering, **or placing a sign** on real estate **without the owner's consent.**
- **(12)** using prizes, money, gifts or other valuable consideration **as an inducement to secure
  customers or clients to purchase specific property** — with general marketing use permitted **provided
  it is not contingent or limited to individuals making an offer or purchasing a specific property.**
- **(24)** **recording a contract, assignment, affidavit or other writing purporting to affect title or
  encumber property for the purpose of collecting a commission or coercing payment.** Recording a judgment
  is not prohibited.

**Compliance and statute**

- **(3)** **failing to comply with §§ 858-351 through 858-363** — the broker-relationships duties.
- **(23)** **violation of the Residential Property Condition Disclosure Act.**
- **(17)** conviction of violating **the federal fair housing laws, 42 U.S.C. § 3601 et seq.**
- **(13)** appraisal compensation **contingent on a predetermined value**, or an appraisal report on
  property in which the licensee has an **undisclosed interest**; compliance with the Oklahoma real estate
  appraisal law; and **written disclosure to the employer of whether the person is licensed or certified
  by the Oklahoma Real Estate Appraiser Board.**
- **(9)** disregarding or violating **any provision** of the Code or the Commission's rules.

**Character and reporting**

- **(1)** a materially false or fraudulent statement in a **licence application.**
- **(15)** unworthiness, or conviction of or plea to **a crime involving moral turpitude.**
- **(19)** conviction of or plea to **forgery, embezzlement, obtaining money under false pretenses,
  extortion, conspiracy to defraud, fraud**, or similar offences.
- **(5)** representing a broker **other than the one you are associated with**, without that broker's
  express knowledge and consent.
- **(26)** **failure to provide an adequate written response to the Commission within fifteen (15) days**
  after receiving written notice of a complaint or investigation.
- **(27)** **failure to notify the Commission** of a felony conviction or plea **within thirty (30) days**
  of the plea and again within thirty days of judgment and sentencing — § 858-301.2.

### 80.3 What the list is actually telling you

Read the twenty-eight together and a pattern appears. **Almost none of them require dishonest intent.**
Failing to account in a reasonable time. Commingling. Advertising without disclosing licensee status.
Recording something to collect a fee. Not responding to the Commission in fifteen days.

**These are process failures, and process failures are what discipline is mostly made of.** The
professional habits that prevent them are the ones this course has repeated: write it down, deliver it to
the broker, disclose in writing, calendar the deadline, respond to the regulator on time.

## Hour 81 — Complaints, investigation, and penalties

### 81.1 How a matter starts

The Commission **may on its own motion**, and **SHALL upon written complaint filed by any person**,
investigate the business transactions of a licensee. It may hold hearings; a hearing examiner may be
appointed; and appeal procedures are set out in 605:10-1-3.

**"Shall upon written complaint" means any person can start one and the Commission must look.**

### 81.2 What a licensee does when a complaint arrives

1. **Tell your broker immediately.** Not eventually.
2. **Preserve everything.** Every file, email, text and note. **Altering or destroying a record after a
   complaint converts a defensible matter into an indefensible one** — and § 858-312(8), untrustworthy or
   dishonest dealings, is broad enough to reach it.
3. **Get counsel** before responding.
4. **Respond within fifteen days.** § 858-312(26) makes **failure to provide an adequate written response
   to the Commission within fifteen (15) days** a ground in itself. **You can be disciplined for ignoring
   the letter even if the underlying complaint was groundless.**

### 81.3 The penalties — § 858-401

**Unlicensed activity.** Any **unlicensed** person who willingly, knowingly or negligently violates the
Code is, on conviction, guilty of a **misdemeanour**, punishable by a fine of **not more than $5,000**,
imprisonment in the county jail for **not more than six (6) months**, or both.

**Administrative.** The Commission may, after notice and hearing and on finding a violation, **impose a
fine of not more than $5,000 or the amount of the commission earned, whichever is greater, for each
violation** involving unlicensed activity; may seek an **injunction**; and may impose administrative fines
**enforceable in the district courts.**

**§ 858-307.3** — a person whose licence is revoked **shall not file an application for reissuance within
five (5) years** of the effective date of revocation.

### 81.4 Errors, and the honest response

Every licensee will eventually make a mistake — a missed deadline, a transposed figure, a disclosure sent
late.

**The professional response is the same every time: tell your broker, tell the affected party, correct it
in writing, and document what happened.**

The alternative — quietly fixing it and hoping — converts an error into a concealment, and concealment is
what turns a survivable problem into a licence problem under § 858-312(8).

## Hour 82 — Federal overlays

### 82.1 Antitrust

Four practices, and each is criminal exposure rather than merely a licence problem:

**Price fixing** — any agreement or understanding among competing firms about **compensation rates or
fees**. Compensation is negotiable between the broker and the party, and **any** conversation with a
competitor about what "we" charge is a conversation to leave.

**Market allocation** — agreeing with competitors to divide territories, property types or customer
groups.

**Group boycott** — agreeing with competitors to refuse to deal with a particular broker or business
model.

**Tying** — conditioning the sale of one product or service on the purchase of another.

**The practical rule: never discuss your firm's compensation structure with a competitor, in any setting,
however casual. Leave the conversation, and be seen to leave it.**

### 82.2 Fair housing, ECOA, ADA

Module OK-13 in full, and the Oklahoma consequence at § 858-312(17) and § 858-353(A)(6).

### 82.3 RESPA and TRID

Module OK-12 Hour 71. **Section 8 kickbacks** and **section 9's title-company prohibition**, the
**affiliated business arrangement** disclosure, and the **three-business-day** Closing Disclosure rule.

Note again the overlap with § 858-312(21) and (14): **a referral arrangement can breach federal law and
Oklahoma licence law with the same act.**

### 82.4 Telephone, text and email marketing

The **Telephone Consumer Protection Act** and the **National Do Not Call Registry** govern telemarketing
calls, and the rules on **autodialled calls and text messages are stricter than most licensees assume.**
Firms must maintain an **internal do-not-call list** and honour requests.

**CAN-SPAM** governs commercial email: accurate headers and subject lines, a physical postal address, and
a **working opt-out that is honoured promptly.**

**This course states no registration period, exemption or penalty amount**, because neither the TCPA nor
the FCC rules nor CAN-SPAM was read from primary text for this project. **Prospecting practice is set by
your firm's written policy, drafted with counsel, and followed.**

### 82.5 Environmental and disclosure regimes

Module OK-15 in full — including the federal **lead-based paint** regime for **target housing built before
1978**, which is one of the very few places a licensee is a **participant** in a disclosure rather than a
bystander to it.

### 82.6 The professional standard, in one paragraph

**Be honest with everyone, competent in what you take on, and prompt in writing.** Disclose what the
statute requires, to everyone entitled to it. Account for other people's money the day it reaches you.
Route what is not yours — legal, tax, appraisal, engineering, environmental, zoning, boundary. Calendar
every deadline the day it is created. And when you get something wrong, say so, in writing, immediately.

**Every one of the twenty-eight grounds is a failure of one of those six habits.**
