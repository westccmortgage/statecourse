## Hour 88 — Leasehold estates and leases

### 88.1 The four leasehold estates

**Estate for years.** A definite beginning and a definite end. **It terminates on its own terms and
requires no notice.**

**Periodic tenancy, or estate from period to period.** Renews automatically — month to month, year to year
— until proper notice by either party.

**Tenancy at will.** Possession with the landlord's consent for an indefinite period, terminable by either
party.

**Tenancy at sufferance.** A **holdover** — the tenant remains after the right to possess has ended. Not a
tenancy the landlord agreed to.

**This course states no Oklahoma notice period, security deposit rule, remedy or eviction procedure**,
because Oklahoma's landlord-tenant provisions were not read from primary text for this project. **Which
estate exists** is what a licensee must be able to identify; **what notice ends it, and every remedy, is
routed to counsel and to the lease.**

### 88.2 A lease is two things at once

A **conveyance** of a possessory interest for a term, and a **contract** of covenants between the parties.

That dual nature is why a lease dispute can be framed either as a breach of contract or as an interference
with possession, and why the **covenant of quiet enjoyment** matters as much as any written clause.

### 88.3 Standard provisions

Parties; premises; term; rent and when and where it is paid; **security deposit** and its handling; use
and occupancy; maintenance and repair, and which side does what; utilities; alterations; **assignment and
subletting**; access by the landlord; insurance; default and remedies; and the condition of the premises
at surrender.

**Trade fixtures**, from Module OK-01 Hour 2: a tenant's business installations remain the tenant's and
**must be removed before the term ends**, after which they generally pass to the landlord by **accession.**

### 88.4 Assignment and sublease

**Assignment** transfers the tenant's **entire** remaining interest. **Sublease** transfers **less than
all** of it, leaving the original tenant as a landlord to the subtenant.

**In both cases the original tenant generally remains liable unless expressly released** — which tenants
routinely assume they are not. Whether either is permitted at all is a question for the lease's own terms.

### 88.5 Lease types by rent structure

**Gross** — the landlord pays operating expenses. **Net**, in its several degrees — the tenant pays some or
all of them. **Percentage** — common in retail; rent includes a share of sales. **Ground** — a long-term
lease of land on which the tenant builds.

## Hour 89 — Property management

### 89.1 The manager's position

The manager provides **brokerage services** to the owner as a **party** under §§ 858-351 to 858-363. **All
seven § 858-353(A) duties apply**, and the two § 858-353(B) duties apply to the owner as the party served.

**And every fair housing obligation runs to applicants and tenants at the same time** — Module OK-13, and
§ 858-353(A)(6), and § 858-312(17).

### 89.2 Functions

Producing the owner's intended return while preserving the asset:

- **Marketing and leasing.**
- **Tenant screening**, applied **identically to every applicant** — written criteria, applied uniformly,
  documented. Fair housing exposure in management is concentrated here.
- **Rent collection**, and enforcement under the lease.
- **Maintenance**, routine and capital.
- **Budgeting and reporting** to the owner.
- **Regulatory compliance.**
- **Risk management** — insurance, habitability, security, documentation.

### 89.3 The management agreement

It is a **brokerage agreement**, so **§ 858-355.1(A)** applies: it **shall incorporate as material terms
the § 858-353 duties.** And **§ 858-363**: it is entered **in the broker's name**, not the associate's.

It must settle: the **scope of authority**; the **compensation** — and **§ 858-353(A)(7)**'s disclosure in
writing, with a term **not exceeding one year** and **defaulting to sixty days** if unstated; the
**term**; **reporting and accounting** obligations; the owner's **approval thresholds for expenditure**;
and the **handling of trust funds.**

**An unwritten scope of authority is how a manager ends up having spent an owner's money on something the
owner did not authorise.**

### 89.4 The operating statement

**Potential gross income**, less **vacancy and collection loss** → **effective gross income**, less
**operating expenses** → **net operating income.**

*Worked.* Potential gross income $184,000. Vacancy and collection loss 6% = $11,040. **Effective gross
income $172,960.** Operating expenses $71,000. **NOI = $101,960.**

**Debt service is NOT an operating expense.** With annual debt service of $76,400: 101,960 − 76,400 =
**$25,560 before-tax cash flow** — a different figure answering a different question.

**And check for a replacement reserve line.** No reserve understates expenses, overstates NOI, and — since
NOI ÷ cap rate produces value — overstates value directly.

## Hour 90 — Trust funds, duties, and what you carry forward

### 90.1 Trust funds — where managers lose licences

**Security deposits and prepaid rent are other people's money from the moment they arrive.**

Three Oklahoma provisions:

**§ 858-353(A)(3)** — a duty to **all parties**, mandatory and unwaivable: **timely account for all money
and property received.**

**§ 858-312(6)** — **failing, within a reasonable time, to account for or to remit any monies, documents,
or other property coming into possession of the licensee which belong to others.**

**§ 858-312(16)** — **commingling** the money or property of others with the licensee's own, **unless the
money or property of others is received by the licensee and held in an escrow account that contains only
money or property of others.**

**Read (16) carefully: the exception requires an account containing ONLY other people's money.** An
operating account with careful internal bookkeeping does not satisfy it.

**An owner who says "just put the deposits in the operating account, it's simpler" is asking you to commit
the conduct § 858-312(16) names.** The answer is no, and it does not become yes because the owner is the
client or because a spreadsheet tracks each balance.

**This course states no Oklahoma trust or escrow account threshold, deadline or procedure**, because those
provisions were not read from primary text for this project. **Read them and your broker's written
procedures before you touch a deposit.**

**Trust account failures are the fastest route out of this profession, and they are almost never theft.
They are convenience, helpfulness and sloppiness — which look identical on an audit.**

### 90.2 A discriminatory instruction from an owner

*No children on the upper floor. Don't rent to anyone from that neighbourhood.*

**Refuse. Say plainly that you cannot follow it. Withdraw from the engagement if it stands.**

Not compliance: following it while noting an objection; applying it informally; asking the owner to put it
in writing. **A written discriminatory instruction is evidence against you.**

Same answer as Module OK-13 Hour 74.5, and the same every time it appears.

### 90.3 What you carry out of ninety hours

**A working knowledge of the transaction** — property and the interests in it, title and its defects,
contracts and their performance, financing and its arithmetic, valuation, closing, leasing.

**The Oklahoma statutory framework**, and specifically:

- **There is no agency.** § 858-360(A) abrogated the fiduciary duties; § 858-360(B) forbids agency,
  subagency and dual agency.
- **Seven duties to all parties**, mandatory and unwaivable — § 858-353(A).
- **Two more to the party you serve** — § 858-353(B).
- **Four confidential items, and only four** — § 858-353(A)(4).
- **Written disclosure before signing, confirmed in or attached to the contract** — §§ 858-355.1(C) and
  858-356.
- **Compensation in writing before the contract's effective date**, capped at one year, defaulting to
  sixty days — § 858-353(A)(7).
- **Twenty-eight grounds** for discipline — § 858-312 — of which most are process failures.

**The federal fair housing framework**, and the practices it forbids including the ones nobody intends.

**The arithmetic.**

**And most durably: a reliable sense of which questions are yours and which are routed** — to your broker,
to counsel, to the lender, to the title company, to the municipality, to a qualified professional, to the
Commission.

### 90.4 What you do not carry

**The right to give legal, tax, engineering, environmental or appraisal advice.**

**The specifics of everything this course could not read from primary text.** Each is named in
`oklahoma/regulatory/primary-text-readings.md`, and the list is not short: **the Residential Property
Condition Disclosure Act**; **the Oklahoma Real Estate Contract Form Committee's promulgated forms**;
59 O.S. §§ 858-208, 858-301.1 and 858-4000.1; the Commission's rules implementing the **deed theft
notification** and its form; the **OREC syllabus**; and Oklahoma's trust account, foreclosure,
landlord-tenant, probate, lien, tax, subdivision, mineral and environmental provisions.

**Each must be read in current text before it is relied on.** 605:10-5-3(k) makes the same point from the
school's side: all materials distributed to students **must be current and up-to-date with the License Code
and Rules and state or federal laws.**

**And a licence.** This course is one requirement among several.
