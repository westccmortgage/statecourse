# Oklahoma Basic Pre-License 90h v1.0 — Hour-by-Hour Curriculum

Regulatory status: **Development; not approved; no enrollment or credit.**

This file contains 90 auditable instructional units. Each unit has its own objectives, reading, state layer, applied work, knowledge check, and remediation.

## OK-01 — Real Estate Economics and Marketing (3 hours)

Authority: OAC 605:10-3-1(a)(1)(A)

## Hour 001 — Economic characteristics of real estate

Module: OK-01 · Required engagement: 60 minutes · Content layer: NC-INV

### Learning objectives

By the end of this hour, the student can:

1. explain economic characteristics of real estate using accurate professional vocabulary;
2. apply economic characteristics of real estate to a state-specific transaction fact pattern; and
3. identify the controlling source, required record, and point for professional referral.

### Instructional reading

**Concept and professional purpose.** Economic characteristics of real estate is studied as part of Real Estate Economics and Marketing. The student begins by identifying the people, property interests, documents, money, deadlines, and public-protection purpose involved. The lesson distinguishes a fact from an assumption, a legal requirement from a provider practice, and a licensee's task from work that must be referred to an attorney, lender, appraiser, surveyor, inspector, tax professional, or other qualified specialist.

**Transaction application.** The student applies the concept to a realistic file. The file is read chronologically: source document, consumer communication, decision point, required disclosure or agreement, performance, and retained evidence. A compliant response identifies controlling authority, verifies material facts from an appropriate source, communicates within the licensee's role, documents the action, and escalates unresolved legal or technical issues. Marketing language, form selection, compensation, and technology do not override the state rule.

**State-law layer.** Oklahoma layer: apply OAC 605:10-3-1(a)(1)(A), the Oklahoma License Code, current OREC rules and forms, and the Broker Relationships Act as applicable. The Basic Course is Part I of a two-part series and is not the 45-hour post-license course. Source dates and form editions are checked at controlled release. If a cited source changes, the lesson is held from new enrollment until the course owner completes impact review and records a new content version.

**National-core relationship.** This unit may reuse the concepts in NC-INV, but its state examples, terminology, forms, citations, assessment record, and approval mapping are versioned independently for Oklahoma.

### Applied activity

Analyze a Oklahoma transaction fact pattern involving **economic characteristics of real estate**. Build a five-entry audit note: (1) known fact, (2) missing fact, (3) controlling source, (4) permitted licensee action, and (5) referral or follow-up. Explain why payment status, course progress, and regulatory credit are separate records.

### Knowledge check and remediation

1. Which source controls this issue in Oklahoma, and what is the effective-date check?
2. What fact must be verified before the licensee communicates or acts?
3. What evidence belongs in the transaction or course audit record?

If any response is incorrect, the LMS assigns the source excerpt, the worked scenario, and a new parallel check. The original answer is not exposed as a substitute for remediation. Hour 1 is complete only after the tracked instructional sequence and required check are complete.
