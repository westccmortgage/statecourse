# Oklahoma assessment source supplement

Version: OK-ASSESSMENT-SUPPLEMENT-2026-09-06-v2. Authored review draft, not an approved course revision.

This supplement accompanies the repaired protected final forms. Read the relevant section with the
linked module before attempting an assessment. It corrects or supplements older statements of what
had not yet been read. It adds no claimed clock hours, approval, instructor signoff or examination
authorization. Final-bank coverage is a provider mapping, not proof of instructional sufficiency.
Current editions and verification limitations are recorded in the final-exam source manifest.

## capacity

Related module 09, §47.1 and §48.1. A contract requires capable parties, consent, a lawful object and
consideration under 15 O.S. 2. Capacity is not established merely by a signature. Oklahoma's 15 O.S. 17
restricts a minor's power to contract about real property, except as specially provided. Under 15 O.S.
24, a judicially determined incapacity is not cured by an informal statement that the person now feels
competent; judicial restoration and the actual order matter. Do not import the generic claim that
every minor's real-property contract is simply voidable. Seek legal guidance on authority to sign.
A single unlawful contractual object is not made lawful by payment (15 O.S. 102–104). Fraud, duress
and qualifying mistake may undermine consent (15 O.S. 53, 58, 63); remedy and rescission depend on facts.

## writing

Related module 09, §48.3. Under 15 O.S. 136(4), real-property sales and leases longer than one year
generally require a signed writing; agent authority for such an agreement must also be written.
For example, a proposed eighteen-month tenancy raises this writing requirement. This is not a rule
that every oral promise is enforceable or that every disputed oral transaction has the same remedy.
Read the actual agreement and refer exceptions and enforcement to counsel. Acceptance must conform
to the offer (15 O.S. 71). If the offer prescribes a particular method, evaluate compliance with that
method (15 O.S. 68); do not confuse a hypothetical receipt deadline with a universal receipt rule.
Section 69 addresses when properly transmitted consent is deemed communicated.

An assignment transfers contractual rights; it does not by itself establish the original obligor's
release. A novation case expressly supplies substitution and release with required consent. That
distinction is taught as a contractual concept, not falsely attributed to an unrelated statutory
novation section. Written amendments record changed terms. Under 15 O.S. 237, a written contract can
also be altered by an executed oral agreement; that exception is not advice to rely on unperformed
oral changes. Stipulated damages require lawful entitlement, not simply a broker's preference. In
real-estate sales, 15 O.S. 215(B) distinguishes amounts at or below five percent of the price from
larger amounts whose reasonableness must be established. This is not an automatic right to disburse
escrow. Counsel resolves disputed remedies and the holder follows applicable escrow requirements.

## electronic

Related module 09, §48.3. The January 2026 OREC Residential Sale form, printed page 1, states that
electronic signatures have the same force and effect as original signatures and permits identical
contract counterparts, including electronic copies. This is a
specific current-form term, not a claim that every electronic message forms a contract. The parties,
material terms, assent, signature and delivery still must be established. A screenshot of negotiations
does not substitute for the agreed executed instrument. Preserve the final version and its delivery
record. Never enter a signature for another person without authority.

## forms

Related module 09, §53.2. The January 2026 Residential Sale PDF begins with a separate one-page
acknowledgment of disclosures, followed by the seven-page Residential Contract. Its first printed
contract page lists supplemental agreements and addenda to identify which are part of the agreement;
the listed or attached documents must be read together. The Parties clause makes signatures with
delivery to their respective brokers, if applicable, binding. It supersedes prior negotiations and
requires a further written agreement of buyer and seller for modification or assignment. Negotiation, counteroffer,
execution, delivery, notice, amendment and closing are distinct events. An addendum supplies terms;
an amendment changes existing terms; a notice communicates or exercises an existing right. In an
assessment that explicitly supplies a different deadline or clock convention, calculate from those
stated facts rather than inventing an OREC deadline.

OREC develops standardized forms for voluntary use under 59 O.S. 858-208(14). Availability of a form
does not make every form mandatory or make a licensee qualified to draft custom legal clauses. Select
the suitable current form, complete factual fields accurately, attach applicable documents and refer
unfamiliar legal drafting to counsel. Neither a published form nor a course draft is evidence that
StateCourse has provider, instructor or course approval.

## finance

Related module 11, §63.1. A conventional mortgage is not insured or guaranteed by a federal housing
program. This does not mean every conventional loan has identical down-payment, insurance or credit
rules. FHA insures eligible mortgages and VA guarantees a portion of eligible loans; neither label
eliminates lender underwriting or guarantees approval. Eligibility is not the same as affordability.

For the stated hypothetical, loan-to-value equals loan divided by value, and a payment-to-income ratio
equals the specified monthly housing payment divided by gross monthly income. Use the value and
cost definitions provided; do not invent a universal qualification threshold. A discount point is one
percent of the loan amount. Compare upfront points with the quoted periodic savings and expected
holding period; a simple break-even calculation excludes time value and future refinance risk unless
the question includes them. Interest is calculated on the applicable outstanding principal, while
principal reduction builds equity. Mortgage insurance protects the lender from a covered loss; it is
not a homeowner warranty or a promise to forgive borrower debt. Sources: CFPB conventional, FHA, VA,
points and amortization explainers listed in the source manifest.

## closing

Related module 12, §§71.2–71.3. The Closing Disclosure must be distinguished from an earlier estimate
and reconciled to the actual contract, lender instructions and settlement figures. Under Regulation
Z, 12 CFR 1026.19(f)(2)(ii), a new three-business-day waiting period is triggered by an inaccurate APR,
a loan-product change, or addition of a prepayment penalty. Other corrections can require a corrected
disclosure without automatically restarting that period. The lender determines the applicable APR
tolerance and timing; do not substitute the phrase "every correction restarts the clock."

In a settlement worksheet, a seller credit reduces buyer cash needed and seller proceeds. Recording
it twice understates both amounts. Reconcile credits, deposits, payoff figures and prorations against
their source; do not fix an imbalance by inventing a fee. Possession follows the agreement, not merely
the delivery of keys or a signature. Under 59 O.S. 858-364 a broker gives the required deed-theft notice
at closing and obtains the buyer's signed acknowledgment. Account and confidentiality duties can
survive termination under 858-358; closing does not erase them.

## authority

Related module 14, §78.2. OREC's authority under 59 O.S. 858-208 includes licensing discipline and
approval of qualifying educational entities, courses and instructors. Its availability of application
forms is not an approval decision. A provisional sales associate must satisfy the specified post-license
requirement; the category is not an independent broker license. Under 858-363, associates perform acts
through the broker's name and supervision. Inactive status is not authority to continue licensed acts.
Apply the statutory categories rather than assuming that payment or finishing a course confers a
license. Required pre-enrollment notices in OAC 605:10-5-1(m) concern specified criminal-history and
immigration-status eligibility review; do not promise eligibility or ask students to conceal issues.

## supervision

Related module 03, §18.4. Opening a branch is not just creating a web page. Under 59 O.S. 858-310(B),
branch licensing and separate broker direction requirements apply. The responsible broker must
supervise licensed activity; an associate cannot appoint themselves independent broker by using a
different trade name. OAC 605:10-9-1 supplies further brokerage responsibilities. Check the actual
license, assignment and activity, not merely an office sign or an individual's seniority.

## trust

Related modules 10, §59.2 and 16, §90.1. Trust money belongs to the entitled party, not to the firm.
Commingling and failure to account/remit are distinct concerns under 59 O.S. 858-312(16) and (6).
OAC 605:10-13-1 requires bookkeeping that identifies ownership, receipts, disbursements and balances.
If three separate ledgers hold $3,100, $2,400 and $1,200, their liability total is $6,700; an unrelated
firm expense cannot properly reduce a client's balance. This arithmetic example prescribes no
deadline or deposit rule. Reconcile the bank and transaction ledgers, investigate discrepancies and
preserve records. For a disputed deposit, preserve funds and use authorized disposition procedures,
including the applicable rule or interpleader route; do not decide a party's legal entitlement by
preference or pay office expenses while waiting.

## records

Related module 12, §72.4. OAC 605:10-13-1(l) requires transaction records for at least five years from
consummation or termination, and trust records for five years from disbursal. The different triggering
events matter. Paragraph (m) addresses original trust documents, later retention of qualifying copies
and record accessibility. Five years is a minimum, not permission to destroy material subject to a
longer legal hold. Preserve legible, retrievable files and audit trails rather than merely an empty
folder. Section 858-312(26) addresses an adequate written response to a Commission request within
fifteen days; acknowledgment alone is not necessarily adequate.

## wholesaling

Related module 14 and the license-code study area. Under 59 O.S. 858-301, public marketing and
advertising of an equitable interest in a contract to purchase real property fall within the license
requirement. Relabeling the activity does not remove that statutory language. Section **858-314(C)**
gives the homeowner a two-business-day cancellation right without penalty for a covered wholesaler
contract. Related written disclosures and prohibited conduct in that section must also be considered.
Do not cite 858-312.1 for this cancellation right: that is a different provision concerning suspended
or revoked licensees. The assessment tests the specified public-marketing activity and covered
contract, not a claim that every private contractual assignment is identical.

## disclosure

Related module 15, §§83.1 and 83.4. Oklahoma's Residential Property Condition Disclosure Act distinguishes
a disclosure statement from a disclaimer. Under 60 O.S. 833(A)(1), a disclaimer route requires both
that the seller has never occupied the property and that the seller has no actual knowledge of a
defect. "I did not live there" alone does not erase known defects. Under 832(10), the disclosure is
based on actual knowledge and is not a warranty. The broker's own actual knowledge of an omitted
defect requires disclosure under 836(C). Section 836(E) does not impose an independent property
inspection or independent verification duty on the licensee; referral to an inspector is not a reason
to conceal known facts.

Timing is part of the duty. Under 834(B), if the buyer receives the disclosure or disclaimer after
making the offer, acknowledgment and confirmation are required before acceptance. Under 834(C), a
newly discovered defect before acceptance requires prompt written, signed and dated information;
paragraph (D) requires buyer acknowledgment. Retain the delivered version, update and receipt, not
just a note saying "disclosures done." An out-of-date assertion cannot be treated as current merely
because it once appeared in the file.

## lead

Related module 15, §85.1. For covered pre-1978 housing, federal lead disclosure requires the applicable
pamphlet, known information, available records/reports and contractual warning/acknowledgments before
the buyer or tenant becomes obligated. Relevant common-area reports can matter in multifamily
housing. A purchaser generally receives a ten-day opportunity for a lead inspection or risk assessment;
the parties can change that period by written agreement and a buyer may waive the opportunity. The
rule does not itself require the seller to test or guarantee absence of lead. Do not treat a seller's
lack of knowledge as permission to omit the required paperwork. Source: EPA real-estate lead disclosure
guidance and its linked federal disclosure rule.

## environment

Related module 15, §§84.1–84.3. Radon cannot be reliably detected by ordinary senses; testing is the
way to determine its level (EPA Home Buyer's and Seller's Guide to Radon). A seller's inability to
smell it is not evidence of absence. Do not disturb suspect asbestos material to make a showing look
better; professional evaluation is appropriate because disturbance can release fibers (EPA asbestos
guidance). Flood risk is not zero outside a mapped high-risk zone; FEMA explains that maps, insurance
requirements and actual exposure are related but different questions. Refer detailed determinations
to qualified sources and disclose known facts. Do not guarantee environmental safety from appearance.

## tenancy

Related module 16, §§89.2 and 90.1. Read the Oklahoma Residential Landlord and Tenant Act, not only the
management contract. Under 41 O.S. 118(A), landlord obligations include specified habitability and
maintenance duties; supplied plumbing and common areas in covered multifamily premises cannot simply
be ignored. The section has qualifications and limited written-agreement provisions, not a blanket
rule that every conceivable repair is always landlord-paid. Under 41 O.S. 127, the tenant has duties
concerning cleanliness, proper use, damage and peaceful enjoyment. A complaint is evaluated under the
applicable duty and facts, without retaliatory or discriminatory treatment. Security deposits are
handled under 41 O.S. 115; where an assessment expressly supplies lawful deductions, subtract those
amounts without inventing another fee. This example does not establish deduction eligibility or a
return deadline. Federal fair-housing accommodation obligations can apply during an existing tenancy,
not just at application. Evaluate a disability-related request individually rather than imposing an
automatic no-exceptions rule; a request is not automatically granted regardless of reasonableness.

## wire

Related module 15, §86.2. A changed wire instruction received by email may be business-email compromise.
Verify independently using a previously established number or other trusted channel, not the contact
details in the suspect message. A convincing sender name or logo does not establish authenticity. If
funds have been sent, immediately contact the financial institution and follow its fraud-response
instructions, preserve evidence and report through the appropriate law-enforcement channels. The FBI
recommends immediate bank contact; neither investigation nor recovery is guaranteed. Never ask a
customer to post account numbers publicly to prove a transfer.

## conflicts

Related module 14, §80.2 and module 03. A broker serving both parties must provide the written notice
required by 59 O.S. 858-355.1(D). Section 858-312(3), not (6), makes failure to comply with the Broker
Relationships Act a disciplinary ground. Section (6) concerns accounting and remittance. Payment alone
does not establish a brokerage relationship under 858-359(A). The Oklahoma Broker Relationships Act
abrogates common-law agency duties within its scope (858-360), but permits additional agreed services
that do not displace statutory duties. The word "agent" as a trade description does not recreate a
different common-law regime (858-361).

Confidential information under 858-353(A)(4) includes specified price/financing flexibility, motivation
and designated information; the statutory exceptions include written consent, legal requirement and
information becoming public from a source other than the firm. Do not call every physical defect
confidential or disclose negotiations just because a transaction ended. Both-side service does not
permit a broker to sacrifice honesty, accounting or timely communication. Fees can be separately
negotiated under 858-359(D); competing firms cannot agree on a common fee floor. An independent firm's
own fee policy is not, by itself, an agreement among competitors. Sources include the current OREC
Code and the DOJ's primary horizontal-price-fixing enforcement record.
