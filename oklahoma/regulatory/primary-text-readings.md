# Oklahoma — primary text readings

**Read on 2026-08-20** from the Oklahoma Real Estate Commission's own publication, *Oklahoma Real
Estate License Code and Rules*, effective 1 November 2025, obtained from `oklahoma.gov` — 109 pages,
containing both Title 59 O.S. (the Code) and Oklahoma Administrative Code Title 605 (the Rules).
Everything below was read from that operative text, not from a summary.

**Source-control rule.** Official regulator, legislature and secretary-of-state rule text, and
current regulator forms control. Third-party summaries may aid navigation but cannot create a
requirement. A missing or ambiguous requirement becomes a submission question or a release gate; it
is not inferred from another state.

## 605:10-3-1(a)(1) — the twenty-two areas of study

This is the rule that defines the course. Read in full:

> To qualify an applicant for examination and licensure as a provisional sales associate, the course
> shall consist of at least ninety (90) clock hours of instruction or its equivalent as determined by
> the Commission. In order for a provisional sales associate to obtain a sales associate license, the
> provisional sales associate must, following issuance of a provisional license, complete additional
> education as required in Section 858-302 of the Code. **The pre-license course of study shall be
> referred to as the Basic Course of Real Estate, Part I of II** and shall encompass the following
> areas of study:

> (A) Real Estate Economics and Marketing
> (B) Nature of Real Estate
> (C) Rights and Interest in Real Estate
> (D) Legal Descriptions
> (E) Title Search, Encumbrances, and Land Use Control
> (F) Transfer of Rights
> (G) Service Contracts
> (H) Estimating Transaction Expenses
> (I) Value and Appraisal
> (J) Marketing Activities
> (K) Fair Housing
> (L) Contract Law Overview
> (M) Contract Law and Performance
> (N) Offers and Purchase Contracts
> (O) Financing Real Estate
> (P) Closing a Transaction
> (Q) Regulations Affecting Real Estate
> (R) Disclosures and Environmental Issues
> (S) Property Management and Leasing
> (T) Risk Management
> (U) Professional Standards of Conduct
> (V) Law of Agency

**Twenty-two areas, and the rule allocates NO hours among them.** So the topic list is the
Commission's and the hour allocation is StateCourse's — the same division Wisconsin requires under
REEB 25.033, and the opposite of Maine, where the Commission sets the weights.

The rule also defines a **broker** course of ninety hours in 605:10-3-1(a)(2), the *Advanced Course in
Real Estate*, with its own twenty-three areas of study (A) through (W). **This project is building
the provisional sales associate course only.** The broker list was read and is not reproduced here,
because nothing in this build addresses it and a topic list sitting in the wrong file is how a course
gets submitted against the wrong rule.

**605:10-3-1(c)** — for an in-class offering, a person must be **physically present during all of the
in-class offering time** and successfully complete all course requirements including an examination.

**605:10-3-1(d)** — for a distance education offering, a person must successfully complete **all
course requirements to include all modules and an examination.** That is the provision StateCourse's
delivery actually runs under, and "all modules" is why module completion is server-authoritative.

## 59 O.S. § 858-302(A) — ninety hours, and the three-year clock

> Any person eighteen (18) years of age or older who shall submit to the Commission evidence of
> successful completion of ninety (90) clock hours or its equivalent as determined by the Commission
> of basic real estate instruction in a course of study approved by the Commission, may apply to the
> Commission to take an examination for the purpose of securing a license as a provisional sales
> associate. **The education required in this subsection shall only be valid for a period of three (3)
> years from the date the school certified successful completion of the course**; thereafter, the
> applicant shall be required to successfully complete an additional ninety (90) clock hours or its
> equivalent in basic real estate instruction.

**Three years, measured from the date the SCHOOL CERTIFIED completion** — not from the date the
student finished the last module, and not from the date of application. The distinction matters
because the certification date is the school's record, and a student who lets three years pass does
the entire ninety hours again.

Note also the structure the section creates: the ninety hours qualifies a person for a **provisional
sales associate** licence, and § 858-302 requires **further postlicense education** after the
provisional licence issues before a full sales associate licence can be obtained. 605:10-5-3(j)
requires each entity to **promote the Basic Course of Real Estate as Part I of a two part series and
the Provisional Postlicense Course of Real Estate as Part II of that series.** A student who thinks
ninety hours is the whole of the education has been misled, and this course says so plainly.

## 605:10-5-3 — standards for Commission approved courses

The operative rule for how the course is delivered and graded.

**(a) Approved instructor.** Each course offering shall be conducted by a Commission approved
instructor, **available during normal business hours as posted by the instructor** to answer questions
about the course material and provide assistance as necessary.

**(b)** To certify a student as passing, the entity requires that the student either attend the
required hours of in-class instruction, or **complete all instructional modules required for distance
education instruction**, or both where the course uses both.

**(c)** The student must **successfully complete an examination covering the contents of the course
material.**

**(d) Student transfers.** Except with the Commission's prior approval, a student transferring from
one course to another **may not count any portion of the student's attendance or work in the former
course** toward passing. A student may not transfer credit between entities unless the receiving
entity offers the identical series and agrees to accept and examine the student through completion.

**(e) Course examinations** — read exactly, because two figures live here:

> Each approved prelicense provisional sales associate course and postlicense course offering shall
> conclude with an **end-of-course examination consisting of no less than one hundred and fifty (150)
> questions** administered by the approved entity. Each approved prelicense broker course shall
> conclude with an end-of-course examination consisting of no less than two hundred (200) questions
> administered by the approved entity. … **End-of-course examination questions may not be the same as
> any previously used questions covering the respective course content.**

That last sentence is the strongest item-security provision this project has read in any state. It is
not merely that the key stays secret — **an item, once used, may not be reused.** A course that ships
a single fixed 150-item final and administers it repeatedly does not comply.

**(f) Successful completion** — the Commission's passing score:

> In order for a student to successfully complete a prelicense, postlicense or distance education
> course, the entity must require that the student complete all class material and/or modules and
> **achieve a passing score of at least seventy five percent (75%) on the entity's final examination.**
> An entity shall require the student to complete sufficient material or modules to ensure mastery of
> the course offering, and shall require the student to complete the end-of-course examination. **An
> entity may allow any student who fails to achieve a passing score the opportunity to take another
> examination without repeating instruction.**

**75 percent is the Commission's figure for the COURSE final examination.** It must not be confused
with the state licensing examination score below, which is a different number for a different
examination administered by a different body.

**(g) Grading standards.** The entity's policy must at least equal the requirements in the rule, other
grading standards must accord with generally accepted educational standards, and **the entity shall
publish grading standards and give them to a student in written form at the beginning of the course.**

**(h)** The Commission may sanction an entity, director or instructor that **fails to provide proper
security for its course evaluation or examination.**

**(i) Additional distance education requirements** — the ones this build lives under:

1. Each course shall contain **suitable learning objectives**.
2. **Overview statements** must be included for each course, giving a quick preview of the offering.
3. A complete set of questions and an answer key must be provided to the Commission with each course
   application. **"An answer key may not be included in any course materials provided to the student."**
4. From the date of enrolment the course has a **validity period of six (6) months** in which to allow
   successful completion.
5. Entities must clearly inform the student of the **completion time frame, passing and examination
   requirements**, and any other relevant information.
6. Each course must include a statement that the information presented **should not be used as a
   substitute for competent legal advice.**
7. Course offerings must be **sufficient in scope and content to justify the hours requested.**

**(j)** Each entity shall promote the Basic Course as **Part I of a two part series**, with the
Provisional Postlicense Course as Part II.

**(k)** All materials distributed to students **must be current and up-to-date with the License Code
and Rules and state or federal laws.**

## 605:10-5-1 — approval of a pre-license course

**(a)(3)** The application requires an approved course syllabus **encompassing the contents enumerated
in 605:10-3-1 and divided by instructional periods**, with the primary textbook's name, author and
publisher — or a statement that the entity will use the OREC syllabus.

**(d) Advertising.** No person or entity **shall advertise the course as approved prior to the course
receiving approval from the Commission.** Further, no person or entity **shall advertise that it is
endorsed, recommended or accredited by the Commission**, although it may indicate that a course of
study has been approved by the Commission.

**(f) Instructor examination.** Final instructor approval follows a passing score of **80% or more** on
an applicable examination — a third distinct percentage, applying to instructors rather than students.

**(h) Guest instructors.** Permitted where an approved instructor is also present, and **total guest
instruction and lectures shall not consume more than thirty percent (30%) of the total course time.**

**(i) Instructor and entity requirements:**

1. **Instructor must be present** in the same room during all in-class course instruction for students
   to receive credit.
2. **Retention of records** — enrolment records and roll sheets **including the number of hours
   completed by each student**, kept for **five (5) years**.
3. Each individual successfully completing an approved course shall be **furnished a certificate
   certifying completion**.
4. A Commission designee **may audit any offering and inspect the entity's records at any time**
   during its presentation or during reasonable office hours.
5. **"Not more than one clock hour may be registered within any one sixty (60) minute period and no
   more than ten (10) minutes of each hour shall be utilized for breaks."**

That last item is the Oklahoma clock hour, and it works the way Missouri's does rather than the way
Maine's or Wisconsin's do: **a sixty-minute period, of which at most ten minutes is break — so at
least fifty minutes of instruction.**

**(l)** Course approval **expires thirty-six (36) months** after issuance and is renewed by electronic
application immediately preceding expiration, with all proposed course material and a renewal fee.

**(m) Entity required to post notice.** Each entity must post or provide a notice easily observed by
any person desiring to enrol, including at least this language:

> "Applicants convicted of felony crimes referenced in Title 59 Section 858-301.1 or 858-4000.1 may
> be ineligible to obtain an Oklahoma Real Estate License for a predetermined number of years. For
> clarification, please contact the Commission and/or review the cited section of law as referenced
> herein. The Commission will allow the applicant to seek preapproval prior to enrolling in a
> pre-license course."

and a statement that applicants who are not permanent U.S. citizens should review their eligibility
on the Commission's website **prior to enrolling in pre-license education.**

**Both notices are given before enrolment, not after.** The first is the more consequential: Oklahoma
expressly offers **preapproval before enrolling**, and a student for whom it may matter should be told
that the route exists before they pay for ninety hours.

## 605:10-3-5 — the state licensing examination

**(f) Passing percentile** — read exactly, because it is the figure most likely to be confused with
605:10-5-3(f):

> A score of seventy-five percent (75%) or more shall be considered a passing grade on the **broker**
> examination. A score of **seventy percent (70%) or more shall be considered a passing grade on the
> provisional sales associate/sales associate examination.**

**So Oklahoma has three different percentages in play and they belong to three different things:**

| Percentage | What it governs | Rule |
|---:|---|---|
| **75%** | the entity's **course** final examination | 605:10-5-3(f) |
| **70%** | the **state licensing** examination for provisional sales associate / sales associate | 605:10-3-5(f) |
| **80%** | an **instructor** applicant's qualifying examination | 605:10-5-1(f) |

A course document that states "the passing score is 70 percent" without saying which examination it
means has misstated the regulator. Every document in this build names the examination.

**(g)** Passing examination results are **valid for one (1) year** from the date of the examination.

**(d)** An applicant who fails may review their missed questions at the end of the examination and may
challenge the validity of a question, with **no notes, pencils or electronic devices** allowed during
the review and nothing leaving the examination area.

**(e)** The original examination application is **valid for one (1) year** from filing.

**605:10-3-3(a)(1)** — an approved licence application is **valid for one (1) year**.

## 59 O.S. §§ 858-351 to 858-364 — broker relationships, read in full

**This is the section that makes Oklahoma unlike almost every other state in this project, and getting
it wrong would misteach the single most important topic in the course.**

### § 858-360 — Oklahoma abrogated common-law agency

Read it exactly, because everything else follows from it:

> **A.** The duties and responsibilities of a broker specified in Sections 858-351 through 858-363 of
> The Oklahoma Real Estate License Code **shall replace and abrogate the fiduciary or other duties of a
> broker to a party based on common law principles of agency.** The remedies at law and equity
> supplement the provisions of Sections 858-351 through 858-363…
>
> **B.** A broker may cooperate with other brokers in a transaction. Pursuant to Sections 858-351
> through 858-363…, **a broker shall not be an agent, subagent, or dual agent and an offer of subagency
> shall not be made to other brokers.**

So in Oklahoma there is **no agency, no subagency and no dual agency in real estate brokerage.** The
common-law fiduciary duties — the OLD CAR list taught everywhere else — **do not apply.** A broker
provides **brokerage services** to **parties**, and the duties are the statutory list and nothing more.

**§ 858-361** nevertheless permits a broker and their associates **to use the word "agent" in a trade
name and as a general reference** for designating themselves as licensees. So the word survives as
vocabulary while the legal relationship it names does not. Any course that teaches Oklahoma agency out
of a national textbook will teach it wrongly, and this one says so.

### § 858-351 — definitions

- **"Broker"** — a real estate broker, an associated broker associate, sales associate, **or
  provisional sales associate** authorised by a real estate broker to provide brokerage services. Note
  that the statutory word "broker" therefore includes the student taking this course once licensed.
- **"Brokerage services"** — those services provided by a broker to a party in a transaction.
- **"Firm"** — a sole proprietor, corporation, association or partnership.
- **"Party"** — a person who is a seller, buyer, landlord or tenant, or a person involved in an option
  or exchange.
- **"Transaction"** — an activity or process to buy, sell, lease, rent, option or exchange real estate,
  which may include soliciting, advertising, showing or viewing real property, presenting offers or
  counteroffers, entering into agreements, and closing them.
- **"Deed theft"** — intentionally altering, falsifying, forging or misrepresenting a document relating
  to real property with intent to deceive, defraud or unlawfully transfer or encumber ownership rights;
  misrepresenting oneself as the owner or the owner's authorised representative in order to obtain
  ownership or possession; or with intent to defraud, taking, obtaining, stealing or transferring title
  or ownership by fraud, forgery, larceny or any other fraudulent or deceptive practice.

### § 858-353(A) — seven duties to ALL parties, mandatory and unwaivable

> A broker shall have the following duties and responsibilities to all parties in a transaction, **which
> are mandatory and may not be abrogated or waived by a broker**:

1. **Treat all parties with honesty and exercise reasonable skill and care.**
2. **Unless specifically waived in writing by a party**: (a) receive all written offers and
   counteroffers; (b) **reduce offers or counteroffers to a written form upon request of any party**;
   and (c) **present timely all written offers and counteroffers.**
3. **Timely account for all money and property received** by the broker.
4. **Keep confidential information received from a party or prospective party confidential** — and the
   statute then does something no other state read in this project does. It **lists exhaustively what
   is confidential**:

   > The following information shall be considered confidential and **shall be the only information
   > considered confidential in a transaction**:
   > a. that a party or prospective party **is willing to pay more or accept less** than what is being
   > offered,
   > b. that a party or prospective party **is willing to agree to financing terms that are different
   > from those offered**,
   > c. **the motivating factors** of the party or prospective party purchasing, selling, leasing,
   > optioning or exchanging the property, and
   > d. **information specifically designated as confidential by a party** unless such information is
   > public.

   Confidentiality is not disclosed without the disclosing party's **written consent**, unless
   disclosure is **required by law** or the information **is or becomes public from a source other than
   the firm.**
5. **Disclose information pertaining to the property as required by the Residential Property Condition
   Disclosure Act.**
6. **Comply with all requirements** of the Code and all applicable statutes and rules.
7. **Disclose** (a) **information pertaining to compensation and fees assessed on each transaction to
   the represented party, communicated in writing BEFORE the effective date of the contract** for sale
   or lease; and (b) **the time frame for which the compensation agreement is valid, not to exceed one
   (1) year — and if no time frame is specified, the compensation agreement defaults to sixty (60)
   days.**

### § 858-353(B) — two further duties, owed only to a party the broker is serving

1. **Inform the party in writing when an offer is made** that the party will be expected to pay certain
   costs, **brokerage service costs and the approximate amount** of those costs.
2. **Keep the party informed regarding the transaction.**

### § 858-353(C) — both parties

> When working with both parties to a transaction, the duties and responsibilities set forth in this
> section **shall remain in place for both parties.**

Because there is no dual agency in Oklahoma, serving both sides is not a special status with reduced
duties. **The full duty set simply applies to both.**

### § 858-355.1 — brokerage agreements and the written disclosures

- **A.** All brokerage agreements **shall incorporate as material terms** the § 858-353 duties.
- **B.** A broker **may provide brokerage services to one or both parties.**
- **C.** A broker providing services to one or both parties shall **describe and disclose in writing**
  the § 858-353 duties **prior to the party or parties signing a contract** to sell, purchase, lease,
  option or exchange real estate.
- **D.** A firm providing brokerage services **to both parties** shall provide **written notice to both
  parties** that it is doing so, **prior to the parties signing a contract.**
- **E.** A broker intending to provide **fewer brokerage services than those required to complete a
  transaction** shall provide written disclosure to the party served, **describing the steps for which
  the broker will not provide services**, and **stating that the broker assisting the other party is
  not required to provide assistance with those steps in any manner.**

### § 858-356 — confirmation in writing

The § 858-355.1(C) disclosures **shall be confirmed by each party in writing in a separate provision,
incorporated in or attached to the contract.** Where a broker is involved but does not prepare the
contract, **compliance shall be documented by the broker.**

### § 858-358 — after termination

Except as a written brokerage agreement provides, the broker owes **no further duties after termination,
expiration or completion**, except (1) **to account for all monies and property relating to the
transaction**, and (2) **to keep confidential all confidential information received** during the
relationship.

### § 858-359 — compensation

- **A.** **The payment or promise of payment of compensation by a party does not determine what
  relationship, if any, has been established** between the broker and a party.
- **B.** Receiving a fee **based on a selling price or lease cost is not a breach of duty** to any party.
- **C.** A broker or real estate owner of record **may compensate or offer compensation to any real
  estate professional licensed by the Commission** for providing brokerage services.
- **D.** Nothing requires or prohibits **charging a separate fee for each duty or service**, and nothing
  **requires a broker or owner of record to offer compensation** to any real estate professional.

### § 858-362 — no vicarious liability

> A party to a real estate transaction **shall not be vicariously liable for the acts or omissions of a
> real estate licensee** who is providing brokerage services under Sections 858-351 through 858-363.

### § 858-363 — associates act in the broker's name

Each broker associate, sales associate and provisional sales associate **shall be associated with a real
estate broker.** Associates **shall not enter into a brokerage agreement with a party in the associate's
name** and shall only enter the agreement **in the name of the broker.** A broker may authorise
associates to provide brokerage services in the broker's name, which may include executing written
agreements.

### § 858-364 — deed theft notification (new)

> As part of **each closing** of a transaction, notification of the signs and risks of **deed theft**, as
> defined in Section 858-351, **shall be required to be given to the buyer by a licensed real estate
> broker. The buyer shall confirm receipt of such notification by affixing his or her signature** to
> such notification. The Oklahoma Real Estate Commission shall promulgate rules to enact the provisions
> of this section.

**The Commission's implementing rules and the form of the notification were not located in the
publication read**, and this course therefore **states no content, wording or format for the deed theft
notification** — it states that the obligation exists, that it is delivered at each closing, and that
the buyer signs. The form is obtained from the Commission.

Relatedly, **§ 858-307.2(F)** requires the Commission to mandate a specified number of hours of
continuing education on deed theft, to be completed by licensees **on or before 1 November 2028.**

## 59 O.S. § 858-312 — cause for suspension or revocation

Read in full: twenty-eight grounds. The Commission **may on its own motion, and SHALL upon written
complaint filed by any person**, investigate the business transactions of any licensee.

The ones that bear directly on what this course teaches:

- **(2)** substantial misrepresentations or false promises in the conduct of business, or through
  licensees, or **advertising**, intended to influence, persuade or induce.
- **(3)** **failing to comply with §§ 858-351 through 858-363** — so a breach of the broker-relationships
  duties is itself a licence ground.
- **(4)** an associate **accepting a commission or other valuable consideration except from the broker
  with whom the associate is associated.**
- **(6)** failing within a reasonable time **to account for or remit any monies, documents or other
  property belonging to others.**
- **(10)** **guaranteeing future profits** which may result from the resale of real estate.
- **(11)** advertising or placing a sign **without the consent of the owner** or the owner's authorised
  representative.
- **(12)** using prizes, money, gifts or other valuable consideration **as an inducement to secure
  customers or clients to purchase specific property** — with marketing use permitted **provided it is
  not contingent or limited to individuals making an offer or purchasing a specific property.**
- **(15)** unworthiness, or conviction of or plea to **a crime involving moral turpitude.**
- **(16)** **commingling** the money or property of others with the licensee's own, unless held in an
  escrow account containing only the money or property of others.
- **(17)** conviction of having violated **the federal fair housing laws, 42 U.S.C. § 3601 et seq.**
- **(20)** advertising to buy, sell, rent or exchange real estate **without disclosing in writing that the
  licensee is a real estate licensee.**
- **(21)** paying any part of a fee or commission **to any person not licensed.**
- **(22)** **offering, loaning, paying, or making to appear to have been paid, a down payment or earnest
  money deposit** for a purchaser or seller.
- **(23)** **violation of the Residential Property Condition Disclosure Act.**
- **(26)** **failure to provide an adequate written response to the Commission within fifteen (15) days**
  after receiving written notice of a complaint or investigation.
- **(27)** failure to notify the Commission of a felony conviction or plea **within thirty (30) days** of
  the plea and again within thirty days of judgment and sentencing — § 858-301.2.
- **(28)** soliciting, selling or offering for sale real property **by means of a deceptive practice.**

**§ 858-401** — penalties. Unlicensed activity is a **misdemeanour**, punishable on conviction by a fine
of **not more than $5,000**, imprisonment in the county jail for **not more than six (6) months**, or
both. The Commission may also, after notice and hearing, **impose a fine of not more than $5,000 or the
amount of the commission earned, whichever is greater**, for each violation involving unlicensed
activity, and may seek an injunction.

## What was NOT read, and is therefore stated nowhere in this build

Named explicitly, because an unnamed gap becomes an invented fact:

- **The OREC syllabus** referenced at 605:10-5-1(a)(3) as an alternative to an entity's own. It was not
  obtained. No document in this build claims to follow it, and reconciling against it — or electing to
  use it — is a submission decision.
- **The Oklahoma broker course topic list** at 605:10-3-1(a)(2). Read, but deliberately not reproduced
  or built against; this course is the provisional sales associate course.
- **The Oklahoma Real Estate Contract Form Committee's promulgated forms.** Oklahoma uses mandatory
  contract forms in residential transactions. **This project has not read them, and no document in this
  build reproduces, describes or paraphrases any form or any of its provisions.**
- **59 O.S. § 858-301.1 and § 858-4000.1**, cited in the required enrolment notice. The notice's text is
  reproduced above because the rule prescribes it; the underlying felony provisions were not read, and
  **this course states no offence, period or eligibility rule from them** — it routes to the Commission
  and to the sections themselves, exactly as the prescribed notice does.
- **59 O.S. § 858-208**, the sanctions section § 858-312 cross-refers to. Cited; its content is not
  stated, so this course names no sanction available under it.
- **The Residential Property Condition Disclosure Act**, 60 O.S. § 831 et seq. It is named in
  § 858-353(A)(5) as a duty and in § 858-312(23) as a ground for discipline, and both of those facts
  ARE stated in this build because they were read. **The Act's own text was not read**, so this course
  states no form, deadline, exemption or content requirement from it, and routes every specific to the
  Act and to counsel.
- **The Commission's rules implementing § 858-364**, the deed theft notification, and the form of that
  notification. Not located in the publication read; no wording or format is stated.
- **Oklahoma's trust account, foreclosure, landlord-tenant, probate, mechanic's lien, property tax,
  subdivision and environmental provisions.** None was read. No figure, period or threshold from any of
  them appears anywhere in this build; where a course reaches one, the authored answer is the routing.
- **Oklahoma's residential property condition disclosure regime.** Not read. **This course states no
  Oklahoma seller disclosure requirement, form, deadline or exemption.**

## Version warnings

- The publication read is dated **1 November 2025**. Oklahoma amends both the Code and Title 605
  regularly, and the Commission publishes a rulemaking-intent notice each legislative session. **The
  entire Code and Rules book is re-read at submission**, and 605:10-5-3(k) independently requires that
  all materials distributed to students be current with the Code, the Rules, and state and federal law.
- **605:10-5-1(l)** expires a course approval thirty-six months after issuance, so the re-reading has a
  fixed outside date once approval issues.

Every source above is re-read at submission, at approval, at every controlled release, and before
enrolment after any law, rule or form change.
