# Oklahoma Basic Pre-License 90h v1.0 — Syllabus

**Regulator:** Oklahoma Real Estate Commission  
**Hours:** 90  
**Delivery:** Online distance education — approval pending  
**Status:** Development; not submitted or approved; enrollment disabled  
**Instructor:** Unassigned contract-instructor slot. Activation requires recorded OREC instructor approval, qualification evidence, and 80% or better on both applicable state and national examinations.

## Outcomes

Students who complete the approved version will be able to explain the required real-estate concepts, apply Oklahoma law and forms to entry-level transaction scenarios, perform required calculations, recognize when referral is necessary, and document decisions consistent with consumer protection.

## Completion

Complete every module within six months of enrollment and score at least 75% on the 150-item end-of-course examination.

## Assessment

Module knowledge checks and quizzes diagnose mastery and trigger remediation. Final structure: 2 secure form(s), 150 multiple-choice items per form, 75% passing standard. Primary and retake forms; retake may be offered without repeating instruction.

## Integrity and access

The authenticated student must complete the state-configured identity, timing, module, assessment and final controls. Payment grants only the purchased course after a verified webhook; it never establishes completion or regulatory credit.
