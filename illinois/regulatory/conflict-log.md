# Conflict and interpretation log

## C-01 — PSI Broker paragraph says “Managing Broker”

- **Sources:** PSI candidate booklet dated June 24, 2026, p. 2, “Endorsement Qualification (Broker)”; 225 ILCS 454/5-60.5(b); Rule 1450.460; Form 505-0347.
- **Observed:** the paragraph heading and every listed qualification describe Broker endorsement, but its introductory sentence says “Managing Broker.”
- **Resolution:** treat the sentence as a vendor drafting error. Statute, rule, current form, heading, age threshold, license classification and course title all control Broker classification.
- **Operational effect:** never quote the mistaken sentence as authority.

## C-02 — home-study proctor location

- **Sources:** 225 ILCS 454/30-15(i)(6); Rule 1450.1105(h); Rule 1450.1155; Form 505-0767 p. 3 item 10; Form 505-0645 p. 2.
- **Observed:** the Act/provider form require an in-person proctored examination for home study; the rule also says remote proctoring must comply with state and federal law and the course form allows electronic exam format.
- **Resolution:** the remote-proctor sentence does not reliably remove the higher-law/in-person constraint.
- **Operational effect:** StateCourse selects **Online Distance LMS**, not Home Study/Correspondence. A home-study product stays blocked until IDFPR answers in writing.

## C-03 — seven required courses and optional endorsement sequence

- **Sources:** Rule 1450.1105(e); Form 505-0767 p. 5; Form 505-0645 p. 2.
- **Observed:** only providers offering the complete seven-course pre/post portfolio may provide the endorsement course. The sources do not say whether a new provider can submit all seven plus the endorsement course simultaneously.
- **Resolution:** full portfolio is a hard requirement; filing sequence is unresolved.
- **Operational effect:** build the seven first and ask IDFPR Education whether the eighth application may be in the initial package before paying its nonrefundable fee.

## C-04 — 30 “hours” versus 1,500 instructional minutes

- **Sources:** Broker and Managing Broker endorsement curricula; Rule 1450.100 definition of credit hour.
- **Observed:** each curriculum prints 1,500 instruction minutes (25 clock hours) plus 300 break minutes as a 30-hour course. Illinois defines a credit hour as at least 50 minutes of instruction.
- **Resolution:** thirty credit hours equal 1,500 instructional minutes. Breaks make elapsed course time 1,800 minutes but are never credited as instruction.
- **Operational effect:** the LMS time ledger separately records credit instruction and breaks.

## C-05 — California Broker versus Illinois Managing Broker

- **Sources:** 225 ILCS 454/5-60.5(a); Rule 1450.570; California BPC §10131; California DRE public record.
- **Observed:** California Broker has broad independent-practice authority, but Illinois requires “managing broker or its equivalent” and two immediately preceding years of active managing practice.
- **Resolution:** functional similarity is not an IDFPR equivalence determination.
- **Operational effect:** Broker endorsement is the confirmed preparation path; Managing Broker remains blocked pending written IDFPR classification and acceptable experience evidence.

## C-06 — older-dated curricula on a current site

- **Sources:** current IDFPR Real Estate Brokerage hub; 2020 75-hour and 45-hour PDFs.
- **Observed:** the curricula are dated January 1, 2020.
- **Resolution:** they are not treated as superseded merely because of age; IDFPR still links them as the current mandatory curricula on September 4, 2026.
- **Operational effect:** lock both the document revision date and retrieval date; recheck the hub immediately before submission.
