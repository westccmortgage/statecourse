## Launch controls

Illinois enrollment remains closed until all of the following are independently verified: Illinois foreign-LLC admission; Illinois education-provider license; every offered course's Illinois license; active Illinois instructor license and roster entry; approved delivery method; live compliance/records controls; current public registry; and a final claim audit. Submission is not approval.
