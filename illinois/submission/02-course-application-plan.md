# Form IL 505-0645 course package plan

Create a separate, immutable directory/export for each application. Every export contains:

1. current signed Form 505-0645 and its course-specific ePay number;
2. course description;
3. measurable learning objectives;
4. comprehensive timed outline with time on every topic and total credit time, excluding exam time;
5. every student-facing and instructor-facing material, including links;
6. Anatoliy's resume and active Illinois instructor number;
7. delivery-method narrative and reviewer credentials;
8. final, answer key, written proctor agreement and proctor policy wherever required;
9. a manifest of filenames, versions and hashes;
10. a traceability matrix from every curriculum topic to page/screen and assessment/evidence.

## Delivery selection

Select **Online Distance — Learning Management System (LMS)** and provide administrator-level reviewer credentials. Do not select Home Study/Correspondence/Internet (Non-LMS). Hybrid may not use home study and is not the present design.

## Examination matrix

| Course family | Provider final | Participation controls |
|---|---|---|
| 60h Broker Topics | Waiver possible for approved online distance | recorded responses, ≥75% comprehension, ≤250 questions/course, randomized retry, identity at start/end, five-year retention |
| 15h Broker Applied | Waiver possible for approved online distance | same, plus interactive applied scenarios |
| Three 15h Broker post-license courses | **Required:** separate 50-question final for each, ≥75% | final-exam waiver does not apply |
| 30h MB Topics | Waiver possible for approved online distance | Rule 1450.1175 controls |
| 15h MB Applied | Waiver possible for approved online distance | same, plus interactive applied management/supervision |
| 30h endorsement | No final is expressly required for online distance if waiver controls are accepted | align to mandatory curriculum and Illinois-law exam preparation; do not reproduce live PSI items |

Application approval remains course- and delivery-specific. A complete upload is not permission to enroll students.

September 6 clarification: the 250 limit in the table means **required questions in one course** under 1450.1175(d)(1), not total bank size or questions per exam. The rule describes qualifying conditions for the final-exam exemption and evidence on request; it specifies no separate waiver application. Postlicense finals remain required; proctoring is not added solely by importing home-study rules into online-distance courses. See the source foundation audit for detailed scope.
