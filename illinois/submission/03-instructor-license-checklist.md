# Illinois pre-license instructor checklist

## Can Anatoliy qualify?

**Likely through the active-broker expertise route, but only IDFPR can issue the license.** Rule 1450.1115(a)(2)(A) requires an active broker or managing-broker license for the immediately preceding two years. It does not say “Illinois” in that clause. The official California record shows an active Broker license since 2009. Submit official certification and a resume; do not replace IDFPR's decision with our interpretation.

## Required evidence

- [ ] Choose a currently listed IDFPR-approved 12-hour instructor-training program.
- [ ] Complete 6 hours instructor training/development.
- [ ] Complete 6 hours pre-license-course content training.
- [ ] Retain provider completion certificate/transcript.
- [ ] Create resume/bio: education, broker issue date/status, brokerage and mortgage experience, subject expertise, teaching/presentation experience, publications/professional organizations.
- [ ] Obtain certified California DRE license history.
- [ ] Register with PSI for the instructor examination.
- [ ] Pass each required portion at 75% or better; retain score report.
- [ ] Confirm current exam fee directly in PSI before payment; the June 2026 bulletin does not clearly list it.
- [ ] File through IDFPR online portal.
- [ ] Pay $150 initial application fee.
- [ ] User enters confidential identity/history fields and signs attestation.
- [ ] Record issued instructor license number, status, expiration and restrictions.

## After issuance

Only after verification may StateCourse place the license number on course applications or represent Anatoliy as an Illinois-licensed instructor. Renewal is June 30 in odd-numbered years; the evidence path is teaching one qualifying course during the term or completing the 12-hour program again.
