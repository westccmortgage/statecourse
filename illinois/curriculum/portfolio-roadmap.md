# Illinois mandatory portfolio roadmap

StateCourse must be capable of providing the seven courses in Rule 1450.1105(b)-(d) before it may provide the 30-hour endorsement course or CE core. This is the build order, not a claim that IDFPR requires submissions in this exact sequence.

| Build order | Course | Credit hours | Primary structural source | Critical constraint |
|---:|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Broker Pre-License Topics | 60 | IDFPR 75-hour curriculum, Parts 1-2 | complete timed outline; Online Distance participation controls |
| 2 | Broker Pre-License Applied Real Estate Principles | 15 | IDFPR 75-hour curriculum, Part 3 | applied situations/cases; no home study |
| 3 | Broker Post-License Applied Brokerage Principles | 15 | IDFPR 45-hour curriculum | 50-question final, ≥75%; substantial applied work |
| 4 | Broker Post-License Risk Management/Discipline | 15 | same | separate 50-question final, ≥75% |
| 5 | Broker Post-License Transactional Issues | 15 | same | separate 50-question final, ≥75% |
| 6 | Managing Broker Pre-License Topics | 30 | current IDFPR MB curriculum linked from official hub | management/administration depth |
| 7 | Managing Broker Applied Management and Supervision | 15 | current IDFPR MB curriculum | interactive/online-distance applied delivery |
| 8 | Broker Pre-License Endorsement | 30 | IDFPR 2024 endorsement curriculum | optional; only after full portfolio condition is satisfied |

## Package completion definition

A course is not “built” merely because prose exists. It needs: fixed official sources; page-numbered student text; instructor materials; learning objectives; exact timed outline; activity/assessment bank; required final and answer key where applicable; delivery/identity/attendance records; accessibility review; reference manifest; and a course-application export whose hashes are stable.

The provider form requires separate course applications and fees. Because the simultaneous initial filing of the seven courses plus endorsement is not explicit, obtain written IDFPR Education guidance before paying the optional eighth nonrefundable fee.
