## Confirmed legal architecture

Rule 1450.1105(b), (c) and (d) each expressly requires the listed courses of a provider offering pre-license courses. This supports the seven-course requirement for a prelicense provider; it is not solely an endorsement prerequisite. Subsection (e) additionally limits core CE and endorsement courses to providers supplying the complete portfolio. No authority was found for a new provider to operate only a 60+15 offering while omitting the other five required courses. Simultaneous filing of all courses remains a procedural question already sent to IDFPR, not a new curriculum barrier.

For online distance, 1450.1175(a),(d) permits the prelicense final exemption when participation/comprehension/identity/retention conditions are met. It specifies no separate waiver application. The provider must demonstrate compliant operation and provide evidence on request. The maximum is **250 required questions in any one course** under that exemption provision. It does not cap the total item bank or give 250 questions per exam. Retry mechanics must randomize questions, answer choices and answers relative to the previous presentation. How cumulative mandatory retries are counted is not separately specified; build a bounded completion path and seek guidance if the implementation could compel more than 250 question responses. Optional practice must be genuinely optional.

The waiver expressly excludes 45h postlicense. Each 15h postlicense course requires its own 50-question provider-administered final with at least 75%. No general requirement to add proctoring to these online-distance finals is inferred from the home-study rule; proctor policy is included where required by the actual delivery/application decision. Classroom, interactive webinar and online distance are authorized for the applied/postlicense courses; noninteractive home study is excluded for those courses.
