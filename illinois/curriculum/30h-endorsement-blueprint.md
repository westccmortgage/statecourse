# 30-hour Broker endorsement blueprint

Status: structural build input only. No course approval; no instruction may be offered for Illinois credit.

Illinois labels this a 30-hour course and prints **1,500 minutes of instruction plus 300 minutes of breaks**. Rule 1450.100 defines a credit hour as at least 50 minutes of instruction. The timed outline must therefore preserve 1,500 credited instructional minutes: breaks are scheduled but never credited as instruction.

| Part | Credit hours | Instruction minutes | Mandatory coverage |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| I. Licensing Requirements | 10 | 500 | exemptions; licensed activities; license types; assistants; eligibility/exam/renewal/CE; changes; out-of-state application/education; recovery and administrative funds |
| II. Laws & Rules Regarding Practice | 10 | 500 | purpose; advertising; agreements/minimum services; assistants; broker relationships/compensation; ownership; funds/documents; scope; transfer tax/affordable housing; intestacy; descriptions/Plat Act; taxes/exemptions; Human Rights Act; DEI; contract interference; discipline |
| III. Disclosures | 10 | 500 | designated/dual/non-agency; prohibited transactional brokerage; contemporaneous offers; sponsoring-broker compensation; ownership advertising; residential disclosure; HIV; stigma; material facts; radon |
| **Total credited instruction** | **30** | **1,500** | exact mandatory curriculum |

The eventual submission outline must divide every listed subtopic into page/screen-level lessons and allocate minutes without double counting. Every controlling Illinois proposition will cite the current Act, rule or official form; no exam-prep blog or commercial course will control content.

## Online Distance LMS design constraints

- Record participation and answers throughout the course.
- Require at least 75% comprehension.
- Keep the total required question count at or below 250 for the course.
- Randomize question, option and answer order on remediation.
- Verify identity at both beginning and conclusion, with date/time stamps.
- Retain responses, identity evidence and completion records at least five years.
- Give IDFPR administrator-level reviewer access.
- Keep assessment integrity separate from the live PSI licensing examination; do not reproduce or solicit live items.
