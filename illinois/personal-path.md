## Step-by-step — personal Broker endorsement

1. **Choose an existing IDFPR-approved provider** for the 30-hour Broker Pre-License Endorsement Course. Verify the provider and exact course in IDFPR's live registries on the purchase date.
2. **Complete the 30-hour Broker endorsement course.** Obtain a Uniform Real Estate Transcript. The education is currently treated by PSI as valid for two years after satisfactory completion.
3. **Follow the provider/PSI eligibility upload process.** Do not schedule an ordinary national-plus-state Broker exam. The endorsement authority requires the Illinois-specific law exam.
4. **Pass the Illinois-law examination.** Current PSI planning figure: Broker state portion, 40 questions, 90 minutes, 75%. The June 24, 2026 booklet lists the Broker examination at $58 and allows unlimited Broker Endorsement attempts during the two-year education eligibility window. Confirm the exact endorsement product and live price before paying.
5. **Order official California certification/license history** for license 01385024. It must show profession, license number, original issue date, expiration, active/good-standing status, and disciplinary history. IDFPR accepts its own certification page or another official certification containing all requested data.
6. **Complete Form IL 505-0347, Broker.** California is the only other state ever licensed, based on the user's statement. Answer all personal-history questions personally. Enter SSN/ITIN only on the signed form, never in the repository.
7. **Sign the nonresident consent/certifying statement.** California residency makes this attachment applicable.
8. **Choose status.** Without an Illinois Sponsor Card, an approved license is issued inactive. Include a completed Sponsor Card only if the user has deliberately selected an actively licensed Illinois sponsoring brokerage.
9. **Pay the $150 nonrefundable Broker fee through IDFPR ePay** and record the approved payment number on the cover page.
10. **Email the signed application, payment page, URET, exam score report, official license history and nonresident consent** to the current address printed on the form, `FPR.Realestate@illinois.gov`, or mail to the printed PO box. Recheck the current form on the official hub the day of filing.
11. **Retain the complete transmitted packet and delivery evidence.** PSI instructs a passing candidate to apply to IDFPR within one year after passing. Submission is not issuance.
