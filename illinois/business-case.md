# Illinois business case

## Decision

Illinois is commercially credible, but it is not a cheap “single-course” expansion. The demand base is large; the regulatory entry package is also unusually broad because a provider that wants the 30-hour endorsement course must first provide the full seven-course pre/post portfolio.

## Official demand evidence

- IDFPR Board minutes reported **59,006 active Brokers** and **13,898 active Managing Brokers** as of February 2026: **72,904 combined**.
- The FY2024 IDFPR report recorded **4,212 Broker applications** and **3,972 Broker licenses granted**.
- That report also recorded **449 Managing Broker applications / 421 granted**, **8 education-provider applications / 6 granted**, and **57 pre-license-instructor applications / 45 granted**.

The FY2024 application and grant counts are not a cohort conversion rate: IDFPR warns that an application opened in one year can be decided in another. The February 2026 counts are a dated snapshot, not a September 2026 live total.

## Commercial interpretation

The 72,904 active Broker/Managing Broker population supports recurring post-license and CE demand as well as prelicense demand. The 4,212 annual Broker applications establish a meaningful new-license funnel. These figures do not establish revenue by themselves; price, paid acquisition cost, completion rate and approved product mix remain business assumptions.

## Regulatory cost floor

| Item | Official fee |
|---|---:|
| Illinois foreign LLC admission, LLC-45.5 | $150 |
| Initial education-provider license | $1,050 |
| Education-provider virtual office | $200 |
| Seven required pre/post course applications | $1,050 |
| One optional endorsement course | $150 |
| User's pre-license instructor application | $150 |
| User's Broker endorsement application | $150 |
| **Known government filing subtotal** | **$2,850** |

Excluded: Illinois registered-agent service, instructor-training tuition, the 30-hour endorsement course bought from another provider, PSI exam charges, content production, legal review, and any resubmission/revision costs. Adding both Broker and Managing Broker endorsement courses would add another $150.

## Go/no-go view

**GO for Phase 0 and portfolio development. NO-GO for filing fees and sales today.** The commercial opportunity is real, but the sensible order is: personal instructor credential, Illinois foreign-LLC admission, seven-course portfolio and LMS evidence, provider/course submission, approvals, then launch. The optional endorsement product should not drive the first filing unless IDFPR confirms simultaneous submission is allowed.
