## Separate performance duties and termination rights

Several contract provisions can operate at once. Earnest money is not the same thing as the option fee. Under paragraph 5, timely payment of earnest money does not cure a missing option fee. If an amount is omitted or the option fee is not timely delivered, paragraph 5D removes the unrestricted option right. That does not automatically erase every other obligation under the sale contract.

For a valid option exercised with timely notice under paragraph 5B, the option fee is not refunded and earnest money is refunded. A buyer’s reason for terminating may be unrestricted during a valid option, but the act of termination still has notice and timing requirements. Paragraph 21 calls for written notices. The option has a specific 5 p.m. property-local-time deadline. A voicemail or an internal calendar entry should never be assumed to satisfy a required written act.

“As Is” addresses acceptance of condition; it is not a command to stop investigating. Paragraph 7D preserves inspection, negotiation of a repair amendment and a valid option termination. Those are separate rights. A repair request alone does not obligate the seller or extend a deadline. If the option expires while parties exchange repair proposals, the ongoing conversation is not itself an extension.

For example, an inspector finds a $4,000 problem on day six of a seven-day option. The buyer sends a repair request. The transaction now needs two tracks: negotiate the requested repair and preserve the buyer’s existing decision deadline. A signed amendment may change obligations. Silence, optimism and an agent’s promise do not.

Paragraph 19 also matters after signing: contractual representations survive closing, and an untrue seller representation at closing is addressed as a default. Do not promise that signing the deed extinguishes every earlier representation.
