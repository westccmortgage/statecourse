## Authority, contract choices, and written terms

A real estate agent assists the parties in expressing their decisions using authorized documents. That role does not include inventing legal protections or determining the legal effect of a disputed instrument. [TREC’s publication of rule 537.11](https://www.trec.texas.gov/agency-information/rules-and-laws/trec-rules) distinguishes informational items from language defining rights, duties or remedies. It also requires the agent to advise principals that the instrument is binding. TREC identifies the Secretary of State’s Texas Administrative Code as the official rule text; TREC’s page is the Commission’s convenient publication of it.

Suppose a buyer asks the agent to write a lifetime foundation warranty. The buyer’s concern is legitimate, but the requested language creates future liability. The agent should help the buyer explain the concern to the buyer’s attorney. A clause copied from another transaction remains legal language; changing the author’s name does not make it an informational blank.

There are exceptions to mandatory use of Commission forms. One concerns an owner-required contract prepared by the owner’s attorney. Another set of requirements applies when no mandatory form exists. The agent must identify the applicable exception and document source; an exception is not a general permission to improvise. A principal may specifically instruct a conspicuous change in writing under rule 537.11(d)(2). That limited clerical action still differs from the agent recommending the legal wording or guaranteeing its effect.

The written document set matters. Paragraph 22 of 20-19 identifies the complete agreement and requires the parties’ written agreement for changes. If a signed October 20 closing is discussed for October 30 by telephone, record the amendment as pending until the agreement is properly documented. Keep the operative date on the calendar while arranging the proposed change. Match the checked addenda to the actual attachments, and retain the complete executed set.

Practice method: identify the decision maker, the existing signed term, the requested change and the document needed. Never substitute an agent’s convenience for the parties’ instruction.
