## Texas financing approval and contract protection

TREC’s current Third Party Financing Addendum is [40-11](https://www.trec.texas.gov/forms/third-party-financing-addendum-0), effective January 3, 2025 as reviewed. It is used for financing supplied by a third party. Seller financing and assumption involve different documents and issues. This lesson focuses on the selected third-party clauses, not every financing arrangement.

Paragraph 1 requires prompt application and reasonable efforts to obtain approval, including lender-required information. A contingency is not permission to withhold bank statements until the last minute. Keep the application timeline and document requests in the file.

Paragraph 2 separates Buyer Approval from Property Approval. An income-and-credit approval does not establish that the lender accepts the property. Property issues can include appraisal, insurability and required repairs. A preapproval letter should be read for conditions rather than announced as unconditional final approval.

Where the contract is subject to Buyer Approval, paragraph 2A provides a negotiated deadline and requires the buyer to give the seller both termination notice and a copy of the lender’s written reasons for the determination. If the buyer does not timely use that route, the contract ceases to be subject to Buyer Approval under that paragraph. Do not confuse “the lender declined” with “the contract has already terminated.”

Paragraph 2B has its own property-approval deadline: on or before the third day before the Closing Date, with notice and lender reasons, unless another applicable provision changes the analysis. In paragraph 1G, Other Financing includes a specific choice about waiving all 2B termination rights for that loan. Never preselect that waiver silently. Explain where the choice appears, obtain the buyer’s direction and recommend legal advice when needed.

Paragraph 4 contains distinct FHA/VA provisions, including its own treatment of the three-day property-approval notice requirement. Do not copy conventional-loan conclusions into those transactions. This collection tests the ordinary 2B path only where the question says no special waiver applies. Full FHA/VA program underwriting, Texas home-equity rules and seller-finance compliance require additional instruction.
