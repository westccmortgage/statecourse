Apply to offer Real Estate QE Courses

QE providers must:

  • Submit a qualifying course application for each course that they propose to offer
  • Not offer a qualifying course until you have received a course approval letter from the Commission
Offering Real Estate QE Courses
Apply by Mail
  • Complete the Qualifying Real Estate Course Application
  • Complete the corresponding Qualifying Real Estate Course Approval Form
  • Pay the required fee
  • Checklists
  • List of Real Estate QE Courses

Use the below checklist to ensure the application is complete:

  • Real Estate QE Classroom Course
  • Real Estate QE Distance Education Course
Below is a list of all real estate QE courses:
  • Principles of Real Estate I
  • Principles of Real Estate II
  • Law of Agency
  • Law of Contracts
  • Promulgated Contract Forms
  • Real Estate Finance
  • Real Estate Brokerage
  • Real Estate Appraisal
  • Real Estate Law
  • Real Estate Marketing
  • Real Estate Math
  • Residential Property Management
  • Real Estate Investments
  • Residential Inspection for Real Estate Agents

 

Offering Real Estate QE Secondary Courses

QE providers may request approval to offer a QE course approved for another QE provider. Permission to offer the course must be included.

  • Application
  • Checklists
Apply by Mail
  • Complete the Real Estate QE Secondary Course Application
  • Complete the corresponding Qualifying Real Estate Course Approval Form
  • Pay the required fee

Use the below checklist to ensure the application is complete:

  • Real Estate QE Secondary Classroom Course
  • Real Estate QE Secondary Distance Education Course
