§535.131 Unlawful Conduct; Splitting Fees
A broker licensed in Texas may cooperate with a foreign broker and share earned commissions with a foreign broker.
Only Texas license holders may handle negotiations physically conducted within Texas.
A resident of a foreign country or territory that does not require a person to be licensed to act as a real estate broker is considered to be licensed as a foreign broker for the purposes of §1101.651 of the Act, if the person practices as a real estate broker in compliance with the law of the foreign country or territory.
