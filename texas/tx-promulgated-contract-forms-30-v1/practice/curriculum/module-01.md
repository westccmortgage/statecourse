# Texas Promulgated Contract Forms: a practical form-reading workshop

This collection contains four lessons and 24 original practice questions. It is free practice on selected current forms, not the complete 30-hour qualifying course and not licensing credit. Source review: September 6, 2026. The worked dates and amounts are fictional.

Download the [current resale form 20-19](https://www.trec.texas.gov/sites/default/files/pdf-forms/20-19_4.pdf) and compare it with [TREC’s current contract inventory](https://www.trec.texas.gov/node/34). Read the printed page numbers; a PDF viewer may count its pages differently. Do not fill or sign a real transaction document as part of this exercise.


## Select the current document for the transaction

Choose a contract by its transaction category before completing its blanks. A previously occupied duplex being resold belongs in the one-to-four-family resale category. A condominium is a different ownership form even when only one unit is sold. A new home sold by a builder has completed-construction and incomplete-construction forms. A vacant residential lot has an unimproved-property form. A farm or ranch has its own form. “One family buying” or “four bedrooms” does not determine the legal category.

As reviewed on September 6, 2026, the current inventory lists resale 20-19, condominium resale 30-18, completed new home 24-20, incomplete new home 23-20, unimproved property 9-18 and farm/ranch 25-17. These listed contracts took effect July 1, 2026. Check the current index when preparing a later transaction; the numbers in this lesson are dated evidence, not a promise that forms never change.

An amendment modifies an existing agreement; it does not replace the need for the initial contract. TREC’s current Amendment to Contract is 39-11. Paragraph 22 of 20-19 requires the parties’ written agreement to change terms. If the parties change price after execution, reconcile the amendment with the existing contract and attachments. Do not silently edit an executed PDF or discard the original.

Worked selection: a buyer wants a resale condominium and also needs a loan. Start with the condominium resale category, then evaluate the applicable financing addendum. Do not start with 20-19 merely because it is familiar. The form library is a set of documents with different purposes, and an addendum supplements the selected contract.
