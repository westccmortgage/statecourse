# Instructor qualification dossier — Texas qualifying education

**Status: template. No instructor has been approached, named, or recorded. No dossier exists.**

**The provider documents the qualification, and the provider is answerable for it.** This project
does not use the idea of a universal current "TREC instructor licence"; no source read here creates
one. What the provider must be able to show is that a named person is qualified **for the specified
course**.

**The three routes are drawn from the Texas work order's statement of § 535.63. The rule itself
could not be read — 22 TAC is unreachable from this environment — so each route below is marked
UNVERIFIED and must be confirmed against the adopted text before any determination is signed.**

---

## The routes — a candidate satisfies one

**Route 1 — degree and teaching.** *(UNVERIFIED against § 535.63)*
- a college degree in the subject area, and
- three years of teaching or training experience.

**Route 2 — licensed experience and teaching.** *(UNVERIFIED)*
- five years of active Texas licence-holder experience, and
- three years of teaching or training experience.

**Route 3 — provider-documented equivalent.** *(UNVERIFIED)*
- professional experience, research, authorship, or other significant real estate endeavours,
  documented by the provider as equivalent.

**Specialised subjects.** For law, finance, appraisal, investments, taxation and other specialised
subjects, collect specialised training or work-experience evidence in addition to the route.
