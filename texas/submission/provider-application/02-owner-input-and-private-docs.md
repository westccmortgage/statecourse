# Owner-controlled and private documents

This list is the handoff between the repository packet and Anatoliy Kanevsky. Items remain outside Git and are inserted only in the regulator upload or physical filing.

## Gather once

- IRS EIN confirmation letter for StateCourse LLC.
- Government-issued identification if requested by REALM or a background form.
- Current ownership list and percentage owned by each member.
- Résumé/CV and California broker licence history for the instructor dossier.
- Any legal/background explanations and supporting orders for a “yes” answer; otherwise the signed negative answers.
- Bank evidence with all but the last four digits of account numbers redacted where TREC accepts redaction.
- CPA/bookkeeper-signed or finalized financial statements.
- Original surety bond and a scan of the complete executed bond.
- Texas registered-agent consent and the executed/notarized power of attorney.

## Do not submit until checked

- The legal name on every bank, bond, Texas registration and application document must be exactly `StateCourse LLC`.
- The bond obligee must be the Texas Real Estate Commission.
- The Certificate of Fact must be current for the TREC filing; the published checklist says no older than 30 days.
- The owner and Operations Manager must both sign where the checklist requires both signatures, even if the same person holds both roles.
- Never email private records to TREC unless TREC specifically instructs it; current REALM guidance says requested documentation should be uploaded in the portal.
