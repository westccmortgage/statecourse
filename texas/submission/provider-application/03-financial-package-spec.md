## Review before filing

1. All three financial statements/budget use the same legal entity and consistent reporting period.
2. Cash in the balance sheet reconciles to the operating and reserve evidence.
3. The reserve is not counted as first-year revenue and is not spent in the operating budget.
4. The budget separately shows regulator filing costs, Texas compliance/registered-agent costs, bond premium, technology, course production, instruction/student support, marketing, accounting/legal and contingency.
5. “Original $20,000 bond” is not recorded as a $20,000 cash expense; only the quoted bond premium is an expense.
6. No document claims that StateCourse is approved or may enroll students.
