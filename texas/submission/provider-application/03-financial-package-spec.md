# Financial package specification

The current TREC provider page and its published initial-provider checklist require the following six items. The $10,000 reserve is an asset and liquidity requirement, not an application fee or an expense.

| Deliverable | Minimum acceptance rule |
|---|---|
| Current income statement | StateCourse LLC legal name; current period and year-to-date; GAAP basis; preparer and preparation date identified |
| Current balance sheet | same “as of” date used by the bank evidence where practical; assets, liabilities and equity reconcile |
| First-year proposed budget | monthly or quarterly revenue and expense plan; filing fees and operating costs included; assumptions stated |
| Market survey | anticipated first-year enrollment stated as students and course enrollments; method and source dates stated |
| Operating bank account | StateCourse LLC account; evidence of funds sufficient for the plan; account number redacted except last four digits if allowed |
| Reserve account | StateCourse LLC account holding at least **$10,000**; evidence dated close to filing |

## Review before filing

1. All three financial statements use the same legal entity and consistent reporting period.
2. Cash in the balance sheet reconciles to the operating and reserve evidence.
3. The reserve is not counted as first-year revenue and is not spent in the operating budget.
4. The budget separately shows regulator filing costs, Texas entity registration, bond premium, technology, course production, instruction/student support, marketing, accounting/legal and contingency.
5. “Original $20,000 bond” is not recorded as a $20,000 cash expense; only the quoted bond premium is an expense.
6. No document claims that StateCourse is approved or may enroll students.
