## Revenue model — owner input required

No filing revenue number is stated until tuition and fees are approved as business decisions.

Let:

- `T` = final tuition per 30-hour course;
- `F` = any separately disclosed mandatory fee per course, if adopted;
- `E_q` = approved course enrollments available in quarter `q`.

Then:

- Quarterly gross tuition revenue = `E_q × T`
- Quarterly gross mandatory-fee revenue = `E_q × F`
- Base-case annual gross revenue, if all six courses are available for the full year = `600 × (T + F)`

No revenue may be recognized in this forecast as regulatory-credit revenue before the provider and applicable course approvals are actually effective.
