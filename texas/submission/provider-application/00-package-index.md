## Filing sequence

1. Register StateCourse LLC as a foreign LLC in Texas and appoint a consenting Texas registered agent.
2. Order a Texas Certificate of Fact — Status dated no more than 30 days before the TREC filing.
3. Open or identify the operating account and a separate reserve account holding at least $10,000.
4. Obtain the original $20,000 school surety bond payable to TREC.
5. Have a CPA/bookkeeper finalize the GAAP income statement, balance sheet and first-year budget.
6. Complete the private owner/principal/background fields and execute the Principal Information Form.
7. Designate the Operations Manager and Records Manager; for the current one-owner structure the recommended draft designation is Anatoliy Kanevsky for both.
8. Execute the notarized Texas power of attorney required for an out-of-state provider and identify the Texas resident who will accept service and act as Texas records custodian.
9. Finalize tuition, fees and refund terms in the pre-enrollment agreement.
10. Log in to REALM and reconcile the effective 09/03/2025 application fields and filing channel against this packet before paying or submitting.

## Course submission order

The current TREC course page says approved QE providers submit a separate application for each course and may not offer a course before its approval letter. The operating order is therefore:

1. file and secure provider approval;
2. submit the first fully complete course package — Law of Agency is the strongest completed package;
3. submit Principles of Real Estate II;
4. finish and submit Principles of Real Estate I;
5. finish Law of Contracts, Promulgated Contract Forms and Real Estate Finance, then file each separately.

Course authoring continues during provider review. Enrollment, payment and Texas credit remain disabled until both provider and course approvals exist.
