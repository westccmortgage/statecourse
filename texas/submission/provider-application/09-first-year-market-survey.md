# First-year market survey and anticipated enrollment

**Applicant:** StateCourse LLC  
**Market:** Texas sales-agent qualifying education, distance delivery  
**Forecast period:** first 12 months after the first course approvals  
**Recommended filing forecast:** **100 unique students / 600 course enrollments** if all six 30-hour courses are approved and offered for the full period.

## Observed demand

TREC's current provider exam-passage dataset covers 12/1/2023 through 11/30/2025 and reports **29,980 first-attempt sales-agent examinations** attributed to education providers, or approximately **14,990 per year**. It also reports 2,326 broker examinations, which are not included in this sales-agent forecast.

The exam count is a conservative market proxy, not an enrollment count: it excludes students who do not reach the licensing exam and groups results by the provider from which the student took the majority of qualifying courses.

## Competitive shape

The same dataset shows strong concentration: AceableAgent accounts for 8,516 sales-agent examinations and Champions School of Real Estate for 15,547 during the two-year window. A new provider therefore enters a large but competitive market and should not forecast rapid share capture without advertising evidence.

## Forecast method

- Annual market proxy: 14,990 sales-agent first attempts.
- StateCourse year-one student target: 100.
- Implied share of the annual proxy: approximately **0.67%**.
- Required curriculum: six separate 30-hour courses.
- If each student buys and completes the full pathway: **600 course enrollments**.

Ramp assumption: 25 students in the first quarter after approval, then 25 per quarter. This is deliberately lower than a mature-provider forecast and is suitable for a newly approved brand with no Texas historical enrollments.
