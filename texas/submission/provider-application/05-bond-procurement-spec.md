# Texas real-estate-school bond procurement specification

Provide this specification to the surety broker. It prevents the most common wrong-product quote.

## Required bond

- Principal: **StateCourse LLC**.
- Obligee/payee: **Texas Real Estate Commission**.
- Bond type: qualifying real-estate education provider / real-estate school bond.
- Penal sum (face amount): **$20,000**.
- Delivery: original instrument with original or raised seal, plus a complete scan for the electronic record.
- Effective date: coordinated with the provider application.
- Cancellation/nonrenewal: broker must state notice procedure and lead time so the compliance calendar can track it.

## Quote request must include

- annual premium;
- underwriting documents required;
- whether a personal indemnity is required;
- renewal premium and renewal process;
- time to issue the original sealed instrument;
- mailing method and replacement-original procedure.

## Reject the bond if

- the principal is written as the StateCourse brand without `LLC`;
- the obligee is not TREC;
- the face amount is not exactly $20,000;
- only a digital certificate is supplied when TREC requires the original sealed instrument;
- the effective date leaves a gap before approval or during the provider term.

The $20,000 amount is not a cash deposit. StateCourse budgets the quoted premium and separately maintains the $10,000 reserve account.
