## Timing and examination-review limits

The 140-word-per-minute figure and exercise allowances are provider planning assumptions. The new evidence model counts actual authored instruments and disjoint text segments; optional material, metadata, bibliographies, replacements, remediation and final examinations cannot fill a required-instruction deficit. A surplus in one topic cannot repair another. No pilot time study has yet been performed.

Every protected final receives nearest-formative-text candidates for substantive review. A low similarity score does not establish that the underlying fact pattern or reasoning is different. Legal correctness, calculation accuracy, and significant exam separation require content review in addition to structural tests.
