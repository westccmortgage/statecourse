# Quiz bank, keys and remediation

All items are objective and have one definitive keyed answer. Production delivery draws versioned forms and withholds the key until submission. The explanations below populate required remediation. Three quizzes × 20 items = 60 items.

## Quiz 1 — Maine Commission and agency relationships

1. Which body prescribes Maine real estate education standards? A) Registry of Deeds B) Real Estate Commission C) MLS D) Title insurer. **Key: B.** The Commission oversees licensing and education; private organizations do not.
2. A consumer complaint is filed. Which statement is correct? A) It automatically revokes a license B) It begins a process that may involve investigation and hearing C) It is decided by the MLS D) It is a criminal conviction. **Key: B.** Allegation and final disciplinary action are distinct.
3. Which source controls licensure conduct? A) Office tradition B) Commission statute/rules C) Franchise slogan D) Social-media advice. **Key: B.** Ethics/office/MLS rules may also apply but do not replace law.
4. A sales agent may practice independently of an agency. A) True B) False. **Key: B.** A Maine sales agent practices through the licensed agency/designated-broker structure.
5. Which record best shows a client relationship was created? A) A handshake B) A written brokerage agreement satisfying Maine requirements C) A yard sign D) A commission invoice. **Key: B.** Maine client agency requires the required written agreement.
6. Which statement about compensation is correct? A) Whoever pays is always the client B) Compensation alone does not determine agency C) Compensation eliminates disclosure D) Only clients may pay. **Key: B.** Relationship is determined by law and agreement.
7. An advertisement hides the agency identity and implies the licensee owns the listed home. Primary concern? A) Misleading advertising B) Proration C) Zoning variance D) Depreciation. **Key: A.** Advertising must accurately present the brokerage context.
8. A licensee is buying property for personal benefit. What is required? A) Conceal status B) Disclose self-interest as required C) Use a net listing D) Become an appraiser. **Key: B.** Self-interest presents a disclosure/conflict issue.
9. A net listing is best described as: A) Commission-approved B) Prohibited minimum-practice concern C) A deed D) A survey. **Key: B.** The Sales Agent outline identifies net listings as prohibited.
10. Which source is strongest for a recorded easement? A) Tax card B) Registry/title record C) Listing photograph D) Verbal rumor. **Key: B.** Recorded title documents, subject to professional review, address recorded easements.
11. A mortgage loan inspection necessarily proves the boundary. A) True B) False. **Key: B.** It is not automatically a boundary survey.
12. “Unknown” on a disclosure permits ignoring an observed moisture problem. A) True B) False. **Key: B.** Red flags require honest follow-up/escalation; unknown is not concealment.
13. Maine's default non-agency relationship identified in the outline is: A) Undisclosed dual agency B) Transaction brokerage C) Universal agency D) Subagency. **Key: B.** The course covers the presumption of transaction brokerage.
14. Which is a fiduciary duty? A) Loyalty B) Price fixing C) Concealment of material defects D) False advertising. **Key: A.** Loyalty is a core duty to a client, bounded by law.
15. A buyer tells the buyer agent the maximum price. The agent should generally: A) Publish it B) Protect it as confidential unless authorized/required C) Tell the seller agent automatically D) Place it in advertising. **Key: B.** Confidential client information is protected.
16. Which document explains brokerage relationship options but is not itself every required brokerage agreement? A) Real Estate Brokerage Relationships Form B) Deed C) Closing Disclosure D) Tax card. **Key: A.** The form is a consumer disclosure/education tool.
17. Disclosed dual agency can be handled by simply remaining silent. A) True B) False. **Key: B.** It requires prescribed disclosure/consent procedures.
18. Appointed agency is: A) An undocumented desk assignment B) A relationship managed under law and company policy C) A deed covenant D) An appraisal method. **Key: B.** Appointment requires formal procedures and documentation.
19. After agency ends, confidentiality may survive. A) True B) False. **Key: A.** Termination does not necessarily erase continuing duties.
20. When a consumer asks for a custom legal clause, the safest course is: A) Invent one B) Use unauthorized advice C) Identify the issue and refer to counsel within company policy D) Ignore it. **Key: C.** Licensees avoid unauthorized practice of law.

## Quiz 2 — Product knowledge and valuation

1. An item attached and adapted to a building with intent to remain is most likely: A) Fixture B) Emblement C) License D) Easement. **Key: A.** Attachment, adaptation and intent support fixture treatment.
2. Which is not a legal description by itself? A) Metes and bounds B) Lot on recorded plan C) Street address D) Rectangular survey. **Key: C.** Street address is not necessarily the legal land description.
3. A life estate measured by another person's life is: A) Fee simple B) Pur autre vie C) Tenancy at sufferance D) Easement. **Key: B.** The measuring life belongs to someone other than the life tenant.
4. A future interest retained by the grantor after a life estate is generally a: A) Reversion B) Lease C) Lien D) Fixture. **Key: A.** A reversion returns possession to the grantor/estate after the life interest.
5. Which leasehold has a definite ending date? A) Estate for years B) Periodic estate C) Tenancy at sufferance D) Tenancy at will. **Key: A.** Estate for years runs for a fixed term.
6. Zoning is an exercise of: A) Private covenant B) Police power C) Escheat D) Accession. **Key: B.** Government land-use regulation is police power.
7. A private covenant permits short-term rentals but zoning prohibits them. Which controls use? A) The less restrictive rule only B) Both must be satisfied C) The listing D) Neither. **Key: B.** Compliance with one layer does not eliminate the other.
8. Which source is best for municipal zoning status? A) Local governing authority B) Lender's estimate C) Seller's memory only D) Appliance manual. **Key: A.** Verify with current municipal records/officials.
9. In unorganized territory, the outline identifies which agency? A) LUPC B) FHA C) MLS D) EEOC. **Key: A.** The Land Use Planning Commission is a key Maine source.
10. A licensee sees possible mold. Appropriate action? A) Diagnose species B) Conceal it C) Report known observation and recommend qualified evaluation D) Guarantee safety. **Key: C.** Licensees disclose/verify without diagnosing.
11. Which deed provides the broadest traditional covenants? A) General warranty deed B) Quitclaim without covenant C) Tax card D) Lease. **Key: A.** A general warranty deed traditionally carries broad covenants.
12. An undischarged mortgage is a potential: A) Title defect B) House style C) Lease type D) Appraisal principle. **Key: A.** It can encumber title and needs resolution.
13. Market value and assessed value are always identical. A) True B) False. **Key: B.** They serve different purposes and can differ.
14. The principle that a buyer will not pay more than a comparable substitute is: A) Substitution B) Anticipation C) Assemblage D) Progression. **Key: A.** Substitution anchors comparison behavior.
15. An improvement costing $40,000 necessarily adds $40,000 in value. A) True B) False. **Key: B.** Contribution reflects market response, not cost alone.
16. In the market-data approach, adjustments are generally made to the: A) Subject B) Comparable C) Tax rate D) Mortgage note. **Key: B.** Comparable prices are adjusted to model the subject.
17. NOI divided by capitalization rate estimates: A) Value B) Loan points C) Tax D) Commission. **Key: A.** Direct capitalization: value = NOI/rate.
18. GRM should be applied to: A) Gross rent using a market-derived multiplier B) NOI C) Depreciated cost D) Tax assessment only. **Key: A.** Do not mix gross and net measures.
19. An obsolete floor plan is usually: A) Physical depreciation B) Functional depreciation C) External depreciation D) No depreciation. **Key: B.** Design inadequacy is functional.
20. A sales agent's CMA is automatically a licensed appraisal. A) True B) False. **Key: B.** Scope, representation and applicable appraisal law matter.

## Quiz 3 — Financing, contracts and closing bridge

1. Loans are originated in the: A) Primary market B) Secondary market only C) Registry D) MLS. **Key: A.** The primary market is where borrower and originator transact.
2. The document evidencing the borrower's debt is the: A) Note B) Deed C) Survey D) Listing. **Key: A.** The mortgage secures; the note evidences debt.
3. A clause allowing the lender to call the balance after default is: A) Acceleration B) Defeasance C) Habendum D) Alienation by will. **Key: A.** Acceleration makes the debt due under its terms.
4. Which program is associated with eligible veterans? A) VA B) FHA only C) FIRPTA D) ADA. **Key: A.** VA-guaranteed financing serves eligible borrowers.
5. A balloon loan generally has: A) No balance at maturity B) A remaining lump sum at maturity C) No interest D) No documents. **Key: B.** Scheduled payments may not fully amortize the debt.
6. Two discount points on a $200,000 loan equal: A) $400 B) $2,000 C) $4,000 D) $20,000. **Key: C.** One point is 1% of principal; two points = $4,000.
7. A side agreement hiding seller financing from the lender is acceptable. A) True B) False. **Key: B.** False/undisclosed terms create fraud and underwriting risk.
8. A contract lacking lawful purpose cannot be valid. A) True B) False. **Key: A.** Lawful purpose is a formation requirement.
9. A counteroffer generally: A) Accepts unchanged B) Proposes changed terms C) Records a deed D) Issues title insurance. **Key: B.** It rejects/modifies the original proposal in ordinary analysis.
10. Communicated acceptance is important because it may: A) Create the contract under applicable facts B) Set zoning C) Issue a license D) Appraise property. **Key: A.** Communication and timing affect formation/effective date.
11. The Statute of Frauds commonly requires real-estate agreements to be: A) Oral B) Written C) Advertised D) Recorded before signing. **Key: B.** Covered agreements generally require writing.
12. Which can terminate a contract? A) Full performance B) Expiration C) Unmet contingency D) All of these. **Key: D.** Each can end obligations depending on terms/law.
13. A seller client agreement creating agency must be: A) Written as required B) A text rumor C) Only an MLS entry D) A title policy. **Key: A.** Maine requires the client brokerage agreement in writing.
14. An option gives the holder: A) An obligation to buy B) A contractual right to choose within its terms C) A license D) A deed automatically. **Key: B.** The holder may elect under the option terms.
15. A right of first refusal is identical to an option in every respect. A) True B) False. **Key: B.** It typically depends on a triggering event/third-party proposal.
16. A licensee asked to invent a custom legal remedy should: A) Draft it regardless B) Refer to counsel and follow approved-form authority C) Alter signatures D) Ignore deadlines. **Key: B.** Avoid unauthorized practice.
17. Contract deadlines should be managed with: A) Memory only B) A documented calendar and notice protocol C) A tax card D) A deed covenant. **Key: B.** Reliable controls protect performance.
18. A Closing Disclosure is associated with: A) Covered mortgage settlement disclosures B) Zoning variance C) Brokerage licensing D) Survey monuments. **Key: A.** It is part of TILA-RESPA integrated disclosure.
19. A proration allocates: A) A shared periodic item between parties B) A license C) An easement D) Agency loyalty. **Key: A.** Taxes/rents/fuel may be divided by time/quantity.
20. Title defects should be resolved by the sales agent giving legal advice. A) True B) False. **Key: B.** Escalate to title/legal professionals.
