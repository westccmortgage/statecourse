The student course page presents, in order: Course Overview; Your Progress; required active time; instructor; learning objectives; lesson; key terms; Maine Law Note; real-world example; case study; knowledge check; quiz/remediation; next lesson.

## How to study each unit

1. Read the objective before starting the timer.
2. Work through the lesson in sequence; use linked definitions and current official sources.
3. Complete the interaction (classification, calculation, source map or decision tree).
4. Answer the knowledge check. An incorrect response produces explanation and a directed reread.
5. Send substantive course questions through “Ask the Instructor.”
6. Complete the unit checkpoint; only server-verified active minutes carry forward.

## Core reference shelf

- Current Maine Real Estate Commission statutes and Rules Chapters 360, 390, 400 and 410.
- Current Commission Sales Agent Course outline/guidelines.
- Current Maine property disclosure and Real Estate Brokerage Relationships forms/guidance.
- Commission offer/counteroffer guidance and current federal consumer sources referenced in the text.

Final filing records exact edition/effective dates. Students receive links, accessible copies where licensed/permitted, and change notices.

## Learning tools

- **Key term:** concise definition with context and cross-link.
- **Maine law note:** state-specific rule or source; never substituted with Virginia text.
- **Regulatory note:** what must be documented or escalated.
- **Real-world example:** facts followed by a decision sequence, not legal advice.
- **Knowledge check:** low-stakes item with explanation.
- **Case study:** required integrated work retained in the gradebook.
- **Progress:** instruction minutes, assessment minutes, completed objectives and remaining gates.

## Student responsibilities

Use your legal identity; maintain sole control of the account; do not share or reproduce secure questions; remain actively engaged; give truthful responses; ask for clarification; keep contact information current; complete evaluation. Payment does not grant course completion.

## Help and accessibility

Technical and instructor contact, response hours, accommodation-request path and escalation contact are **pending named provider fields before filing**. Timers pause during support failures so students are not credited or penalized for unverified time. Accessibility does not reduce competencies, minutes or assessment standards; it changes delivery where reasonable and approved.

## Course-end checklist

- 3,000 verified instruction minutes
- 300 verified assessment minutes
- all unit interactions complete
- three quizzes complete with required remediation
- two case studies complete
- final exam complete
- numeric final course grade ≥75%
- integrity exceptions resolved
- course evaluation offered/completed status recorded
- approval active on completion date before qualifying certificate issuance
