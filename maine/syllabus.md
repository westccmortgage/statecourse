## Purpose

The course introduces the elements of Maine real estate brokerage and prepares a student to demonstrate the Commission-prescribed Sales Agent competencies. It is qualifying education, not the state licensing examination and not a promise of licensure.

## Learning outcomes

On successful completion, a student will be able to:

1. Explain the Commission's role, licensing structure, enforcement authority and minimum standards of practice.
2. Distinguish client agency, disclosed dual/appointed agency and transaction brokerage and document the relationship correctly.
3. Analyze property interests, descriptions, transfers, title, land use, environmental concerns, construction and disclosure sources.
4. Apply the market-data, income and cost approaches at an entry level while respecting the statutory boundary on appraisal activity.
5. Compare financing markets, instruments, programs, repayment methods and qualification concepts.
6. Identify contract elements; trace offer/counteroffer/acceptance; complete standard agreements without unauthorized practice of law.
7. Interpret the closing process, settlement documents, title/recording, costs, proceeds and prorations.
8. Recognize fair-housing, civil-rights, antitrust, ADA, TILA/ECOA, tax/FIRPTA and marketing-control issues.

## Time, attendance and engagement

Completion requires 3,300 verified active minutes, including 3,000 instruction minutes and 300 assessment minutes. The server—not the browser—owns the ledger. Idle, hidden-tab, disconnected, duplicate-session and skipped-sequence periods do not accrue. See `distance-education-controls.md`. This is not the live-delivery 90% attendance rule.

## Assessments and grade

- Three objective 20-item quizzes (60 items total), given at Hours 15, 34 and 46.
- Immediate explanation and targeted remediation follows every missed objective.
- Two required case studies are embedded in Product Knowledge and Contracts.
- An 80-item objective final exam is administered at the end and weighted to official topic percentages.
- A numeric final course grade of **75% or higher** is required. First-pass weighting is final exam 50%, quizzes 30% (10% each), case studies 20% (10% each); acceptance of this weighting for the submitted distance product is **pending regulator confirmation**.
- The LMS progression threshold is configured to require 75% on each quiz as a conservative quality control; because the Sales Agent guideline expressly requires an overall 75% but does not clearly state a per-quiz gate, this provider rule is labeled **pending regulator confirmation**.

## Remediation and retakes

After a quiz attempt, the learner receives objective-level explanations without exposing the secure final bank. Required remediation reopens the relevant lesson and adds a fresh practice set. Quiz retake rules, attempt limits, waiting periods and final-exam retake/security rules will be finalized in the filed administration policy and written approval; no unapproved retake promise is made.

## Integrity

The student must be the enrolled person, must not share access, and must not use unauthorized assistance or reproduce secure items. Risk events trigger re-authentication and instructor review. Confirmed misconduct may invalidate an attempt subject to documented due process. See `identity-integrity.md`.

## Student support and instructor access

Every content unit includes “Ask the Instructor.” Responses are attached to the enrollment audit record. Named instructor, support channels and response-time commitment are **pending before filing**.

## Completion record

After all required minutes, units, quizzes, cases and final examination are complete and the server calculates a final grade of at least 75%, an authorized official may issue the Maine qualifying-education certificate/transcript. It contains course title, student legal name, numeric grade, start/completion dates and signature. Issuance is disabled until course approval is active.

## Important licensing notice

The Commission's current rules require a Sales Agent applicant to complete the course with a minimum final grade of 75% and pass the licensing examination within one year after course completion. Students should verify current application and examination requirements directly with the Commission and Pearson VUE.
