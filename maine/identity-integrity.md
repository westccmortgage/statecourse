# Student identity and academic-integrity procedures

## Controls

1. **Enrollment identity:** collect legal name, address and contact fields needed for the Commission enrollment report; verify email/phone; record identity assertion and policy version.
2. **Account access:** unique account, strong credential/passkey support, MFA/risk challenge, device/session log; never permit shared credentials.
3. **Re-entry:** require authentication after inactivity, new device, risk event and before each graded assessment.
4. **Continuity:** detect impossible concurrent sessions, abnormal answer speed, automation patterns and repeated device/account switching. Signals prompt review; they do not alone establish misconduct.
5. **Assessment security:** versioned pools, randomized choice order where pedagogically safe, one active attempt, server timestamps, copy/print restrictions as deterrents, no answer key before submission.
6. **Final exam:** final identity/proctor/security pathway is **pending Commission acceptance**. If remote proctoring is used, vendor, consent, privacy, accommodations and retention are filed before activation.
7. **Attestation:** before each quiz/case/final, student attests that work is their own and assistance complies with policy.

## Incident process

The system records signal, affected activity and evidence. A trained reviewer notifies the student, allows response, distinguishes technical/accessibility facts, and decides with reason. Remedies are proportionate: re-authentication, oral validation, replacement attempt, invalidation or escalation. Student may request second review. Every action is append-only and auditable.

## Privacy and payment separation

Only identity data necessary for education/reporting is stored. Raw card number/CVV are never collected by StateCourse. Stripe identity/payment signals cannot determine study time, grade, exam eligibility, completion or certificate issuance.
