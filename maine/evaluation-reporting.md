# Student evaluation and Commission reporting procedures

## Student evaluation

At conclusion, present the current official Commission evaluation form. Preserve these dimensions: objectives clear/met; organization/depth; recommendation; website navigation; instructions; technical support; free-text most/least useful and comments. The instructor cannot condition grade/certificate on favorable responses.

Individual evaluations remain with the provider and are **not** sent with the routine report under the current official packet. Retain originals and a copy of the instructor summary under the final retention schedule.

## Evaluation summary

Aggregate counts for each 1–5 response, summarize comments without unnecessary identifiers, document planned changes, instructor signature/date and link changes to the controlled-release record. Substantial changes require Commission review/approval before use.

## Enrollment/completion report

Within 30 days after completion of each course administration/cohort, a compliance reviewer prepares the current Commission-approved report containing at least:

- provider/school and instructor;
- course and delivery method;
- start and end dates;
- total initially enrolled, dropped, incomplete and grade ≥75%;
- each student's full legal name, address and status (dropped, incomplete or numeric grade);
- instructor comments and signature/date;
- instructor summary of evaluations.

The LMS opens a due-date task on completion, supports reviewer reconciliation to enrollment/grade/certificate ledgers, records submission method/date and preserves proof of delivery. If continuously available students have individual dates, StateCourse will obtain written Commission direction on report grouping before launch. **Grouping/“course administration” treatment is pending regulator confirmation.**

## Transcript/certificate

Provide every satisfactory student a signed record with course title, student name, final numeric grade, beginning and completion date; the StateCourse template also includes school, instructor and unique serial consistent with the application packet.
