# Instructor manual

## Instructor status

Instructor of record, biography, contact information, qualifications and résumé are **pending before filing**. The provider must demonstrate ability and competence to teach this curriculum in the Chapter 360 submission.

## Role

The instructor answers content questions, reviews cases/remediation, monitors assessment and integrity exceptions, approves educational corrections, participates in current-law review and signs/authorizes records only within delegated authority. The instructor does not alter server time, prices, payment status or regulatory approval fields.

## Opening protocol

1. Confirm the currently approved/pending Commission statement.
2. Confirm each student received the Commission guideline, syllabus, objectives, technology requirements, instructor contact, integrity policy and deadline.
3. Do not display the “approved” statement until the approval letter is recorded.
4. Confirm no recruiting content exists on the platform.

## Assessment administration

- Quiz items are drawn from versioned objective pools; no verbatim recycling into final.
- After quiz submission, review objective-level explanation and assign remediation. Do not disclose future secure items.
- Cases use the published rubrics. Record feedback against criteria, not intuition.
- Final opens only after all instruction and cases. Security/proctor method is **pending Commission acceptance**.
- Grade changes require reason, old/new value, instructor identity, timestamp and second-level admin approval.

## Instructor response process

Questions are categorized as content, technical, accommodation, integrity or legal-advice boundary. Content replies cite the course unit/official source. Legal questions receive an educational explanation and referral—not a legal conclusion. SLA and coverage schedule are **pending before filing**.

## Content currency

Before launch and at least annually, plus after Commission notices/rule changes, instructor and compliance owner compare course against official sources. Substantial changes are placed in “awaiting Commission approval” and are not released until approved.

## Incident handling

Do not make a guilt finding from a single signal. Preserve evidence; pause only affected high-stakes function; notify learner; allow explanation; document decision/appeal. Timer edits are append-only corrections, never silent overwrites.
