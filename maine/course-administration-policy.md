# Maine course administration policy

## Approval and enrollment

StateCourse will not advertise this product as approved, enroll for Maine qualifying credit, begin a licensing-credit class or issue a qualifying certificate before written Commission approval. A sandbox QA account is labeled non-credit and cannot produce a certificate.

## Provider and instructor

Provider-school eligibility, Maine proprietary-school status if applicable, instructor identity and résumé are release gates. Substitute instructors require qualification review and any required advance Commission approval.

## Course access and deadline

Continuously available asynchronous access is planned. Exact completion-period/expiration term is **pending provider decision and regulator confirmation**. The deadline displayed at enrollment is immutable for that enrollment except a documented accommodation/approved extension procedure.

## Grading

Minimum satisfactory completion is 75%. Proposed weighting: final 50%; quizzes 30%; cases 20%. The provider will file and obtain acceptance of the grading/retake method. Gradebook calculations are server-side and retain every attempt/version.

## Refunds/cancellation

Maine-specific price, refund and cancellation terms are **pending adoption before enrollment**. They are versioned by course/state and acknowledged before Stripe Checkout. Virginia or national boilerplate is not inherited. Refunds do not erase educational/audit records and Stripe does not decide course completion.

## Instructor access

Students have in-course instructor messaging. Named hours, SLA and emergency coverage are pending. Admins cannot impersonate an instructor without a visible, audited support session.

## Academic integrity

See `identity-integrity.md`. Unauthorized assistance, impersonation, access sharing or secure-item reproduction can invalidate affected work after documented review and appeal.

## Change control

Every release records source snapshot, curriculum version, policy versions, assessment versions and responsible approvers. Substantial syllabus/content/instructor/schedule changes are reported and approved before offering. Emergency legal corrections can suspend new enrollment while current students receive written direction.

## Recruiting prohibition

No course page, instructor message, webinar, community space or virtual platform may recruit students to affiliate with a brokerage. Career information must remain neutral and separate from brokerage solicitation.

## Records

Retain identity, enrollment, policy acknowledgments, access, time, interaction, assessment, remediation, case, grade, evaluation, certificate and reporting audit records according to the final approved retention schedule. The current Chapter 360 text specifies the report/transcript duties but not a universal QE retention period in the passages reviewed; retention duration is therefore **pending regulator/provider records-counsel confirmation**, not imported from CE rules.
