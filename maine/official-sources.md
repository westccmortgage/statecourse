## Important source interpretation

- The current Commission Sales Agent guideline says a distance course shall be ARELLO-certified **or** meet the Commission's stated alternative guidelines. The official Maine sources reviewed do not impose a separately named “IDECC” requirement. Because certification/acceptance can affect filing review, StateCourse records the selected pathway as **pending regulator confirmation** and will include either the current ARELLO/IDECC certificate or written confirmation that the submitted alternative controls are accepted.
- The 2022 packet is an official application artifact but predates the 2025 rules and 2026 outline. It is used only where consistent with those later sources. The current form linked from ME-1 must be downloaded again on the filing date.
- No Commission approval is claimed. Chapter 360 bars advertising as approved, enrolling, or conducting classes for licensing credit before written approval.
