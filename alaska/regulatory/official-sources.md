# Alaska 40-Contact-Hour Prelicense Course — Official Source Register

**Course:** Real estate salesperson prelicense education, 12 AAC 64.063(c)
**Control date:** August 19, 2026
**Source policy:** Alaska Statutes, the Alaska Administrative Code, and current Real Estate Commission forms control. Federal material uses official federal sources. Commercial exam-prep material and competitor outlines establish nothing.

## Why Alaska was chosen

It is the cheapest viable state screened, and for a reason the screening pass did not anticipate: **Alaska prescribes the curriculum itself.** 12 AAC 64.063(c) sets out eight topics with their contact hours, totalling exactly 40. There is no module map to design and defend — the Commission has already written it, and the build's job is to fill it.

Set against the alternatives: Kansas needs a Kansas Board of Regents certificate before its Commission approval is worth anything; Idaho, Mississippi and Montana each gate distance delivery on ARELLO certification; South Dakota is 116 hours; and Arizona is 96 hours behind a lock-down browser and a proctor network. Alaska is 40 hours with no ARELLO requirement anywhere in the education article.

## The curriculum, as the rule states it

12 AAC 64.063(c) — an applicant for an initial real estate licence shall document completion of:

| # | Topic | Contact hours |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 | Licensee relationships, disclosure, and conflict of interest | 6 |
| 2 | Forms of ownership | 4 |
| 3 | Property law, public and private rights and limitations | 4 |
| 4 | Forms of conveyances and recording of documents | 4 |
| 5 | Contracts and transaction documents | 8 |
| 6 | Financing instruments and accounting principles | 6 |
| 7 | Alaska real estate license law and Alaska landlord tenant law | 6 |
| 8 | Federal fair housing and RESPA laws | 2 |
| | **Total** | **40** |

**One contact hour is a minimum of 50 minutes of instruction** — 12 AAC 64.063(g)(1).

## Controlling Alaska sources

1. **AS 08.88** — Alaska Statutes, Real Estate Brokers and Salespersons. AS 08.88.081, .091, .095 and .171 supply the Commission's authority over education, course certification, and instructor approval.

2. **12 AAC 64.063 — Minimum education requirements for licensure.** The topic-and-hour table above; the 18-month completion window before application (24 months for endorsement); what the applicant lists on the application, including the **commission course approval number**; the 50-minute contact hour; and the licensee's own duty to retain course certificates for three years.

3. **12 AAC 64.400 — Purpose of course certification and instructor approval.** Uniform standards, currency and relevance of certified courses, and minimum instructor qualifications.

4. **12 AAC 64.410 — Minimum classroom and virtual course requirements.** Only certified courses meet AS 08.88.091 and .095. A certified course must add to practical knowledge in the areas identified in 12 AAC 64.063, 64.064 and 64.500; be applicable statewide; be taught by a commission-approved instructor; hold students to a **strict attendance policy**; require a student in a **virtual** course to be present on a device with **video and audio**; require a course and instructor evaluation on a form meeting or exceeding the Commission's; and issue a certificate of completion carrying the student name, the city, the course title **and course approval number**, the delivery date, the contact hours awarded, and the instructor's name and signature.

5. **12 AAC 64.420 — Application for course certification.** The application, the fee, and the required information including the course outline. A course is certified if the Commission or its designee does not disapprove within 45 days of a complete application; applications are filed 45 days before the first offering.

6. **12 AAC 64.430 — Correspondence courses.** The asynchronous route. A correspondence course must meet 12 AAC 64.420, submit the complete course materials including published texts or videos, and submit the instructions for completing each lesson. **All correspondence courses must include an unanswered examination, or a project activity, to be turned in to the instructor for grading on completion of the course**, and that instrument goes to the Commission for prior approval.

7. **12 AAC 64.440 — Instructor approval.** Only a course taught by a commission-approved instructor meets the education requirement. An applicant supplies the application, the fee, the topic list, and a résumé, and must have adult teaching or training experience plus at least one of: a baccalaureate degree in real estate or a related field; five years as a real estate licensee with documented experience in the topic area; or another qualification the Commission accepts. An ARELLO, NAR, REEA, IRWA or CAI instructor workshop may substitute for the teaching experience. An instructor must not have been disciplined by any real estate regulating authority or professional association within five years. **Instructor approval expires on 1 April of years ending in 0 and 5.**

8. **12 AAC 64.470 — Withdrawal or denial of course certification.** The Commission may withdraw certification where a course no longer meets 12 AAC 64.410, on the findings of an audit or on review at recertification, and **may audit a certified course at any time**. Withdrawal takes effect 30 days after written notice unless the sponsor files an acceptable revision of the course outline.

9. **12 AAC 64.500 — Continuing education requirements**, whose subject list also bounds what a certified course may cover under 12 AAC 64.410(a)(1).

10. **Alaska Real Estate Commission Statutes and Regulations, September 2024** — the compiled document the above was read in.

11. **Initial Course Application Instructions, form 08-4146** — the current course certification application and its instructions.

## Open questions that must be answered before filing

1. **The graded instrument.** 12 AAC 64.430(b) requires a correspondence course to include an examination or project activity **turned in to the instructor for grading**. This is the first screened state where automatic server-side scoring does not satisfy the rule on its own. The build must produce an instrument an approved instructor grades, and a workflow that records who graded it and when. Whether the Commission will accept a machine-scored component alongside an instructor-graded one is a question to put to the Commission rather than assume.

2. **Postsecondary authorisation.** AS 14.48 regulates postsecondary institutions through the Alaska Commission on Postsecondary Education. Two exemptions plausibly reach this course — AS 14.48.030(b)(6), a short course of study of not more than 15 days or 120 hours, and (b)(8), a programme regulated by another agency of the state as to the quality of course contents, facilities and operation — but both are discretionary ("the commission **may** exempt"). This is Alaska's version of the second-regulator question that Michigan and Kansas both turned out to have, and it goes to ACPE before filing rather than being assumed.

3. **Instructor approval.** 12 AAC 64.440 requires adult teaching or training experience plus a qualifying degree or five years of licensed practice in the topic area. StateCourse's principal holds a California broker licence with documented practice; whether the Commission accepts out-of-state licensure toward the five years is a question for the Commission.

4. **Virtual against correspondence.** 12 AAC 64.410(a)(7) requires a student in a **virtual** course to be present on a device with video and audio, which is synchronous. This course is filed as a **correspondence** course under 12 AAC 64.430, and every document must keep the two categories distinct — describing an asynchronous course as "virtual" would import a requirement Alaska attaches to a different delivery type.

## Change-control rule

Before submission and before every production release:

1. Recheck AS 08.88 and 12 AAC 64.063, 64.400–64.470, and 64.500.
2. Record the review date and reviewer.
3. Diff the current text against the course source notes, watching for changes to the 12 AAC 64.063(c) topic hours, which are the course's structure.
4. Update affected modules on the rule's effective date.
5. Treat any change to the approved course outline as requiring a filed revision — 12 AAC 64.470(b) makes an acceptable revision the answer to a withdrawal notice.

### Findings that changed how the course is written

**1. AS 08.88.405 permits an Alaska licensee to prepare documents.** "Notwithstanding AS 08.08, a person licensed as a real estate broker, associate real estate broker, or real estate salesperson under this chapter may prepare real property contracts, earnest money agreements, leases, and other documents related to real property if the documents are prepared by the person in the course of the person's work" as a licensee.

This is the single largest divergence from California and Michigan practice found in this project so far, and a course that imported another state's caution here would teach an Alaska student to be afraid of something the legislature deliberately authorised. Module 05 teaches it as written, with its three limits — personal to the licensee, documents related to real property, and in the course of licensed work — and pairs it with **AS 08.88.620(3)**, the duty to advise a represented person to seek expert advice beyond the licensee's expertise. **AS 08.88.403** allows a broker to contract with and pay an attorney or associate broker to review a transaction before closing, which is how the two fit together in practice.

**2. Alaska's two foreclosure routes trade off precisely, and in opposite directions.**

- **Trustee's sale, AS 34.20.070 – .130.** Notice of default recorded not less than 30 days after default and not less than 90 days before sale; certified mail within 10 days to the persons .070(c) names; public auction at the front door of a superior court courthouse under .080(a). **AS 34.20.090(a): no redemption unless the deed of trust so declares. AS 34.20.100: no deficiency.**
- **Judicial foreclosure, AS 09.45.170 – .220.** **AS 09.45.180: a deficiency may be enforced by execution. AS 09.45.190 with AS 09.35.250: redemption before confirmation, or within 12 months of the order of confirmation**, on payment of the purchase money with interest at eight percent a year plus taxes and certain expenses.
- **AS 34.20.070(a) makes the election binding**: a beneficiary who forecloses judicially or sues on the note and takes judgment may not then exercise the nonjudicial remedies.

**3. AS 34.70 is more detailed, and more dangerous to get wrong, than a summary would suggest.** Delivery is required **before the transferee makes a written offer** (.010). Late delivery gives the buyer **three days from in-person delivery or six days from mailing** to terminate (.020). **Disclosure removes the transferor's liability** for a disclosed defect (.030) — which is the argument that persuades a reluctant seller. A **wilful** violation exposes **up to treble actual damages** plus costs and fees (.090(c), (d)); a negligent one, actual damages (.090(b)). The chapter can be **waived by written agreement** (.110) and does not apply to a **never-occupied first sale** (.120) — but .070 preserves other disclosure obligations required by law, so neither waiver nor exemption touches 12 AAC 64.130(14) or AS 08.88.615. The Commission establishes the form (.050 with AS 08.88.071(a)(9)), and the form must notify transferees about **convicted sex offender information** and about **agricultural facilities and operations**, including aircraft.

**4. The trust account rules are more specific than most states'.** 12 AAC 64.180 (federally insured Alaska bank; the account name must contain "trust account" or "trustee account"; the broker is trustee and responsible for every signatory; **not more than $100** of broker money for maintenance; demand accounts only). 12 AAC 64.200 (**five days**, with an express exception for geographical location, weather, and transportation — an Alaska provision with an Alaska reason). 12 AAC 64.210 (interest disclosed to the trustor; interest does not belong to the broker). 12 AAC 64.220 (**a transaction code and ledger for each written offer to purchase**, not each closing; a ledger for every transaction **including deposits held by a cooperating broker, title company, or third party**; **monthly three-way reconciliation** ending in a cross-check of the bank balance against the sum of the ledgers). 12 AAC 64.240 (each commission withdrawal separate and identified to a transaction; **within 15 days** of settlement; out-of-state licensee payments documented and retained three years). 12 AAC 64.250 (seven prohibitions, including no salesperson commission from the trust account, no commingling beyond the $100, and **no refusing to return a non-accepted buyer's deposit**, subject to one business day after the cheque clears). **12 AAC 64.260 characterises failure to comply as fraudulent and dishonest conduct** within AS 08.88.071(a)(3)(A)(iv).

**5. Non-cash earnest money has two separate rules.** 12 AAC 64.130(12): communicated to the owner **before acceptance** and **identified as a non-cash substitute on the earnest money receipt**. 12 AAC 64.271: **control surrendered to the broker**, and the broker informs the principal parties.

**6. AS 34.03 carries Alaska-specific figures the course must state exactly.** Deposits capped at **two months' periodic rent**, inapplicable above **$2,000 a month** (.070(a)); a separate **one month** pet deposit for a pet that is **not a service animal**, separately accounted for and applied only to pet damage (.070(h)); trust deposit with **per-tenant accounting** and no commingling with other funds (.070(c)); refund and itemised notice within **14 days**, or **30** where damage costs are deducted or where the tenant did not give complying notice (.070(g)); **up to twice the amount withheld** for a wilful failure (.070(d)); and damages excluding normal wear and tear **and deterioration caused by the landlord's failure to prepare for expected conditions** (.070(b)(2)(B)) — a phrase written for a state where a landlord who does not winterise has caused the freeze damage.

Also **AS 34.03.100(b)**, excusing a landlord of a single family residence in an undeveloped rural area, or where public sewer or water has never been connected, from (a)(3) and (a)(5) if the unit had no running water, hot water, sewage, or sanitary facilities at the start; **AS 34.03.140(c)**, 24 hours notice and consent for access; **AS 34.03.220**, whose notice periods vary by ground (24 hours to five days for deliberate damage **over $400** or illegal activity; ten days with a cure right for material noncompliance; **seven days** for unpaid rent; five days for a tenant-caused utility shutoff); **AS 34.03.290**, 14 days week-to-week and 30 days month-to-month; **AS 34.03.250**, abolishing landlord liens and distraint; and **AS 34.03.330(b)**, whose eight exclusions apply **unless created to avoid the application of the chapter**.

**7. Alaska's fair housing classes exceed the federal seven.** **AS 18.80.240** reaches **marital status, changes in marital status, pregnancy, parenthood, and age** in addition to the Fair Housing Act's race, colour, religion, national origin, sex, disability, and familial status. **AS 18.80.240(3) prohibits the inquiry or record itself** — not merely acting on the answer — which is stricter than federal law and is the provision a licensee trained elsewhere will miss. **AS 18.80.240(5)** extends to the protected class of a person **associated with** the prospect. And **12 AAC 64.130(19)** makes being found guilty of violating **local, state, or federal** fair housing law a ground for revocation or suspension of the licence.

**8. Licensing detail that shapes module 07.** **AS 08.88.161** puts property management and community association management inside the licence requirement in their own right, and reaches assisting, holding out, and attempting; **AS 08.88.401(g)** makes violation a class A misdemeanor. **AS 08.88.171(c)** requires the salesperson applicant to pass the examination, apply within six months, complete the AS 08.88.091 education, be **at least 19**, and hold **errors and omissions insurance** under AS 08.88.172. **AS 08.88.095(e) lapses an initial licence automatically** 30 days after the end of the first year if the 30 post-licensing hours are not completed and certified. **AS 08.88.475(a)** caps recovery fund liability at **$50,000 per licensee**, distributed pro rata under (b). **12 AAC 64.135(b)** permits a **non-noticed audit**, with a discretionary 24-hour delay during which the auditor **seals the records**. **12 AAC 64.620** suspends a licence **as of the date** errors and omissions coverage terminated.

**9. Property management has its own article.** 12 AAC 64.550 (activity in the registered company name; a prior written contract stating responsibilities, authority, period, and fee; owner's written authorisation before transferring funds between that owner's accounts; sale of a managed property authorised by specific contract language or a separate listing; a licensee-owner disclosing the licence and company to every tenant and prospective tenant). **12 AAC 64.560 makes failure to deposit prepaid rents or security deposits as AS 34.03 requires fraudulent or dishonest conduct** within AS 08.88.071(a)(3)(A)(iv) — the provision that ties the two halves of module 07 together. 12 AAC 64.570 governs a licensee managing property they own, including the requirement that deposits sit in the broker's trust account or in the licensee's own trust account **to which the broker has access for oversight and review**, while **rents may be paid directly to the licensee as lessor**.
