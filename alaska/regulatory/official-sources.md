# Alaska 40-Contact-Hour Prelicense Course — Official Source Register

**Course:** Real estate salesperson prelicense education, 12 AAC 64.063(c)
**Control date:** August 19, 2026
**Source policy:** Alaska Statutes, the Alaska Administrative Code, and current Real Estate Commission forms control. Federal material uses official federal sources. Commercial exam-prep material and competitor outlines establish nothing.

## Why Alaska was chosen

It is the cheapest viable state screened, and for a reason the screening pass did not anticipate: **Alaska prescribes the curriculum itself.** 12 AAC 64.063(c) sets out eight topics with their contact hours, totalling exactly 40. There is no module map to design and defend — the Commission has already written it, and the build's job is to fill it.

Set against the alternatives: Kansas needs a Kansas Board of Regents certificate before its Commission approval is worth anything; Idaho, Mississippi and Montana each gate distance delivery on ARELLO certification; South Dakota is 116 hours; and Arizona is 96 hours behind a lock-down browser and a proctor network. Alaska is 40 hours with no ARELLO requirement anywhere in the education article.

## The curriculum, as the rule states it

12 AAC 64.063(c) — an applicant for an initial real estate licence shall document completion of:

| # | Topic | Contact hours |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 | Licensee relationships, disclosure, and conflict of interest | 6 |
| 2 | Forms of ownership | 4 |
| 3 | Property law, public and private rights and limitations | 4 |
| 4 | Forms of conveyances and recording of documents | 4 |
| 5 | Contracts and transaction documents | 8 |
| 6 | Financing instruments and accounting principles | 6 |
| 7 | Alaska real estate license law and Alaska landlord tenant law | 6 |
| 8 | Federal fair housing and RESPA laws | 2 |
| | **Total** | **40** |

**One contact hour is a minimum of 50 minutes of instruction** — 12 AAC 64.063(g)(1).

## Controlling Alaska sources

1. **AS 08.88** — Alaska Statutes, Real Estate Brokers and Salespersons. AS 08.88.081, .091, .095 and .171 supply the Commission's authority over education, course certification, and instructor approval.

2. **12 AAC 64.063 — Minimum education requirements for licensure.** The topic-and-hour table above; the 18-month completion window before application (24 months for endorsement); what the applicant lists on the application, including the **commission course approval number**; the 50-minute contact hour; and the licensee's own duty to retain course certificates for three years.

3. **12 AAC 64.400 — Purpose of course certification and instructor approval.** Uniform standards, currency and relevance of certified courses, and minimum instructor qualifications.

4. **12 AAC 64.410 — Minimum classroom and virtual course requirements.** Only certified courses meet AS 08.88.091 and .095. A certified course must add to practical knowledge in the areas identified in 12 AAC 64.063, 64.064 and 64.500; be applicable statewide; be taught by a commission-approved instructor; hold students to a **strict attendance policy**; require a student in a **virtual** course to be present on a device with **video and audio**; require a course and instructor evaluation on a form meeting or exceeding the Commission's; and issue a certificate of completion carrying the student name, the city, the course title **and course approval number**, the delivery date, the contact hours awarded, and the instructor's name and signature.

5. **12 AAC 64.420 — Application for course certification.** The application, the fee, and the required information including the course outline. A course is certified if the Commission or its designee does not disapprove within 45 days of a complete application; applications are filed 45 days before the first offering.

6. **12 AAC 64.430 — Correspondence courses.** The asynchronous route. A correspondence course must meet 12 AAC 64.420, submit the complete course materials including published texts or videos, and submit the instructions for completing each lesson. **All correspondence courses must include an unanswered examination, or a project activity, to be turned in to the instructor for grading on completion of the course**, and that instrument goes to the Commission for prior approval.

7. **12 AAC 64.440 — Instructor approval.** Only a course taught by a commission-approved instructor meets the education requirement. An applicant supplies the application, the fee, the topic list, and a résumé, and must have adult teaching or training experience plus at least one of: a baccalaureate degree in real estate or a related field; five years as a real estate licensee with documented experience in the topic area; or another qualification the Commission accepts. An ARELLO, NAR, REEA, IRWA or CAI instructor workshop may substitute for the teaching experience. An instructor must not have been disciplined by any real estate regulating authority or professional association within five years. **Instructor approval expires on 1 April of years ending in 0 and 5.**

8. **12 AAC 64.470 — Withdrawal or denial of course certification.** The Commission may withdraw certification where a course no longer meets 12 AAC 64.410, on the findings of an audit or on review at recertification, and **may audit a certified course at any time**. Withdrawal takes effect 30 days after written notice unless the sponsor files an acceptable revision of the course outline.

9. **12 AAC 64.500 — Continuing education requirements**, whose subject list also bounds what a certified course may cover under 12 AAC 64.410(a)(1).

10. **Alaska Real Estate Commission Statutes and Regulations, September 2024** — the compiled document the above was read in.

11. **Initial Course Application Instructions, form 08-4146** — the current course certification application and its instructions.

## Open questions that must be answered before filing

1. **The graded instrument.** 12 AAC 64.430(b) requires a correspondence course to include an examination or project activity **turned in to the instructor for grading**. This is the first screened state where automatic server-side scoring does not satisfy the rule on its own. The build must produce an instrument an approved instructor grades, and a workflow that records who graded it and when. Whether the Commission will accept a machine-scored component alongside an instructor-graded one is a question to put to the Commission rather than assume.

2. **Postsecondary authorisation.** AS 14.48 regulates postsecondary institutions through the Alaska Commission on Postsecondary Education. Two exemptions plausibly reach this course — AS 14.48.030(b)(6), a short course of study of not more than 15 days or 120 hours, and (b)(8), a programme regulated by another agency of the state as to the quality of course contents, facilities and operation — but both are discretionary ("the commission **may** exempt"). This is Alaska's version of the second-regulator question that Michigan and Kansas both turned out to have, and it goes to ACPE before filing rather than being assumed.

3. **Instructor approval.** 12 AAC 64.440 requires adult teaching or training experience plus a qualifying degree or five years of licensed practice in the topic area. StateCourse's principal holds a California broker licence with documented practice; whether the Commission accepts out-of-state licensure toward the five years is a question for the Commission.

4. **Virtual against correspondence.** 12 AAC 64.410(a)(7) requires a student in a **virtual** course to be present on a device with video and audio, which is synchronous. This course is filed as a **correspondence** course under 12 AAC 64.430, and every document must keep the two categories distinct — describing an asynchronous course as "virtual" would import a requirement Alaska attaches to a different delivery type.

## Change-control rule

Before submission and before every production release:

1. Recheck AS 08.88 and 12 AAC 64.063, 64.400–64.470, and 64.500.
2. Record the review date and reviewer.
3. Diff the current text against the course source notes, watching for changes to the 12 AAC 64.063(c) topic hours, which are the course's structure.
4. Update affected modules on the rule's effective date.
5. Treat any change to the approved course outline as requiring a filed revision — 12 AAC 64.470(b) makes an acceptable revision the answer to a withdrawal notice.

Findings recorded from this reading:

- **The curriculum is prescribed**, topic by topic, to 40 contact hours — 12 AAC 64.063(c).
- **A contact hour is at least 50 minutes of instruction** — 12 AAC 64.063(g)(1).
- **Education must be completed within the 18 months before application** — 12 AAC 64.063(a)(2).
- **The certificate must carry the course approval number**, along with the student name, the city, the course title, the delivery date, the contact hours, and the instructor's name and signature — 12 AAC 64.410(a)(9). It issues only after the student completes a course and instructor evaluation — 12 AAC 64.410(a)(8).
- **A correspondence course's graded instrument goes to an instructor**, and to the Commission for prior approval — 12 AAC 64.430(b).
- **A virtual course is synchronous** and requires video and audio presence; correspondence is the asynchronous route — 12 AAC 64.410(a)(7) against 12 AAC 64.430.
- **The Commission may audit a certified course at any time**, and withdrawal takes effect 30 days after notice unless an acceptable revision is filed — 12 AAC 64.470(b) and (c).
- **ARELLO appears in Article 7 only as one of several bodies whose instructor workshop may substitute for teaching experience** — 12 AAC 64.440(d). It is not a certification requirement for a course, which distinguishes Alaska from Idaho, Mississippi, Montana and Kansas.
