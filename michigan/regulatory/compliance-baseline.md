# Michigan Compliance Baseline — 40-Clock-Hour Prelicensure Course

Every row is a requirement taken from the controlling text, the state the build is in, and the citation. A row that says *Modeled* is enforced by code and locked by a test. A row that says *Blocked* names what it is waiting on.

| Requirement | State | Citation |

| 100% attendance; distance credit requires hours attended to equal hours approved | **Modeled.** Every module must reach completed status; the course cannot complete with a module outstanding. | R 339.22152(1)–(2) |
| A makeup policy | **To draft.** For an asynchronous course the policy is about resuming an incomplete module, not about a missed class date. | R 339.22152(3), R 339.22141(1)(i) |

## What Michigan does not require, and where that matters

- **No prescribed final examination.** MCL 339.2504 and the rules require attendance and completion; R 339.22154(2)(c) records "the student's grade, if an examination is required to determine successful completion", which leaves the examination to the school. StateCourse will still administer a graded final, because a course that cannot distinguish completion from attendance is worth less to the student and to the Department — but it is a StateCourse standard here, not a Michigan rule, and every document must say so.
- **No proctoring rule.** Michigan's identity requirement attaches to certificate issuance and to the accuracy of reported attendance, not to supervised examination. Importing California's proctor requirement would be inventing a Michigan rule.
- **No stated retention period**, because the record is permanent. A Michigan record is never aged out.
