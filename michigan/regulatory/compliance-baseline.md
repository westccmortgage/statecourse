## What Michigan does not require, and where that matters

- **No prescribed final examination.** MCL 339.2504 and the rules require attendance and completion; R 339.22154(2)(c) records "the student's grade, if an examination is required to determine successful completion." The Michigan submission draft uses scored module assessments and mandatory remediation and includes no separate provider final. Adding one later requires a secure bank, stated threshold, grade-retention rule, and change-control review.
- **No proctoring rule.** Michigan's identity requirement attaches to certificate issuance and to the accuracy of reported attendance, not to supervised examination. Importing California's proctor requirement would be inventing a Michigan rule.
- **No stated retention period**, because the record is permanent. A Michigan record is never aged out.
