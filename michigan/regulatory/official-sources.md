# Michigan 40-Clock-Hour Prelicensure Course - Official Source Register

**Course:** Principles of Real Estate - Michigan Salesperson Prelicensure
**Control date:** August 31, 2026
**Source policy:** the Michigan Occupational Code, current LARA/BPL rules and forms, and Michigan LEO Post Secondary Schools materials control. Federal material uses official federal sources. Commercial exam-prep material and competitor course outlines establish nothing.

## A warning that governs this whole register

The Real Estate Brokers and Salespersons general rules were **renumbered and largely rescinded effective June 4, 2025** (2025 MR 12). Everything in the old R 339.22301–R 339.22667 range now reads "Rescinded." The operative prelicensure-education rules are R 339.22141 through R 339.22159.

LARA's own current course-approval form, `BPL/REPRELICCOURSEAPPROVALAPPL` (rev. 3/23), predates that renumbering and cites the superseded rules — "Rule 618(1)(e)", "Rule 626(2)(b–f)", "Rule 618(2)". Those citations still describe the right obligations, but the numbers on the form are no longer the numbers in the rules. Every citation in this project uses the current numbering, with the form's old number noted where the form is the source of a requirement.

## Controlling Michigan course-approval sources

1. [MCL 339.2504 — Prelicensure education](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=MCL-339-2504)
   At least 40 clock hours of approved prelicensure courses in principles of real estate, including at least 4 clock hours on civil rights law and equal opportunity in housing, completed within the 36 months preceding the application. A person offering a prelicensure course must first obtain department approval and comply with the department's rules on curriculum, instructor qualification, and grading.

2. [R 339.22141 — Application for approval to offer prelicensure courses](https://ars.apps.lara.state.mi.us/AdminCode/DownloadAdminCodeFile?FileName=R+339.22101+to+R+339.22667.pdf)
   The application contents, the summary of topics and hours, the certificate of completion fields, the attendance methodology, the asynchronous attestation form, the makeup policy, and the enrollment application that discloses the prior year's completion percentage. The department decides within 60 days and must give substantive reasons for a denial.

3. **R 339.22143 — Expiration and renewal.** Course approval expires **one year** after approval. Renewal is filed not less than 60 days before expiry. A proprietary real estate school licensed under the Proprietary Schools Act, 1943 PA 148, MCL 395.101 to 395.103, must comply with that act to keep its approval.

4. **R 339.22145 — Program coordinator.** At least one designated individual, responsible for supervising the program and for compliance.

5. **R 339.22147 — Instructors.** The department approves every instructor before they teach. Two qualification routes. Instructor duties include assuring and certifying accurate attendance. Qualifications are filed not less than 60 days before instruction begins.

6. **R 339.22149 — Syllabus.** Course title, times and dates, coordinator and instructor contact details, and a detailed outline of the subject matter.

7. **R 339.22152 — Attendance.** A student must attend **100%** of a prelicensure course. Credit for a distance-learning course requires completion of the entire course, meaning the hours attended equal the hours the course is approved for. The school must have a makeup policy.

8. **R 339.22154 — Student records, certificate, identity, reporting.** The record is **permanently maintained**. Certificate contents come from R 339.22141(1)(f). Photo identification is presented before the certificate issues, and for distance learning the school must ensure the student whose attendance is reported is the one who completed the course. Course schedule and geographic location filed not less than 30 days before courses are held; names of successful students submitted **within 5 business days** of the conclusion of the last course.

9. **R 339.22155 — Denial, suspension, rescission.** Including for revealing, discovering, soliciting, or inducing anyone to reveal the questions on a licensing examination.

10. **R 339.22157 — Distance learning.** Prior approval; the six required elements; ARELLO certification accepted, with approval **suspended** if the certification expires or is withdrawn; an equivalent delivery system may be approved instead; and a detailed description of how the school remedies hardware and software failures.

11. **R 339.22158 — Advertising.** All advertising for approved prelicensure courses must include the name of the approved school.

12. **R 339.22159 — Solicitations.** Organizational-membership, employment, business-related, and other noneducational presentations or solicitations are prohibited during prelicensure courses and earn no clock-hour credit, including when offered with an approved course.

13. [LARA Prelicensure Course Approval Application, BPL/REPRELICCOURSEAPPROVALAPPL (rev. 3/23)](https://www.michigan.gov/lara/-/media/Project/Websites/lara/bpl/Real-Estate-Brokers-and-Salespersons/Licensing-Forms/Prelicensure-Course-Approval-Application.pdf)
   The operative submission format. Course type is selected as Non-Distance (classroom) or **Distance (online)**. Parts A, B and C; instructor qualifications; the seven required attachments.

14. [Michigan Real Estate Salesperson Licensing Guide (rev. 1/26/2026)](https://www.michigan.gov/lara/-/media/Project/Websites/lara/bpl/Real-Estate-Brokers-and-Salespersons/Licensing-Information/Real-Estate-Salesperson-Licensing-Guide-and-FAQ.pdf)
   Confirms the 40 hours including 4 of civil rights, the PSI examination, and the MiPLUS application path.

## Controlling proprietary-school sources

1. [LEO Post Secondary Schools - Distance Education Schools, Non-Degree Granting](https://www.michigan.gov/leo/bureaus-agencies/wd/pss)
   Expressly states that non-degree proprietary or trade schools with no physical presence in Michigan that provide distance education to Michigan residents must be licensed under the Proprietary Schools Act. This resolves whether a licence is required. The page links that category to the Distance Education Provider guide while the Act and real-estate materials point to proprietary-school requirements, so the exact MiPSS application route, document set, fee, and bond treatment remain a written prefiling question.

2. [Specialized Licensing Requirements](https://www.michigan.gov/leo/-/media/Project/Websites/leo/Documents/WD/Programs_Services/PSS/Specialized_Licensing_Requirements_9-29-21_PSS.pdf)
   Identifies private real-estate prelicensing schools as requiring the proprietary-school licence and separate BPL approval of their programmes and instructors.

3. [Proprietary Schools Act, 1943 PA 148](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-Act-148-of-1943) and [Proprietary Schools Administrative Rules, R 390.561–R 390.566](https://www.michigan.gov/-/media/Project/Websites/leo/Documents/Executive/Administrative_Rules.pdf)
   Control the permit/licence, surety, personnel, contract/catalog consistency, records, programme approval, and advertising. For a nonaccredited school, surety is $200 per student with a $5,000 minimum. **R 390.564** requires the student application and contract to correspond with the catalog and the refund policy to appear in the contract.

4. [MiPSS Proprietary Schools License User Guide](https://www.michigan.gov/-/media/Project/Websites/leo/Documents/WD/Programs_Services/PSS/MiPSS_User_Guide.pdf)
   Shows the portal workflow and the school, programme, course, surety, and upload fields. The fee amounts illustrated in the older guide are not treated as current; the live portal's indexed fee controls at filing.

5. [Proprietary School Guidebook for Document Requirements](https://www.michigan.gov/-/media/Project/Websites/leo/Documents/WD/Programs_Services/PSS/Proprietary-School-Guidebook-for-Document-Requirements/Proprietary-School-Guidebook-for-Document-Requirements.pdf), [Q&A](https://www.michigan.gov/-/media/Project/Websites/leo/Documents/WD/Programs_Services/PSS/Proprietary-School-Guidebook-for-Document-Requirements/Prop-School-Document-Guide_Q-and-A.pdf), and [official surety-bond form](https://www.michigan.gov/-/media/Project/Websites/leo/Documents/WD/Programs_Services/PSS/Proprietary-School-Guidebook-for-Document-Requirements/Sample-Surety-Bond.pdf)

## Resolved decisions and filing questions

1. **PSS applicability is resolved: a licence is required; the portal route is not.** The open question recorded on August 19 is superseded by LEO's express distance-school guidance. Because the same page links the non-degree/out-of-state category to a separate Distance Education Provider guide, PSS must confirm the exact MiPSS application button, document set, fee, and bond treatment before StateCourse pays or files.
2. **ARELLO is not the Michigan filing route.** R 339.22157(3) accepts an ARELLO-certified delivery system, and (4) allows the Department to approve an equivalent one. StateCourse files on the equivalence route and supplies the direct evidence; ARELLO may still be pursued separately for multi-state scale.
3. **Instructor route (1)(b) is the submitted basis, not a guarantee.** An active California DRE broker licence is a licence from another governmental agency in the precise real-estate disciplines the rule names. BPL must still approve the instructor, and the qualifications must be filed at least 60 days before instruction.
4. **Cross-agency sequence needs written confirmation.** The BPL form requests a Michigan proprietary-school number while MiPSS requests secondary-agency approval. The package proposes opening the MiPSS record first, filing BPL against that record, then uploading BPL approval to MiPSS.
5. **The one-year approval term is an operating control.** R 339.22143(1) expires course approval one year after grant, with renewal filed at least 60 days before expiry.

## Change-control rule

Before submission and before every production release:

1. Recheck MCL 339.2504 and R 339.22141 through R 339.22159.
2. Record the review date and reviewer.
3. Diff the current rules against the course source notes, watching specifically for further renumbering.
4. Update affected modules on the rule's effective date.
5. Treat any change to approved topic allocation or hours as requiring a change report to the department within 30 days under R 339.22141(2).

MCL 339.2504 was read through the Michigan Legislature's published section text.

Findings recorded from this reading:

- **40 clock hours, of which at least 4 are civil rights law and equal opportunity in housing**, completed within the 36 months before application (MCL 339.2504).
- **Attendance is total.** A student must attend 100% of the course, and distance-learning credit requires hours attended to equal hours approved (R 339.22152).
- **Student records are permanent**, not kept for a term of years, and their contents are prescribed (R 339.22154(1)–(2)).
- **Completion reporting is 5 business days** from the conclusion of the last course, in a department-approved format (R 339.22154(7)); the schedule and location are filed 30 days ahead (R 339.22154(6)).
- **Identity is verified before the certificate issues**, and for distance learning the school must ensure the student reported is the student who completed the course (R 339.22154(5)).
- **The certificate must carry the school's approval number** along with the course name as approved, the completion date, the student name, and the clock hours (R 339.22141(1)(f)).
- **An asynchronous course needs a student-signed attestation** of clock hours completed (R 339.22141(1)(h)).
- **The enrollment application must publish a completion percentage** for the prior calendar year, refreshed by February 1 (R 339.22141(1)(j)).
- **Distance learning has six mandatory elements** — prior approval, modules, a learning objective per module, a structured learning method, assessment during each module, and remediation repeating the module until the content is understood (R 339.22157(2)).
- **Advertising must name the approved school** (R 339.22158). Michigan's advertising rule is lighter than California's §3004(b): it requires the school's name, not the course approval number. The certificate rule is where Michigan puts the approval number.
- **No noneducational solicitations occur inside the credited course** and no clock hours attach to organizational membership, employment, business-related, recruiting, upgrade, or other noneducational presentations (R 339.22159).

Additional findings recorded from this reading:

- **Two regulatory tracks apply.** LEO PSS licenses/authorizes the school and approves the program; BPL approves the real-estate prelicensure course and each instructor. PSS licensure must never be advertised as PSS approval or endorsement of the school.
- **Surety is coverage, not a cash deposit.** A nonaccredited school supplies $200 per student, not less than $5,000, using the state form and a June 30 end date; the premium is set by the surety.
- **Name and address must become one controlled pair before filing.** `StateCourse LLC` is fixed. `407 N Pacific Coast Highway, Unit 1028, Redondo Beach, CA 90277` is the proposed filing/mailing address, but PSS must first confirm whether an out-of-state online-only school should use that address, the California principal office, or separate physical and mailing fields. The final agency-confirmed address must then match the MiPSS application, bond, uploaded documents, and proposed advertising.
- **Remote-only does not mean silent omission.** Facility-related portal fields receive explicit not-applicable statements; the equipment upload names the online platform.
- **PSS position descriptions are general job descriptions, not résumés.** The separate BPL instructor dossier supplies the candidate-specific evidence.
