# Michigan 40-Clock-Hour Prelicensure Course — Official Source Register

**Course:** Principles of Real Estate (Salesperson Prelicensure)
**Control date:** August 19, 2026
**Source policy:** the Michigan Occupational Code, the Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs administrative rules, and current LARA forms control. Federal material uses official federal sources. Commercial exam-prep material and competitor course outlines establish nothing.

## A warning that governs this whole register

The Real Estate Brokers and Salespersons general rules were **renumbered and largely rescinded effective June 4, 2025** (2025 MR 12). Everything in the old R 339.22301–R 339.22667 range now reads "Rescinded." The operative education rules are R 339.22141 through R 339.22158.

LARA's own current course-approval form, `BPL/REPRELICCOURSEAPPROVALAPPL` (rev. 3/23), predates that renumbering and cites the superseded rules — "Rule 618(1)(e)", "Rule 626(2)(b–f)", "Rule 618(2)". Those citations still describe the right obligations, but the numbers on the form are no longer the numbers in the rules. Every citation in this project uses the current numbering, with the form's old number noted where the form is the source of a requirement.

## Controlling Michigan course-approval sources

1. [MCL 339.2504 — Prelicensure education](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=MCL-339-2504)
   At least 40 clock hours of approved prelicensure courses in principles of real estate, including at least 4 clock hours on civil rights law and equal opportunity in housing, completed within the 36 months preceding the application. A person offering a prelicensure course must first obtain department approval and comply with the department's rules on curriculum, instructor qualification, and grading.

2. [R 339.22141 — Application for approval to offer prelicensure courses](https://ars.apps.lara.state.mi.us/AdminCode/DownloadAdminCodeFile?FileName=R+339.22101+to+R+339.22667.pdf)
   The application contents, the summary of topics and hours, the certificate of completion fields, the attendance methodology, the asynchronous attestation form, the makeup policy, and the enrollment application that discloses the prior year's completion percentage. The department decides within 60 days and must give substantive reasons for a denial.

3. **R 339.22143 — Expiration and renewal.** Course approval expires **one year** after approval. Renewal is filed not less than 60 days before expiry. A proprietary real estate school licensed under the Proprietary Schools Act, 1943 PA 148, MCL 395.101 to 395.103, must comply with that act to keep its approval.

4. **R 339.22145 — Program coordinator.** At least one designated individual, responsible for supervising the program and for compliance.

5. **R 339.22147 — Instructors.** The department approves every instructor before they teach. Two qualification routes. Instructor duties include assuring and certifying accurate attendance. Qualifications are filed not less than 60 days before instruction begins.

6. **R 339.22149 — Syllabus.** Course title, times and dates, coordinator and instructor contact details, and a detailed outline of the subject matter.

7. **R 339.22152 — Attendance.** A student must attend **100%** of a prelicensure course. Credit for a distance-learning course requires completion of the entire course, meaning the hours attended equal the hours the course is approved for. The school must have a makeup policy.

8. **R 339.22154 — Student records, certificate, identity, reporting.** The record is **permanently maintained**. Certificate contents come from R 339.22141(1)(f). Photo identification is presented before the certificate issues, and for distance learning the school must ensure the student whose attendance is reported is the one who completed the course. Course schedule and geographic location filed not less than 30 days before courses are held; names of successful students submitted **within 5 business days** of the conclusion of the last course.

9. **R 339.22155 — Denial, suspension, rescission.** Including for revealing, discovering, soliciting, or inducing anyone to reveal the questions on a licensing examination.

10. **R 339.22157 — Distance learning.** Prior approval; the six required elements; ARELLO certification accepted, with approval **suspended** if the certification expires or is withdrawn; an equivalent delivery system may be approved instead; and a detailed description of how the school remedies hardware and software failures.

11. **R 339.22158 — Advertising.** All advertising for approved prelicensure courses must include the name of the approved school.

12. [LARA Prelicensure Course Approval Application, BPL/REPRELICCOURSEAPPROVALAPPL (rev. 3/23)](https://www.michigan.gov/lara/-/media/Project/Websites/lara/bpl/Real-Estate-Brokers-and-Salespersons/Licensing-Forms/Prelicensure-Course-Approval-Application.pdf)
   The operative submission format. Course type is selected as Non-Distance (classroom) or **Distance (online)**. Parts A, B and C; instructor qualifications; the seven required attachments.

13. [Michigan Real Estate Salesperson Licensing Guide (rev. 1/26/2026)](https://www.michigan.gov/lara/-/media/Project/Websites/lara/bpl/Real-Estate-Brokers-and-Salespersons/Licensing-Information/Real-Estate-Salesperson-Licensing-Guide-and-FAQ.pdf)
   Confirms the 40 hours including 4 of civil rights, the PSI examination, and the MiPLUS application path.

## Open questions that must be answered before filing

1. **Proprietary Schools Act.** R 339.22143(2) binds a proprietary real estate school to 1943 PA 148. Whether StateCourse is a proprietary school within that act, and whether an out-of-state distance provider is one at all, is a legal question and not a drafting one. It is Michigan's analogue of the California BPPE question and carries the same risk: evidence of the wrong answer surfaces after filing.
2. **ARELLO or equivalence.** R 339.22157(3) accepts an ARELLO-certified delivery system; (4) allows the department to approve an equivalent one. Filing on the equivalence route means the department evaluates our delivery system directly. The register in `docs/states/online-delivery-screening.md` sets out why certification is worth starting anyway.
3. **The one-year approval term.** R 339.22143(1) expires approval one year after it is granted, with renewal 60 days ahead. This is far shorter than Virginia's three years or California's course-life, and it makes renewal an operational routine rather than an occasional event.

## Change-control rule

Before submission and before every production release:

1. Recheck MCL 339.2504 and R 339.22141 through R 339.22158.
2. Record the review date and reviewer.
3. Diff the current rules against the course source notes, watching specifically for further renumbering.
4. Update affected modules on the rule's effective date.
5. Treat any change to approved topic allocation or hours as requiring a change report to the department within 30 days under R 339.22141(2).

## Verification record

**August 19, 2026 — reviewer: StateCourse compliance build.** Retrieved and read the full text of the Real Estate Brokers and Salespersons general rules (R 339.22101 to R 339.22667, all history lines showing 2025 MR 12, effective June 4, 2025) from the LARA administrative rules service; the LARA Prelicensure Course Approval Application (rev. 3/23); and the Michigan Real Estate Salesperson Licensing Guide (rev. 1/26/2026). MCL 339.2504 was read through the Michigan Legislature's published section text.

Findings recorded from this reading:

- **40 clock hours, of which at least 4 are civil rights law and equal opportunity in housing**, completed within the 36 months before application (MCL 339.2504).
- **Attendance is total.** A student must attend 100% of the course, and distance-learning credit requires hours attended to equal hours approved (R 339.22152).
- **Student records are permanent**, not kept for a term of years, and their contents are prescribed (R 339.22154(1)–(2)).
- **Completion reporting is 5 business days** from the conclusion of the last course, in a department-approved format (R 339.22154(7)); the schedule and location are filed 30 days ahead (R 339.22154(6)).
- **Identity is verified before the certificate issues**, and for distance learning the school must ensure the student reported is the student who completed the course (R 339.22154(5)).
- **The certificate must carry the school's approval number** along with the course name as approved, the completion date, the student name, and the clock hours (R 339.22141(1)(f)).
- **An asynchronous course needs a student-signed attestation** of clock hours completed (R 339.22141(1)(h)).
- **The enrollment application must publish a completion percentage** for the prior calendar year, refreshed by February 1 (R 339.22141(1)(j)).
- **Distance learning has six mandatory elements** — prior approval, modules, a learning objective per module, a structured learning method, assessment during each module, and remediation repeating the module until the content is understood (R 339.22157(2)).
- **Advertising must name the approved school** (R 339.22158). Michigan's advertising rule is lighter than California's §3004(b): it requires the school's name, not the course approval number. The certificate rule is where Michigan puts the approval number.
