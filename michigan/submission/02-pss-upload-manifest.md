## Upload rules that prevent predictable deficiencies

1. Use **StateCourse LLC** exactly, including capitalization and spacing.
2. After PSS confirms the correct online-school address treatment, use that exact address on the application, bond, advertising, and every filing artifact. The current proposed mailing/administrative address is **407 N Pacific Coast Highway, Unit 1028, Redondo Beach, CA 90277**.
3. Do not use “certified,” “state approved,” or any equivalent claim for the school, programme, course, or instructor before written approval.
4. Do not confuse the two personnel requirements: submit general position descriptions **and** the Department-form candidate data/evidence for every administrative, supervisory, and instructional person.
5. Do not leave a required facility upload silently empty; use the correct remote-only N/A statement.
6. Do not quote the fee from the 2021 user guide as current. Record the amount the live portal displays at submission.
7. Do not submit the BPL PDF until the coordinator has reviewed every statement and signed and dated it.
