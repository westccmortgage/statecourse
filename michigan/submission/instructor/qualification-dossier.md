The wording provides a strong textual fit for BPL. It does not make approval automatic: BPL must approve the instructor before teaching. PSS is a separate review, and its position-description guidance says an instructor preparing students for a licensed occupation must possess the same credential. The PSS prefiling inquiry therefore asks whether later BPL approval of this California broker under route (1)(b) satisfies PSS, or whether PSS independently requires a Michigan credential. The candidate remains blocked for instruction until both agencies' requirements are resolved in writing.
