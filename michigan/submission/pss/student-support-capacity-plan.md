# Michigan Student-Support Capacity Plan

**Proposed initial cap:** 25 concurrently active Michigan students per BPL-approved and PSS-acceptable instructor. A student is active from paid enrollment until completion, withdrawal, cancellation, or access-period expiry.

The cap is a conservative StateCourse launch control submitted for PSS review, not a number prescribed by Michigan. Its purpose is to support current and accurate instruction, ten mandatory instructor-scored memoranda per student (plus revisions), a learning environment, accurate attendance certification, student assistance, and the stated one-business-day academic-response/review standard.

## Hard controls

1. The enrollment service counts active Michigan students against the approved-instructor roster before accepting a paid enrollment.
2. The 26th active student is refused or wait-listed; payment is not captured merely to exceed capacity.
3. Enrollment pauses earlier if a pending memorandum review, revision review, academic question, or remediation-support flag will miss the one-business-day standard, the instructor is unavailable, or monitoring shows that attendance/completion review cannot be performed accurately.
4. Administrative support may route and acknowledge a question or memorandum but may not score the rubric, replace the approved instructor's academic response, or certify attendance.
5. Capacity increases only after another instructor is qualified, submitted at least 60 days before scheduled instruction, approved by BPL, entered in PSS personnel records, trained, and added to tested assignment/escalation routing.
6. A load increase is versioned and reviewed for agency notice before release. It never takes effect merely because demand rises.

## Acceptance evidence

Before PSS filing, StateCourse demonstrates enrollment refusal at the cap, wait-list/no-charge behavior, memo and support queue aging, one-business-day escalation, instructor-unavailable pause, role-restricted rubric review/response, and re-opening only after capacity is valid. Monthly operating review records active counts, pending memo/revision counts, oldest pending memo and open academic request, review/response-time compliance, support flags, instructor availability, pauses, and any approved staff addition.

The PSS prefiling inquiry asks whether the agency requires another load metric or portal field. StateCourse adopts the agency's direction before filing and does not represent this proposed cap as approved.
