# Michigan Student Complaint Policy

**School:** StateCourse LLC<br>
**Policy version:** MI-COMPLAINT-1.0-submission-draft<br>
**Status:** proposed for publication in the Michigan student catalog, enrollment agreement, and public website before any filing or enrollment.

## Student choice

A student may ask StateCourse LLC to resolve a concern internally, file a complaint directly with Michigan LEO Post Secondary Schools (PSS), or do both. A student does not have to finish the school's internal process before contacting PSS. Nothing in the enrollment agreement waives that right, restricts truthful communication with a regulator, or permits retaliation.

## Internal resolution

A student may submit a written complaint through the StateCourse account or to **info@statecourse.com** with the subject “Michigan Student Complaint.” The complaint should identify the student and course, describe the events, include available supporting material, and state the requested resolution.

StateCourse LLC will:

1. acknowledge receipt within three business days;
2. assign a person who was not the subject of the complaint when practicable;
3. preserve the complaint, relevant course/payment records, communications, evidence, findings, and disposition;
4. provide a written response within ten business days, or explain in writing why additional time is necessary; and
5. identify any refund, record correction, operational change, or regulator report resulting from the review.

These response periods are StateCourse service standards, not limits on a student's statutory rights.

StateCourse will provide a copy of the signed enrollment agreement, catalog version, payment/refund record, progress record, correspondence, and other relevant school records to the student or PSS as law permits or requires.

## Non-retaliation and preservation

StateCourse LLC will not penalize, dismiss, withhold a record, alter a grade, obstruct a refund, or retaliate because a student raised a concern, provided information, or contacted a regulator. Complaint records are retained with the student's permanent Michigan school record and made available for lawful regulator inspection.

This policy implements MCL 395.102c and supplements any complaint right available through BPL, another agency, or law.
