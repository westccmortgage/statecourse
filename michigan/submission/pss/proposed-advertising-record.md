## Claims prohibited before approval

- Michigan approved, state approved, approved school, approved course, approved provider, or approved instructor.
- Certified school/course/instructor where StateCourse is not the certifying body.
- Guaranteed licence, examination pass, employment, earnings, placement, or completion.
- An unqualified “40-hour Michigan course” statement that could imply current credit eligibility.
- Any price, refund, facility, equipment, instructor, or approval statement that differs from the application, contract, catalog, or evidence.

This prohibition does not end at licensure. Under R 390.566(5), StateCourse never represents that PSS, the Board, or Michigan recommends, endorses, or approves the school. After authorization, it may state the exact factual status — for example, that the school is licensed under Michigan law — only while that status is current and without converting licensure into endorsement.
