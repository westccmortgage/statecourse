## Proposed refund and cancellation policy

The contract and catalog use the same words:

> A student may cancel and receive a 100% refund of tuition paid through the later of seven business days after enrollment or completion of the first instructional section, provided the student has not received a completion certificate. After that period, a student who withdraws after completing 60% or less of the course is eligible for a pro rata refund of unearned tuition based on the percentage of the 40-hour course not completed. No tuition refund is due after more than 60% of the course has been completed or after a completion certificate has been issued, whichever occurs first. A request may be submitted through the student's account or in writing to info@statecourse.com; the effective date is the date received. StateCourse LLC provides the written eligibility decision and calculation within three business days after receipt and returns any refund due to the original payment method within 10 business days after the effective cancellation or withdrawal date.

The recorded course-completion percentage used only for this refund calculation is the lesser of 100% or **validated active instructional minutes completed divided by 2,400 required minutes, multiplied by 100**. It comes from the retained server-side attendance ledger, is calculated to two decimal places, excludes merely leaving a page open and excludes all noncredit-feature time. It is independent of the rule that partial modules earn no course credit. Thus 600 validated instructional minutes equals 25% for refund purposes.

For the Complete Licensing Path, after the full-refund window and while refund-calculation completion is 60% or less, the same unearned percentage — 100% minus that recorded percentage — applies separately to the $199 qualifying-course allocation and the $100 noncredit allocation, whether or not a noncredit feature was opened. For example, at 25% refund-calculation completion the refund is 75% of $199 plus 75% of $100. Amounts are rounded to cents. The ordinary student-withdrawal policy pays no refund after more than 60% refund-calculation completion or certificate issuance.

If StateCourse loses or cannot verify required school authorization, program approval, course approval, instructor approval, or surety while a student is active, it immediately suspends credited instruction, completion processing, certificates, and completion reports/exports; notifies the affected student and the appropriate agency; and resumes only under written agency-acceptable restoration or teach-out direction. If lawful restoration or teach-out is not permitted, StateCourse cancels the enrollment and refunds at least the tuition for education it can no longer lawfully deliver, using the unearned-percentage calculation above, or any greater amount required by PSS/BPL or applicable law. The ordinary 60%/certificate cutoff does not reduce that regulatory-loss refund, and the student's complaint, surety-bond, and other legal rights remain intact.

This is a provider policy, not a claim that Michigan prescribed the percentages or periods. R 390.564(2) requires the refund policy to appear in the contract and requires the application, contract, and catalog to correspond.

## Suspension and school-initiated termination

StateCourse may start a suspension/termination review only for documented identity fraud or credential sharing, work completed by another person, attempted licensing-examination compromise, intentional unauthorized system access/platform abuse, or threats/harassment that materially impair safety or instruction. It gives written notice of the specific ground and evidence and at least five business days to respond and, where curable, cure. Access may be suspended immediately only as reasonably necessary to protect safety, records, examination security, or systems; suspension is not a final termination. A person not the subject of the complaint reviews the response when practicable and issues a reasoned written decision within five business days after the response period. The student may appeal in writing to the programme coordinator within five business days; a final written decision issues within five business days. StateCourse permits no discretionary dismissal outside these grounds/process. A school-initiated termination uses the same refund calculation and payment deadline as a student withdrawal, measured on the termination effective date; it does not create automatic tuition forfeiture.

The student separately accepts:

- photo-identification before certificate issuance and verified identity before every completion report/export;

- assessment status: scored module assessments and mandatory remediation are StateCourse standards; Michigan prescribes no final examination and this version includes no separate provider final;
- refund/cancellation terms and the prior-year completion-rate disclosure;
- records, reporting, privacy, complaints, and contact terms.

## Complaint publication control

The full procedure at `student-complaint-policy.md` appears in the student catalog, executed-form enrollment agreement, and public website. Internal review is optional: a student may file directly with Michigan LEO Post Secondary Schools, waives no regulator right, and is protected against retaliation.

## Release rule

The contract, catalog, course page, checkout, receipt, student record, refund processor, and PSS/BPL attachments must carry the same version. A changed price or policy creates a new version and is reviewed for regulator-reporting obligations before release.

For an enrolled student, R 390.564(2) is stricter than publishing a new version: any modification of application or contract terms contained in the catalog is effective only when **all parties agree to the modification in writing**. Silence, continued login, a website edit, or a click-wrap presented after enrollment does not amend that student's agreement. The school retains the signed modification with the original agreement and never rewrites the historical terms.
