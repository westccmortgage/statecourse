# MiPSS Personnel Entries and Evidence Checklist

R 390.564(1) requires candidate-specific Department-form information on education, training, and experience for administrative, supervisory, and instructional personnel. This is separate from the general position descriptions and separate from BPL instructor approval.

## Initial personnel matrix

| Function | Proposed person | Department-form entry | Evidence to attach | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owner/authorized representative | Anatoliy Kanevsky | Identity, role, ownership authority, education/training/experience as requested | Filed LLC record; government ID or confidential portal data only where requested | Portal fields/signature pending |
| Programme coordinator / school compliance administrator | Anatoliy Kanevsky | Administrative and supervisory education, training, experience | Signed résumé; relevant education/training records; licensing and management evidence | Candidate confirmation/exhibits pending |
| Real-estate prelicensure instructor | Anatoliy Kanevsky | Instructional qualifications and relevant subject experience | Signed résumé; California DRE broker public record; education/experience exhibits; BPL dossier and later BPL approval; PSS answer on whether that out-of-state credential satisfies its “same credential” guidance | BPL approval and PSS credential acceptance pending |
| Student records and support administrator | Anatoliy Kanevsky initially, unless another person is designated before filing | Administrative/records/support education, training, experience | Signed résumé and supporting experience evidence | Final staffing designation pending |

One person may fill multiple roles only if each role is separately identified and the person meets every applicable qualification. Every person added before or after filing receives their own MiPSS entry and evidence; a role title or general job description never substitutes for candidate data.

## Filing controls

1. Confirm the final initial staff roster with the owner.
2. Complete the current MiPSS/Department personnel fields for **every** administrative, supervisory, and instructional role.
3. Reconcile dates, titles, education, training, and experience against signed source records.
4. Keep tax identifiers, identification documents, and other confidential portal-only data out of git.
5. Cross-check the instructor record against the BPL qualification dossier without treating either agency's review as the other's approval.
6. Record later personnel changes through the PSS and BPL change processes before the person performs regulated duties.
