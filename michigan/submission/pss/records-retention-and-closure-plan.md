# PSS Student Records, Transcript, and Closure Plan

This plan supplements BPL's permanent real-estate course record. It implements the separate PSS duties in R 390.564(3).

## Record classes

| Record class | Minimum control |
| --- | --- |
| Enrollment/application and executed agreement | Retain at least three years after graduation/completion or last attendance, withdrawal, cancellation, or termination, as applicable |
| Payment, refund, and cancellation records | Retain at least three years after graduation/completion or last attendance, withdrawal, cancellation, or termination, as applicable |
| Attendance, active-time, progress, assessment, remediation, attestation, identity, support, and incident/correction records | Retain at least three years after graduation/completion or last attendance, withdrawal, cancellation, or termination, as applicable; retain longer wherever the permanent BPL record or another lawful requirement depends on them |
| Awards, certificates, interviews, and placement records, if any | Retain at least three years after graduation/completion or last attendance, withdrawal, cancellation, or termination, as applicable; do not create placement claims where no placement service exists |
| Grades and transcripts | Maintain perpetually and make available to the student on request |
| BPL permanent student record | Maintain permanently with every field R 339.22154 requires |

Routine completion rosters use only the Department-approved fields and omit date of birth and the last four Social Security digits unless the approved format or lawful request requires them. The complete protected record remains available to an authorized Department representative for inspection.

## Transcript request service

Students request a transcript through the authenticated account or in writing to info@statecourse.com. StateCourse verifies identity, records the request and fulfillment, delivers the protected record to the student or authorized recipient, and never conditions access on buying another product. The production workflow and a sample fulfilled request must pass acceptance before PSS filing.

## Closure and custodian transfer

Before any closure, StateCourse freezes deletion, exports the complete grade/transcript and required student-record set in an agency-readable format, reconciles counts and hashes, designates a custodian, notifies PSS/BPL as directed, and transfers records to the Board or other agency-directed custodian. Students receive the agency-approved notice and transcript-request route. Corporate dissolution, hosting termination, or vendor shutdown may not strand the records.

Closure export, count reconciliation, encrypted transfer, receipt, and post-transfer access are documented. This plan is not operational evidence until a restore/export and agency-transfer drill has passed.
