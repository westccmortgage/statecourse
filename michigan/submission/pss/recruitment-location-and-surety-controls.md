# PSS Recruitment, Location, and Surety Controls

## Location changes — R 390.564(7)

The agency-confirmed school address is controlled across MiPSS, the bond, contracts, catalog, website, advertising, BPL records, and student records. StateCourse gives PSS the required prior notice and obtains any direction/approval before changing a school location or the address PSS treats as the licensed location. A website, mailbox, principal office, records location, or administrative-address change is reviewed for PSS treatment before release; BPL's separate course-change/reporting rules do not replace this PSS notice.

## Surety continuity — R 390.562a(1)-(2)

A bond cancellation notice, lapse, missing replacement, or unverified coverage closes enrollment immediately. If coverage will expire or terminate without continuously effective replacement, StateCourse gives the Department the required written notice and retains proof of delivery. Without replacement surety, authorization expires: no new student may enroll, and active students cannot continue credited instruction or receive completion processing, certificates, or completion reports/exports. The same active-student kill switch applies when required school authorization, program approval, course approval, or instructor approval expires, is suspended/revoked, or cannot be verified.

StateCourse notifies affected students and the appropriate agency, preserves every student and authorization record, and resumes only under written agency-acceptable restoration or teach-out direction. If lawful restoration or teach-out is not permitted, StateCourse cancels affected enrollments and provides at least the regulator-compliant refund stated in the catalog/agreement, without applying the ordinary 60%/certificate cutoff; complaint, bond, and other legal rights remain intact. The gate reopens only after every agency-acceptable authorization and replacement-surety record is effective and the legal name, address, amount, bond number, and dates reconcile.

## Recruitment and advertising — R 390.566

- Advertising names a staff or faculty member only if that person is regularly employed to give instruction in, or directly supervises instruction in, the school's regular subjects.
- StateCourse uses no blind advertisement, help-wanted advertisement, employment listing, or job-opportunity framing to recruit students.
- StateCourse does not induce a person to leave another educational institution.
- StateCourse does not induce a student who is enrolled in another school and has paid that other school a deposit to change the student's educational or occupational plans.
- Employment, placement, earnings, licensing, approval, transfer, and completion statements require substantiation and may not imply a guarantee.
- Separate career information or noncredit product marketing never appears inside the 40 credited hours and earns no clock-hour credit under R 339.22159.

The pre-filing website/advertising archive and production acceptance tests cover these controls. A written policy without enforcement is not treated as readiness evidence.
