# Michigan Proprietary-School Surety Checklist

**This is bond coverage, not a cash deposit.** StateCourse pays the surety's premium; the penal amount protects students.

## Amount and period

- Nonaccredited-school formula: **$200 per student enrolled in the preceding July 1–June 30 school year, with a $5,000 minimum** under R 390.562a(3)(b).
- For a new school with no prior Michigan enrollment, prepare **$5,000** of coverage unless PSS instructs otherwise.
- The official form requires the bond to expire on **June 30** and be renewed/adjusted annually.
- The start date must cover the filing/permit period. If filing after March 2, ask the surety to write through June 30 of the following year consistent with PSS's published Q&A, then confirm the period with PSS.
- Do not order the bond until PSS confirms that this route requires it and confirms the online-school address to use.

## Cancellation kill switch

Under R 390.562a(1)-(2), a surety cancellation notice is not an ordinary renewal reminder. If coverage will expire or terminate without continuously effective replacement, StateCourse gives the Department the required written notice and retains delivery proof. If replacement surety is not continuously effective, the school's authorization expires and StateCourse must stop enrollment immediately. It also suspends active students' credited instruction, completion processing, certificates, and completion reports/exports; notifies students and agencies; and follows only written agency-acceptable restoration or teach-out direction. If neither is permitted, StateCourse cancels affected enrollments and applies the catalog/agreement's regulatory-loss refund without the ordinary 60%/certificate cutoff, preserving complaint and bond rights. The production authorization record therefore needs the bond number, penal amount, effective/expiration dates, cancellation-notice date if any, Department-notice evidence, replacement evidence, and hard new-enrollment and active-student gates. No student is enrolled or receives credited activity while coverage is cancelled, expired, or unverified.

## Purchase request to a bond agent

> After PSS confirms the proprietary-school bond route and address: StateCourse LLC is applying as an out-of-state, nonaccredited proprietary distance school under Michigan Public Act 148 of 1943. Please quote and prepare the Michigan Proprietary School Surety Bond on Michigan's official form, payable to the State of Michigan, for $5,000 coverage unless the agency requires a higher amount. The school is distance-only and has no prior Michigan enrollment. Use the exact principal name and the address confirmed by PSS, include the bond number and all insurer information, and set the period to the date confirmed for filing through June 30 of the applicable renewal year. The original completed form must include both signatures and the surety seal for upload to MiPSS.

## Final checks

- Exact legal name and address match MiPSS, BPL, advertising, and every upload.
- All blanks complete.
- Both signatures and surety seal present.
- Bond number in the portal matches the form.
- Coverage and effective dates in the portal match the form.
- Original completed bond uploaded; copies retained by school and surety.
- Enrollment refusal and the active-student instruction/completion/certificate/reporting suspension are tested for a missing, expired, cancelled, or unverified bond and remain closed until replacement coverage is recorded without a gap and accepted by the agency.
- Any expected expiration/termination without replacement has a dated written Department notice and retained proof of delivery.
