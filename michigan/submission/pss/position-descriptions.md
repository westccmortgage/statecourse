# General Position Descriptions

These are positions, not descriptions of the current candidate. They remain valid if a different qualified person fills a role.

## Programme Coordinator / School Compliance Administrator

**Main duties**

- Supervise the proprietary-school programme and its compliance with PSS and BPL requirements.
- Maintain the active school authorization and the approved program, course, instructor, address, advertising, bond, and policy records.
- File required changes, schedules, renewals, and reports by their deadlines.
- Supervise student records, contract/catalog consistency, refunds, complaints, identity verification, attendance, certificates, and regulator access.
- Ensure that enrollment remains closed when a school, programme, course, instructor, surety, or operational gate is not active.

**Minimum education:** bachelor's degree or an equivalent combination of relevant postsecondary education and professional experience.<br>
**Minimum experience:** three years in regulated education, professional licensing, real-estate brokerage administration, compliance, or comparable programme operations.<br>
**Licence/certification:** no separate coordinator licence is stated in R 339.22145; knowledge of the Michigan proprietary-school and real-estate education rules is required.

## Real Estate Prelicensure Instructor

**Main duties**

- Provide current, accurate instruction and responses on the approved course material.
- Maintain an environment conducive to learning and assist students with course questions.
- Assure and certify accurate attendance and completion information.
- Review and score every required applied memorandum and resubmission under the filed rubric within the one-business-day standard; provide criterion-level feedback; and answer remediation/course questions.
- Protect licensing-examination content and comply with all applicable laws, rules, approval conditions, and change controls.

**Minimum education:** high-school diploma or equivalent, plus documented real-estate, finance, law, business, or related education/training sufficient to support the BPL qualification filing; BPL route (1)(b) itself states no degree minimum.<br>
**Minimum experience:** at least two years of full-time-equivalent experience in one or more of appraisal, real-estate finance, marketing, brokerage management, property management, real-estate counselling, real property law, or a closely related subject.<br>
**Licence/certification:** before teaching, the person must be approved by BPL under R 339.22147 and meet route (1)(a) or (1)(b). PSS guidance separately says an instructor preparing students for an occupation requiring a licence/certification must possess the same credential. The candidate therefore must hold the exact credential or out-of-state equivalent that PSS confirms in writing satisfies that requirement; BPL approval alone is not treated as resolving PSS eligibility.

## Student Records and Support Administrator

**Main duties**

- Respond to technical and administrative student requests.
- Preserve enrollment, payment, attendance, progress, grade, completion, attestation, identity, certificate, and reporting records for the required periods.
- Escalate academic questions to the approved instructor and compliance questions to the programme coordinator.
- Process approved cancellations/refunds consistently with the signed contract and catalog.
- Prepare regulator inspection and reporting exports using least-necessary data.

**Minimum education:** high-school diploma or equivalent; postsecondary administrative, records, or information-systems training preferred.<br>
**Minimum experience:** two years in customer support, student services, records administration, regulated operations, or comparable work.<br>
**Licence/certification:** none; this role may not provide licensed real-estate advice or perform instructor duties without separate approval.

At launch, one individual may perform more than one administrative role if qualified and approved where required. Combining roles does not combine or waive their duties.
