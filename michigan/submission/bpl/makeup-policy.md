# Makeup and Interrupted-Study Policy

This asynchronous course has no live class session to miss. “Makeup” therefore means completing every unfinished or remediated part without reducing the 40 course hours requested for approval.

1. **Progress persists.** Server-side records preserve completed sections, active study time, written-memorandum versions and instructor rubric/feedback, applied-confirmation and assessment attempts, and remediation state. A student resumes at the same incomplete point after a device, browser, power, or internet interruption. This full Michigan workflow must pass prefiling acceptance testing before the policy is represented as an operating capability.
2. **No partial credit becomes course credit.** An unfinished module remains incomplete. The student receives no credit for that four-hour module until every required element is complete.
3. **Every deficiency is cured.** A needs-revision memorandum receives criterion-level feedback, sends the student back to relevant instruction, and is resubmitted until it earns 8/10 with no zero for legal accuracy or professional boundaries. A missed applied confirmation returns the student to the relevant instruction and is retried until correct. An unsuccessful engagement assessment serves an alternate explanation and separately authored five-item set; the module cannot complete until the student reaches the StateCourse 80% threshold on that set.
4. **No attempt/version is consumed by interruption.** A machine-scored attempt exists only after the answers are submitted. A memorandum version is submitted only after server acknowledgment; a partial receipt is reconciled before retry, and every confirmed version and feedback record remains preserved.
5. **Instructor help is available.** The BPL-approved instructor reviews each memorandum within the one-business-day standard. The launch workflow must also create an instructor-support flag after three unsuccessful machine-scored remediation attempts and pass both behaviors in acceptance testing. A student may ask for help at any time.
6. **Enrollment period controls timing, not hours.** The student may return to unfinished work during the signed enrollment period. An extension or re-enrollment does not waive any required content or hour.
7. **Every student completes 100%.** The policy changes when unfinished work is completed, never whether it must be completed.

This policy implements R 339.22141(1)(i) and R 339.22152(3) for a distance-only course.
