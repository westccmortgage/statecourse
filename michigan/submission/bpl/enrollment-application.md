## Prior-year completion disclosure

> **Completion rate:** This course was not offered during the previous calendar year, so there is no prior-year completion percentage to report. Beginning with the first calendar year in which students enroll, StateCourse LLC will publish the percentage calculated as students who successfully completed the course divided by students who enrolled during that calendar year, and will update this disclosure no later than February 1 each year as R 339.22141(1)(j) requires.
