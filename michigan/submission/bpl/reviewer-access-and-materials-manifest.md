# BPL Reviewer Access and Materials Manifest

## Course materials

- Ten curriculum modules at `michigan/mi-salesperson-prelicensure-40-v1/curriculum/`.
- Forty-hour module/objective map at `michigan/submission/module-map.md`.
- Ten written applied memoranda with a common instructor rubric, plus 110 distinct secure-bank items: applied confirmation, engagement, and remediation forms.
- Completion, attendance, remediation, identity, attestation, reporting, certificate, and incident-control descriptions in the BPL packet.

The reviewer environment is not represented as ready until the Michigan pre-filing acceptance gate proves every described workflow.

## Reviewer account procedure

1. Create a named, time-limited reviewer account after the agency identifies the reviewer or requests credentials.
2. Grant read-only navigation through every module and a safe test path through assessment/remediation states.
3. Transmit the username and initial password through a channel separate from the application package.
4. Require password change on first use and record reviewer access without creating student credit.
5. Disable the account after the review window or agency instruction.

No credential, access token, answer key, or private student record is committed to git.

## What the reviewer will be able to verify after acceptance

- Every module and objective listed in the application exists.
- Required sections and assessments cannot be skipped into completion.
- An unsuccessful assessment enters remediation and an unresolved module blocks the course.
- A written memorandum cannot complete until the approved instructor records a satisfactory rubric result; every version and feedback record remains reviewable.
- Active time and completion are server decisions, not browser claims.
- Identity and signed-attestation gates attach before certificate issuance, and identity verification separately blocks every completion report/export until the reported student is established as the student who completed the course.
- The certificate refuses to issue without every identifier BPL confirms is required.
- The public Michigan course page and enrollment flow remain closed before approval.
