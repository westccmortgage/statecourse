## Course objectives

After completing all modules, the student can:

- identify Michigan licensed activity, licence relationships, supervision, discipline, and unlicensed limits;
- analyse property rights, estates, co-ownership, land description, title transfer, recording, liens, taxes, and land-use controls;
- apply Michigan agency, service-provision-agreement, disclosure, contract, and seller-disclosure rules to transaction facts;
- recognize and avoid fair-housing, civil-rights, lending-discrimination, steering, advertising, and accessibility violations;
- explain real-estate finance, settlement, valuation, appraisal limits, transaction mathematics, closing, trust-account, and professional-practice principles; and
- complete a Michigan transaction analysis while identifying issues that require a supervising broker, lawyer, lender, appraiser, title professional, or regulator.

## Learning and assessment method

Each module contains stated objectives, six instructional sections with checkpoints, Michigan examples, key terms, practice problems, a required written transaction memorandum, one applied confirmation item, and a five-item engagement assessment. A BPL-approved instructor reviews the memorandum under the common 10-point rubric; satisfactory requires 8/10 with no zero on legal accuracy or professional boundaries, and a needs-revision memo is resubmitted after feedback. The applied confirmation requires 100%. Engagement and the separately authored five-item remediation set require 80%. These are StateCourse standards rather than Michigan-prescribed scores, and the module remains incomplete until every applicable requirement passes.

Michigan does not prescribe a final examination. This submission draft uses the scored assessment and remediation inside every module and includes no separate provider final. If StateCourse later proposes one, its threshold, security, grade record, and completion effect will be documented and reviewed as a course change before release.

## Attendance, identity, and completion

- The student completes 100% of all ten modules; partial hours do not produce credit.
- Active study time, required content, written-memo versions/rubric/feedback, applied-confirmation, assessment, and remediation states are recorded server-side.
- The student signs the 40-clock-hour asynchronous attestation.
- Government photo identification and identity assurance are completed before certificate issuance and before any completion report/export.
- The school issues no certificate until every course and regulatory gate is satisfied and the required approval number exists.
- Successful completion is reported in the Department-approved format within five business days.

No organizational-membership, employment, business-related, account-upgrade, recruiting, or other noneducational presentation or solicitation occurs inside the 40 credited hours. No such presentation earns credit. This separation is a release gate under R 339.22159.

## Support and conduct

Students submit academic or technical questions through their account or to info@statecourse.com. The response commitment is one business day. Three unsuccessful remediation attempts create an instructor-support flag. Students may not seek, reproduce, distribute, or induce disclosure of licensing-examination questions.
