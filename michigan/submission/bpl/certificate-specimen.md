## Issuance controls

The specimen contains the fields required by R 339.22141(1)(f): completion date, the course identified by its Department-approved name, school name and approval number, student name, and clock hours. Because the BPL form distinguishes PSS, Sponsor, and Course numbers, the specimen keeps all three placeholders separate until BPL confirms which number or numbers satisfy the certificate rule. Assigned values are copied from their approval records, not hand-entered from memory.

No certificate issues unless:

1. PSS school licensure/program approval and BPL course/instructor approvals are active;
2. all ten modules and required assessments/remediation are complete;
3. the student has completed all 40 credited hours;
4. photo identification and distance-student identity assurance are complete;
5. the student has signed the asynchronous-hour attestation;
6. every identifier BPL requires on the certificate has been assigned; and
7. the certificate is written to the permanent completion record.

Bracketed fields are specimen placeholders that cannot be mistaken for an assigned government number.
