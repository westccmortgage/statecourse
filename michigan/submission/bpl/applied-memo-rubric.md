# Michigan Written Applied-Memorandum Rubric

**Course:** Principles of Real Estate - Michigan Salesperson Prelicensure<br>
**Course version:** 1.0-submission-draft<br>
**Scope:** one required written transaction memorandum in each of ten modules<br>
**Reviewer:** BPL-approved instructor only

## Scoring

| Criterion | 0 points | 1 point | 2 points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Michigan law/rule accuracy | Material rule is wrong, missing, or from another jurisdiction | Core rule identified but incomplete or imprecise | Controlling Michigan/federal rule accurately identified and used |
| Fact analysis and calculation | Material facts/calculations omitted or unsupported | Most relevant facts addressed; minor gap | Relevant facts, calculations, and consequences fully connected to the rule |
| Professional boundary and referral | Gives unauthorized legal/appraisal/lending advice or misses a required escalation | Identifies a boundary but referral/escalation is incomplete | Correctly distinguishes licensee work, broker escalation, and specialist referral |
| Transaction action and timing | No usable action/deadline sequence | Action is generally sound but timing/evidence is incomplete | Clear action, responsible person, timing/deadline, and verification method |
| File record and communication | No adequate documentation/communication plan | Partial record or communication plan | Complete, neutral file record and appropriate client/professional communication |

**Satisfactory:** at least **8 of 10 points**, with no zero in Michigan law/rule accuracy or professional boundary/referral. This is a StateCourse standard submitted for BPL review, not a Michigan-prescribed score.

## Workflow and record

The student submits the memo inside the module. The system records the student, enrollment, course/module/version, submission version, text/file hash, and submission time. A BPL-approved instructor records criterion scores, satisfactory or needs-revision status, criterion-level feedback, reviewer identity, and review time within the one-business-day support standard.

A needs-revision result keeps the module incomplete. The student receives the feedback, repeats the relevant instruction, and submits a new version; no earlier version or feedback is overwritten. There is no attempt limit. Administrative support may route the record but may not score it. The module can complete only after a satisfactory memo, correct applied-confirmation item, successful engagement/remediation state, and every other module requirement.

Before PSS filing, acceptance evidence must prove submission, role-restricted review, feedback, resubmission, version retention, response timing, and completion refusal. This rubric is the filed design; it is not evidence that the production workflow already exists.
