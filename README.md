## Status and approval disclaimer

The complete first-draft course and submission framework is in development and validation. It is **not** represented as approved by the Virginia Real Estate Board. The course must not be offered for Virginia prelicense credit until formal Board approval is issued.

Regulatory content is grounded in current official Virginia sources. Before filing or delivery, the named Board-certified instructor and qualified legal/compliance reviewer must validate the curriculum, assessments, forms, and legal-update log, and the LMS must produce the required timer evidence.
