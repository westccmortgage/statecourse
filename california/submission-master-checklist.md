# California First-Priority Submission Master Checklist

## Proposed filing order

1. Real Estate Principles - first filing, consistent with RE 303's advice that a first-time applicant submit one course before multiple courses.
2. Real Estate Practice - separately filed after initial format feedback; includes SB 1495 evidence.
3. Mortgage Loan Brokering and Lending - separately filed after initial format feedback.

## Release gates common to every package

- Confirm StateCourse LLC filing/mailing address, public phone, public support email, website, California/out-of-state entity status, and attach current Secretary of State evidence.
- Obtain written BPPE applicability/exemption determination and insert the exact basis.
- Insert exact tuition, all charges, cancellation, and refund terms; reconcile with website and enrollment agreement.
- Obtain independent college professor certification on official letterhead for each exact manuscript.
- Complete and sign RE 336; attach qualification evidence; await DRE instructor determination.
- Provision secure student/regulator accounts and transmit passwords separately.
- Perform legal, editorial, accessibility, exam-item, security, and cross-document consistency QA.
- Verify the current RE 206E fee on filing day.
- Obtain original or compliant electronic signatures; no rubber-stamp/digital image signature.

No item labeled pending may be converted to a favorable claim without documentary evidence.
