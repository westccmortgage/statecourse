# California DRE Reviewer Access - Real Estate Principles

StateCourse provisions two distinct accounts for this correspondence/Internet method when the RE 306 package is filed. Credentials are transmitted to DRE through the filing channel or a separate secure message and are never committed to source control.

- **Student login:** shows the course and final-exam information exactly as a student sees it, including sequential controls and timing messages. It cannot receive official credit because it is a reviewer fixture.
- **Regulator login:** shows every lesson, assessment, remediation path, Form A and Form B, and audit view while bypassing study-time restrictions. A permanent banner states: "California DRE Regulatory Review Mode - study-time restrictions bypassed; no student credit generated."
- Reviewer events use isolated account IDs, cannot create a paid enrollment or certificate, and are excluded from completion and reporting.
