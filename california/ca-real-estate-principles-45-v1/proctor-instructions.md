# Exam Proctor/Monitor Instructions - Real Estate Principles

## Eligibility and independence

The proctor is designated by StateCourse and may not be related to the student by blood, marriage, or domestic partnership and may not have another relationship - including a prospective responsible-broker relationship - that could reasonably influence administration. The proctor must not be selected solely by the student without StateCourse approval.

## Before the examination

1. Confirm the secure appointment, course, form, student, and authorized attempt.
2. Inspect acceptable current photo identification and match the person, name, photograph, signature, and identification number. Record document type, issuing authority, last four characters, verification result, and timestamp; do not retain an unnecessary full image.
3. Inspect the testing space and confirm the closed-book rule, no second device, no notes, no recording, no assistance, and no other person present.
4. Open the secure proctor session. The exam is never sent directly to the student and is not printable or downloadable.

## During the examination

Maintain supervision for the 180-minute session. The system records start, timeout, reconnection, submission, and incident events. Do not interpret questions, identify answers, permit copying, or extend time except under an approved accommodation. End and report a material security event.

## After the examination

Submit the secure exam, confirm that no exam content remains with the student, and complete the certification below. The LMS returns the completed exam to StateCourse and preserves the attempt; the student does not transmit the final.

## Certification
