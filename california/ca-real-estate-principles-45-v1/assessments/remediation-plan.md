# Remediation Plan - Real Estate Principles

Every missed lesson item maps to its topic and course reference. The LMS presents: (1) an alternate explanation, (2) a new California scenario, (3) a parallel scored question, and (4) instructor-support escalation after three unsuccessful attempts on the same concept. Remediation results are version-locked and auditable. DRE does not prescribe the 80% lesson threshold; this is StateCourse's proposed academic control and remains subject to course approval.
