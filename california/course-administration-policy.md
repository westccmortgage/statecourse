# California Correspondence Course Administration Policy

## Course independence

Each 45-hour course is a separate DRE approval, course version, enrollment, 15-assignment record, exam pair, certificate, and completion event. A commercial bundle never merges regulatory records.

## Time and sequence

The access-granted timestamp starts the 420-hour minimum. A successful final before the timestamp is technically impossible. The portfolio service also prevents more than two StateCourse California correspondence completions in 35 days and requires a truthful disclosure of outside-school course completions. All 15 assignments are sequential. Course access expires after 365 days. These controls implement the California timing rules; they do not import Virginia-style per-hour active timers.

## Assessment and final

Lesson quizzes and remediation are provider-selected academic controls. The DRE final is separate: 100 questions, 60%, two forms maximum, one retake, designated independent proctor, identity check, no student custody, no printing/download, and automatic timeout.

## Approval gate

Checkout, enrollment, final administration, completion credit, and certificates remain disabled until the exact course and delivery approval is recorded. Reviewer accounts generate no student credit. Material changes create a new development version and cannot replace an approved version without DRE review.
