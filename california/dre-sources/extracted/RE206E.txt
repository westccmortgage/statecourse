The Education 
and Research Section of the Department of Real Estate reviews Pre-
license/Statutory course submissions and Continuing Education course submissions 
pursuant to Article 24 and 25 of the Regulations of the Real Estate Commissioner.
Please review the Course Provider Handbook and Frequently Asked Questions 
page for and questions on course submissions prior to contacting the Education 
and Research Section. These resources can be found on the DRE website at  
https://www.dre.ca.gov/Licensees/CourseProviders.html
Please direct course review submissions to:
Department of Real Estate, Education and Research Section
651 Bannon Street, STE 504
Attn: Education Section
Sacramento, CA 95811
(916) 576-3366
Please note that CE course submissions require 90 days for review and Pre-license/
Statutory course submissions require 60 days for review.
