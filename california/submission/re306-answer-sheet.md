## The 15B question

Item 15B offers three boxes: IN PERSON PROCTORED, INTERNET, MOBILE DEVICE APP. Our examination is **both**: it is delivered through the internet platform (non-printable, non-downloadable, hard timeout at 180 minutes — the RE 303 online-exam conditions) **and** it is administered and supervised in person by a school-designated proctor, as Regulation 3000(a)(2)(B) requires for correspondence courses.

Which single box DRE wants checked for that arrangement is not answerable from the form. **Call the Education Section at (916) 576-3366 before filing**, record the answer and the name of who gave it, and check the box they name. Do not guess: a form whose 15B answer contradicts the attached proctor instructions is a deficiency letter.

## School certification (page 3)

The certification consents to DRE inspection and monitoring — direct-mail evaluation of past students and drop-in monitoring of course offerings. Nothing in the platform obstructs either: completion rosters are producible on request, and the reviewer account (RE 303 item 15) gives DRE the same drop-in access to an internet course that a monitor gets to a classroom. Signed by Anatoliy Kanevsky for StateCourse LLC at filing.

## What must exist before these three forms can be signed

In dependency order:

1. **BPPE answer** (item 8) — YES with approval, or NO/EXEMPT with the citation. Counsel or a written BPPE determination.
2. **FBNS decision** (items 2, 10) — counsel; if YES, file the FBNS first and attach it.
3. **RE 206E** — the fee amount (checklist item 2).
4. **RE 313** — the General Information Page content (item 21 and checklist item 9).
5. **RE 311** — the completion certificate fields (checklist item 16).
6. **15B answer** from the Education Section.
7. **Phone, public email, website** (items 3, 6, 7).
8. **Three RE 336 instructor certifications** with substantiation (checklist item 8).
9. **Printed instructional materials** for the envelope (checklist item 6) — RE 303 requires printed copies; DRE cannot access digital media.
10. **Reviewer credentials** issued on the production system (checklist item 15).

None of these is engineering. Items 1, 2 and 6 are questions to people; 3, 4 and 5 are documents DRE supplies on request; 7 is administrative; 8 is recruitment; 9 is a print run; 10 is deployment.
