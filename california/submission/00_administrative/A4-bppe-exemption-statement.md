## What changed, and why

**An earlier draft of this sheet claimed the exemption at Education Code § 94874(d)(2)(A)** —
license examination preparation approved by a licensing agency other than the Bureau. **That
basis is withdrawn.** It failed on two independent grounds, and each on its own is enough.

**First, it does not describe what these courses are.** § 94874(d)(2) exempts *"continuing
education or **license examination preparation**"*. The three courses are the statutory
qualifying education **Business and Professions Code § 10153.2** requires **before an applicant
may sit** the licensing examination. Qualifying to take an examination and preparing for one are
different things: an examination-preparation provider teaches a candidate to pass a test they are
already eligible to take, and a statutory course is what makes them eligible in the first place.
Reading "license examination preparation" to cover statutory qualifying education is an argument,
not a reading, and item 8 is not the place for an argument.

**Second, and decisively, the Bureau's own evidentiary rule makes the claim unsupportable at the
time of filing.** **5 CCR § 71395(b)(7)**, effective 1 July 2026, requires an institution claiming
an exemption under **§ 94874(d)(2)**:

> *"a **letter from that entity** indicating the validity of the accreditation or approval, and the
> name and contact information for a representative of that entity"*

The entity would be the Department of Real Estate, and the letter would confirm an approval.
**StateCourse LLC holds no DRE approval — that is what this application is for.** The earlier draft
recognised the circularity and spent two paragraphs arguing around it. The regulation removes the
argument: the Bureau asks for a document that cannot exist yet.

## The exemption relied on

**Education Code § 94874** opens: *"Except as provided in Sections 94874.2, 94874.7, and 94927.5,
the following are exempt from this chapter:"* and at subdivision (f):

> **(f)** *"An institution that **does not award degrees** and that **solely provides educational
> programs for total charges of two thousand five hundred dollars ($2,500) or less** when **no part
> of the total charges is paid from state or federal student financial aid programs.** The bureau
> may adjust this cost threshold based upon the California Consumer Price Index and post
> notification of the adjusted cost threshold on its internet website as the bureau determines,
> through the promulgation of regulations, that the adjustment is consistent with the intent of
> this chapter."*

## Applying it, element by element

| Element of § 94874(f) | StateCourse LLC |
|---|---|
| **An institution that does not award degrees** | StateCourse LLC awards no degree of any kind. What a student receives on completion is the RE 311 course completion certificate, which is evidence that a course was completed and is not a degree, a diploma, or a certificate of occupational competence. |
| **…solely provides educational programs for total charges of $2,500 or less** | Every price is published on RE 313 and every one is far below the threshold: **$79** for a single course, **$199** for the three-course required-education package, **$299** for the Complete Licensing Path. **$299 is the highest charge for anything StateCourse LLC sells.** There is no product at any price above it, so the word "solely" is satisfied without qualification. |
| **…when no part of the total charges is paid from state or federal student financial aid programs** | **StateCourse LLC is not approved to offer federal student financial assistance, has not applied to be, and accepts no state or federal student financial aid.** Payment is by card through Stripe, from the student. |
| **Not caught by § 94874.2** | That section removes the exemption only from an institution approved to participate in **veterans' financial aid programs** under 38 C.F.R. § 21.4253 that is not an independent institution of higher education. StateCourse LLC is not approved for veterans' financial aid programs and does not seek to be. |
| **Not caught by § 94874.7** | That section removes no exemption. It lets an exempt institution **request** verification of its exemption. It is optional. See below. |
| **Not caught by § 94927.5** | That section does not reach an institution of this description. |

**Every element is a question of fact with a checkable answer.** None requires an interpretation
of what a course is for, and none depends on an approval that does not yet exist.

## Why this basis is the right one for THIS filing

**5 CCR § 71395(b)(9)** states what the Bureau asks of an institution claiming § 94874(f):

> *"The **total institutional charges for the educational program**, and **whether or not the
> institution is approved to offer federal financial assistance**"*

Both are facts StateCourse LLC can state today, in one line each, with nothing outstanding:
**$299 maximum**, and **not approved to offer federal financial assistance**. Compare the
(d)(2) route, which asks for a letter from DRE confirming an approval that this application is
seeking. One basis is provable on the day of filing; the other cannot be.

## § 94874(d)(2)(A), noted but not relied on

If the Department prefers the licensing-agency route once approval issues, § 94874(d)(2)(A) may
also describe StateCourse LLC at that point: the Department of Real Estate is a government agency
other than the Bureau that licenses persons in a particular profession, and an approved
statutory course would be an approved program. **That is a possibility for the future and is not
the basis of this filing.** Nothing in this application depends on it, and item 8 does not cite it.

## Optional strengthening step

**Education Code § 94874.7** allows an exempt institution to request written verification of its
exemption from the Bureau, valid up to two years, for a fee, on the application at
**5 CCR § 71395**. **This is not required and this application does not wait on it.** It is worth
doing afterwards, because a verification letter answers item 8 with a document rather than a
statement on every renewal and on every other state's form that asks the same question. On the
§ 94874(f) basis the application is straightforward: the charges and the financial-aid answer are
all § 71395(b)(9) asks for.

**Recommendation:** file on § 94874(f) as stated, and pursue § 94874.7 verification separately and
without urgency.

## What is asserted here, and what is not

**Asserted:** StateCourse LLC is exempt from the Private Postsecondary Education Act under
Education Code § 94874(f), and no BPPE approval or registration document is therefore required to
be attached under RE 307 Regulation 3002(a)(2)(E).

**Asserted:** StateCourse LLC is a private vocational school **for purposes of the Real Estate
Law**, because Regulation 3000(b) says the simultaneous instruction of two or more students in a
§ 10153.2 course is one. That classification and the Education Code exemption are separate
questions under separate statutes, and both answers are given here.

**Not asserted:** that BPPE has verified the exemption. It has not been requested.
**Not asserted:** that any StateCourse course is approved by DRE. None is.
**Not asserted:** that § 94874(d)(2)(A) applies. It is noted as a possible future basis and is not
relied on.
