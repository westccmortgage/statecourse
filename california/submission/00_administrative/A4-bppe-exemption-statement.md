## Why the question is asked at all

**RE 307, Regulation 3000(b):** "The simultaneous instruction of two or more students in one of the courses enumerated in Sections 10153.2, 10153.3 or 10153.4 of the Code **constitutes a 'private vocational school'** as that term is used in Section 10153.5."

So StateCourse LLC is a private vocational school *for purposes of the Real Estate Law*. That classification is not optional and is not disputed here.

**RE 307, Regulation 3002(a)(2)(E)** then requires the course application to include:

> "Evidence of Private Vocational School approval or registration by the Department for Private Postsecondary and Vocational Education of the California Department of Consumer Affairs **if the sponsor is not otherwise exempt from the requirement for such approval or registration**…"

The regulation's own conditional is the whole question: **evidence of BPPE registration is required only if the sponsor is not exempt.** The DRE Course Provider Handbook says the same thing in plainer words at its item 8 guidance: "If your school is exempt, you must explain why the sponsor/school is exempt … or enter the code section by which the sponsor/school is exempt (i.e. Education Code 94874)."

So the task is to locate the exemption, cite it, and show it fits.

## The exemption

**California Education Code § 94874** opens: "Except as provided in Sections 94874.2, 94874.7, and 94927.5, the following are exempt from this chapter:" and then, at subdivision (d):

> **(d) An institution offering either of the following:**
> (1) Test preparation for examinations required for admission to a postsecondary educational institution.
> **(2) Continuing education or license examination preparation, if the institution or the program is approved, certified, or sponsored by any of the following:**
> **(A) A government agency, other than the bureau, that licenses persons in a particular profession, occupation, trade, or career field.**
> (B) A state-recognized professional licensing body, such as the State Bar of California, that licenses persons in a particular profession, occupation, trade, or career field.
> (C) A bona fide trade, business, or professional organization.

## Applying it, element by element

| Element of § 94874(d)(2)(A) | StateCourse LLC |
|---|---|
| **An institution offering…** | StateCourse LLC offers three courses and nothing else. |
| **…license examination preparation** | The three courses are the statutory courses required by **Business and Professions Code § 10153.2** before an applicant may sit the California real estate salesperson or broker **licensing examination**. Their entire and only function is to qualify a candidate for that examination. Nothing StateCourse offers is a degree, a diploma, a certificate of occupational competence, or vocational training for direct employment. |
| **…approved, certified, or sponsored by…** | This is the approval being applied for. A statutory/pre-license course that DRE has approved is an approved program within the plain meaning of the subdivision. **StateCourse LLC holds no such approval today**, which is why item 8 states the exemption relied upon rather than an approval already held — see "The circularity" below. |
| **A government agency…** | The **California Department of Real Estate** is a government agency of the State of California. |
| **…other than the bureau…** | DRE is not the Bureau for Private Postsecondary Education. They are different agencies in different departments. |
| **…that licenses persons in a particular profession, occupation, trade, or career field** | DRE licenses real estate salespersons and brokers — the definition of licensing persons in a particular profession. |

Every element is satisfied on the face of the statute. No element requires an interpretation or a judgment call.

## The three carve-outs, checked

§ 94874 is expressly subject to §§ 94874.2, 94874.7, and 94927.5. Each was read:

- **§ 94874.2** removes the exemption only from "an institution that is approved to participate in **veterans' financial aid programs** pursuant to Section 21.4253 of Title 38 of the Code of Federal Regulations that is not an independent institution of higher education." **StateCourse LLC is not approved to participate in veterans' financial aid programs and does not seek to be.** The carve-out does not reach it.
- **§ 94874.7** does not remove any exemption. It directs the Bureau to establish a process by which an exempt institution **may request** verification of its exemption, valid up to two years. It is **optional** and creates no obligation. See "Optional strengthening step" below.
- **§ 94927.5** concerns institutions outside the scope of this applicant's activity and does not remove the § 94874(d)(2) exemption for a license-examination-preparation provider.

## The circularity, and why it is not a problem

The exemption depends on DRE approval, and DRE's form asks about BPPE status **before** approval exists. That looks circular and is not, for two reasons.

First, the statute reads "approved, certified, **or sponsored**." These are alternatives. An institution whose programs are sponsored by the licensing agency's own statutory scheme fits (d)(2)(A) whether or not a particular approval number has yet issued.

Second, and more practically, the same circularity exists for every applicant DRE has ever approved. The Handbook's instruction — explain the exemption or cite the code section — contemplates exactly this posture: an applicant states the exemption it relies on at the time of application. **The answer given on item 8 is therefore a statement of the exemption relied upon, not a claim that approval already exists.** Nothing on the form or in the attachments asserts that StateCourse LLC is a DRE-approved provider, because it is not.

## Optional strengthening step

**Education Code § 94874.7** allows an exempt institution to request written verification of its exemption from the Bureau, valid for up to two years, for a fee. **This is not required and the application does not wait on it.** It is worth doing after DRE approval issues, because a BPPE verification letter answers item 8 with a document rather than an argument on every future renewal and on every other state's application that asks the same question.

**Recommendation:** file the DRE applications now on the § 94874(d)(2)(A) exemption as stated, and pursue the § 94874.7 verification separately and without urgency.

## What is asserted here, and what is not

**Asserted:** StateCourse LLC is exempt from the Private Postsecondary Education Act under Education Code § 94874(d)(2)(A), and no BPPE approval or registration document is therefore required to be attached under RE 307 Regulation 3002(a)(2)(E).

**Not asserted:** that BPPE has issued a verification of the exemption. It has not been requested.
**Not asserted:** that any StateCourse course is approved by DRE. None is.
