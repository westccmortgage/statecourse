## One check or three

**Three separate checks of $500, one clipped to each application.** Two reasons, and both are practical rather than legal:

1. **A problem with one application cannot hold the other two.** If DRE returns one course for a deficiency, its fee travels with it and the other two proceed. A single $1,500 check ties the three together in DRE's cashiering whether or not the reviews stay together.
2. **The cashier can match payment to file without reading a memo line.** A check clipped to the application it pays for needs no interpretation.

Write on each check's memo line: **the course title**. For example — *RE 306 — Real Estate Principles*.

**If paying by credit card instead:** use form **RE 909**, one per application, and send no check. RE 909 is held at `california/dre-sources/re909.pdf`.
