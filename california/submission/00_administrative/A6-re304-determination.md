# RE 306 / RE 303 item 5 — Consent to Service of Process (RE 304)

**Applicant:** StateCourse LLC
**Prepared:** August 20, 2026
**Determination: RE 304 is NOT required. Do not include it in the submission.**

## The answer

**RE 304 (Rev. 7/18) states its own scope on its face:**

> "This form is to be used by **nonresident sponsors** for the approval of a continuing education offering or a real estate pre-license course."

**The DRE Course Providers page states the same rule twice**, once for pre-license providers and once for CE providers:

> "**All out-of-state course providers will also need form RE 304.**"

Both authorities condition the form on the sponsor being a nonresident or out-of-state provider. Neither describes StateCourse LLC.

## Why StateCourse LLC is not a nonresident sponsor

| **Principal office in California** — 150 E Olive Avenue, Suite 112, Burbank, CA 91502 | Articles of Organization |
| **Mailing address in California** — 407 N Pacific Coast Highway, Unit 1028, Redondo Beach, CA 90277 | Articles of Organization |
| **Agent for service of process is a California resident at a California address** — Anatoliy Kanevsky, Redondo Beach | Articles of Organization |
| The sole member and proposed instructor holds a **California DRE broker licence, #01385024** | DRE licence record |

The purpose of a consent to service of process is to give a California regulator a way to serve a sponsor that is otherwise beyond its reach. **StateCourse LLC is already within reach**: it is a California entity, formed here, with a California principal office and a California agent for service of process designated in a public filing with the California Secretary of State. There is nothing for RE 304 to accomplish.

## The previous analysis is superseded

An earlier version of this analysis was written when the intended applicant was a **Wyoming** LLC. Under that fact pattern RE 304 would very likely have been required, and a foreign-qualification step with the California Secretary of State would have preceded it.

**That fact pattern no longer exists.** The Wyoming entity is not the applicant, is not referenced anywhere in this submission, and is retained for an unrelated purpose. Every conclusion that depended on it is withdrawn, including the earlier "probably not required — confirm with counsel" hedge, which was hedging against a foreign-entity risk that has since been eliminated by forming in California.

## What would change this

RE 304 would become required if StateCourse LLC ceased to be a California entity, moved its principal office out of California, or lost a California agent for service of process. None is contemplated.

## What is asserted here, and what is not

**Asserted:** RE 304 is not required for this applicant, on the authority of the form's own scope statement and DRE's published instruction to course providers.
**Not asserted:** that DRE has confirmed this in writing. It has not been asked, because the published authorities are unambiguous and asking would delay a filing on a question the forms already answer.

**If a DRE reviewer nonetheless requests RE 304**, it is a one-page form signed by the same person who signs everything else in this package. Complying takes an afternoon. That is the reason for stating the determination plainly and moving on rather than filing a form the instructions say does not apply.
