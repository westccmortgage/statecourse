## What the reviewer is actually deciding

Not whether the paperwork is complete. **B&P Code § 10153.5** asks whether the course of study is *equivalent in quality* to real estate courses offered by colleges and universities accredited by the Western Association of Schools and Colleges, and **RE 307 Regulation 3000(a)** lists the criteria the Commissioner considers. Those criteria are the spine of this submission:

| Regulation 3000(a) criterion | How this submission answers it |
|---|---|
| (1) A correspondence course shall consist of **not less than 15 separate lesson assignments** | Exactly 15, each 180 minutes, 45 hours total |
| (2)(B) A correspondence course must provide for a final examination **administered and supervised by a person designated by the school**; the school sends the materials to that person and the completed examination is returned to the school | The proctor instructions and the examination delivery model, checklist items 12 and 13 |
| (3) Instructors, instructional material and facilities **adequate to achieve the objective** | ~450 pages of original text per course — DRE's own stated college-equivalence benchmark — plus the RE 336 instructor package |
| (4) Records sufficient to allow **preparation of a duplicate certificate on request** | Completion records retained; duplicates produced from the record, corrections appended rather than overwritten |
| (5) **No deceptive or misleading advertising** | No StateCourse document or page claims approval; enrollment is closed and priced at nothing until approval exists |
| (6) Within **15 days** of successful completion, deliver a document evidencing completion containing the approval number, student name, course title, dates, and school name and address | Modelled in the platform, with the delivery deadline computed from the completion date; blocked entirely until DRE assigns an approval number |
| (7) An **appropriate method of assessing student knowledge** | Per-assignment applied exercises and scored assessments, plus the 100-question proctored final |
| (8) **Instructor credentials** — community college credentials, a comparable credentialing agency, Title 5 § 53400 qualifications, or qualifications the Commissioner judges equivalent by education or experience | A7 — the equivalence route, on education and experience |
| (9) Notify each student that an **evaluation form is available on the Department's website** | RE 306 item 22; three notifications, the operative one at completion |

## Timing

**Pre-license/statutory course submissions require 60 days for review** (RE 206E; DRE Course Providers page). The fee is non-refundable and must be received before the application is processed.
