## ADDITIONAL SCHOOL POLICY AND PROCEDURES — text to enter

> **Delivery and progression.** This is a correspondence/independent study, internet course of 15 separate lesson assignments totalling 45 hours. Assignments are delivered in sequence; each segment must be completed before the next opens. Active study time is recorded by the platform against each assignment.
>
> **Final examination.** Closed book. 100 multiple-choice questions in 180 minutes, 60% to pass. The examination is delivered over the internet and is administered and supervised in person by a proctor designated by the school. It is not printable and not downloadable, and it times out when the allotted time expires. One original examination and one retake on a different examination form. A student who fails the retake does not receive credit and must retake the course.
>
> **Identification.** The student must present acceptable photo identification to the proctor immediately before the examination begins, as stated in the identification statement on this form.
>
> **Completion certificate.** Delivered within 15 days of successful completion. A duplicate certificate is available on request for as long as the school holds the record.

## The one field that must not be guessed

**REFUND/CANCELLATION POLICY** is left for entry at signing rather than filled here, because no policy has been decided. It must match, exactly, the policy version the enrollment flow presents to the student and records against the enrollment. Writing a policy here that the platform does not implement would put a DRE-filed document at odds with what a student actually agrees to, and that is the kind of discrepancy that ends an approval.

**This is an outstanding item. RE 313 cannot be printed without it.**

## Delivery to the student

RE 313's own header states the requirement: **"To be provided to the participant prior to registration/enrollment."** The mechanism is described in the RE 306 item 21 answer sheet, and it is enforced by the platform rather than by a policy: no enrollment can be accepted without a recorded acknowledgment of this page.
