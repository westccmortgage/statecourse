- **Assignment 1: California mortgage brokerage and regulatory structure** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with DRE real estate broker mortgage authority, MLO endorsement orientation, DRE and other regulator boundaries, licensed and exempt activity, broker supervision, consumer complaint and enforcement context.
- **Assignment 2: Money, interest, notes, and loan mathematics** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with principal interest and term, simple interest and amortization, promissory note terms, points fees and annual percentage rate, loan-to-value and combined loan-to-value, payment and yield calculations.

- **Assignment 6: Underwriting, collateral, and approval conditions** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with ability-to-repay orientation, automated and manual underwriting, appraisal and collateral review, title insurance and hazard insurance, conditions and verification, approval denial and adverse action workflow.
- **Assignment 7: Federal consumer mortgage law** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with Truth in Lending and Regulation Z, RESPA and Regulation X, ECOA and Regulation B, HMDA and Regulation C, FCRA and credit reporting, SAFE Act and federal registration context.
- **Assignment 8: California broker law, trust funds, and records** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with California broker mortgage activities, advance fees and compensation, trust fund handling, broker records and retention, advertising and license identification, DRE audit and enforcement risk.
- **Assignment 9: Mortgage disclosures, compensation, and closing costs** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with Loan Estimate and Closing Disclosure orientation, broker compensation disclosure, affiliated business and referral risk, fee tolerance and change circumstances, prepayment balloon and adjustable-rate disclosure, consumer comparison and acknowledgment.
- **Assignment 10: Origination, processing, and closing workflow** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with inquiry and prequalification, application and disclosures, document collection and verification, submission and condition management, clear-to-close and settlement, post-closing quality control.
- **Assignment 11: Appraisal, title, escrow, and settlement** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with appraisal independence, valuation review, title exceptions and liens, escrow instructions and funds, settlement statements, recording funding and document custody.
- **Assignment 12: Private money, seller financing, and nontraditional transactions** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with private investor loans, business-purpose lending, seller carryback financing, hard-money risk and suitability, broker-arranged loan documentation, usury exemptions and legal referral.
- **Assignment 13: Servicing, payoff, default, and foreclosure** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with payment processing and servicing records, escrow accounts, payoff demand and reconveyance, delinquency and loss mitigation, nonjudicial foreclosure orientation, borrower communication and complaint handling.
- **Assignment 14: Fair lending, fraud prevention, and ethical mortgage practice** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with ECOA and fair housing lending protections, redlining and pricing disparities, occupancy income and appraisal fraud, identity and cyber fraud, suspicious facts and escalation, ethical product presentation and documentation.
- **Assignment 15: Integrated California mortgage brokerage file** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with consumer inquiry and scope, application and identity, product comparison and disclosures, underwriting and conditions, closing and compensation, post-closing audit and regulator-ready file.
