# RE 336 Course Matrix - Real Estate Practice

Instructor candidate: Anatoliy Kanevsky, California broker 01385024.

- **Assignment 1: California practice standards and the regulated professional** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with DRE authority and license categories, broker supervision, professional competence and scope, office policies and delegation, consumer-first communication, transaction file accountability.
- **Assignment 2: Building a lawful real estate practice** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with business planning, prospecting and solicitation, advertising identification, antitrust and competition, privacy and information security, records and retention workflow.
- **Assignment 3: Agency disclosure and fiduciary performance** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with seller and buyer agency, dual agency consent, California disclosure sequence, fiduciary duties in practice, compensation and conflicts, agency documentation case.
- **Assignment 4: Seller counseling and listing agreements** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with property and client intake, listing agreement structure, pricing discussion, seller disclosures, showing and access controls, listing cancellation expiration and protection.
- **Assignment 5: Market analysis and pricing strategy** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with market selection, comparable adjustment reasoning, active pending and sold evidence, seller net and pricing consequences, appraisal versus CMA, pricing presentation and file support.
- **Assignment 6: Marketing, advertising, and fair access** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with truthful property advertising, digital and social media compliance, fair housing advertising, accessibility and accommodations, open house and showing practices, marketing approval and archive.
- **Assignment 7: Buyer representation and property selection** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with buyer consultation, representation agreement, financial readiness, property search and steering avoidance, investigation and inspections, offer strategy and documentation.
- **Assignment 8: Purchase agreements, counteroffers, and contingencies** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with California purchase agreement architecture, offer presentation, counteroffers and multiple offers, financing appraisal and inspection contingencies, deadlines notices and removal, amendment cancellation and dispute prevention.
- **Assignment 9: Disclosures and property-condition risk** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with transfer disclosure framework, agent visual inspection duties, natural hazard and environmental disclosure, lead-based paint, material facts and stigma limits, disclosure delivery and rescission timing.
- **Assignment 10: Financing the transaction** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with preapproval and loan terms, conventional government and alternative financing, financing disclosures, appraisal and underwriting, closing cost and seller credit, financing failure and contingency response.
- **Assignment 11: Escrow, title, closing, and possession** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with opening escrow, title and vesting review, transaction coordination, final verification and closing, recording disbursement and possession, post-closing file audit.
- **Assignment 12: Property management and leasing practice** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with management agreement, tenant selection and fair housing, rents deposits and accounting, maintenance habitability and access, lease enforcement and termination, owner reporting and risk controls.
- **Assignment 13: Ethics, risk management, and dispute prevention** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with honesty and fair dealing, errors omissions and supervision, fraud warning signs, conflict and referral disclosure, complaint response, documentation escalation and legal referral.
- **Assignment 14: Implicit bias and fair housing participatory practicum** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with implicit explicit and systemic bias, historical and social impacts, recognition and interruption strategies, consumer role-play experience, real estate professional role-play response, fair housing action plan and reflection.
- **Assignment 15: Integrated California residential transaction** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with intake and agency, listing and marketing, offer and negotiation, disclosure and investigation, escrow finance and closing, complete file compliance audit.

This mapping supports review; it is not DRE approval and does not replace documentary substantiation.
