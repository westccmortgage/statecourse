# RE 336 Course Matrix - Real Estate Principles

Instructor candidate: Anatoliy Kanevsky, California broker 01385024.

- **Assignment 1: The real estate profession and California regulatory structure** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with real property as an economic and legal asset, DRE jurisdiction and consumer protection, broker and salesperson license relationships, professional scope and unauthorized legal practice, sources of California real estate law, transaction participants and accountability.
- **Assignment 2: Real property, personal property, and fixtures** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with real versus personal property, fixtures and the fixture tests, trade fixtures, natural and artificial attachments, water and mineral interests, fixture disputes in a sale.
- **Assignment 3: Estates, ownership, and common interests** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with freehold and leasehold estates, fee simple and life estates, joint tenancy and tenancy in common, community property orientation, condominiums and common interest developments, trust and entity ownership basics.
- **Assignment 4: Land description, measurement, and boundaries** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with metes and bounds, government survey system, lot and block descriptions, recorded maps and assessor parcels, survey and boundary evidence, area and linear measurement.
- **Assignment 5: Encumbrances, liens, easements, and restrictions** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with voluntary and involuntary liens, easements and licenses, deed restrictions and covenants, encroachments and boundary conflicts, priority and recording, title exceptions and risk allocation.
- **Assignment 6: Government powers, land use, and environmental controls** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with taxation eminent domain police power and escheat, zoning and general plans, subdivision and building controls, environmental hazards and disclosure, water conservation and natural-hazard context, public and private land-use due diligence.
- **Assignment 7: Agency and brokerage relationships** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with creation of agency, fiduciary duties, seller buyer and dual agency, California agency disclosure, broker supervision and salesperson authority, compensation conflicts and termination.
- **Assignment 8: Contracts and transaction formation** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with contract elements, offer acceptance and counteroffer, consideration capacity and legality, Statute of Frauds, contingencies and performance, breach rescission and remedies.
- **Assignment 9: Transfer, deeds, title, and recording** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with deed requirements, grant deed and quitclaim deed, delivery and acceptance, recording and constructive notice, title insurance and preliminary reports, voluntary and involuntary transfer.
- **Assignment 10: Escrow, settlement, and transaction chronology** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with escrow holder role, escrow instructions, deposit and document handling, proration and closing statements, recording and disbursement, post-closing records.
- **Assignment 11: Real estate finance foundations** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with promissory notes, deeds of trust and mortgages, loan markets and sources, interest amortization and points, loan-to-value and qualifying ratios, default foreclosure and deficiency orientation.
- **Assignment 12: Valuation and appraisal foundations** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with value price and cost, highest and best use, sales comparison approach, cost approach, income capitalization approach, appraisal independence and CMA limits.
- **Assignment 13: Taxation, investment, and real estate mathematics** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with property taxation and assessments, transfer and income tax orientation, cash flow and return, leverage and risk, commissions prorations and area math, investment comparison and documentation.
- **Assignment 14: Fair housing, civil rights, and consumer protection** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with federal protected classes, California fair housing protections, prohibited conduct and advertising, reasonable accommodations and modifications, fair lending and appraisal bias, complaints documentation and professional response.
- **Assignment 15: Leasing, property management, license law, and integrated transaction** - candidate résumé/evidence must demonstrate direct education or experience with lease formation and tenant interests, property management agreements, trust funds and records, California licensing and discipline, material fact disclosure, integrated California transaction audit.

This mapping supports review; it is not DRE approval and does not replace documentary substantiation.
