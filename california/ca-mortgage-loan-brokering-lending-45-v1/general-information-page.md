## Final examination criteria

- 100 multiple-choice questions; closed book; 180 minutes.
- Passing score: 60%.
- Two total attempts maximum. Form B contains different questions and is used only after a failed Form A.
- A second failure earns no course credit. Re-enrollment and complete repetition of the course are required before another final.
- The final is never printable or downloadable, times out at 180 minutes, and is delivered only through the proctor-controlled session.

## California correspondence timing

- A single course may not be completed in less than 2.5 weeks (420 elapsed hours) from the date the student receives course access to the date the final is successfully passed.
- No more than two courses may be completed in any five-week period.
- The course must be completed within one year after registration; otherwise the student must re-enroll and repeat the requirements.
- Sequential segment controls require completion of each lesson assignment before the next lesson opens.

## Identity

Immediately before the final, the participant presents government-issued photo identification or current identification from a recognized real-estate-related trade organization issued within the preceding five years bearing photograph, signature, and identification number. The proctor records only the minimum verification metadata; a raw identity document is not placed in general student content storage.
