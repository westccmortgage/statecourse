# Student Instructions - Mortgage Loan Brokering and Lending

1. Review the General Information Page before registration. Do not enroll while the public status is In Development.
2. After approval and enrollment, access starts when the LMS records delivery of the course materials. That timestamp starts the 420-hour minimum elapsed period.
3. Complete all 15 lesson assignments in sequence. Each assignment includes reading, applied work, a five-item assessment, and targeted remediation when required.
4. Instructor support remains available even though the course is self-guided. Use the help control for a course question; legal or transaction-specific advice is outside the course.
5. The final unlocks only after all 15 assignments, remediation, identity/proctor scheduling, the 420-hour minimum, the cross-course five-week rule, and all compliance exceptions are satisfied.
6. Present acceptable photo identification to the proctor. Do not receive, print, download, copy, photograph, or retain final-exam content.
7. Form A contains 100 multiple-choice questions, is closed book, has a 180-minute limit, and requires 60%. If failed, one different Form B may be taken under a new proctored session.
8. A second failure earns no credit. Re-enrollment and completion of the entire course are required before another examination.
9. A certificate is issued within 15 days only after approval is valid and every completion condition is verified.
