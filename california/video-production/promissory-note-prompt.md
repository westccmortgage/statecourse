```

Delivery: calm, clear, authoritative, conversational, not theatrical, not rushed.
Use the avatar mainly for introduction and the closing judgment point.
For document explanations, prioritize the actual document on screen rather than keeping the

SOURCE DOCUMENT
I will attach a two-page specimen fixed-rate promissory note (fictional transaction, used for
instruction only).

Use ONLY the attached document for document visuals.
Do not invent fields, numbers, names, or provisions not printed on the attached pages.
Do not alter the wording printed on the document.
Treat the uploaded pages as a locked source layer. Do not use generative AI to reconstruct,
enhance, repair, replace, or rewrite any portion of the document. The only permitted motion is
a hard cut between Page 1 and Page 2 — no zoom, no pan, no crop, no camera movement of any kind
on either page.

LEARNING OBJECTIVE

By the end of this video, the student should be able to locate and explain five things on this
promissory note:

1. The principal and the parties (Section 1 — Borrower's Promise to Pay)
2. The interest rate (Section 2 — Interest)
3. The payment amount, due date, and maturity date (Section 3 — Payments)
4. The late charge (Section 5 — Late Charge for Overdue Payments)
5. The link to the security instrument (Section 9 — Secured Note, page 2)

The purpose is NOT to teach every clause. The purpose is to teach where these five things live
on the page, what each one means at a basic level, and the relationship this note has to its
companion document, the deed of trust: the note is the promise to repay and creates the debt;
the deed of trust pledges the property as security for that debt.

SCENE STRUCTURE

Scene 1 — Avatar introduction
Visual: Professional avatar, medium shot, clean StateCourse branded background.
On-screen title: "The Promissory Note"
Script: "Every mortgage transaction rests on two documents, and they do different jobs. The
promissory note is the promise to repay — it creates the debt. The security instrument, the
deed of trust, pledges the property as security for that debt. On this note, let's locate the
terms that make it enforceable."
Duration: 13-15 seconds.

Scene 2 — Page 1, full-page static hold begins
Visual: Show the complete, full first page of the attached note. Camera comes to a full stop
and does not move again until Scene 6 ends. Do not show the avatar.
Script: "This is the promissory note behind our running example. It's organized in numbered
sections. Section 1 states the promise to pay. Section 2 sets the interest rate. Section 3 sets
the payment amount and schedule. Further down, Section 5 covers the late charge — and on page
two, Section 9 names the deed of trust as this note's security instrument."
Duration: 16-18 seconds.

Scene 3 — Section 1 (held on the same static Page 1 frame, no highlight drawn)
Script: "Locate Section 1, the Borrower's Promise to Pay. The borrower promises to pay the
lender, ResiCentral, LLC, a principal of $1,189,150 — the amount actually borrowed, plus
interest."
Duration: 11-13 seconds.

Scene 4 — Section 2 (same static frame)
Script: "Section 2 sets the interest rate: 7.5 percent per year. On a fixed-rate note like this
one, that rate applies both before and after any default."
Duration: 10-12 seconds.

Scene 5 — Section 3 (same static frame)
Script: "Section 3 sets the payment terms: a monthly payment of $8,314.71, due the first of
each month, beginning September 1, 2026. If any balance remains on the Maturity Date, August 1,
2056, it's due in full that day."
Duration: 13-15 seconds.

Scene 6 — Section 5 (same static frame — Page 1 hold ends after this scene)
Script: "Section 5 is the late charge. Miss the fifteen-day grace period, and the borrower owes
5 percent of the overdue principal and interest payment — charged once per late payment, not
compounding."
Duration: 12-14 seconds.

Scene 7 — Page 2, full-page static hold
Visual: Hard cut to the complete, full second page of the attached note. Camera comes to a full
stop and does not move again for the rest of this scene.
Script: "Turn to Section 9, Secured Note, on page two. It names the deed of trust, dated the
same day as this note, as the security instrument that protects the lender if these promises
aren't kept. This is the link back to where we started: the note is the debt, the deed of trust
is the remedy."
Duration: 14-16 seconds.

Scene 8 — Avatar judgment point and close
Visual: Return to avatar.
Script: "Every note earns its terms by section, not by feel: principal, rate, payment, and
maturity in Sections 1 through 3, the late charge in Section 5, and the link to the security
instrument in Section 9. Read it that way, and the second document — the deed of trust — will
already make sense before you turn to it."
Duration: 14-16 seconds.

END OF VIDEO.
Do NOT add a recap scene after the avatar's closing judgment.
Do NOT add an end card, title card, or "Next:" slide.
The video ends on the avatar's final sentence.

VISUAL RULES

- Use the uploaded document pages as the central visual, exactly as printed.
- No highlight box, outline, or margin label anywhere in this video — no highlighting of any

- Keep all overlay panels, titles, and lists fully clear of the StateCourse logo — no text may

- Avoid decorative imagery unrelated to the transaction. No images of houses, keys, smiling
  families, handshakes, or money unless instructional context genuinely requires them.

ACCESSIBILITY

Deliver captions as a separate synchronized caption file (SRT); do not burn captions into the
video image.
Create a clean transcript.
Maintain high text/background contrast.

IMPORTANT EDUCATIONAL RULE

This video is instruction, not the mastery check. Do NOT put a quiz question or answer at the
end of this video. The student reaches retrieval and application on a separate screen.

QUALITY CONTROL BEFORE RENDERING

Verify internally before rendering: from the start of Scene 2 to the end of Scene 6, the frame
composition never changes — full Page 1, fully visible, undistorted, on every frame, no zoom, no
crop, no color shift. The only change during the whole document portion of the video is the

held fully visible and undistorted, without motion, through the end of Scene 7. If the camera
```
