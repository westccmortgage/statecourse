# Pennsylvania licensure and the regulatory framework

## Standard salesperson path

For an ordinary new salesperson applicant, Pennsylvania’s Real Estate Licensing and Registration Act (§ 521, as amended in 2018) and the State Real Estate Commission’s current standard-method instructions call for 75 qualifying education hours. The Commission identifies two subjects: Real Estate Fundamentals and Real Estate Practice. StateCourse’s practice questions and explanations award no hours or credit.

The current Commission guide says to complete the qualifying coursework within five years before successfully completing the licensing examination. Keep the official transcript and verify that the provider and courses qualify. Some academic backgrounds or out-of-state education can qualify under different rules. The Commission guide and the examination vendor’s current instructions explain which application route and examinations apply.

Older wording in 49 Pa. Code § 35.272 still refers to 60 hours and ten years. For the ordinary new applicant, use the later statute and the Commission’s current instructions.

## Records, affiliation and consumer protection

A salesperson practices through an affiliated licensed broker. Keep the license and affiliation current, distinguish regulatory notices from brokerage agreements, and direct matters outside a licensee’s expertise to the appropriate professional. The Real Estate Recovery Fund has statutory requirements. An ordinary transaction dispute alone does not establish eligibility.

The Commission regulates transcripts and delivery standards for qualifying courses. A transcript from a qualifying provider documents the course taken and its completion date. A practice score in this public tool is a private study result on this device.
