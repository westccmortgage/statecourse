## Personal broker license

- [GREC reciprocity guide](https://grec.state.ga.us/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Reciprocity-for-Real-Estate-Licensees.pdf) — comparable-license threshold, Florida-resident exception, published $170 fee.
- [GREC Real Estate Reciprocal Application, rev. 02/2025](https://grec.state.ga.us/wp-content/uploads/pdfs/RealEstate/GRECReciprocalApp.pdf) — one-year certified history, 60-day law-enforcement report, active/inactive and firm/affiliation choices.
- [2026 GREC rules compilation](https://rules.sos.ga.gov/download_pdf.aspx?dept=Departments&pdf=Department+520+GEORGIA+REAL+ESTATE+COMMISSION&st=GASOS&year=2026) — controlling Chapters 520-1 and 520-2, including each-jurisdiction reciprocity certification, criminal-history escalation, fees, school, instructor, and course rules.
- [2026 PSI Candidate Information Bulletin](https://test-takers.psiexams.com/api/content/bulletin/11484) — current examination-vendor bulletin, effective 07/01/2026; its $175 transaction amount conflicts with the older reciprocal form's $121 Florida-resident instruction.
- [Application to Open a Real Estate Firm](https://grec.state.ga.us/wp-content/uploads/pdfs/RealEstate/GRECOpeningAFirm.pdf) and [Change of Qualifying Broker](https://grec.state.ga.us/wp-content/uploads/pdfs/RealEstate/GRECChangeofQB.pdf) — separate active firm/QB paths; the older Open Firm form's $275 conflicts with current-rule arithmetic.
- [Georgia Secretary of State foreign-entity guide](https://sos.ga.gov/how-to-guide/how-to-guide-register-foreign-entity) — foreign-entity authority and published $235 filing total.

## School, instructor, and course

- [GREC Chapter 520-2](https://rules.sos.ga.gov/gac/520-2) — controlling standards for schools, instructors, courses, distance delivery, examinations, reporting, advertising, and records.
- [Instructor application, rev. 03/2024](https://grec.state.ga.us/wp-content/uploads/pdfs/schools/RE_Instructor2024.pdf) — filing evidence; page 7 links, but does not contain, the separate worksheet.
- [Instructor points workbook, rev. 06/2024](https://grec.state.ga.us/wp-content/uploads/pdfs/schools/Points%20Worksheet%20v%206-7-2024.xlsx) — current 65-point guide, formulas, and category maxima.
- [GREC Education Guide, rev. 07/17/2026](https://grec.state.ga.us/wp-content/uploads/pdfs/schools/2026%20GREC%20Education%20Guide%20v%207-17-2026.pdf) — current operational guidance; rules prevail if wording conflicts.
- [Application to Open a Real Estate School](https://grec.state.ga.us/wp-content/uploads/pdfs/RE_Open_School.pdf) — located official form, but revision 10/2015; confirm currency and fee.
- [Real Estate Course Code Request, rev. 07/2026](https://grec.state.ga.us/wp-content/uploads/pdfs/schools/RE_Course_Code_Appl.pdf) — course request and no-delivery/no-advertising constraint pending listing.
- [Instructor renewal, rev. 02/2021](https://grec.state.ga.us/wp-content/uploads/pdfs/schools/RE_Instructor_Renewal.pdf) and [school renewal, rev. 02/2021](https://grec.state.ga.us/wp-content/uploads/pdfs/schools/RE_School_Renewal.pdf) — operational forms retained to expose, not hide, conflicts with current rule text.
