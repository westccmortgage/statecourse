# Georgia compliance baseline

## Authority and status vocabulary

- **Controlling:** current statute or rule.
- **Official operational evidence:** current regulator form, bulletin, or published instruction.
- **Internal policy:** a StateCourse control that may exceed the minimum and must never be labeled as Georgia law.
- **Pending written confirmation:** a conflict, silence, or implementation choice that cannot be resolved safely from published text.

`GO` means only that research supports the next controlled step. It never means licensed or approved. `NO-GO` closes the named activity until its evidence exists.

## Non-negotiable gates

1. No Georgia brokerage claim until GREC issues the personal license.
2. No instructor claim or teaching until GREC approves the instructor.
3. No school claim until the school is listed/approved by GREC and the GNPEC status is documented.
4. No course approval claim, Georgia advertising, Georgia enrollment, payment, certificate, or credit until the course and all operational methods are accepted as required.
5. No curriculum authoring before written approval/direction for the self-paced methodology.
6. No stale fee is paid without a live check.
7. No owner evidence, signature, payment, or legal attestation is fabricated or inferred.
8. No reciprocal broker filing proceeds without certification from every real-estate licensing jurisdiction covering examination, prelicense/CE compliance, good standing, and discipline.
9. A residence-state criminal-history report that identifies another-jurisdiction record—or cannot be obtained—triggers the published FBI escalation path.

## School controls

The filing must address ownership, director/coordinator responsibility, the director's no-more-than-60-day law-enforcement criminal-history report and lawful-presence/identity evidence, student standards, Notice to Students, ADA/nondiscrimination, media/equipment, the online model's treatment of the learning environment, instructor evaluation, electronic vendor/reporting capability, timed outline, bibliography, hourly measurable objectives, exercises/assessments, and a five-year record system. The operating plan must also include a qualifying continuing-education offering every calendar year and required change reporting.

## Distance-course controls

The design must map all 15 fixed salesperson subjects, ethics, required outside reading, extensive graded written work/certification, and applied proficiency assessments before it proves personal participation, module completion, time, mastery, remediation, identity oversight, instructor access, final-exam security, proctoring, two forms, pilot validity, and recoverable five-year records. The pilot categories must name licensed salespersons/CAMs and document varied computer and real-estate skill levels. A timer attached to reading is not enough.

Only multiple-choice format, at least four choices per question, and at least 100 questions form the salesperson-final safe harbor from examination submission; this format is not mandatory. Failure of any of those three elements requires submission and prior approval. Content buckets, two secure forms, proctoring, scheduling, passing score, security, and retake controls are independent requirements and remain mapped either way.

## Evidence rule

Every claimed regulator-controlled fact must point to a unique material-claim ID in the source lock or a dated written regulator response. Exact files are checked by hash, bytes, MIME type, and signature; dynamic pages require source-specific semantic anchors. A source being reachable is not proof that the previous reading remains correct. Offline, blocked, skipped, stale, soft-404, or drifted results keep application, development, approval, commerce, and enrollment gates closed.
