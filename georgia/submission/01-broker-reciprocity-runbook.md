**Published fee:** $170.

**Regulator decision required:** yes. No license is promised.

**Owner facts confirmed 2026-09-02:** California legal resident since 1994; no real-estate license has ever been issued by a jurisdiction other than California. These are owner attestations, not regulator findings, and must be reconfirmed on the signed application.

## Before ordering expiring documents

1. Reconfirm California legal residence on the application date. The owner confirmed California legal residence since 1994 on 2026-09-02.
2. If residence changes to Florida before filing, stop this runbook and use the Georgia Law and Practice supplement-exam path after confirming the current PSI fee.
3. Reconcile the final application against the owner's 2026-09-02 confirmation that California is the only jurisdiction that has ever/currently licensed the owner in real estate. On that confirmed record, Rule 520-1-.04(3)(g) calls for one certification, from California; any later-discovered jurisdiction must be added.
4. Confirm the California broker license is current, in good standing, not lapsed/expired, and originally obtained through a state-administered real-estate examination.
5. Ask GREC only about operational delivery/format if a jurisdiction cannot produce all required elements or offers certified electronic data.

## Assemble in this order

1. Order an original California certification issued within the prior 12 months. It must show the qualifying examination, satisfaction of California's prelicense and continuing-education requirements, current good standing, and no disciplinary action. If California transmits certified data electronically, retain GREC's acceptance instruction. Add another certification only if the final owner reconciliation identifies another real-estate licensing jurisdiction.
2. Obtain the current GREC reciprocal form directly from GREC and confirm it still says revision 02/2025 and $170.
3. Complete the form with **Broker** and **Inactive** selected. Leave firm/qualifying-broker/affiliation paths out of the first filing.
4. Complete the lawful-presence form and obtain notarization plus an accepted secure/verifiable identity document.
5. When every other item is ready, order the criminal history from a law-enforcement agency in the legal residence state. It must be no more than 60 days old when submitted. A commercial background-check vendor is not a substitute. If that report shows a record in another jurisdiction, or the required equivalent report cannot be obtained, stop and complete the fingerprints, fees, authorization, and other steps GREC requires to obtain an FBI report.
6. Answer every criminal and professional-discipline disclosure yourself. If any answer is yes, attach every required certified disposition/order and the detailed personal statement GREC requests.
7. Make the required cashier's check or money order payable exactly as the live form instructs.
8. Sign/date and mail the complete package using the current form address; keep a complete private copy and tracking receipt.

## After submission

- Record delivery and regulator correspondence privately; store only a redacted status record in Git.
- Do not conduct or advertise Georgia brokerage until the wall certificate/license is issued and the selected status permits the activity.
- If GREC issues inactive status, decide separately whether to affiliate as an associate broker, become a qualifying broker, or open/register a firm.
- Affiliation requires the active Georgia broker to complete and sign Section C and certify that a written affiliation agreement exists. A qualifying-broker change uses the separate Change of Qualifying Broker filing; the qualifying broker must be the LLC's member/manager, corporation's officer, partnership's partner, or sole proprietor, as applicable, and must be authorized to bind the firm in a contested case.
- Opening a foreign-LLC firm requires Georgia authority to transact business, a Georgia registered agent/office, the GREC Open Firm application, qualifying-broker evidence, and any required trade-name material. The Secretary of State currently publishes $235; GREC's older Open Firm form says $275 while current-rule arithmetic is $200. Confirm before payment.

## Stop conditions

Stop and obtain direction if legal residence is Florida, any jurisdiction's certification omits examination/prelicense/continuing-education/good-standing/discipline elements, status is not current/good standing, a residence-state report requires FBI escalation, the current form/fee differs, or a disclosure is positive.
