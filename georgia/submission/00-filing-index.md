# Georgia filing index

<!-- material-claims: ga-broker-activation-paths ga-change-qb-form -->

Nothing in this directory is a filed application. Owner signatures, attestations, payments, background data, and regulator responses must remain outside Git unless deliberately redacted.

## Track A — owner broker license

1. `01-broker-reciprocity-runbook.md`
2. `06-owner-action-checklist.md`
3. GREC Real Estate Reciprocal Application, completed outside the repository
4. original certification from every real-estate licensing jurisdiction, each no more than 12 months old and covering examination, prelicense/CE compliance, good standing, and discipline
5. state-of-residence law-enforcement criminal history, no more than 60 days old; FBI escalation material if the residence-state report shows another-jurisdiction record or cannot be obtained
6. notarized lawful-presence verification and secure/verifiable document
7. required disclosure records, owner signature, and $170 payment

Selected request: **Georgia Broker — inactive**. No firm filing or affiliate signature is included in the first package. Later activation is a separate project: affiliation needs an active Georgia broker to complete/sign Section C and certify the written affiliation agreement; a QB change uses its own form and requires the qualifying broker's entity-specific role plus authority to bind the firm in a contested case; a foreign-LLC firm needs Georgia authority to transact business, a registered agent/office, and a separately confirmed GREC Open Firm package/fee.

## Track B — instructor

1. `02-instructor-approval-map.md`

6. current form/fee, owner signature, and submission

## Track C — institution

1. `04-school-approval-map.md`
2. GNPEC determination from `regulator-inquiries/GNPEC-exemption-inquiry.md`
3. GREC answers from `regulator-inquiries/GREC-prefiling-inquiry.md`
4. current school form/fee and complete school package
5. approved instructor roster
6. director criminal-history/lawful-presence/identity evidence and explicit media/equipment and online learning-environment treatment
7. operating plan for the mandatory annual continuing-education offering and required change reporting

## Track D — distance course

1. `05-distance-course-approval-map.md`
2. written GREC direction/approval for methodology before development
3. curriculum, assessments, two final forms, pilot, records/reporting demonstrations
4. current course-code request and approval/listing evidence

## Release rule

All four tracks must be read independently. A personal broker license does not approve an instructor, a school approval does not approve a course, a course code does not establish GNPEC status, and a submitted form does not authorize commerce.
