## Two-regulator entry gate

Before offering education to Georgia residents, obtain:

1. GREC school/instructor/course approvals as applicable; and
2. a written GNPEC determination establishing the applicable exemption or authorization path.

Do not assume the other-state-agency exemption. GNPEC makes the determination.

## GREC school package

- current application and verified fee;
- entity identity, ownership, and addresses;
- designated school director and coordinator with responsibility for completion certification;
- school director's law-enforcement criminal-history report issued no more than 60 days before filing, plus certified dispositions/statements for any required disclosure;
- school director's notarized Lawful Presence Verification and secure/verifiable identity document;
- proposed instructor list and approval evidence;
- five-year records plan covering attendance/module completion, all scores, exams/keys, materials, instructor records, software version, completion dates, and final scores;
- electronic exam-vendor registration and regulator completion-reporting capability;
- entrance, attendance, makeup, grading, conduct, ADA, and nondiscrimination policies;
- statement that required course materials and Commission-required audio/video equipment will be available, with applicability to the online-only model stated rather than silently omitted;
- appropriate learning-environment statement, or an explicit documented treatment of the in-class requirement if no in-class instruction is planned;
- instructor evaluation process;
- Notice to Students;
- course list/schedule;
- detailed timed outline, bibliography, planned instructional/homework exercises, student evaluations, and measurable objective for every instructional hour.

## Initial and renewal fee controls

- The 10/2015 Open School form says **$175**.
- Current Rule 520-1-.04 arithmetic is **$200**: $75 original-approval activation plus $125 renewal through December 31 of the fourth calendar year after approval.
- The 02/2021 School Renewal form says **$125 paper / $100 online**. The rule expressly lists a $125 school renewal but its online-$100 clause names firms, individual licensees, and instructors—not schools—even though the form advertises the $25 online discount.
- Treat all three figures as a documented conflict. Obtain the live form, filing channel, approval term, and exact amount from GREC before payment.

## Timing controls

- GREC's published action window is 60 days after a **complete** application.
- A request for additional/revised data must be answered within 120 days or the application may be deemed abandoned.
- Initial approval runs to December 31 of the fourth calendar year following approval under the current rule; renew before expiration on the authorized form.
- Every approved school must offer at least one qualifying continuing-education course every calendar year, in addition to its prelicense and postlicense offerings.
- Required school, director/coordinator, contact, and rule-based instructor-termination changes must be reported promptly, and required records retained for at least five years.

## Commerce control

The public site may describe Georgia only as an unapproved regulatory-development record. It may not advertise an approved school/course, accept a Georgia enrollment/payment, issue Georgia credit, or imply that Phase 0 is regulator approval.
