# Georgia market evidence and limits

<!-- material-claims: ga-market-license-records ga-market-population -->

## Decision

Georgia is a **Phase 0 GO**. It is large enough to justify the regulatory work and offers the owner a comparatively direct broker-reciprocity filing. This is not yet a revenue forecast.

| Measure | Official value | What it supports | What it does not prove |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 2025 population estimate | 11,302,748 | broad addressable population | annual course buyers |
| Total license records, FY2025 | 118,387 | regulator/market scale | unique people or students |
| New licenses, FY2025 | 3,452 | new-entry activity | unique 75-hour students |

The 3,452 total includes resident and nonresident records and multiple license classes. FY2025 reports a decline from 7,393 in FY2023 and 5,191 in FY2024, so a forecast must not extrapolate one headline number.

## Evidence needed for a real business case

- unique Georgia salesperson applications and first-time exam candidates;
- exam attempts and pass rates by first-time/repeat status;
- online 75-hour enrollments and completions;
- approved-school count, product format, price, refund, and review evidence;
- realistic organic/paid acquisition cost;
- regulator-processing lag, payment conversion, refund/chargeback, and support cost.

Until those exist, use scenarios rather than a single forecast. The current evidence is sufficient to fund Phase 0 and prefiling—not to promise sales.

Sources: GREC FY2025 Annual Report and the U.S. Census Vintage 2025 state population CSV, both locked in `georgia/regulatory/source-lock.json`.
