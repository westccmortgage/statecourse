## School/course preparation

- [ ] Obtain the GNPEC written determination.
- [ ] Select director/coordinator roles and evidence.
- [ ] Obtain the school director's no-more-than-60-day law-enforcement criminal-history report, disclosure records if applicable, notarized lawful-presence verification, and secure/verifiable identity document.
- [ ] Approve school policies and five-year record design.
- [ ] Document media/equipment availability and how the learning-environment requirement applies to an online-only school.
- [ ] Add an operating commitment to offer a qualifying continuing-education course every calendar year and report required changes.
- [ ] Obtain GREC methodology direction before curriculum development.
- [ ] Keep Georgia advertising, enrollment, payment, certificate, and credit functions disabled until independently verified approvals exist.

## Never delegated to the repository

Personal attestations, disclosure answers, notarization, identity documents, background records, signatures, certified payments, course attendance, and regulator approval decisions require the owner or regulator. Drafting may be automated; truth and execution may not.
