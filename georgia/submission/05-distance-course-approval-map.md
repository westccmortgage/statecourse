# Georgia 75-hour distance-course approval map

## Pre-development gate

Obtain GREC's written acceptance/direction for the computer-based methodology **before** authoring the course. Phase 0 therefore contains no curriculum or examination items.

## Fixed salesperson-course content controls

The eventual 75-hour curriculum must cover these 15 fixed Rule 520-2-.04(4)(c) areas:

1. real-estate contracts, including extensive form practice involving FHA, VA, conventional loans, assumptions, brokerage engagements, and leases;
2. real-estate instruments and conveyances;
3. RESPA closing procedures and the salesperson's closing responsibilities;
4. agency law and agency disclosure;
5. pricing real property;
6. real-estate financing and extensive cost/payment-estimation practice;
7. Georgia Residential Mortgage Fraud law, detection, and reporting;
8. community-association-management and property-management activities;
9. environmental laws;
10. taxation;
11. city and urban development;
12. fair housing;
13. antitrust laws;
14. safety precautions; and
15. Georgia real-estate license law.

Any additional subject requires prior written authorization. Ethical implications must be acknowledged and covered throughout. Every unit needs appropriate out-of-class reading and extensive graded written exercises bearing the required personal-completion certification. Students must demonstrate proficiency through authorized assessments in completing contracts, pricing forms, and financing/cost forms. Instruction also needs applied practice such as writing/presenting offers, calculating costs, pricing property, and fair-housing compliance.

## Method package to demonstrate

- 75 instructional hours, with each distance-education credit hour defined as 60 minutes;
- assessment/test/final time no more than 10% of required time;
- one or more measurable objectives per instructional unit and an objective for every instructional hour in the school filing;
- structured, genuinely interactive instruction—not primarily reading screens, watching video/audio, or drilling state-exam questions;
- regular diagnostics, mastery threshold, individualized remediation, and locked progression;
- time validation that measures engaged instruction rather than an open browser tab;
- student certification of personal completion for modules/assignments;
- approved instructor availability, oversight, support response standard, and completion certification;
- identity and electronic-signature controls that GREC authorizes;
- recoverable five-year records including exact software version and every module/date/score;
- electronic vendor registration and completion reporting.

## Pilot

Plan a documented nine-person pilot with at least:

- three unlicensed participants;
- three licensed salespersons or community association managers; and
- six non-educators.

The categories can overlap only as the rule permits. The sample must document varied levels of computer skill/knowledge and real-estate skill/knowledge. Retain participant classification, consent, skills baseline, timing, completion, defects, remediation, and development/testing records. Obtain written confirmation of the proposed pilot protocol before running it.

## Final examination

Selected Phase 0 **examination-submission safe harbor**, which avoids submitting the final examination itself only if all three conditions are met:

- at least 100 multiple-choice questions;
- at least four choices per question.

The following are separate examination mandates, not safe-harbor conditions:

- at least five questions in each of the following 13 fixed subject buckets: brokerage engagements; legal descriptions and legal aspects of contracts; methods of payment and earnest money; special stipulations and writing sales contracts; leases and fair housing; licensees acting as principals; antitrust; basic finance; loan types; pricing property; seller costs; qualifying purchasers and purchaser costs; and contract closing;
- at least five questions on any additional matters GREC later requires or authorizes;
- two secure forms;
- proctored administration;
- if the school elects to offer a retake, it occurs on another day; failure of a second final requires repeating the full course.

The 100-question/multiple-choice/four-choice design is not a mandatory Georgia exam format. A school must submit the final to GREC for approval before administration if **any of those three safe-harbor conditions** is not met. Submission does not waive the independent content, security, scheduling, retake, passing-score, or proctoring rules.

Do not set the numeric passing score, remote-proctor vendor/process, asynchronous exam-day implementation, identity evidence, or electronic signature method until GREC answers in writing.

## Submission gate

After method clearance: author, internally validate, run the approved pilot, fix findings, assemble reviewer access and records demonstrations, and submit the current course-code request. No advertising or credit-bearing delivery before the course is listed/approved and every institutional prerequisite gate has been satisfied and formally released.
