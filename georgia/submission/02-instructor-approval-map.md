## The license is not the instructor approval

Even after Georgia Broker issuance, the owner cannot be represented as an approved prelicense instructor until GREC separately approves the instructor application.

## Published prerequisite sequence

Within one year before applying, provide evidence of both prerequisites:

1. complete a Commission-approved 75-hour Salesperson Prelicense Course;
2. complete a Commission-approved Instructor Development Workshop; and
3. preserve completion evidence that identifies provider, course, date, and approval basis.

Rule 520-2-.04(12)(d) recognizes a corresponding out-of-state prelicense course when its hours are similar and its delivery is consistent with Georgia standards. Do not assume that the owner's particular course qualifies: first obtain GREC's written answer and identify the exact approved workshop.

These two courses are prerequisites under the current rule; they are not point categories in the June 2024 points workbook. There is also a nonresident alternative for a person already approved as an instructor by another jurisdiction's real-estate regulator with at least five years of proven active prelicense teaching. No evidence currently establishes that route for the owner.

## Evidence dossier

- current instructor form and live fee confirmation—the 03/2024 form says $175, while current-rule arithmetic is $200;
- qualifying criminal background report and disposition records if applicable; for a non-Georgia resident, an equivalent residence-state report no more than 60 days old, with FBI escalation if that report shows an out-of-jurisdiction record or cannot be obtained;
- chronological resume with no unexplained claims;
- official transcripts and degree/certificate evidence;
- current and historical real-estate license evidence;
- transaction ledger identifying role, date, property type, and evidence location without exposing client data in Git;
- teaching record: topic, audience, delivery hours, dates, evaluator/contact, and proof;
- course-development record: course, role, hours/scope, deliverables, dates, and proof;
- prerequisite course/workshop completion documents; and
- a point-by-point crosswalk to the worksheet.

## Reproducible preliminary point position

The current workbook is separate from the application PDF and uses these maxima:

| Section | Formula | Maximum | Current unverified assumption |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Real-estate licensure | 1 point per active-license year (max 10), plus broker license 5, plus CAM years if applicable (max 5) | 20 | 10 + 5 = 15 |
| Teaching experience | GREC courses taught (max 20), non-GREC training (max 5), GREC courses written (max 5), informal teaching (max 5), formal-institution teaching (max 5) | 40 | 0 until documented |
| Formal general education | associate 2, bachelor 3, or graduate degree 5 | 5 | 3–5 |
| Real-estate experience | 0.3 per documented brokerage transaction (max 30) plus 1 per auxiliary-field year (max 5) | 35 | 30 + 5 = 35 |

Provisional total: 15 + 0 + 3–5 + 35 = **53–55**; provisional gap to the 65-point favorable-review guide = **10–12**. Every input is currently unverified, categories must not be double-counted, and GREC remains the final decision-maker.

## Renewal planning warning

The current rule reviewed for Phase 0 carries 60 hours of instruction and 36 hours of instructor education for renewal. At least 12 of those 36 hours must come from GREC's annual meetings for approved schools and instructors; the remainder may come from qualifying courses the instructor teaches or courses GREC approves for instructor education. An older form says 24 education hours and lists $125 paper with a $25 online discount. Plan to the current rule and confirm the operational fee. Do not convert this planning note into a claim about an issued approval term.

## Gate

The route remains NO-GO until GREC confirms the prerequisite treatment, the owner closes the evidence gap, all time-sensitive documents are current, and the Commission issues approval.
