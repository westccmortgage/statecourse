## Proposed learner sequence

Begin with identity/access instructions, orientation and a clear statement of course obligations. Course-specific content will be divided into approved major units and modules only after methodology authorization. Every learning objective will identify an observable action, the relevant teaching/activity evidence and an objective-level mastery criterion. A unit's design cannot rely on a high average to hide an unmastered objective.

Each module will alternate explanation, an original worked example, a learner decision or document task, diagnostic assessment, targeted feedback and a new-fact application. A reading screen followed only by exam-style questions is insufficient. The learner must use the concept in a task that requires a decision, calculation, comparison, document completion or explanation; the method is designed to reveal the reason for an error rather than merely disclose a correct letter.

Instruction will adapt to diagnosed deficiencies. A wrong finance sign, for example, calls for revisiting debit/credit direction and a new-fact calculation; it does not require replaying unrelated ownership instruction. The actual course-specific tasks and examples are not supplied by this methodology proposal before clearance.

## Initial research rationale

The proposed sequence draws on the Institute of Education Sciences' [Organizing Instruction and Study to Improve Student Learning](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1), September 2007, reviewed November 5, 2026. Its recommendation 2 supports alternating worked examples with independent problem solving; recommendation 5b supports retrieval through repeated exposure to key content; recommendation 7 supports explanatory questions. The guide rates those recommendations moderate, strong and strong respectively. This supports proposing examples, changed-fact practice, retrieval and explanation together rather than passive reading alone.

The guide does not validate StateCourse's particular adult real-estate implementation, proposed 80% criterion, software, course hours or regulatory compliance. The instructional designer must document the final course-specific rationale and its limits; the required actual pilot and independent review must test the implementation. Research support for a general strategy is not evidence that this course or platform has already succeeded.

## Mastery elements and evidence design

| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Approved major units | Maintain a regulator-reviewed major-unit manifest | Approved method/units and frozen version record |
| 2. Modules within units | Each module belongs to one unit with explicit prerequisites | Module manifest and tested sequencing |
| 3. Comprehensive objectives | Map every required outcome to teaching, activity and assessment | Independent completeness review; no unmapped objectives |
| 4. Quantitative mastery criterion | Evaluate every objective separately; proposed minimum 80% for each objective's diagnostic criterion, with all critical document/calculation rubric elements correct | Objective-specific scoring rules, rationale and GREC authorization where needed |
| 5. Structured learning method | Explanation, worked example, applied task, diagnosis and changed-fact transfer | Demonstrated learner flow and retained attempt evidence |
| 6. Regular diagnostics for every concept | Assess during the module at concept transitions, before aggregation or completion | Diagnostic-to-objective matrix and actual response records |
| 7. Tailored instruction | Assign remediation by the diagnosed objective and error type | Observed branching and accessible fallback paths |
| 8. Remediation until mastery | Repeat targeted teaching and a fresh application until the objective criterion is met | Attempt history and observed unresolved/mastered states |
| 9. Every objective mastered before completion | Module release requires each required objective, not a course-wide average | Negative tests showing that one unresolved objective blocks release |
| 10. Interactive required time | Actual active teaching/work must reasonably require the offered hours | Representative observed timing, pilot evidence, revisions and frozen-course evidence |

The percentages in this table are proposed provider criteria, not a claim that GREC prescribes those numbers. A final course-examination passing standard, administration method and any alternate assessment require the applicable rule/GREC determination. Approval of a teaching method would not approve every future item, course, instructor or exam process.

## Active time and completion are separate

For the proposed distance course, apply the rule's sixty-minute distance credit hour and keep assessment/test/final time within the applicable ten-percent ceiling. Do not convert a fifty-minute classroom convention into distance time or count breaks and an unattended browser tab. The final time ledger must identify instruction, active application, graded testing, idle intervals, administrative work and exclusions.

An inactivity mechanism will suspend counted participation and require a clear resumption action. Student visibility, interaction and recovery behavior will be tested, including keyboard and assistive-technology use. A timer alone cannot grant mastery, complete required written work or certify the learner's identity. Interactions and observed time are evidence to be reviewed, not a guarantee that a numeric hour label is valid.

Students will personally certify the required written work. The existing rule-prescribed certification and signature requirements must be delivered and retained in the form the Commission accepts; do not assume that a checkbox or an unauthenticated typed name is an authorized electronic signature. Required assignments without the prescribed certification are not graded as though certified. Approved instructional staff will review the required written work under the applicable rule and the approved assessment method.

## Instructor, identity, accessibility and support

An approved instructor must be designated with actual qualifications/approval evidence. The owner has expressly deferred instructor selection; neither the owner nor any other person is assigned by this document. Define actual availability, escalation and support arrangements once staff are selected. The proposed operating target is an acknowledgment within one business day and substantive response or escalation within two business days; this is a provider proposal subject to staffing and review, not an assertion that this service already operates or that GREC mandates those intervals.

Identity, assessment security, electronic certification and proctoring require an accepted process. Document intended evidence, access restrictions, attempts and incident handling. Do not claim remote proctor acceptance from an available vendor account or ordinary video call. Provide accessible equivalents that preserve the learning objective and required evidence; review accommodations against law and Commission requirements before using them.

Technical support documentation will specify supported devices, browsers, connectivity and accessible document formats. Retain failure/recovery evidence showing that a disconnected learner can resume without silently losing an attempt, earning idle time or bypassing an unresolved objective. Actual platform behavior must be demonstrated before it is described as operational.

## Required development pilot after authorization

Plan at least nine actual participants for the salesperson course: at least three unlicensed, at least three licensed salespersons or community association managers, and at least six non-educators, with varied computer and real-estate skill. Record eligibility/classifications and allowable overlap from the rule; do not count invented people or simulated model sessions. Seek Commission direction on the proposed protocol and any requested exception before relying on it.

For each participant retain consent, initial skill classification, version used, actual start/end and active-time observations, concept/interaction errors, diagnostic/remediation paths, assignment quality, accessibility/support findings and completion/disposition. Distinguish a participant's test time from instructional work. Retain aggregate findings and the exact corrections and retesting prompted by them. Pilot completion is not inferred from successful automated checks.

## Records and reviewer demonstration

The proposed record system will preserve the exact course/software version, module/objective identifiers, response/score history, remediation, active-time evidence, certifications, written work, grader disposition, final administration and reporting events for at least the applicable five-year period. The final design must make records retrievable for Commission inspection and preserve corrections with an audit trail. Collect only necessary private information and restrict access by role.

Before a filing is described as complete, demonstrate an ordinary learner path; a wrong-answer remediation path; an unresolved-objective block; a missing-certification block; inactivity and reconnect behavior; accessible task completion; a version change; staff access restrictions; and record retrieval/restoration. These are required demonstrations to perform, not assertions that they have already occurred. Keep credit, certificates and reporting closed until the applicable course, school, instructor, delivery and operational gates are actually satisfied.
