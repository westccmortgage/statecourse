# Instructor points — evidence working sheet

<!-- material-claims: ga-instructor-application ga-instructor-points -->

**This is not a GREC score.** The official application worksheet identifies 65 points as the favorable-review threshold, but the Commission reviews the evidence and decides the application. Never round, double-count, or claim an item without the source document.

**Controlling worksheet for this calculation:** separate GREC XLSX, revision 06/2024. The instructor application PDF links it but does not contain it.

## Current preliminary position and formula

| Section | Current assumption | Formula | Provisional points | Section maximum |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 — Real-estate licensure | 10 active-license years; broker license held; no CAM points | min(years, 10) + 5 broker + min(CAM years, 5) | 15 | 20 |
| 2 — Teaching experience | none yet supported against the workbook categories | category formulas below | 0 | 40 |
| 3 — Formal general education | qualifying bachelor's or graduate degree | bachelor = 3; graduate = 5; do not stack degrees | 3–5 | 5 |
| 4 — Real-estate experience | at least 100 supported brokerage transactions; at least 5 supported auxiliary-field years | min(transactions × 0.3, 30) + min(auxiliary years, 5) | 35 | 35 |
| **Provisional total** | all assumptions remain unverified | 15 + 0 + (3–5) + 35 | **53–55** | 100 |
| **Gap to favorable-review guide** | 65 − provisional total | 65 − (53–55) | **10–12** | — |

The range comes from user-reported professional history and may fall after documentary review. It must be recomputed from the live worksheet. The Commission-approved salesperson course and instructor-development workshop are application prerequisites; the current workbook does not award them points.

## Rules for completion

1. Attach one proof item to each claim.
2. Record workbook revision 06/2024 and the exact criterion/formula.
3. Do not count the same experience twice unless the form expressly allows it.
4. Keep a conservative score and a disputed score separate.
5. Send ambiguous categories to GREC in writing.
6. Treat prerequisite completion separately from point scoring.
7. Submit only after the documented conservative total reaches the filing position selected by the owner and GREC confirms any disputed interpretation.
