An assessed concept can recur in instruction and on an exam. An independently assessed application must not simply reproduce the taught or formative fact pattern and decision with renamed participants or changed numbers. The replacements change the operative prerequisite, stage, task, actor, evidence, or decision. Supplied hypothetical terms and counsel findings are explicitly identified where the task is applying those facts instead of deriving a broad legal doctrine.

REWRITE means the case/decision was substantively replaced or an original answerability defect required reconstruction. REPAIR keeps that decision but improves wording, sources, or distractors. RETAIN means an affirmative semantic decision with its specific comparison recorded below. These labels classify the response, not legal severity or an automated defect count.
