## DBPR equivalency decision

- Provide any California scope or basis-license supplement DBPR requests.
- Record the exact date and all grounds in any adverse finding.
- If the only proposed denial ground is a finding that the basis license is not substantially equivalent or otherwise insufficient, section 455.213(15), Florida Statutes, permits a secretary-review request within **7 business days after notice**. Obtain counsel if needed and do not miss the window.
- This review right is not an approval guarantee.

- diploma/course descriptions;

- written direction on evaluator, evaluation type and direct-send/sealed-record protocol.

The subject appears facially aligned with `finance`, but DBPR decides equivalency. Current real-estate materials do not publish a definitive foreign-degree protocol.

File only after broker and degree gates close. Select `[1030]`; supply the issued Florida broker number/status, transcript/equivalency package, protected data and supporting records; sign/date; pay **$62.75**; and confirm fingerprint reuse or a new capture. If DBPR accepts `[1030]`, no separate instructor exam is required. `[1010]` remains fallback only.

## Fallback control

Do **not** purchase a 72-hour broker course now. Only after a final endorsement denial, any timely review and a deliberate owner decision may `[2501/1012]` become the route; that fallback uses the 72-hour course because a Finance degree is not a four-year real-estate-degree exemption and leads to the full 100-question broker examination. A written DBPR instruction addressing `[1047]` must be handled as such, not silently relabeled as the fallback.
