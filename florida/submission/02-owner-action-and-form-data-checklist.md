# Owner action and official-form data checklist

These are the items automation cannot truthfully invent, certify, purchase or perform. They are staged once so the owner is not asked repeatedly.

For the complete dependency order, exact controlled entity facts, known costs and launch boundary, use `05-anatoliy-filing-runbook.md`. This file is the compact data checklist; the runbook is the execution control.

## DBPR RE 3 - instructor examination route [1010]

Owner supplies or confirms privately in the official filing channel:

- legal name exactly as shown on the Social Security card;
- SSN, birth date, gender, physical residence and mailing address;
- primary phone and email;
- every current/prior business or professional license, jurisdiction, dates, number and name used;
- every prior name or alias;
- high-school graduation or equivalency response;
- all four background answers and any required supporting legal documents;
- transaction 1010 selection;
- perjury declaration, signature and date;
- $62.75 DBPR fee.

After filing, the owner personally completes registered Livescan and pays the vendor's current fee. DBPR must authorize the examination before Pearson scheduling. The owner then personally attends the physical Pearson test center. The repository does not pre-answer background questions or place protected information in source control.

## Pearson instructor examination

Verified: physical Pearson center, closed book, 100 equal-weight multiple-choice questions, 3.5 hours, passing score 75. Still confirm at live scheduling:

- current Pearson fee;
- available/nearest authorized test center and date;
- accepted identification;
- whether the generic CIB instruction about presenting a pre-license completion certificate applies to an instructor-exam candidate, because RE 3 and section 475.451 do not state that prerequisite.

## DBPR RE 5 - school permit

Do not execute until the instructor number exists and the location/entity questions are resolved. Owner supplies/confirms:

- school permitholder's protected personal data;
- issued Florida instructor permit number as qualification basis;
- StateCourse LLC legal name, ownership type and exact school/DBA name;
- final physical school address, mailing treatment, county/country, phone and email;
- foreign-qualification/registered-agent/fictitious-name decision and evidence if applicable;
- perjury declaration, signature and date;
- $109 fee.

## DBPR RE 6 - add/activate instructor

After school and instructor permits are issued, enter both permit numbers, select Add Instructor, obtain the school permitholder's signature and file the $0 transaction.

## DBPR RE 18 - Course I

Only the issued school permitholder may sign the school application. Before signature, re-download the form linked on DBPR's Education page and compare its identity and Section V requirements to the held current copy. Required filing data includes:

- school permit number;
- permit holder/requestor name and StateCourse LLC organization name;
- confirmed mailing and physical business addresses;
- phone/email;
- FREC, Sales Associate Pre-Licensure, Distance and NEW selections;
- course title `Course I` and 63 hours;
- final title/version of course material;
- signature and date.

RE 19 and RE 20 are not included in the permitted-school Course I package under the current linked RE 18; both are marked Providers Only.
