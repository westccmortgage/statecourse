### Personal Florida broker route — selected parallel objective

Do not buy that course before transaction-specific written direction or a deliberate post-denial fallback decision.

## 2. School-permitholder authority

Section 475.451(2)(a), Florida Statutes, provides two alternatives: an active or voluntarily inactive Florida broker license, **or** passing a Commission-approved instructor examination. RE 3 `[1010]`, RE 5 and RE 6 reflect that alternative.

DBPR's 2 September response resolves the prior operational question in favor of using the issued instructor permit on RE 5. It does not itself approve Anatoliy's RE 3 application, examination admission, permit, school or course.

## 6. Course I package

The current RE 18 requires, for an initial distance Course I submission by a permitted school:

1. timed outline listing subjects, major topics and subtopics;
2. learning objectives at three per hour;
3. course materials and delivery-method explanation;
4. two end-of-course examinations for distance prelicensing;
5. an answer key for each form citing the page where the question and correct answer rest;
6. access to the course in the form offered to students under Rule 61J2-3.008(4)(b).

RE 19 and RE 20 are marked “Providers Only” on the current RE 18 and are not school attachments.
