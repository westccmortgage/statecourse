## Questions closed by the response

1. **No Florida broker license is required solely for the school route.** An individual who otherwise qualifies through the RE 3 instructor-examination process may complete that process, pass the required examination and receive an instructor permit without first holding a Florida broker license.
2. **The issued instructor permit may qualify the RE 5 school permitholder.** The instructor permit must be issued before the applicant relies on it as the method of qualification on RE 5.
3. **The company record must match the Division of Corporations.** If StateCourse LLC operates the school, the business information on RE 5 must correspond with the business information registered with the Florida Division of Corporations. DBPR did not decide whether the California LLC needs a Florida foreign-LLC certificate of authority and directed that question to the Division of Corporations.
4. **No Florida classroom is required solely for distance delivery.** RE 5 should list the school's actual physical business address. If the school operates from California, that California physical address should be used instead of inventing a Florida classroom address.
5. **A registered-agent-only address does not require RE 9.** RE 9 is for an actual additional school location. A Florida registered-agent address alone is not an additional location when no school operations or instruction occur there.
6. **Sequence:** obtain the instructor permit first; then file the school permit; treat course approval as a separate process; do not offer Course I until the school and course have the applicable approvals.
7. **Distance controls remain applicable:** course administration, student monitoring, instructor availability, technical support and course access.
