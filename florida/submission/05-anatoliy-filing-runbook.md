## Stage 5 — DBPR equivalency review and review right

The five-year threshold is necessary, not a guarantee. DBPR/FREC compares the California basis license and scope against Florida and may request more evidence.

Section 455.213(15), Florida Statutes, imposes a specific process before an endorsement denial:

- DBPR/FREC must make a finding whether the other-jurisdiction basis license is substantially equivalent or otherwise sufficient;
- if it finds the basis license not substantially equivalent or otherwise insufficient, and there is no other denial ground, the applicant has **7 business days after notice** to request secretary review;
- the secretary then has **7 business days after receiving the request** to review the finding;
- if the secretary disagrees, the application must be approved unless another denial ground exists.

This is a short procedural right, not a promise of approval. On any adverse notice, record the receipt date immediately, identify every stated ground and obtain Florida counsel if needed. Do not let the seven-business-day window expire while informally corresponding.
