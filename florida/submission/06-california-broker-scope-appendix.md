## Purpose

The DBPR RE 2 endorsement instructions require a current certified license history and copies of the statutes and/or rules defining the scope of work for the current out-of-state license. This cover identifies the official California provisions attached for that purpose.

This packet does **not** assert that California and Florida licenses are substantially equivalent. DBPR/FREC makes that determination under Florida law. The summaries below are an index only; the attached official California text controls.
