## Signature-time gate

Florida Administrative Register Vol. 52, No. 138 (July 17, 2026), pp. 3080-81 proposes amending Rule 61-35.027 to adopt revised RE 18 effective June 2026 as Ref-19807. Because the verified rule page did not yet show the amendment as final while DBPR already linked the new form, execute this gate immediately before filing:

1. open DBPR's current Education page;
2. download the linked RE 18 without reusing a saved browser URL;
3. verify title, page count, Section V items 4-8 and incorporation line;
4. compare hash and text against the held current revision;
5. if different, stop signature, re-read every changed requirement, update the package and rerun Florida tests;
6. record retrieval time, URL, hash and reviewer.

Passing this gate establishes only the correct paper form. It does not establish school or course approval.
