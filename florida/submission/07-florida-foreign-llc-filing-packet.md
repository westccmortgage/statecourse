## Filing decision

Use a Florida foreign-LLC qualification before filing DBPR RE 5.

This is a conservative filing decision, not a conclusion that every California-based online
business must qualify. Section 605.0905, Florida Statutes, excludes some activities—including
interstate commerce—from the Chapter 605 meaning of “transacting business.” But subsection (4)
expressly says those exclusions do not decide regulation under other Florida law. StateCourse is
seeking a Florida real-estate-school permit under Chapter 475, and current RE 5 instructs the
applicant to provide the school name as registered with the Florida Division of Corporations.
DBPR's 2 September 2026 written response likewise says the RE 5 business information must
correspond with the Division record.

Qualifying the existing California LLC removes that avoidable filing mismatch. It does not create a
Florida classroom: DBPR separately confirmed that the actual California physical school address may
be used for distance-only delivery and that a registered-agent-only address is not an additional
school location.

## Official filing set

1. **Application by Foreign Limited Liability Company for Authorization to Transact Business in
   Florida** (Florida Department of State form CR2E027).
2. **California Certificate of Status**, issued by the California Secretary of State and dated no
   more than 90 days before Florida receives the application. Order directly through California
   bizfile Online; the current certificate fee is $5.
3. **Florida registered agent acceptance.** The agent must provide a Florida street address and
   sign the acceptance on the application. A P.O. box is not sufficient.
4. **$125 mandatory Florida filing total:** $100 application filing fee plus $25 registered-agent
   designation. A $5 Florida certificate of status and $30 certified copy are optional and are not
   included unless deliberately selected.
5. Cover letter and payment/online-filing receipt. Retain the acknowledgment and the resulting
   Florida document number.

## Privacy and truth controls

Florida corporate filings are public records. Do not place an SSN, birth date, fingerprint
identifier, payment credential, background record or unnecessary residence information on this
filing or in the repository. Keep the FEI and service credentials outside the repository while
preparing the packet, and assume any field placed on the filed application will become public.
Recheck all facts at signature; this draft is not a signed filing.
