61J2-17.014 Guest Lecturers.
Guest lecturers may be used by a real estate school offering Commission prescribed or approved equivalent courses so long as:
(1) Guest lecturers provide no more than 20% of the total course;
(2) An instructor licensed with such school is available on the premises.
