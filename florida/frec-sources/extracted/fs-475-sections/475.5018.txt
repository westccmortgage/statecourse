475.5018
Facsimile signatures or writing accepted.
—
When any act performed under this part must be performed in writing or acknowledged with a signature, the provision of an instrument or writing by electronic means or facsimile, including a signature transmitted by electronic means or facsimile, is binding and sufficient.
History.
—
s. 13, ch. 98-250.
