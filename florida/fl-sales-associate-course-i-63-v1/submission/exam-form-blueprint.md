# FREC Course I — secure examination form blueprint

**Architecture GO; bank content and form assembly NO-GO. No examination or answer key is generated.**

The current DBPR Education-page-linked RE 18 requires **two** examination forms for distance
pre/post-license education. The legacy RE 18 that asked for five across all distance courses is
superseded and forbidden for filing. Item count remains unresolved on the face of the rule: 100,
120 and 126 are identified readings. StateCourse therefore builds 126 items per form pending a
written DBPR answer; 126 is provider policy, not a Florida minimum.

## Fixed form design

| Form | Planned items | Application | Comprehension | Knowledge | Student form | Key | Release |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| A | 126 | 89 (70.6%) | 25 (19.8%) | 12 (9.5%) | not generated | not generated | disabled |
| B | 126 | 89 (70.6%) | 25 (19.8%) | 12 (9.5%) | not generated | not generated | disabled |

The 252 secure slots have unique identifiers and target zero cross-form question reuse. Zero is a
StateCourse design target; Florida's confirmed ceiling is 15%. Each slot fixes a syllabus section,
objective, curriculum page, required cognitive level, four-choice requirement and authoring rule.
The form order is interleaved and does not follow course order. Across the two forms every one of
the 180 incorporated objectives is represented; no objective repeats within a form.

## Section allocation per form

The allocation is proportional to the 180 syllabus objectives and uses the largest-remainder method
to total exactly 126: **5, 8, 5, 7, 6, 6, 7, 6, 6, 7, 8, 6, 7, 7, 4, 11, 6, 6, 8** for sections
1 through 19. This is provider examination design, not a Florida per-section weighting.

- independent legal/content review: **not complete**;
- final pagination lock: **not complete**.

A unique planned-item identifier is not proof that two authored questions differ. Cross-form
duplication can be measured only after stems, correct propositions and choice sets exist. The final
integrity gate must compare all three, not merely IDs or wording.

## Authoring and release order

1. Expand and independently review the curriculum, then lock final pagination.
2. Author each remaining slot against its assigned objective, page and cognitive-level direction.
3. Require four distinct choices, one defensible key, a rationale and curriculum-page support.
4. Reject any item that repeats a formative fact pattern or another form's tested proposition.
5. Run within-form and cross-form stem, correct-proposition and choice-set duplication checks.
6. Assemble only forms whose application/knowledge percentages, order and page references pass.
7. Generate separate secure reviewer keys only after every item passes; never place a key in a
   learner-facing course, video, transcript, marketing surface or public build.
8. Keep release disabled until the school permit, instructor permit and course approval are held.
