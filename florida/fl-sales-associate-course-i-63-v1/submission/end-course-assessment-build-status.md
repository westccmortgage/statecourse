# FREC Course I — end-course assessment build status

**NO-GO for form assembly.** The regulatory architecture is fixed, but the secure bank is not
complete.

## Fixed controls

- passing score: 70%;
- 150 minutes;
- at least 100 items under the confirmed regulatory-floor reading;
- four choices per item;
- at least 70% application level and no more than 10% knowledge level;
- order must not track course order;
- no more than 15% duplication between forms;
- unique page-referenced key for each form;
- no hint, back or retry;
- all syllabus sessions complete before access;
- different form for a permitted retake/repeat.

StateCourse's conservative working policy remains 126 items and five forms, clearly labelled as
policy. The rule/checklist floor says two forms while DBPR RE 18 asks for five; the conflict remains
a regulator question and is not averaged away.
