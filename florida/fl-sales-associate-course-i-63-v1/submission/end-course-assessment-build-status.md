## Fixed controls

- passing score: 70%;
- 150 minutes;
- at least 100 items under the confirmed regulatory-floor reading;
- four choices per item;
- at least 70% application level and no more than 10% knowledge level;
- order must not track course order;
- no more than 15% duplication between forms;
- unique page-referenced key for each form;
- no hint, back or retry;
- all syllabus sessions complete before access;
- different form for a permitted retake/repeat.

StateCourse's conservative working policy remains 126 items and five forms, clearly labelled as
policy. The rule/checklist floor says two forms while DBPR RE 18 asks for five; the conflict remains
a regulator question and is not averaged away.

## Complete blueprint, incomplete authored bank

25 comprehension and 12 knowledge slots per form. Every slot carries its section, objective,
curriculum page and authoring direction; order is interleaved rather than course-sequential. Across
the five forms all 180 objectives appear, with no objective repeated inside one form.

Nineteen application-level multiple-choice drafts exist, one per syllabus section, each with four
options, rationale, objective mapping and curriculum-page support. Each occupies exactly one
application slot. **611 slots remain unwritten.** The narrower milestones are 81 more items for one
100-item regulatory-floor form and 107 more for one 126-item policy form. No form is represented as
assembled.

Unique IDs do not prove unique questions. After authoring, the duplication audit must compare
normalized stems, the correct proposition at its source page, and choice sets across all forms.
The zero-reuse target is StateCourse policy; Florida's confirmed maximum is 15%.

No student form or publishable answer key is generated while those gates remain open.
