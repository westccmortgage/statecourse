# Florida Course I — records, identity and attendance SOP

**Operating draft; inactive until permits and course approval are held.**

## Record created for each learner

- registration and enrolment agreement version;
- identity-verification results at enrolment, participation and completion;
- section/component events and active-time evidence;
- every periodic-assessment attempt, feedback and remediation result;
- final form identifier, start/end time and answer record when a final is authorized;
- completion decision and the exact certificate issued.

Audit events are append-only. Corrections add a new event with actor, reason and time; they do not
overwrite the original.

## Retention

Registration records, rosters, attendance/participation records, each examination and progress-test
copy, and student answer records are retained at least three years after course commencement under
s. 475.451(8), Fla. Stat. Completion certificates are retained five years under Rule
61J2-3.015(6)(f), F.A.C. The periods are separate and the longer certificate period is not used to
misstate the school-record rule.

## Attendance in distance delivery

Attendance is demonstrated by completed required work and event evidence, not by a browser tab or
timer. A section cannot complete without its reading checkpoints, primary-source submission,
application and periodic assessment. Support staff may not manually award time to cure missing work.

## Certificate gate

No certificate issues unless all 19 sections, all remediation, completion identity verification,
the prescribed certification statement and the authorized end-course examination are complete.
Credential numbers remain blank until DBPR actually issues them; placeholders may not reach a
student artifact.
