# FREC Course I — distance delivery method and controls

**Reviewer-preparation draft. Not submitted or approved.**

## Delivery method

The course is asynchronous and sequential. Each of the 19 syllabus sections contains authored
reading, required primary-source work, an application lab and periodic assessment. Elapsed time
alone completes nothing. A learner must submit the evidence specified for every component, and any
assigned remediation, before the next section unlocks.

The 3,000 instructional minutes exclude optional video. The end-course examination occupies a
separate 150-minute budget. The final remains disabled until secure forms, page-referenced keys and
regulatory approvals exist.

## Identity

Identity is verified at three distinct points required by Rule 61J2-3.008(4)(b)3.c:

1. **Enrolment:** government-ID result, legal-name/date-of-birth match, time and decision.
2. **Participation:** step-up challenge during the course, session/device record, time and result.
3. **Completion:** final re-verification, prescribed personal-completion certification, time and
   completion decision.

Failures stop progression and enter a review queue. The records contain verification results, not
unnecessary copies of identity documents.

## Participation and assessment

Every objective has a constructed-response or worked-application task. First attempts are
preserved. StateCourse's 80% module threshold is provider policy, not a Florida passing-score rule.
Below-threshold work returns the learner to a cited curriculum page and assigns a new fact pattern.
All remediation must be resolved before the end-course examination unlocks.

## Examination controls

The final has no hint, back or retry function, is time-limited to 150 minutes, uses a form identifier
and preserves the answer record. The engine does not presently enable a final because no complete,
independently reviewed secure form exists and no course approval is held. The statutory removal of
a monitoring requirement is not treated as a ban on proctoring.

## Continuity

The continuity design includes service monitoring, a redundant read-only course copy, queued-work
preservation, a learner status page, telephone/email instructor support and an incident record. No
instructional credit is granted for unavailable time. Restoration requires a lost-work check and
learner notice.

## Accessibility policy

Keyboard operation, semantic structure, sufficient contrast, captions/transcripts for any future
media and mobile-responsive presentation are StateCourse policy. They are not represented as a
Florida-specific media mandate.
