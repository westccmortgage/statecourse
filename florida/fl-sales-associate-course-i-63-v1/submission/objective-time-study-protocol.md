# FREC Course I — objective time-study protocol

**Required before submission; protocol complete, participant observations not yet collected.**

Rule 61J2-3.008(4)(b)3.e requires evidence from an objective study that stated hours are consistent
with actual completion time. The 3,000-minute outline is therefore a testable design hypothesis, not
the study result.

## Study population

Recruit at least 20 adult participants who have not completed a Florida prelicense course, including
at least five mobile-primary users and a range of prior education and real-estate familiarity.
Exclude author/reviewer accounts from timing statistics.

## Instrumentation

Record active time per component, pauses, focus loss, submitted work, assessment attempts,
remediation, support contacts, device category and accessibility-mode use. Cap no participant's
observed time merely because the design allocation expired. Report interrupted sessions separately.

## Procedure

1. Freeze the curriculum bytes, page map and engine version.
2. Run the complete course without optional video and without the end-course examination.
3. Require the same reading, source, application and assessment evidence intended for students.
4. Review telemetry for idle-time inflation and missing-event compression.
5. Compute per-section and total median, mean, standard deviation, 25th/75th percentiles and the
   completion-time range; stratify by device and material learner characteristics without exposing
   personal data.
6. Investigate any section where the median differs from design by more than 10% or where a required
   objective lacks observable work.
7. Revise content or allocation, freeze a new version and rerun affected sections. Allocations must
   still total 3,000 minutes.

## Acceptance rule

The study is submission-ready only when every objective has completion evidence, the course median
is consistent with 3,000 instructional minutes, no component relies on unattended elapsed time, and
the final report identifies limitations and preserves de-identified raw observations. A word-count
estimate or author walkthrough alone does not satisfy this protocol.
