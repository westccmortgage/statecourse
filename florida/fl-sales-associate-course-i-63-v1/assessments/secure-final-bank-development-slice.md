## 1. FL-FD-001

A seller asks a sales associate for an independent opinion of market value for use in a condemnation trial. Which referral best respects the professional boundary taught in Course I?

A. Property manager
B. State-certified appraiser
C. Mortgage loan originator
D. Home inspector

**Key:** B  
**Rationale:** An independent valuation for litigation is appraisal work; a brokerage CMA is not a substitute.
