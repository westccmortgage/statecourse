## Learning objectives

1. State what a broker must be able to explain about financing conditions without advising on products.
2. State what a broker must be able to explain about housing market conditions, and from what data.
3. State the advertising rules that govern how any trend claim may be published.
4. State where Division "hot issues" come from and identify what this build did not obtain.
5. State the rule that makes currency the broker's own continuing obligation.

## A. Financing trends — outline Topic VII.A

### A.1 What a broker must be able to do

**Explain how the current financing environment affects a client's transaction**, using figures the **lender
supplies**:

- What a change in rate does to a payment, and to a buyer's qualifying position.
- What products are available in the market the client is buying in — and that availability is the
  **lender's** statement, not the broker's.
- What **points** cost and what the break-even is: **one point is one percent of the LOAN AMOUNT**, and the
  break-even is the cost divided by the annual saving. *(Worked in Real Estate Law and Practice Module 04
  Part D.3, on figures supplied inside that problem.)*
- What an **appraisal below contract price** does — the lender lends against the **lesser** of price or
  appraised value, and the gap is the buyer's to close.
- What **assumption** and **owner carry** structures require of the contract. *(Contracts and Regulations
  Module 04 Part C.)*

### A.2 The line a broker does not cross

**§§ 12-10-404(1)(c)(V), 12-10-405(1)(c)(V) and 12-10-407(2)(b)(II)** each require the broker to **advise
the party to obtain expert advice** as to material matters known to the broker but whose specifics are
beyond the broker's expertise.

**A broker does arithmetic with the lender's figures. A broker does not tell a borrower which product to
take, predict a rate, or forecast approval.**

**And Rule 6.2.A** requires a broker to **possess the necessary experience, training and knowledge** and to
**maintain compliance with applicable law** — which in a shifting financing environment means **knowing what
they no longer know.**

### A.3 What this build states about financing figures

**Nothing.** **No rate, index, margin, cap, qualifying ratio, credit score, down payment minimum, loan
limit, mortgage insurance threshold or programme requirement is stated anywhere in this build**, and every
figure in every worked calculation across the six courses is **supplied inside that calculation as a
given.**

**RESPA, TILA, TRID and ECOA were NOT read by this project**, and **no disclosure requirement, timing rule,
tolerance or penalty from any of them is stated anywhere.**

## B. Current housing trends — outline Topic VII.B

### B.1 What a broker must be able to do

**Describe the market a client is transacting in, from data the client can check.**

| Measure | What it says |
|---|---|
| **Inventory and months of supply** | How much is available relative to the rate of sale |
| **Days on market** | How long it is taking |
| **List-to-sale price ratio** | What is happening between asking and selling |
| **Absorption rate** | How quickly the available stock is being taken up |
| **New listings and closed sales** | Direction of supply and of activity |
| **New construction and permits** | Supply arriving later |
| **Rents and vacancy** | For investors, and for the buy-versus-rent question |

**Absorption worked, on figures supplied as a given:** if a submarket has **180 active listings** and has
been closing an average of **45 sales a month**, the months of supply is **180 ÷ 45 = 4.0 months.** *(Both
figures are given inside this problem. Neither describes any actual market.)*

### B.2 The two constraints on how it is said

> **1. Fair housing.** A broker describes **the property and the physical, economic, governmental and legal
> characteristics of an area** using sources a client can check — **not** the protected characteristics of
> the people in it. **§§ 12-10-404(1)(e), 12-10-405(1)(e) and 12-10-407(2)(d)** each make compliance with
> fair housing and civil rights statutes part of the brokerage relationship duties.
>
> **2. Accuracy, with the source cited.** **Rule 6.10.C**: firms and brokers are **responsible for ensuring
> all advertising is accurate**, including MLS entries — with **no duty to investigate** information from
> the seller or a third-party source.

### B.3 What this build states about market figures

**Nothing.** **No price, inventory figure, days-on-market number, absorption rate, ratio, forecast or trend
is stated anywhere in this build.** The absorption calculation above uses figures **given inside the
problem** and describes no market.

## C. Marketing and advertising trends — outline Topic VII.C

**Marketing changes constantly. The rule that governs it does not, and it was read in full.**

### C.1 What counts as advertising

> **Rule 1.2**: *"**Advertise or Advertising**: The promotion, solicitation, or representation of Real Estate
> Brokerage Services requiring a License. Advertising may include, but is not limited to, **business cards,
> brochures, websites, signage, property flyers, mailings (paper or electronic), SOCIAL MEDIA, letterhead,
> EMAIL SIGNATURES, and contract documents.** A **uniform resource locator (URL) and an email address are
> NOT considered Advertising** for purposes of Rule 6.10 **so long as they are not directly used to promote
> or solicit** Real Estate Brokerage Services."*

**Social media and email signatures are advertising.** Whatever the medium is called next year, the
definition reaches *"the promotion, solicitation, or representation of Real Estate Brokerage Services
requiring a License"* — **which is a functional test, not a list.**

### C.2 The rules that apply whatever the medium — Rule 6.10

**Delivered in full in Module 02 Part B.4.** The provisions a broker adopting a new channel checks first:

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **6.10.A.2** | Conduct or promote **only in the name under which the broker or firm appears in the Commission's records** |
| **6.10.A.4** | **All advertising clearly and conspicuously in the name of the broker's brokerage firm** — with the exception for the broker's **own property not listed with the firm** |
| **6.10.A.5–A.7** | **Trade Name filed with the Commission**; **one** trade name only; **trademark with permission**; and where a third party's trade name or trademark is used, one of the two prescribed **"independently owned and operated"** statements, **clearly and conspicuously** |
| **6.10.A.8** | **No professional designation** unless the broker is **in good standing** and it is **easily verifiable**; **awards, memberships and achievements must be verifiable on request** |
| **6.10.B** | **Team names** may not use **Realty, Real estate, Realtors, Company, Corporation, Corp., Inc., LLC, LP or LLP**, or any term implying a separate entity; all team advertising **in conjunction with the firm's legal or trade name**; the firm **provides team member names on request**; **no use of the team name by brokers outside the firm** |
| **6.10.C** | **Accuracy**, including **MLS entries** — and **no duty to investigate** seller or third-party information |
| **6.10.D.1** | Where the broker owns or controls electronic media, **EACH VIEWABLE PAGE carries the firm's name**, and **expired listings removed within THREE DAYS** of expiry |
| **6.10.D.2** | Third-party syndication — the broker is **responsible for accuracy** and must **submit a WRITTEN REQUEST to syndicators** to remove expired listings **within three days** |
| **6.10.D.3** | **Email, chat, instant messages, newsgroups, discussion lists, bulletin boards, blogs or similar** — **the firm's name must be used**, though **not repeated** to a consumer to whom it has already been disclosed |
| **6.10.D.4** | Where space is limited and disclosure is **not reasonable**, the firm's name **within the FIRST CLICK OF THE MOUSE** |
| **6.10.E** | **Past sales data** — **cite the source**, and carry the **disclaimer**, if accurate, that sales **were not necessarily listed or sold by the broker** and are **intended only to show trends** or will **separately identify the broker's own activity** |
| **6.10.F** | **No advertising the availability or price** of a property **without authority from the owner or the owner's broker** |
| **6.10.G** | **The price quoted is the price agreed between the broker and the owner** |

**Rule 6.10.E is the rule that governs a market report**, which is the most common way a broker publishes
trend content — **cite the source, and disclaim.**

**Rule 6.10.D.4's "first click of the mouse" is the provision written for constrained formats**, and it is
the one a broker adopting any new short-form channel should read first.

### C.3 Two more that travel with the medium

- **Rule 6.11** — advertising **square footage** requires **disclosing the source on the Commission-approved
  form**, with its **date of issuance** where it has one.
- **Rule 6.10.C** — advertising must **comply with copyright laws and other applicable laws and
  regulations.** **No copyright statute was read by this project**, and **no rule about images, floor plans,
  listing copy, music or generated content is stated anywhere in this build.**

## E. What makes any of this stick: the currency obligation

**The Commission puts it on the provider**, in this course's own outline:

> *"**Providers should address EACH APPLICABLE STATUTE, RULE AND POSITION STATEMENT during the course.
> COURSE PROVIDERS ARE RESPONSIBLE FOR ENSURING THAT THE STATUTES, RULES AND POSITION STATEMENTS USED DURING
> THE COURSE PRESENTATION ARE ACCURATE AND UP-TO-DATE.**"*

**And backs it with an audit power** — **Rule 2.1.D**: the Commission *"**may audit courses ... at any time
and at no cost**"*, may request **all instructional materials and student attendance records**, and
*"**failure to comply with statutes and these Rules may result in the removal of the course provider,
instructor, and/or the course from the approved provider list.**"*

**The rule puts it on the broker too, and it is continuous:**

> **Rule 6.2.A**: a broker *"**must possess the necessary experience, training, and knowledge to provide Real
> Estate Brokerage Services and MAINTAIN COMPLIANCE WITH THE APPLICABLE FEDERAL, STATE AND LOCAL LAWS,
> RULES, REGULATIONS AND ORDINANCES.**"*

**"Maintain" is the word.** A broker compliant with the rule as it stood is not compliant with the rule as
it stands — and **Rule 6.2.B's first route remains available: decline.**

**And Rule 7.1.A's "for current use" makes the same point about forms.** A superseded form is not a
Commission-approved form, and **§ 12-10-403(4)(c)** requires the broker to **use** one.

> **How this build makes its own currency checkable.** The readings file and every instructor source note
> across all six Colorado courses name **the exact rule version (12355)** and **the exact date read
> (2026-08-20)**. A reviewer does not have to take the build's word that it is current; **they can check the
> CCR docket against those two facts.**
