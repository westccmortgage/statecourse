> ### The Commission's footnote, which governs Modules 02 through 05
>
> Topics II, III, IV and V each carry an asterisk, and the outline explains it once:
>
> > *"\***Educators should make sure students understand HOW COMPETENCY IS GAINED in each specialty** e.g.
> > through working with an experienced mentor or partner and/or taking educational courses to become
> > competent in the field or area **SO NO HARM IS DONE TO A MEMBER OF THE PUBLIC.**"*
>
> **These four modules are competency modules, not surveys.** The question each answers is: **what does a
> broker need to know before taking this kind of work, and what do they do if they do not have it?**
>
> **Rule 6.2 supplies the legal frame for all four**, and its four routes are the answer to the second half
> of that question: **decline**; **obtain** the experience, training and knowledge; **obtain the
> assistance** of the employing broker, a supervisory broker, a competent broker **or legal counsel
> competent in the matter**; or **co-list.**

## Learning objectives

1. State which disclosures are required in a residential transaction and when each is due.
2. State the rule requiring use of Commission-approved forms.
3. State the supervision relationship and how a complaint reaches the employing broker.
4. State how offers pass between listing and selling brokers.
5. State the advertising rules a residential broker works inside daily.
6. State the square footage disclosure requirement.
7. State Rule 6.12's CMA/BPO notice verbatim and the prohibition that accompanies it.
8. State what a broker does and does not do with third-party providers.

## A. Broker relationships — outline Topic II.A

### A.1 Disclosures — II.A.1

**Delivered in full in Contracts and Regulations Module 02**, and summarised here as a residential broker
meets them in order.

| When | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Before eliciting or discussing confidential information** | The § 12-10-408 written disclosure | **Rule 6.5.A** — and 6.5.B excludes *"preliminary conversations or 'small talk' concerning price range, location and property styles"* |
| **Before engaging in any licensed activity, as a transaction-broker** | Written disclosure that the broker **is not acting as agent** and **is a transaction-broker**, with a **signature block** — and **if the party does not sign, the broker notes that on a copy and retains it** | **§ 12-10-408(2)(a)** |
| **Before engaging in any licensed activity, for single agency** | A **written agency agreement** disclosing the § 12-10-404 or § 12-10-405 duties | **§ 12-10-408(2)(b)** |
| **Working with a buyer as the seller's agent** | Written disclosure: broker is the **seller's** agent, **a list of the tasks** the agent will perform with the buyer, and that the buyer is **not vicariously liable** | **§ 12-10-408(2)(c)(I)** |
| **In any written agreement** | That the relationship **exists only with the designated broker** and **does not extend to the employing broker or the firm** | **§ 12-10-408(2)(e)(I)** |
| **At the earliest reasonable opportunity** | Advising other potential parties or their agents of an established relationship | **§ 12-10-408(2)(d)** |
| **On changing from single agent for one to transaction-broker for both** | The Commission-approved **"Change of Status"** form, **at the time the broker begins to assist both** and **not later than when the consumer signs the contract** | **Rule 6.9** |
| **Continuously** | Any **known conflict of interest**, in writing; and that the broker is licensed if buying, selling or leasing **on their own account** | **Rule 6.17.A and B** |

**And the default that stands behind all of it:** **§ 12-10-403(2)** — a broker is a **transaction-broker**
unless single agency is established **in writing**.

### A.2 Approved commission forms — II.A.2

> **§ 12-10-403(4)(c)**: *"**A broker shall use a COMMISSION-APPROVED FORM when such a form exists and is
> appropriate for the transaction.**"*
>
> **Rule 7.1.A**: a Commission-approved form is one *"promulgated by the Commission **for CURRENT USE** by
> Brokers"*; the broker **may advise the parties as to the effects thereof**; and *"**To obtain the forms
> promulgated by the Commission, visit the Division's website.**"*
>
> **§ 12-10-403(4)(c)**, closing sentence: the broker *"**shall advise the parties that the forms have
> important legal consequences and that the parties should consult legal counsel before signing.**"*

**"For current use" means a superseded form is not a Commission-approved form.**

> **NO Commission-approved form was obtained, read or reproduced by this project**, and **no module of this
> build states any form's contents, version, date or provisions.** It is the largest submission gate in the
> Colorado build.

### A.3 Employing brokers and employed licensees — II.A.3

**Rule 6.3** in full, delivered in Module 01 Part A.4: supervision over associate brokers **and all
unlicensed employees**; the employing broker's four responsibilities under **6.3.B**; and the sentence a new
broker should hold onto — *"**whenever a complaint is filed with the Commission against an Associate Broker,
the Commission may investigate whether there have been violations ... by the EMPLOYING BROKER.**"*

**And § 12-10-403(6)(b) with its savings clause.** The **relationship** does not extend to the employing
broker or the firm — but **§ 12-10-408(2)(e)(II)**: *"**Nothing ... shall be construed to limit the
employing broker's or firm's responsibility to supervise licensees ... nor to shield the broker or firm from
vicarious liability.**"*

### A.4 Listing and selling brokers — II.A.4

> **Rule 6.13**: *"A Broker **must present all offers to the OTHER CONSUMER'S BROKER** if such other Consumer
> has an unexpired Listing Contract. If the Broker has made **reasonable, but unsuccessful, attempts** to
> present an offer to the other Consumer's Broker, the Broker **must present the offer to the other
> Consumer's Broker's EMPLOYING BROKER.** If **no Employing Broker exists**, or if reasonable attempts to
> present the offer to the Employing Broker have failed, **the Broker MAY present the offer directly to the
> other Consumer.**"*

**A three-step ladder, and the rule states no time period.** **No module of this build supplies one.**

**And the duty on the receiving side.** **§ 12-10-404(1)(c)(II)** requires a seller's agent to **present all
offers to and from the seller in a timely manner REGARDLESS of whether the property is subject to a contract
for sale** — while **(c)(I)** says the broker **is not obligated to SEEK additional offers** once under
contract. **Not seeking and not presenting are different acts.**

**Compensation between brokers** is governed by **§ 12-10-410**: it may be paid by any party or shared
between brokers; **payment does not establish an agency relationship**; a **buyer's agent must obtain the
buyer's WRITTEN APPROVAL before proposing** to be compensated from the listing side (**(5)**); and the
**identity of anyone paying compensation must be disclosed before the brokerage agreement or contract is
entered into** (**(6)**).

## B. Specialties and competency requirements — outline Topic II.B

### B.1 Current market trends for selling and buying — II.B.1

**Named here and given its own three hours at Topic VII**, which **Module 07** delivers. The outline leaves
market content to the provider's discretion precisely because it changes.

### B.2 Investors — II.B.2

**A residential broker working with an investor is working outside ordinary residential competency**, and
this is where **Rule 6.2** first bites for most new brokers.

**What the work requires that a primary-residence transaction does not:** **income and expense analysis** —
potential gross income, vacancy and collection loss, effective gross income, operating expenses, net
operating income, and capitalisation, all delivered in **Real Estate Law and Practice Module 02 Part C.2 and
D**; **an understanding of leases in place** and what the buyer is inheriting; **tax consequences** the
broker does **not** advise on; and, where the property is tenanted, **everything in Module 03 of this
course.**

**The routing when a broker does not have it:** **Rule 6.2.B** — **decline**, **obtain**, **obtain
assistance**, or **co-list.**

### B.3 Miscellaneous — II.B.3

The outline's own catch-all. **This build states nothing under it** beyond the general point that a
residential broker meets transactions that are not ordinary — new construction, relocation, estates,
divorce, short sales, foreclosures — **and that each is a competency question under Rule 6.2 before it is
anything else.**

**Two of those reach law this build did not read.** **Colorado's foreclosure statutes and public trustee
system were NOT read**, and **its entity, trust and probate statutes were NOT read** — so **no procedure,
period, notice, authority rule or requirement from any of them is stated anywhere in this build.**

### B.4 The advertising rules a residential broker works inside daily — Rule 6.10

**Read in full for this build**, and this is where it belongs in the course.

> **A. Names.**
> **A.1** No broker may be licensed under **more than one brokerage firm** (§ 12-10-203(9)).
> **A.2** No broker or firm may conduct or promote brokerage services **except in the name under which they
> appear in the Commission's records.**
> **A.3** Brokers **will not advertise so as to mislead the public** concerning the identity of the broker or
> the firm.
> **A.4** *"**All Advertising must be done clearly and conspicuously IN THE NAME OF THE BROKER'S BROKERAGE
> FIRM.**"* — **with one exception:** a broker advertising **real property owned by the broker which is NOT
> listed with the broker's firm** is **exempt** from advertising it in the firm's name.
> **A.5** A firm may use a **Trade Name**, which **must be filed with the Commission.**
> **A.6** A firm may use a **Trademark** with the owner's permission — and a firm using a third party's trade
> name or trademark **must use one of two statements, clearly and conspicuously**: *"**Each (insert general
> Trade Name) brokerage business is independently owned and operated**"* or *"**Each office independently
> owned and operated.**"*
> **A.7** **No firm may use more than one Trade Name**; more than one Trademark requires written request and
> Commission consent.
> **A.8** **No professional designation** in advertising unless the broker is **in good standing** and the
> designation is **easily verifiable by the public and the Commission** — and a broker advertising an
> **award, membership or achievement** must be able to **provide verification on request** from any member
> of the public or the Commission.
>
> **B. Teams.**
> **B.1** Team advertising **must not mislead the public as to the identity of the team's brokerage firm**,
> and a team name **may not use**: *"**Realty, Real estate, Realtors, Company, Corporation, Corp., Inc.,
> LLC, LP or LLP, or any other term that would imply a separate entity from the Brokerage Firm.**"*
> **B.2** All team advertising **must clearly and conspicuously include and be in conjunction with the legal
> name or trade name of the brokerage firm.**
> **B.3** On request by a consumer, the Commission, another firm or broker, **the firm will provide the names
> of the brokers belonging to any team.**
> **B.4** Brokers **may not allow use of the team's name by brokers outside the team's firm.**
>
> **C. Accuracy.** Firms and brokers are **responsible for ensuring all advertising is accurate and complies
> with copyright laws and other applicable laws** — *"including ... **the accuracy of information entered
> into Multiple Listing Services** regarding property data, sales information, and the identification of
> Brokers who participated."* And: *"**The Broker does not have a duty to investigate the information
> provided to the broker by the seller or obtained from a third party source (e.g. assessor records,
> building plans).**"*
>
> **D. Electronic Media.**
> **D.1** Where a broker owns or controls electronic media, **EACH VIEWABLE PAGE must include the brokerage
> firm's name**, and **expired listings must be removed within THREE (3) DAYS of a listing contract
> expiring.**
> **D.2** Where a **third party** is authorised for the broker's electronic media advertising, the broker is
> responsible for the accuracy of the information supplied — and **must submit a WRITTEN REQUEST to any
> third party syndicators to remove expired listings within three days.**
> **D.3** A broker communicating by **email, chat, instant messages, newsgroups, discussion lists, bulletin
> boards, blogs or similar means** for advertising purposes **must use the firm's name** — though **once the
> firm has been disclosed to a specific consumer, the broker need not repeat it to that consumer.**
> **D.4** Where space is limited and disclosure is **not reasonable**, the firm's name **must be disclosed
> clearly and conspicuously WITHIN THE FIRST CLICK OF THE MOUSE.**
>
> **E. Past Sales Data.** General sales data advertising recapping activity over a period in a subdivision
> or area **must cite the source** and **include a disclaimer, if accurate**, that reported sales **were not
> necessarily listed or sold by the broker** and **are intended only to show trends** or **will separately
> identify the broker's own sales activity.**
>
> **F. Authority to advertise.** *"**Brokers may not Advertise the availability or price of a property
> whether for sale or lease WITHOUT AUTHORITY FROM THE OWNER OR THE OWNER'S BROKER.**"* An owner's broker
> **may not withhold** that authority unless doing so is **contradictory to the owner's instructions as
> memorialized in the listing contract or other writing.** A broker disseminating **another broker's**
> advertising needs that **broker's written permission**, must **disclose the owner's firm conspicuously**,
> and must be accurate; a broker disseminating an **unrepresented owner's** advertising needs the **owner's
> written permission**, must **disclose conspicuously that the owner is not represented**, and **may not
> submit the information into a property exchange or multiple listing service.**
>
> **G. Price set by owner.** *"**The price quoted in any Advertising will not be anything other than the
> price agreed upon between the Broker and the owner.**"*

**Three of those a new residential broker breaks first:** advertising a property **without the owner's or
owner's broker's authority** (F); a **team name** implying a separate entity (B.1); and **failing to remove
an expired listing within three days** (D.1 and D.2).

## C. Third party providers — outline Topic II.C

| Provider | What a broker does | What a broker does not do |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Title companies** | Deliver the commitment promptly; ensure each **Schedule B-1 requirement** has an owner and a date; confirm the objection deadline | Opine on the **legal effect** of an exception, or advise whether a defect is material |
| **2. Home inspection companies** | Ensure the inspection deadline is known and met; ensure the **Inspection Notice** goes out in time | **Assess the property** — **Rule 6.2** requires competency, and condition assessment is not a broker's field |
| **3. Mortgage companies** | Do arithmetic with figures the lender supplies; track the loan deadlines | **Advise which product to take**, or predict approval |
| **4. Specialty vendors** — *"structural engineer, electricians, industrial hygienists, etc."* | Provide more than one name where possible; let the client choose | **Recommend as guaranteed**, or take undisclosed value for the referral |

**Two Colorado rules govern what a broker may receive for sending work to any of them, and both were read.**

> **Rule 6.21.A**: *"Brokers and Brokerage Firms **will not pay or receive a referral fee EXCEPT IN
> ACCORDANCE WITH RESPA** and unless a **reasonable cause for payment** of the referral fee exists pursuant
> to section 12-10-304(1), C.R.S."*
>
> **Rule 5.17**: the broker and firm *"**must obtain PRIOR WRITTEN CONSENT from the Consumer they represent
> to assess and receive mark-ups and/or other compensation for services performed, REGARDLESS IF FOR THE
> BENEFIT OF THE BROKER OR ANOTHER THIRD PARTY**"*, must **disclose all mark-ups paid for by any Customer**,
> and must **retain accurate on-going records** verifying disclosure and consent.
>
> **Rule 6.18** requires **affiliated business arrangement disclosures**; **Rule 1.3** defines the term by
> reference to **§ 12-10-218**.
>
> **Neither RESPA, nor § 12-10-304, nor § 12-10-218 was read by this project**, and **no requirement,
> threshold, exemption or penalty from any of them is stated anywhere in this build.**

**And Rule 6.17.C for anyone doing property management:** a **continuing duty to disclose in writing any
known conflict of interest in the selection or use of a business or vendor** — including **any ownership,
financial, or familial interest.** Module 03 of this course develops it.

## D. Comparative Market Analysis and Broker Price Opinions — outline Topic II.D

**The rule was read in full, and it prescribes the words.**

> **Rule 6.12**: *"When a Broker prepares a CMA or BPO **for any reason other than the anticipated sale or
> purchase of the property**, the Broker **must include a notice stating**:*
>
> ***"'This evaluation was prepared by a licensed real estate broker and is not an appraisal. This evaluation
> cannot be used for the purposes of obtaining financing.'***
>
> *"Pursuant to section 12-10-602(9)(b)(II), C.R.S, **Brokers are PROHIBITED from completing CMAs or BPOs
> that are used for the purpose of obtaining financing.** Preparation of CMAs or BPOs for reasons other than
> anticipated sale, purchase, or lease **is not considered Real Estate Brokerage Services.** As such, any
> compensation received for such preparation **is not required to be paid to the Broker's Brokerage Firm
> unless stated otherwise in Brokerage Firm's Office Policy Manual.**"*

**Four things, and Module 02 of the Real Estate Law and Practice course works through them.** The trigger;
the prescribed words; the **prohibition** on CMAs or BPOs used to obtain financing; and the compensation
consequence — **check the office policy manual.**

**And the square footage rule a residential broker meets on every listing.**

> **Rule 6.11**: when a broker **advertises the square footage** of a residential property, **including for
> submission to a multiple listing service**, the broker **must disclose the SOURCE ... on the
> COMMISSION-APPROVED FORM.**
>
> **A. Broker measurement.** *"**A Broker is not required to measure** ... If the Broker takes an actual
> measurement, **it does not have to be exact**; however, the Broker's objective **must be to measure
> accurately and calculate competently in a manner that is not misleading**"* — and the broker must
> **disclose the standard, methodology or manner used**; **advise it is for marketing and not for loan,
> valuation or any other purpose**; and **advise that if exact square footage is a concern the property
> should be independently measured.**
>
> **B. Other sources.** Any other source — actual measurement, building plans, prior appraisals, assessor's
> office — **must include the date of issuance, if any**, and be **disclosed in writing in a timely manner on
> the Commission-Approved Form.** *"A Broker **may not provide information to a person from a source known to
> be unreliable** and **is responsible for indicating obvious mismeasurement by others.**"*

**Read Rule 6.11.B's last sentence against Rule 6.10.C's.** **6.10.C** says the broker has **no duty to
investigate** information from the seller or a third-party source. **6.11.B** says the broker **may not use
a source known to be unreliable** and **is responsible for indicating obvious mismeasurement.** **No duty to
investigate is not the same as permission to pass on what you can see is wrong.**

**No Commission-approved square footage form was obtained by this project**, and **no form's contents are
stated anywhere in this build.**
