## A. Broker relationships and overview of practice — outline Topic III.A

**Property management in Colorado is a brokerage relationship like any other.** **§§ 12-10-404 and
12-10-405** speak of *"seller **or landlord**"* and *"buyer **or tenant**"* throughout, so the duty lists run
to a management relationship in their own terms — and **§ 12-10-403(2)** applies: a broker is a
**transaction-broker unless single agency is established through a WRITTEN agreement.**

**What that means at the outset.** A property manager engaged by an owner who signs a management agreement
establishing single agency is the **landlord's agent** under § 12-10-404. One who signs nothing establishing
agency is a **transaction-broker** — **not an agent for either party**, under § 12-10-407(1).

**And the disclosure timing is the same:** **Rule 6.5.A** requires the written § 12-10-408 disclosure
**before eliciting or discussing confidential information**, which in a management context arrives early —
the moment the conversation moves from *"what do units like this rent for"* to the owner's finances or
motives.

**Two structural points a residential broker moving into management has to absorb.**

**First, the money is continuous.** Rent arrives monthly, deposits are held for years, and the trust account
is never empty. **Every rule in Chapter 5 is live all the time**, not just around a closing.

**Second, there are two customers.** The **owner** is the client. The **tenant** is not — but the tenant's
deposit is the tenant's money, and **Rule 5.8** protects it specifically.

## B. Overview of the management agreement — outline Topic III.B

**What it must settle**, delivered at greater length in **Real Estate Law and Practice Module 05 Part D.1**:

1. **Parties**, and the property managed.
2. **Term**, and how it ends.
3. **The manager's authority** — including a **dollar threshold** above which repairs need the owner's
   approval.
4. **Compensation** — how calculated, when taken, out of what.
5. **Trust accounting** — which accounts, and how reported.
6. **Owner reserves** — how much, what for, and when replenished.
7. **Reporting** — what reports, and **when**.
8. **Leasing authority** — the terms the manager may accept.
9. **Maintenance and vendors** — who selects, who pays, and **what disclosures**.
10. **Insurance and indemnity.**
11. **Termination** — including **what happens to funds and documents.**

**Three of those eleven are settled by rule if the agreement is silent**, and a manager should know which:

> **Item 5 — accounting method.** **Rule 5.16**: *"In the absence of a written agreement to the contrary, the
> **'cash basis' of accounting MUST BE USED**"*; another method requires **written agreement with the
> beneficiary** and **separate accounts and records** for that beneficiary.
>
> **Item 7 — reporting.** **Rule 5.15.A**: reports go out **as the management agreement provides**, and *"in
> the absence of a provision in the Management Agreement to the contrary ... **WITHIN THIRTY (30) DAYS after
> the end of the month in which funds were either received or disbursed.**"*
>
> **Item 11 — deposits on termination.** **Rule 5.8.A**: a firm receipting for security deposits *"**will not
> deliver such security deposits to an owner without the tenant's written authorization in a lease or unless
> written notice has been given to the tenant**"* — telling the tenant **who is holding the deposit** and
> **the specific requirements for the procedure in which the tenant may request return.** If the deposit
> does go to the owner, the management agreement **should** place financial responsibility for its return on
> the owner and **must** authorize disclosure of the owner's **true name and current mailing address** in a
> dispute.

> **A gate stated here because a manager meets it on day one.** **C.R.S. § 38-12-101 et seq., the Colorado
> Landlord Tenant Act, was NOT read by this project.** **This course states NO rule on**: security deposit
> limits; deposit return periods or the consequences of wrongful withholding; notice to enter; habitability;
> late fees; repair and deduct; retaliation; lease termination notice; eviction or unlawful detainer
> procedure; or any tenant remedy.
>
> **Any figure or period a manager writes into a lease or a management agreement comes from the statute, the
> firm's forms, and counsel — not from this course.**

## C. When the employing broker has to be involved — outline Topic III.C

### C.1 Office policy — III.C.1

**Rule 6.3.B.3** requires the employing broker to **develop the firm's written policies** under **Rule 6.4**.
**Rule 5.1** requires **written accounting control policies and procedures** with **checks and balances over
the financial activities of the broker, the firm and unlicensed persons**, managing the **risk of fraud or
illegal acts.**

**In a management operation the policy has to answer questions a sales-only firm never asks:** who may sign
on the trust accounts; who reconciles them; what a manager may spend without the owner's approval; how
vendors are selected and how conflicts are disclosed; how deposits are handled on move-out; and what happens
when a ledger is about to go negative.

### C.2 Trust accounts — III.C.2

**This is the part of the outline where the employing broker's involvement is not optional.**

> **Rule 5.3.C**: the **Employing Broker or Independent Broker** are *"**responsible for, must maintain and
> be able to withdraw money from each separate account**"*, and may authorise cosigners — *"**However, such
> authorization will not relieve the Employing Broker or Independent Broker of any responsibility.**"*
>
> **Rule 5.22**: *"The **Employing Broker or Independent Broker are held JOINTLY RESPONSIBLE with the
> Brokerage Firm** in complying with Chapter 5 of these Rules."*
>
> **Rule 6.3.B.1**: employing brokers must **maintain all trust and escrow accounts and records as set forth
> in Chapter 5.**
>
> **Record Keeping outline, Topic III.A.2**: *"**Broker's need to personally perform bank reconciliations or
> periodically compare reconciliations against source documents (transaction files).**"*

**The property management rules specifically:**

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **5.5** | **Separate** trust or escrow accounts — **a minimum of one for rental receipts and a minimum of one for security deposits** |
| **5.7.A** | Property management money deposited **no later than FIVE BUSINESS DAYS** after receipt **or mutual execution of a lease, whichever is later** — where **5.7.B** gives all other money **three** |
| **5.9** | Money belonging to **one beneficiary must not be used for another's**, or for the firm |
| **5.10.A** | Money **due and payable to the firm** must be **withdrawn MONTHLY** |
| **5.10.C** | Fees are *"earned"* and available **only after all contracted services are performed and there is no remaining right of recall** |
| **5.14.B** | *"**No ledger may ever be allowed to have a negative cash balance**"*, and the **sum of ledger balances must agree with the journal AT ALL TIMES** after each posting |
| **5.14.C** | **Three-way reconciliation MONTHLY**, with a report **completed and maintained** |
| **5.11** | Money for **non-brokerage** activities — **guest deposits for short-term rentals**, **deposits on the broker's own rentals**, **buyer deposits where the broker is acting as a builder** — **still goes into a trust or escrow account** |
| **5.10.D and E** | Rental proceeds on a **broker's own property**, with the **20 percent** threshold in **§ 12-10-201(6)(b)(VII)** deciding what counts as the broker's |

**The Record Keeping and Trust Accounts course delivers all of these**, and its Commission outline requires
an exercise in which the student *"shall **reconcile the bank accounts for a property manager managing and
holding security deposits for a MINIMUM OF 8 PROPERTIES.**"*

## D. Management reports — outline Topic III.D

> **Rule 5.15.A**: firms holding Money Belonging to Others *"**must provide DETAILED reports to each
> beneficiary.**"* Reports are prepared and delivered **according to the management agreement**; absent a
> contrary provision, **within 30 days after the end of the month** in which funds were received or
> disbursed.
>
> **Rule 5.15.B**: the firm **must maintain supporting records accurately detailing all cash received and
> disbursed** under any management agreement —
> **1.** every **deposit must identify each person tendering funds, the amount, the types of funds received
> from each person, and the PROPERTY ADDRESS affected**; and
> **2.** every **disbursement must be supported by documents such as bids, invoices, contracts, etc.**, with
> ledger and journal records **identifying the payees, property addresses affected and amount transferred
> for each property.**

**A report that says "maintenance — $840" and cannot be traced to an invoice does not satisfy 5.15.B.2.**
Every disbursement has a document behind it, and the property address is on both sides of the record.

**And Rule 5.10.C's schedule requirement** where a single disbursement of fees or commissions covers more
than one transaction, rental period, occupancy or beneficiary: the firm must, **on request**, produce a
schedule detailing the **individual components** and **identifying the affected beneficiary or property
ledgers.**

## E. Specialties — outline Topic III.E

The outline names **residential**, **office**, and **commercial properties**, with the competency footnote
attached.

| Specialty | What differs |
|---|---|
| **Residential** | Tenancies governed by Colorado's landlord-tenant statutes — **NOT read by this project**; deposits held per tenant; fair housing exposure in screening; turnover-driven |
| **Office** | Longer leases; **net** structures with expense pass-throughs; tenant improvement allowances; common area maintenance reconciliation |
| **Commercial** | The above, plus percentage rent, use and exclusivity clauses, co-tenancy, and the buyer's or tenant's own due diligence |

**The competency point the Commission is making with this list:** the three are **not interchangeable**. A
manager competent in residential turnover is not thereby competent to reconcile CAM on an office building
or to administer a percentage rent clause — and **Rule 6.2.B** gives the four routes.

**And one commercial-specific relief in the duty rules is worth knowing**, though it belongs to Module 04:
**§ 12-10-405** and the commercial exception in other states have no Colorado analogue read for this build.
**Colorado's own statute states no commercial carve-out from the brokerage relationship requirements in any
provision read for this build**, and **none is stated here.**

**What the statute does say, and it was read:** **§§ 12-10-404(1)(e), 12-10-405(1)(e) and 12-10-407(2)(d)**
each require the broker to comply with applicable federal, state and local law *"**including fair housing
and civil rights statutes or regulations**"* — so a fair housing failure in tenant screening is **also** a
brokerage relationship violation, and **§ 12-10-411** makes any Part 4 violation an act the Commission may
investigate.

**The screening discipline that follows:** **one written set of criteria, applied to every applicant in the
same order, with the result recorded.**

## G. Pitfalls — outline Topic III.G

The outline names the topic and leaves it to the provider. **These are the pitfalls the rules read for this
build actually describe**, each with the provision that makes it one.

| Pitfall | The rule |
|---|---|
| **A negative ledger, even for a day** | **Rule 5.14.B** — *"no ledger may ever be allowed to have a negative cash balance"*; and **Rule 5.9**, because the shortfall is another beneficiary's money |
| **Rental receipts and security deposits in one account** | **Rule 5.5** — a minimum of one account for each |
| **Missing the five-business-day deposit deadline** | **Rule 5.7.A** — and the journal's **two dates** (received and deposited) are what prove it either way |
| **Taking a fee before it is earned** | **Rule 5.10.C** — *"only after all contracted services have been performed and there is no remaining right of recall"* |
| **Leaving an earned fee in the trust account past month end** | **Rule 5.10.A** — **withdrawn monthly**; commingling in the other direction |
| **Handing an owner the tenant's deposit** | **Rule 5.8.A** — not without the **tenant's written authorization in a lease or written notice** |
| **Taking over a portfolio without checking the deposits** | **Rule 5.8.B** — **30 days** to disclose, **60 days** for the previous firm to transfer, and the new firm **verifies each against the current lease and discloses any discrepancy** |
| **Sending maintenance to a company the manager has an interest in, silently** | **Rule 6.17.C** — a duty to **disclose in writing any known conflict of interest in the selection or use of a business or vendor**, including **any ownership, financial, or familial interest** |
| **Marking up a repair without consent** | **Rule 5.17** — **prior written consent** from the consumer represented, **disclosure** of mark-ups paid by any customer, and **records verifying both** |
| **A report the owner cannot trace to invoices** | **Rule 5.15.B.2** — disbursements **supported by documents** |
| **Managing the broker's own property in the same accounts** | **Rules 5.10.D and E**, with the **20 percent** threshold |
| **No written accounting controls** | **Rule 5.1** — written, reaching **unlicensed persons**, managing the **risk of fraud** |
| **Advertising a unit without authority** | **Rule 6.10.F** — *"**Brokers may not Advertise the availability or price of a property whether for sale or lease without authority from the owner or the owner's Broker.**"* |
| **Quoting a price the owner did not agree** | **Rule 6.10.G** — *"the price quoted in any Advertising will not be anything other than the price agreed upon between the Broker and the owner"* |
| **An expired listing left up** | **Rule 6.10.D.1 and D.2** — removed within **three days**, including a **written request to third-party syndicators** |

**Fourteen pitfalls, every one of them stateable from rules this project read in full** — and **not one of
them requiring the landlord-tenant statute.** That is the honest shape of what this course can teach: **the
money and the disclosure rules completely, and the tenancy law not at all.**
