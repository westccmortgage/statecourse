## Learning objectives

1. Distinguish a broker prepared closing from a title company prepared closing.
2. State Rule 6.19's allocation of closing responsibility and who signs the closing statement.
3. State what a broker must do if unavailable to attend closing.
4. State the Good Funds rule in Rule 6.19.E and what it prohibits.
5. State what the Commission's outline names about RESPA and withholding, and identify both as unread.
6. State the individual responsibility of the licensee holding the listing or buyer brokerage agreement.

## A. Who closes, and who is responsible — outline Topic I.A

The Commission's outline distinguishes three things at I.A, and Colorado's rule allocates responsibility
across all of them.

| Outline sub-item | What it is |
|---|---|
| **A.1 Broker prepared closing** | The broker prepares the closing documents and closing statements and conducts the closing |
| **A.2 Title company prepared closing** | A title company or other closing entity prepares and conducts it |
| **A.3 Individual responsibility of licensee holding listing or buyer brokerage agreement** | The duty that attaches to the broker with the **brokerage relationship**, whoever conducts the closing |

**Rule 6.19 was read in full and it settles all three.** It is the operative rule of this entire course.

### A.1 The core allocation — Rule 6.19.A

> *"Pursuant to section 12-10-217(1)(j), C.R.S, **at the time of closing, the Broker who has established a
> Brokerage Relationship with one or multiple Consumers in a transaction WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR THE PROPER
> CLOSING OF THE TRANSACTION.** The Broker **must ensure such Consumer receives an ACCURATE, COMPLETE AND
> DETAILED closing statement THAT IS SIGNED BY THE BROKER.** If Broker is licensed with a Brokerage Firm,
> Broker **must deliver closing statements to the Brokerage Firm along with any other closing documents,
> IMMEDIATELY FOLLOWING CLOSING.** Nothing in this Rule **relieves an Employing Broker of the responsibility
> for fulfilling supervisory responsibilities** pursuant to sections 12-10-203(5)(c)(I), 12-10-217(1)(r),
> and 12-10-222, C.R.S."*

**Four separate requirements in one paragraph, and this is the outline's Topic I.A.3 in the rule's own
words:**

1. **The broker with the brokerage relationship is responsible for the proper closing** — **whoever
   conducts it.** Handing the file to a title company does not transfer this.
2. **The consumer must receive a closing statement that is accurate, complete and detailed — and SIGNED BY
   THE BROKER.**
3. **The closing statements and other closing documents go to the brokerage firm IMMEDIATELY FOLLOWING
   CLOSING.**
4. **The employing broker's supervisory responsibility is untouched.**

**Point 2 is the one new brokers do not expect.** The closing statement carries **the broker's own
signature**, and a broker who signs an inaccurate one has certified it.

### A.2 Broker prepared closings — Rule 6.19.B

> *"If closing documents and closing statements are **prepared and closed by a Broker**, the Broker is
> **responsible for the ACCURACY and COMPLETENESS of the closing statements and closing documents.**"*

**A heavier standard than 6.19.A, and it applies when the broker does the work.** Modules 02 through 05
build the skill this rule presupposes.

### A.3 Attendance, and the substitute — Rule 6.19.C

> *"If a Broker has a Brokerage Relationship with a Consumer in a transaction, the Broker **must review
> closing documents AND attend closing OR BE REASONABLY AVAILABLE.** If a Broker will not be available to
> attend closing and review closing documents, **another Broker designated by the Brokerage Firm may review
> and attend closing on the Broker's behalf** and **will assume JOINT RESPONSIBILITY with the absent Broker**
> for its accuracy, completeness, and delivery of the signed closing statement."*

**"Attend closing or be reasonably available" is a real alternative**, and *"reasonably available"* is not
defined by the rule. **This course supplies no definition**, because the rule states none.

**The substitute takes on joint responsibility, and does not relieve the absent broker.** Both are
responsible.

### A.4 Earnest money at closing — Rule 6.19.D

> *"**Any Broker receiving earnest money must deliver earnest money as set forth in Rule 5.12.A.**"*

**Rule 5.12.A**: deliver it **to the earnest money holder to be deposited in accordance with the
contract**, and **obtain a dated and signed receipt** from the person or entity instructed to receive it.

## B. Real Estate Commission statutes and rules — outline Topic I.B

The rules a broker works inside at closing, all read in full for this build.

| Rule | What it requires at closing |
|---|---|
| **6.19** | Responsibility, the signed closing statement, attendance, and Good Funds — Parts A and D of this module |
| **6.20** | **Transaction files retained FOUR YEARS** from consummation or from a non-consummating listing's expiration |
| **5.12** | Earnest money delivered to the holder per the contract, **with a dated and signed receipt** |
| **5.7.B** | If the firm holds it: deposited **no later than three business days** following receipt or mutual execution, whichever is later |
| **5.14** | The **journal**, the **ledger** with **no negative balance ever**, and **monthly three-way reconciliation** |
| **5.10.C** | Commissions are *"earned"* and available to the firm **only after all contracted services have been performed and there is no remaining right of recall** |
| **5.17** | **Prior written consent** for any **mark-up** the broker assesses or receives, and disclosure of mark-ups paid by any customer |
| **§ 12-10-410(6)** | The **identity of anyone paying compensation** disclosed **before** the brokerage agreement or the contract is entered into |
| **6.21.A** | **No referral fee except in accordance with RESPA** and unless a **reasonable cause** exists under § 12-10-304(1) |

**Rule 6.20's four years runs from two possible dates** — **consummation** for a transaction that closes,
and **the listing contract's expiration** for one that does not. And the rule expressly does **not** require
a broker to obtain and retain **existing public records, title commitments, loan applications, lender
required disclosures, or related affirmations from independent third party closing entities after the
closing date.**

**The Commission's Transaction File Checklist, which Rule 6.20 says designates the required documents, was
NOT obtained by this project**, and **no module of this build states what it lists.** That is a submission
gate.

## C. RESPA — outline Topic I.C

The Commission's outline names the **Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act** at Topic I.C.

**What it is, in general terms:** the federal statute governing **settlement services** on covered
residential transactions — the disclosures required, the prohibition on **kickbacks and unearned fees**, and
limits on **required use** of affiliated providers.

> **The RESPA text was NOT read by this project.** **No RESPA disclosure, form, timing requirement, dollar
> threshold, exemption, tolerance or penalty is stated anywhere in this build.**
>
> **What WAS read is Colorado's rule about it.** **Rule 6.21.A**: *"Brokers and Brokerage Firms **will not
> pay or receive a referral fee except in accordance with RESPA** and unless a **reasonable cause for
> payment of the referral fee exists** pursuant to section 12-10-304(1), C.R.S."* — and **§ 12-10-304 was
> not read either.**
>
> **Rule 6.18** requires **affiliated business arrangement disclosures**; its statutory basis,
> **§ 12-10-218**, was **not read**, and **Rule 1.3** defines the term by reference to it.
>
> **This is a submission gate.** A Closings course that teaches RESPA must read it.

## D. Withholding — outline Topic I.D

The Commission's outline names two withholding regimes:

| Outline sub-item | What it concerns |
|---|---|
| **D.1 Non-resident seller** | Withholding from the proceeds of a seller who is not a Colorado resident |
| **D.2 Foreign Investor Real Property Tax Act** | Federal withholding on dispositions of U.S. real property by foreign persons |

> **NEITHER statute was read by this project.** **No withholding rate, threshold, exemption, certificate,
> form, filing deadline or remittance requirement is stated anywhere in this build.**
>
> **What this module states is the practical point that does not depend on the figures:** withholding is
> **taken out of the seller's proceeds at closing**, so a seller who learns of it at the closing table
> learns of it too late. **A broker who knows the seller is a non-resident or a foreign person raises it
> early and routes it to the closing entity, the seller's accountant and counsel** —
> **§§ 12-10-404(1)(c)(V) and 12-10-407(2)(b)(II)** each require advising the party to obtain expert advice
> on material matters beyond the broker's expertise, and this is one.
>
> **This is a submission gate.**

## E. Good Funds — outline Topic I.E, and Rule 6.19.E

The outline names **Good Funds** at Topic I.E, and the rule was read in full.

> **Rule 6.19.E**: *"Pursuant to section **38-35-125, C.R.S**, a Broker or a Brokerage Firm **MUST NOT
> DISBURSE OR AUTHORIZE DISBURSEMENT OF FUNDS UNTIL THOSE FUNDS HAVE BEEN RECEIVED** and are either:*
> **1.** *"**Available for immediate withdrawal AS A MATTER OF RIGHT** from the financial institution in
> which the funds have been deposited; or*
> **2.** *"**Available for immediate withdrawal as a consequence of an agreement** with a financial
> institution in which the funds are to be deposited or a financial institution upon which the funds are to
> be drawn. **The agreement ... must be for the benefit of the Broker and Brokerage Firm providing the
> closing service. If the agreement contains contingencies or reservations, NO DISBURSEMENTS CAN BE MADE
> until these are satisfied.**"*

**Read what the rule prohibits: disbursing OR AUTHORIZING disbursement before the funds are both received
and available.** A broker who releases funds against a deposit that has not cleared, or against an
agreement whose contingencies are unsatisfied, has breached it.

**"As a matter of right" in paragraph 1** means the broker can withdraw the money now, not that the bank
will probably honour it.

**§ 38-35-125 itself was NOT read by this project.** This module states **only what Rule 6.19.E says**, in
the rule's own words, and **states no definition of good funds, no clearing period, and no list of
acceptable instruments from the statute.**

**The Record Keeping and Trust Accounts outline names Good Funds at its Topic III.B.4** and lists the
arrangements a broker meets in practice: **cashier's or teller's check; broker check in advance of closing;
wire transfer; tri-party agreements (broker–title company–broker's bank); netting earned commission against
earnest money deposit; and the requirements of the Great Colorado Payback Program for unclaimed funds.**
That course delivers them.
