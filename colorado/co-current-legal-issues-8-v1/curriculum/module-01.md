## Learning objectives

1. State where Colorado's real estate rules live and how a broker finds the current version.
2. Name the nine chapters of 4 CCR 725-1 and what each governs.
3. State what a Commission position statement is and why this course states none.
4. State what an instructive disciplinary proceeding is and what a broker learns from one.
5. State the rules a broker must check for currency, and how.
6. State the audit and production obligations that make currency enforceable.

## A. Where the rules live, and how a broker finds them

### A.1 The two bodies of authority

| Source | Where it is published |
|---|---|
| **C.R.S. Title 12, Article 10** — the statute | The Colorado Revised Statutes, published by the legislature |
| **4 CCR 725-1 — Rules Regarding Real Estate Brokers** | The **Code of Colorado Regulations**, published by the **Secretary of State** |

**Rules change, and they change by rulemaking.** The Secretary of State's CCR carries **every version** of
4 CCR 725-1 with the rulemaking docket behind it, so a broker can see **what changed, when, and under which
proceeding.**

**The version read for this build** is rule version **12355**, 89 pages, read on **2026-08-20**. **A broker
reading this course later must check whether it is still current**, and the readings file names the version
precisely so that check is possible.

### A.2 The nine chapters

| Chapter | Subject |
|---|---|
| **1** | Definitions |
| **2** | **Licensure Requirements** — including Rule 2.1, the **168 hours** |
| **3** | Licensure, Renewal, License Status, and **Insurance** |
| **4** | Continuing Education Requirement |
| **5** | **Separate Accounts and Accounting** |
| **6** | **Practice Standards** |
| **7** | **Use of Standard Forms** |
| **8** | Declaratory Orders |
| **9** | Commission Review of Initial Decisions and Exceptions |

**Chapters 5, 6 and 7 are where a practising broker spends their time**, and this project's courses deliver
them: **Chapter 5** in the **Record Keeping and Trust Accounts** course; **Chapters 6 and 7** across
**Contracts and Regulations**, **Closings** and **Practical Applications**.

**Chapters 8 and 9 were read but are not taught in this build.** A **declaratory order** proceeding and the
**Commission's review of initial decisions and exceptions** are procedural matters that arise after
something has gone wrong, and **no procedure, deadline, filing requirement or standard of review from either
chapter is stated anywhere in this build.**

## B. Position statements — outline Topic 1, and the second gate

**A Commission position statement states the Commission's view on a question the statute and rules do not
answer on their face.** The Commission publishes them, and its own course outlines cite them:

- The **Record Keeping and Trust Accounts** outline names *"**Position Statements**"* at its Topic I.D.2,
  among the Commission rules and regulations a student must meet.
- The **Practical Applications** outline names one **by number** at its Topic I.D — *"Gaining competency
  (**CP 41**)"*.
- The **Contracts and Regulations** outline names *"**Commission interpretations and positions on the
  license law**"* at its Topic I.A.3.

> **NO Commission position statement was obtained or read by this project.** **No module of this build
> states the content, number, date or effect of one**, and **CP 41 is named only because the Practical
> Applications outline names it.**
>
> **This is the second submission gate in this course**, and it reaches three of the six Colorado courses. A
> course that claims to teach *"Commission interpretations and positions on the license law"* — the
> Contracts outline's own words — **cannot do so from statute and rule alone.**

**What a broker does about it in the meantime.** Position statements are published by the Division, and a
broker whose question is not answered by the statute or the rule **looks for one before guessing** — and
takes it to their employing broker and, where the question is legal, to counsel. **Rule 6.2.B.3** names
*"legal counsel that is competent in the matter"* as one of the four routes.

## C. Instructive disciplinary proceedings — outline Topic 1

**The outline's phrase is "instructive Disciplinary Proceedings", and the word "instructive" is doing the
work.** The Commission's point is not that a student should memorise sanctions; it is that **the proceedings
show which rules get broken and how.**

> **NO Colorado disciplinary decision, stipulation, consent order or final agency order was read by this
> project**, and **no case, outcome, sanction or penalty is stated anywhere in this build.** **C.R.S.
> §§ 12-10-217, 12-10-219 and 12-10-222 were not read**, so **no disciplinary GROUND is stated either.**
>
> **This is the third submission gate in this course**, and the Commission's own note says where the
> material comes from: **staff supplies it to authorized providers.**

**What this module states instead is what the rules themselves say about enforcement**, because those
provisions **were** read:

| Provision | What it says |
|---|---|
| **§ 12-10-411** | A violation of **any provision of Part 4** — the brokerage relationship sections — *"constitutes an act pursuant to section 12-10-217(1)(m) for which the real estate commission **may investigate and take administrative action**"* |
| **Rule 5.21** | Failure to **produce documents or records** within the time the Commission sets *"**will be grounds for disciplinary action**"* absent an extension |
| **Rule 6.3.A.1** | *"**Whenever a complaint is filed with the Commission against an Associate Broker, the Commission MAY INVESTIGATE whether there have been violations of section 12-10-217(1)(r) by the EMPLOYING BROKER.**"* |
| **Rule 2.1.D** | The Commission **may audit courses at any time and at no cost**, may request **all instructional materials and student attendance records**, and **failure to comply may result in removal of the provider, instructor and/or course from the approved provider list** |
| **Record Keeping outline, Topic I.D.3** | *"**Audit(s) — repeated audit findings are subject to disciplinary action (Rules 5.21., 6.25.A.)**"* |

**Three of those five reach somebody other than the licensee who did the thing.** **Rule 6.3.A.1** reaches
the **employing broker** on a complaint against an associate. **Rule 2.1.D** reaches the **school**.
**Rule 5.22** holds the employing or independent broker **jointly responsible with the firm** for all of
Chapter 5. **That is the structure of Colorado enforcement**, and it is stateable from the rules without any
case at all.

## D. What a broker must keep current, and how

**This is the practical core of Topic 1, and it is what a student can take away from a course whose
mandatory content they have not received.**

### D.1 The things that change

| What | Where to check | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **4 CCR 725-1** | Secretary of State's **CCR**, with the rulemaking docket | Every rule in five of this project's six Colorado courses |
| **C.R.S. Title 12, Article 10** | The Colorado Revised Statutes | The statute the rules sit under |
| **Commission-approved forms** | *"To obtain the forms promulgated by the Commission, **visit the Division's website**"* — **Rule 7.1.A** | **§ 12-10-403(4)(c)** requires a broker to **use** a Commission-approved form when one exists and is appropriate |
| **Commission position statements** | The Division | Named by three of the six course outlines |
| **The Transaction File Checklist** | *"may be found on the Division's website"* — **Rule 6.20** | It **designates** the documents a transaction file must contain |
| **Practitioner aids at the auditing website** | Named by the Record Keeping outline, Topic III.A.3 | Trust account compliance |
| **Online Course Approval Checklist** | Named by the Practical Applications outline | Every distance-delivered course in this build |

**Four of those seven were NOT obtained by this project**, and each is recorded as a submission gate:
**the forms**, **the position statements**, **the Transaction File Checklist**, and **the Online Course
Approval Checklist** — together with the **practitioner aids**.

### D.2 The rule that makes currency a form question

> **Rule 7.1.A**: A *"Commission-Approved Form"* is *"a form **promulgated by the Commission FOR CURRENT USE
> by Brokers.**"* A broker *"**must use a Commission-Approved Form when such form exists and is appropriate
> for the transaction.**"*

**"For current use" is the operative phrase.** A superseded form is not a Commission-approved form, and a
broker using last year's version has not satisfied **§ 12-10-403(4)(c)**.

**And where no approved form exists or one is not appropriate**, **Rule 7.1.B** permits an **Attorney
Form** — which **must carry the words *"This form has not been approved by the Colorado Real Estate
Commission"***, must name the attorney or law firm and the broker or firm it was prepared for, and **may not
be altered by the broker other than by completing blank spaces.**

### D.3 The rule that makes currency the provider's problem too

> **Practical Applications outline**: *"**Providers should address each applicable Statute, Rule and Position
> Statement during the course. COURSE PROVIDERS ARE RESPONSIBLE FOR ENSURING THAT THE STATUTES, RULES AND
> POSITION STATEMENTS USED DURING THE COURSE PRESENTATION ARE ACCURATE AND UP-TO-DATE.**"*

**The Commission put the currency obligation on the provider in writing**, in a different outline, and it
applies to this build as much as to any other. **The readings file names the exact rule version and the
exact date read** so that a reviewer can determine whether this build is current, rather than having to
take its word.

## E. The rules a broker checks first

For a broker who finishes this course and wants a short list, these are the provisions read for this build
that a licensee touches most often — and therefore the ones whose currency matters most.

| Rule or section | What it governs |
|---|---|
| **§ 12-10-403(2)** | **Transaction-broker by default** unless single agency is established **in writing** |
| **§ 12-10-406** | **Dual agency prohibited** |
| **§§ 12-10-404, 12-10-405, 12-10-407** | The three duty lists |
| **§ 12-10-408** and **Rule 6.5** | The disclosures, and that they come **before eliciting or discussing confidential information** |
| **§ 12-10-409** | Duration, and the **one-year** backstop |
| **§ 12-10-410** | Compensation — and that **payment does not establish agency** |
| **Rule 5.2, 5.5, 5.7, 5.14** | Trust accounts, separate accounts, deposit deadlines, journal, ledger, **monthly three-way reconciliation** |
| **Rule 6.2** | Competency, and the **four routes** |
| **Rule 6.3** | Employing broker supervision, reaching **unlicensed employees** |
| **Rule 6.10** | **Advertising** |
| **Rule 6.11** | **Square footage disclosure**, on the Commission-approved form |
| **Rule 6.12** | The **CMA/BPO notice**, verbatim, and the **prohibition** on those used to obtain financing |
| **Rule 6.13** | Presenting offers **to the other consumer's broker**, then the employing broker, then the consumer |
| **Rule 6.17** | **Continuing** duty to disclose conflicts in writing |
| **Rule 6.19** | Closing responsibility, the **signed** closing statement, and **Good Funds** |
| **Rule 6.20** | **Four-year** transaction file retention |
