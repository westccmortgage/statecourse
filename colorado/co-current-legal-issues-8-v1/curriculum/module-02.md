## A. How the law reaches a broker

### A.1 Four layers

| Layer | For a Colorado broker |
|---|---|
| **Statute** | **C.R.S. Title 12, Article 10**, and the many other titles a transaction touches — 38 (property), 39 (taxation), and others |
| **Rule** | **4 CCR 725-1**, promulgated by the Commission under the statute |
| **Commission interpretation** | **Position statements** — named by three of the six course outlines, **none read by this project** |
| **Case law** | Court decisions construing all of the above — **none read by this project** |

**A rule cannot contradict its statute, and a court's construction of either governs both.** That is why
the Commission's outline asks for **recent and relevant** case law: the rules a broker reads may have been
construed in a way the text does not reveal.

## B. Recognising when a question has left the rules

**A broker does not need to know the law to recognise a legal question.** These are the signals, and each
one appears somewhere in this build.

| Signal | Example from this build |
|---|---|
| **The question is about who owns something, or what an instrument does** | Survivorship on a deed; what a quitclaim conveys; whether a water right passed |
| **The question is about who may sign** | An entity, a trust, an estate, a power of attorney |
| **The question is about what happens on default** | An owner carry note; a foreclosure; a lease default |
| **The question is about a period, a notice or a procedure** | Adverse possession; eviction; interpleader; redemption |
| **Two parties disagree about money the broker holds** | An earnest money dispute |
| **The answer would change what someone signs** | Any of the above |

**In every one of those, the routing is the same** — and Colorado's own rules say so in three separate
places.

> **Rule 6.2.B** — a broker without the necessary experience, training and knowledge must **1. decline;
> 2. obtain** them; **3. obtain the assistance** of the employing broker, a supervisory broker, a competent
> broker **or legal counsel that is competent in the matter**; or **4. co-list.**
>
> **§§ 12-10-404(1)(c)(V) and 12-10-405(1)(c)(V)** — a single agent must **advise the party to obtain expert
> advice** as to material matters known to the broker but whose specifics are **beyond the broker's
> expertise.**
>
> **§ 12-10-407(2)(b)(II)** — a transaction-broker must **advise the parties regarding the transaction and
> SUGGEST that the parties obtain expert advice** as to material matters known to the broker but beyond
> their expertise.
>
> **§ 12-10-403(4)(c)** — in any transaction where the broker completes a standard form, the broker
> *"**shall advise the parties that the forms have important legal consequences and that the parties should
> consult legal counsel before signing the forms.**"*

**Note the difference between the single agent and the transaction-broker formulations.** A single agent
**advises the party to obtain** expert advice. A transaction-broker **advises the parties regarding the
transaction and suggests** they obtain it. **Both are duties; neither authorises the broker to give the
advice.**

## C. The unauthorized practice of law

The **Current Legal Issues** outline names *"**Unauthorized Practice of Law concerns**"* at its topic area
3.11, and the **Contracts and Regulations** outline names the two decisions that settle the question in
Colorado at its Topic I.B.1 and I.B.2.

> **Neither decision was read by this project.** **No holding, limit or test from either is stated anywhere
> in this build.** **This is a submission gate.**

**What IS stated, because the provisions were read, is the statutory authority a broker actually has:**

> **§ 12-10-403(4)(a)** — a broker *"**whether acting as a single agent or transaction-broker, may COMPLETE
> STANDARD FORMS** for use in a real estate transaction"* when performing licensed activities.
>
> **§ 12-10-403(4)(b)** — **eight kinds** of standard form, of which the first is a **form promulgated by
> the Commission for current use.**
>
> **§ 12-10-403(4)(c)** — a broker **shall use a Commission-approved form when one exists and is
> appropriate**; use of a **client form** or a **government/lender form** is *"**limited to inserting
> transaction-specific information within the form**"*; for an **attorney form**, a **CBA form**, a
> **disclosure-only form**, a **title company form** or a **letter of intent**, the broker *"**may also
> advise the parties as to effects thereof**"*; and in every case the broker **shall advise the parties to
> consult legal counsel before signing.**
>
> **Rule 7.1.A** — of a Commission-approved form: *"The Broker **may advise the parties as to the effects
> thereof.**"*

**The line the statute draws is between completing a form and advising on its effects**, and it draws that
line **differently for different kinds of form.** A broker who knows which category a document falls into
knows what they may do with it — **and knows that drafting a new provision is in none of the categories.**

## D. What a broker does when the law changes

### D.1 Where change shows up

| Change | Where a broker sees it |
|---|---|
| **A rule amendment** | The **Secretary of State's CCR**, with the rulemaking docket behind it |
| **A statutory amendment** | The Colorado Revised Statutes; the Division's **legislative summaries** |
| **A new or revised Commission form** | The **Division's website** — **Rule 7.1.A** directs a broker there |
| **A new position statement** | The Division |
| **A decision construing any of the above** | The mandatory material Commission staff supplies for **this course** |

### D.2 The provider's own obligation, in the Commission's words

> **Practical Applications outline**: *"**COURSE PROVIDERS ARE RESPONSIBLE FOR ENSURING THAT THE STATUTES,
> RULES AND POSITION STATEMENTS USED DURING THE COURSE PRESENTATION ARE ACCURATE AND UP-TO-DATE.**"*

**And the Commission's own audit power backs it.** **Rule 2.1.D**: the Commission *"**may audit courses ...
at any time and at no cost**"*, may request **all instructional materials and student attendance records**,
and *"**failure to comply with statutes and these Rules may result in the removal of the course provider,
instructor, and/or the course from the approved provider list.**"*

**This build states the rule version it read (12355) and the date it read it (2026-08-20)** in the readings
file and in every instructor source note, so that the currency question can be answered by a reviewer
rather than assumed.

### D.3 The broker's own obligation

**Rule 6.2.A** puts it as a competency requirement rather than a courtesy: a broker *"**must possess the
necessary experience, training, and knowledge to provide Real Estate Brokerage Services and MAINTAIN
COMPLIANCE WITH THE APPLICABLE FEDERAL, STATE AND LOCAL LAWS, RULES, REGULATIONS AND ORDINANCES.**"*

**"Maintain compliance" is continuous.** A broker who was compliant with the rule as it stood is not
compliant with the rule as it stands, and **Rule 6.2.B's first route remains available**: **decline.**
