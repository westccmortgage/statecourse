# Colorado Current Legal Issues 8h v1.0 — Course Syllabus

**Regulator:** Colorado Division of Real Estate and Colorado Division of Private Occupational Schools  
**Hours:** 8  
**Status:** Development; not approved; enrollment disabled  
**Instructor:** Unassigned; state approval/qualification and course assignment required

## Learning outcomes

Students completing an approved release will explain the prescribed subjects, apply Colorado law/forms to entry-level facts, perform required calculations, recognize professional-scope limits, and produce an auditable transaction response.

## Delivery and completion

Distance/asynchronous delivery must be included in the DPOS school/program approval. No broker-qualifying ARELLO/IDECC mandate was identified in the controlling DRE/DPOS sources; that certification is not imported from appraiser or another state rules. The exact course configuration controls active time, sequence, units, interactions, assessment, remediation, identity, inactivity, final eligibility, completion period, and proctoring. Payment never determines completion or credit.

## Assessment

Formative checks and module quizzes trigger objective-specific remediation. Draft final configuration: 1 form(s), 25 items/form, passing standard: Provider proposal withheld until regulator-approved standard is recorded. DPOS-approved course policy; no universal DRE course-final score identified. Any item count not prescribed by the regulator is a StateCourse submission proposal.
