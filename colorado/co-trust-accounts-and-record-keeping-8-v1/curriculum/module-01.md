## Learning objectives

1. State what Money Belonging to Others is and where it must be kept.
2. State how a trust or escrow account must be identified with the depository.
3. State in whose name accounts must be maintained and who may withdraw.
4. State the deposit deadlines and how each is measured.
5. State the record retention period and identify its source.
6. Name the rules the Commission's outline names by number and say what each does.

## A. Definition and identification of accounts — outline Topic I.A

### A.1 The core rule — Rule 5.2

> *"**All Money Belonging to Others accepted by a Broker or Brokerage Firm MUST BE KEPT SEPARATE from other
> money belonging to the Broker or Brokerage Firm and MUST BE DEPOSITED into one or more of the Broker's or
> Brokerage Firm's Trust or Escrow Accounts.** The Broker or Brokerage Firm **must identify the FIDUCIARY
> NATURE of each separate Trust or Escrow Account in deposit agreements with a Recognized Depository by the
> use of the WORD 'TRUST' OR 'ESCROW' and a LABEL identifying the purpose of such account**, such as **'sales
> escrow', 'rental escrow', 'security deposit escrow'**, or other abbreviated form defined in the deposit
> agreement. The Broker or Brokerage Firm **must retain a copy of each executed Trust or Escrow Account
> deposit agreement for inspection by the Commission.**"*

**Three requirements, and each is checkable by an auditor in about a minute:**

| # | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **1. Type of account** | A **Trust or Escrow Account** at a **Recognized Depository**, separate from the firm's own money |
| **2. Trust account designation** | The **word "trust" or "escrow"** in the deposit agreement, **plus a label identifying the purpose** |
| **3. The deposit agreement retained** | A copy of each executed agreement kept **for inspection by the Commission** |

**The label is not decoration.** *"Sales escrow"*, *"rental escrow"*, *"security deposit escrow"* — the
label tells the depository, the auditor and the broker what the account is for, and **Rule 5.5** then
requires property management money to be in **separate** accounts by purpose.

### A.2 FDIC insurance coverage — outline Topic I.A.3

The Commission's outline names *"**Relevance to FDIC insurance coverage**"* as a sub-item.

> **No FDIC regulation, coverage limit, pass-through requirement or titling rule was read by this project**,
> and **no coverage amount or requirement is stated anywhere in this build.**
>
> **What this module states is the point that does not depend on the figure:** an account's **titling and
> the records behind it** are what determine how deposit insurance applies to funds held for many
> beneficiaries. **A broker holding other people's money asks their depository how the account is titled
> and what that means**, and confirms it in the deposit agreement **Rule 5.2** requires them to retain.
>
> **This is a submission gate** — the outline names FDIC coverage and this build cannot state its rules.

### A.3 Number of accounts, and when none is needed — Rules 5.3, 5.4 and 5.5

> **Rule 5.3 — whose name.** A firm acting **in the name of the Employing Broker or Independent Broker as a
> sole proprietor** maintains accounts **in that broker's name**; a firm licensed as a **partnership,
> corporation or limited liability company** maintains them **in the entity's name.** The **Employing Broker
> or Independent Broker are responsible for, must maintain and be able to withdraw** from each account, and
> **may authorize other licensed or unlicensed cosigners** — but *"**such authorization will not relieve the
> Employing Broker or Independent Broker of any responsibility under the Commission statutes and these
> Rules.**"*
>
> **Rule 5.4 — from zero to unlimited.** A firm is **not limited** in the number of accounts. *"**If the
> Brokerage Firm is not in possession of Money Belonging to Others, there is no obligation to maintain a
> separate Trust or Escrow Account.**"*
>
> **Rule 5.5 — where property management is concerned, separate accounts are REQUIRED.** A firm engaging in
> property management *"**must deposit rental receipts and security deposits and disburse money collected
> for such purposes in SEPARATE Trust or Escrow Accounts, a MINIMUM OF ONE for rental receipts and a MINIMUM
> OF ONE for security deposits.**"*

**Rule 5.4 is the one brokers are surprised by.** A firm that never holds other people's money **is not
required to have a trust account at all.** Many Colorado firms operate that way, with earnest money going
straight to a title company under **Rule 5.12.A.**

**And Rule 5.3's last sentence is the one employing brokers should read twice.** Authorising a cosigner —
licensed or unlicensed — **transfers none of the responsibility.**

### A.4 Funds must be available — Rule 5.6

> *"Unless otherwise agreed to in writing by the parties, **Money Belonging to Others must not be invested
> in any type of account, security, or certificate of deposit that has a FIXED TERM FOR MATURITY or imposes
> ANY FEE OR PENALTY FOR WITHDRAWAL prior to maturity.**"*

**Other people's money must be reachable on the day it is needed**, which is a different question from
whether it earns anything.

## B. Acting broker's responsibilities — outline Topic I.B

### B.1 Opening and maintaining the account — outline Topic I.B.1

**Rules 5.2 and 5.3 above**, plus:

> **Rule 5.1 — Establishment of Internal Accounting Controls.** *"Any Brokerage Firm or Broker who receives
> Money Belonging to Others **must establish WRITTEN accounting control policies and procedures**, which
> must include **adequate checks and balances over the financial activities of the Broker, Brokerage Firm,
> AND UNLICENSED PERSONS**, as well as **manage the risk of fraud or illegal acts.**"*

**Written**, and reaching **unlicensed persons** expressly — the bookkeeper, the assistant, the
office manager. Module 03 of this course develops why, under the outline's *"Separation of Duties"* and
*"Employee Theft and internal controls."*

### B.2 Timely deposit — outline Topic I.B.2, and Rule 5.7

| Money | Deadline | Measured from |
|---|---|---|
| **Property management** | **No later than 5 BUSINESS DAYS** | receipt of funds **or mutual execution of a lease, whichever is LATER** |
| **All other** Money Belonging to Others | **No later than 3 BUSINESS DAYS** | receipt of funds **or mutual execution of contract, whichever is LATER** |

**Business days, not calendar days.** And the clock starts at **the later** of the two events — so money
received before a contract is mutually executed does not start the clock until it is.

**Rule 5.12** then governs earnest money specifically: a broker receiving it **must deliver it to the
earnest money holder to be deposited in accordance with the contract** and **obtain a dated and signed
receipt**; if the **firm** holds it, **Rule 5.7.B**'s three business days apply; and a transfer to a lawyer
or closing entity also requires **a dated and signed receipt.**

## C. Acknowledgment of trust account status by bank or other depository — outline Topic I.C

**Rule 5.2's second and third sentences are the whole of this sub-topic**: the **fiduciary nature** of each
account is identified **in the deposit agreement** by the **word "trust" or "escrow" plus a purpose label**,
and **a copy of each executed deposit agreement is retained for inspection by the Commission.**

**The deposit agreement is the acknowledgment.** A broker who cannot produce one has not satisfied
Rule 5.2, whatever the account is called on the statement.

## D. Commission rules and regulations — outline Topic I.D

The outline names four sub-items, and three of them name rules **by number.**

### D.1 Record retention period — outline Topic I.D.1

> **Rule 6.20**: *"Both a Broker and a Brokerage Firm **must retain transaction files for all transactions
> for a period of FOUR (4) YEARS** beginning from **the consummation date of the transaction or the
> expiration date of any Listing Contracts that do not consummate.**"*

**Four years, two possible start dates**, and the rule expressly does **not** require a broker to obtain and
retain **existing public records, title commitments, loan applications, lender required disclosures, or
related affirmations from independent third party closing entities after the closing date.**

**The Commission's Transaction File Checklist, which Rule 6.20 says designates the required documents, was
NOT obtained by this project** — a submission gate.

**And Rule 5.14.E** permits the **three-way reconciliation reports, ledgers, journals and bank account
statements** to be **kept electronically**; **Rule 6.24** governs electronic records and production.

### D.2 Position Statements — outline Topic I.D.2

> **NO Commission position statement was obtained or read by this project**, and **no module of this build
> states the content of one.** **This is a submission gate**, and it is the second course in this build to
> record it — the **Practical Applications** outline names **CP 41** by number for the same reason.

### D.3 Audits — outline Topic I.D.3

The outline's own words: *"**Audit(s) — repeated audit findings are subject to disciplinary action (Rules
5.21., 6.25.A.)**"*

> **Rule 5.21 — Production of Documents and Records.** *"A Broker and Brokerage Firm **must produce for
> inspection by the Commission any document or record as may be reasonably necessary for investigation or
> audit** in the enforcement of the Commission statutes and these Rules. **Failure to submit such documents
> or records within the time set by the Commission in its notification will be GROUNDS FOR DISCIPLINARY
> ACTION** unless the Commission has granted an extension of time for such production."*
>
> **Rule 6.25 — Investigations or Audits by Commission.** Read for this build; the outline cites **6.25.A**
> for the proposition it states in its own text — that **repeated audit findings are subject to disciplinary
> action.**

**No disciplinary ground, sanction, penalty or procedure is stated anywhere in this build.** **§§ 12-10-217
and 12-10-219 were not read.** What is stated is what **Rule 5.21** itself says: **failure to produce within
the time set is grounds for disciplinary action.**

### D.4 Overview of key rules — outline Topic I.D.4

The outline names three by number: **5.2., 5.8.A. and 6.3.B.** All three were read.

| Rule | What it does |
|---|---|
| **5.2** | **Trust or escrow accounts** — Part A.1 of this module |
| **5.8.A** | **Owner-held security deposits** — a firm **will not deliver security deposits to an owner without the tenant's written authorization in a lease or unless written notice has been given** telling the tenant **who is holding the deposit** and **the specific requirements for the procedure in which the tenant may request return.** If the deposit goes to the owner, the management agreement **should** place financial responsibility for its return on the owner and **must** authorize disclosure of the owner's **true name and current mailing address** in a dispute |
| **6.3.B** | **The employing broker's responsibilities** — to **maintain all trust and escrow accounts and records as set forth in Chapter 5**; **maintain all transaction records as set forth in Rule 6.20**; **develop the firm's written policies as set forth in Rule 6.4**; and **provide a "Reasonable-Level of Supervision" for all Associate Brokers** |

**And the rule that closes the loop on responsibility:**

> **Rule 5.22**: *"The **Employing Broker or Independent Broker are held JOINTLY RESPONSIBLE with the
> Brokerage Firm** in complying with Chapter 5 of these Rules."*
