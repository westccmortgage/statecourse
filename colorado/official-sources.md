# Colorado Official Source Register

Last verified: **2026-08-16**. Official regulator, legislature/rule publisher, and current regulator forms control. Missing or ambiguous requirements remain filing questions; another state's rule is never substituted.

1. [Colorado DRE — Broker Qualifying Education](https://dre.colorado.gov/division-programs/real-estate-broker/education/broker-qualifying-education) — 168-hour total, six mandatory courses, and official course outlines.
2. [Colorado DRE — Broker Application Requirements](https://dre.colorado.gov/division-programs/real-estate-broker/licensing/broker-license-application-requirements) — First-time broker education breakdown and licensing path.
3. [Colorado DRE — Licensee Application Requirements](https://dre.colorado.gov/licensee-application-requirements) — REC-33 completion certificates and first-time broker examination requirements.
4. [Colorado DPOS — Laws and Regulations](https://cdhe.colorado.gov/dpos-laws-regulations) — Current 8 CCR 1504-1 school, program, agent, instructor/staff, delivery, records, renewal, and fee rules.
5. [Colorado DPOS Connect Resources](https://cdhe.colorado.gov/dposconnect-resources) — School and program submission portal and application resources.
6. [Colorado DPOS Fee Schedule](https://cdhe.colorado.gov/fee-schedule) — Current school/program fees; amounts must be rechecked in DPOS Connect on filing day.

## Release control

All sources are checked again on filing day, on regulator correction, before each controlled release, and before enrollment after a legal/form change. A snapshot, checksum, effective date, reviewer, and impact decision belong in the regulatory-document record.
