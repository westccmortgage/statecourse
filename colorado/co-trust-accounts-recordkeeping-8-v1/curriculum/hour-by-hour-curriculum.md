## COTA-01 — Trust funds and accounts

Authority mapping: Colorado DRE official qualifying course outline

## Unit 001 — Trust-fund identification

Module: COTA-01 · Proposed clock hours: 1 · Content layer: CO-specific

### Learning objectives

By the end of this unit, the student can:

1. explain **trust-fund identification** with accurate entry-level professional vocabulary;
2. apply it to a Colorado transaction fact pattern;
3. distinguish the licensee's permitted task from legal, tax, appraisal, lending, inspection, engineering, or surveying work; and
4. identify the controlling source, required communication, and retained evidence.

### Instructional lesson

**Rule and purpose.** Trust-fund identification is studied within Trust funds and accounts. The student identifies the parties, property, money, documents, deadlines, disclosures, and consumer-protection purpose. The rule is applied only after its current effective date and form edition are checked.

**Transaction method.** Read the file chronologically: source document, known facts, missing facts, decision point, permitted broker action, consumer communication, performance, and retained evidence. A compliant response verifies material facts from an appropriate source, stays within the broker's role, uses the current approved document when required, communicates promptly, and escalates unresolved professional questions.

**Colorado layer.** Apply Colorado DRE official qualifying course outline and the controlled Colorado source register. A national concept may be reused for explanation, but Colorado terminology, forms, examples, citations, assessments, version, and approval record remain independent. When a source or form changes, new enrollment is held until impact review is recorded.

**Common error.** Custom, memory, another state's form, a payment event, or a technology default never replaces the controlling state rule or an approval condition. The audit file records what was checked, when it was checked, the result, and who was responsible.
