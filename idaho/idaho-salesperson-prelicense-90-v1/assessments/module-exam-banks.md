# Idaho Salesperson Prelicense — Module Assessment Bank (Draft)

Draft original questions for Idaho instructor review. The Idaho DOPL applicant education page requires a final exam for each course and 70% passing; it does not specify exam item counts on that page. Questions below are a development pool, not official exam items, and are not by themselves evidence of course approval.

Sources: https://dopl.idaho.gov/rec/rec-education-applicants/ and https://dopl.idaho.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Idaho-Real-Estate-License-Law-Rules-Booklet-FINAL-VERSION.pdf

## Module 1: Fundamental Real Estate Concepts

1. A seller says a wall-mounted bookcase is included, but the agreement is silent. Best next step?  
A Assume attachment decides. B Clarify intent and document it in transaction paperwork. C Tell buyer it is excluded. D Remove it.  
**Key B.** Resolve conveyance intent in writing; attachment alone may not settle it.

2. Which is most likely personal property?  
A Built-in furnace. B Roof-mounted solar array. C Freestanding dining table. D Planted tree.  
**Key C.** It is ordinarily movable personal property.

3. A legal description conflicts with the street address. What should the agent do?  
A Correct from an online map. B Use the street address. C Flag and obtain authoritative title/survey confirmation. D Ask buyer to choose.  
**Key C.** A legal description needs authoritative verification.

4. An easement generally grants:  
A Ownership of the parcel. B A defined use right over another’s land. C A mortgage. D Zoning approval.  
**Key B.** Scope depends on its terms and applicable law.

5. Where should a proposed land use be confirmed?  
A Neighbor. B Old listing. C Local land-use authority and records. D Social media.  
**Key C.** Verify with the governing authority.

6. A CMA is:  
A A guaranteed sale price. B A comparative market analysis, not an appraisal. C A title report. D A lender decision.  
**Key B.** Do not represent it as an appraisal.

7. A buyer asks if a loan is guaranteed.  
A Guarantee on income. B Explain only the lender can assess qualification and refer buyer. C Estimate from score alone. D Advise waiving financing.  
**Key B.** Avoid unauthorized lending representations.

8. A contingency deadline is near and the client has not decided.  
A Wait. B Promptly explain deadline, consult broker as needed, document instructions. C Extend unilaterally. D Delete it.  
**Key B.** Timely communication and client direction matter.

9. A client requests concealment of a known material fact.  
A Comply. B Consult broker and follow applicable disclosure duties. C Tell only one buyer. D Delete the file note.  
**Key B.** Do not conceal required information.

10. A licensee receives financial documents by email.  
A Forward broadly. B Use approved secure process and limit access. C Save to personal device. D Post in group chat.  
**Key B.** Minimize exposure of sensitive data.

11. A complex title exception is presented.  
A Give definitive legal interpretation. B Explain role limits and refer to title/legal professional. C Ignore it. D Promise it is harmless.  
**Key B.** Use the proper expert.

12. An inspection flags possible structural damage.  
A Diagnose it. B Communicate the report and recommend qualified evaluation. C Conceal at seller request. D Estimate repairs without basis.  
**Key B.** Separate evidence from expert conclusions.

13. Which ad is most appropriate?  
A “Perfect for young Christian couples.” B “Ideal for able-bodied families.” C “Two-bedroom home, 1,200 sq. ft., near transit.” D “No children.”  
**Key C.** Use objective property facts, not protected-class preferences.

14. Which is an ownership operating cost?  
A Property tax. B Offer expiration. C Agency disclosure. D Earnest-money receipt.  
**Key A.** It is a recurring ownership cost.

15. A deed is recorded primarily to:  
A Enter the instrument in land records and provide notice under applicable law. B Create a loan commitment. C Replace inspection. D Establish zoning.  
**Key A.** Recording does not replace other due diligence.

16. Which best describes consideration?  
A Exchange of something of legal value. B Title policy. C Recording act. D Inspection.  
**Key A.** It is generally a contract formation element, subject to law.

17. A conflict arises between a licensee’s interest and client’s interest.  
A Proceed silently. B Disclose promptly and seek broker guidance on handling. C Ask other party to hide it. D Destroy messages.  
**Key B.** Disclosure and supervision are appropriate.

18. A buyer gives objective location criteria. The agent should:  
A Apply them consistently. B Substitute demographic assumptions. C Exclude areas based on family status. D Filter by religion.  
**Key A.** Use client-selected, housing-related criteria consistently.

19. An appraisal differs from a CMA because it is:  
A An independent valuation assignment by a qualified appraiser under applicable standards. B Always higher. C Issued by listing agent. D A loan guarantee.  
**Key A.** Different role, standards, and intended use.

20. A signed contract term needs changing.  
A Edit the signed copy. B Use authorized written amendment and required signatures. C Rely on memory. D Ask escrow to infer it.  
**Key B.** Document modifications properly.

## Module 2: Applied Listing, Selling, Buying, and Closing

1. Before acting as a buyer’s advocate, the licensee should:  
A Explain relationship and required disclosures. B Say representation exists automatically. C Avoid answering. D Promise unlimited confidentiality.  
**Key A.** Clarify relationship and limits first.

2. Before advertising a listing, verify:  
A Authority and material property facts. B Only desired price. C Buyer loan. D Future appraisal.  
**Key A.** Confirm authorization and accuracy.

3. Multiple offers differ in price, financing, contingencies, timing. Seller summary should:  
A Recommend highest price only. B Compare material terms neutrally and identify uncertainty. C Hide drawbacks. D Promise closing.  
**Key B.** Support the seller’s informed choice.

4. Buyer considers waiving inspection based on seller assurance.  
A Decide for buyer. B Explain due diligence, suggest qualified inspection advice, document choice. C Guarantee condition. D Waive for buyer.  
**Key B.** Support informed decision-making.

5. Earnest money handling should follow:  
A Brokerage policy, applicable law, and contract instructions. B Personal preference. C Seller’s oral request only. D Licensee’s personal account.  
**Key A.** Use required trust-fund safeguards.

6. A transaction calendar should track:  
A Closing date only. B All deadlines, responsible parties, and follow-up. C Lender dates only. D No contingencies.  
**Key B.** Track each material obligation.

7. Seller discloses a prior roof leak. The licensee should:  
A Ignore if repaired. B Follow disclosure duties and communicate known facts accurately. C Promise sound roof. D Remove the statement.  
**Key B.** Disclose facts as required; avoid unsupported conclusions.

8. An inspection recommends a specialist.  
A Discourage buyer. B Encourage qualified advice and track relevant deadline. C Diagnose issue. D Delete the report.  
**Key B.** Coordinate due diligence.

9. Offer is rejected.  
A Alter and resend without authorization. B Communicate, obtain next instructions, update records. C Pressure seller directly. D Delete offer.  
**Key B.** Preserve the client’s decision trail.

10. Client asks salesperson to draft complex legal clause outside approved form.  
A Draft from memory. B Consult broker and refer legal drafting to attorney as appropriate. C Copy internet text. D Promise enforceability.  
**Key B.** Respect scope and expertise.

11. Buyer sees an unfamiliar settlement fee.  
A Change it. B Ask settlement professional to explain before closing. C Ignore. D Remove line.  
**Key B.** Direct reconciliation to the responsible professional.

12. Closing delay is likely.  
A Wait until deadline. B Inform client/broker promptly and seek written direction for any extension. C Backdate amendment. D Assume automatic extension.  
**Key B.** Escalate and document.

13. Names differ across transaction documents.  
A Correct all documents. B Flag and coordinate authorized correction. C Ignore. D Sign for buyer.  
**Key B.** Confirm identity/vesting with responsible parties.

14. Showing reveals apparent water intrusion.  
A Say harmless. B Record observation, communicate, recommend qualified investigation. C Cover it. D Guarantee repairs.  
**Key B.** Be accurate and evidence-based.

15. Best evidence deadline was handled:  
A Memory. B Dated log plus written client instruction and relevant document. C Empty calendar. D Verbal assurance.  
**Key B.** Preserve contemporaneous records.

16. Seller asks agent to omit a known defect.  
A Comply. B Refuse concealment, consult broker, follow disclosure procedure. C Tell one buyer. D Remove listing only.  
**Key B.** Do not help conceal required information.

17. Buyer changes search area.  
A Refuse. B Update objective criteria with buyer and apply consistently. C Use demographics to filter. D Ask seller.  
**Key B.** Follow client’s housing criteria.

18. File lacks signed disclosure receipt.  
A Recreate signature. B Seek accurate documentation and inform broker of gap. C Mark complete. D Delete checklist.  
**Key B.** Never fabricate records.

19. Buyer receives wire-instruction change by email.  
A Forward immediately. B Independently verify through known channel and warn of wire fraud. C Assume authentic. D Reply with credentials.  
**Key B.** Verify out-of-band and protect funds.

20. After closing, client requests file copy.  
A Follow brokerage retention/access rules and protect others’ confidential data. B Refuse all copies. C Send publicly. D Delete file.  
**Key A.** Apply record and privacy procedures.
