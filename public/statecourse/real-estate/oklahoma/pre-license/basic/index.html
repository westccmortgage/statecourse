Oklahoma law and delivery controls are first-class course data.

The course covers all OREC Basic Pre-License areas, a six-month distance enrollment window, secure examination controls, instructor availability, remediation, and five-year student records.

Instructor gate: no owner or generic instructor is assumed. An OREC-approved contract instructor must be assigned later and satisfy the configured education/experience and 80% Oklahoma state and national examination gates.

Timed curriculum

22 modules totaling 90 hours. Secure primary and alternate examination banks are not public.

Distance completion

All approved material and modules must be completed within six months. Final eligibility remains under the LMS compliance engine, not payment processing.
