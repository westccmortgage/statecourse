Missouri transaction practice in its own course.

The curriculum covers broker disclosure, listings, buyer representation, dual agency and transaction brokerage, compensation, duties, pricing, appraisal, finance, closing costs, contracts, earnest money, misrepresentation, and construction.

Separate-record rule: bundling with the 48-hour course may simplify checkout only. Enrollment, timing, examinations, certificates, reporting, versions, and approvals remain separate.

Timed curriculum

11 modules totaling 24 hours. Secure assessment materials are non-public.

Assessment and response

Two distinct 50-item final forms support the 75% passing standard. Incorrect performance triggers targeted remediation; instructor inquiries are answered by the end of the next regular business day.

Reporting and records

Missouri Real Estate Commission

Four-year records, ten-day completion reporting, and semiannual completion-time analytics remain tied to this 24-hour approval record.
