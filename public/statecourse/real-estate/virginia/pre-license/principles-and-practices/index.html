01

Course Overview

A structured foundation for Virginia real estate practice.

Principles & Practices of Real Estate is organized as a complete 60-hour salesperson pre-license curriculum. Each instructional hour connects legal concepts, professional practice, applied examples, knowledge checks, and remediation to a documented Virginia subject map.

The course is designed for serious independent study. Official study time, assessment eligibility, examination results, and completion records will be maintained by the StateCourse compliance engine after approval. Activity shown in this public preview is informational only.

Course syllabus

  • Delivery60 separately documented course hours
  • InstructionReading, application, and guided practice
  • AssessmentUnit quizzes, remediation, and final exam
  • CompletionSubject to Board approval and LMS controls

02

Learning Outcomes

What students will be prepared to demonstrate.

  1. 01

    Explain the legal framework governing ownership, transfer, use, and financing of real property.

  2. 02

    Distinguish brokerage relationships, duties, disclosures, and required standards of conduct.

  3. 03

    Apply contract principles to listings, purchase agreements, contingencies, and settlement.

  4. 04

    Recognize fair housing, consumer protection, environmental, and risk-management obligations.

  5. 05

    Perform the calculations and document analysis expected in entry-level real estate practice.

  6. 06

    Integrate Virginia-specific law with the national principles tested for salesperson licensure.

Required Virginia Subjects

The curriculum is mapped across the complete statutory subject framework.

  • Virginia license law and regulation
  • Brokerage relationships and duties
  • Property ownership and land use
  • Deeds, title, and transfer
  • Contracts and transaction documents
  • Finance, mortgages, and lending
  • Valuation and real estate mathematics
  • Closings and settlement practices
  • Fair housing and civil rights
  • Disclosures and environmental issues
  • Professional conduct and risk control
  • Federal and Virginia law integration

The detailed subject-to-hour crosswalk remains part of the regulatory submission record.

Exam & Completion Requirements
