Accessibility

StateCourse is designed so students can navigate licensing information, account services, and education with accessible technology.

Our approach

StateCourse targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA. We design for keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic headings and labels, readable contrast, captions and transcripts where appropriate, and responsive layouts without unnecessary horizontal scrolling.

Accessibility support

If you encounter a barrier, email support@statecourse.com. Include the page address, the task you were trying to complete, and the assistive technology or browser involved if you are comfortable sharing it.
