Platform Use

Terms

These terms explain the basic responsibilities associated with a StateCourse account and education services.

Accounts

Provide accurate information, protect your sign-in credentials, and use only your own account. Creating an account does not create a course enrollment or award licensing credit.

Courses and approvals

Course availability, regulatory status, study requirements, examination rules, completion periods, refunds, and certificates are governed by the disclosures and policies presented for the specific state and course. A product marked In Development is not an approved enrollment offer.

Payments and records

When enrollment is available, the checkout terms, price, acknowledgments, and refund policy shown for that course apply to the transaction. Student and completion records remain tied to the specific course version.

Questions

For questions about these terms, email support@statecourse.com.
