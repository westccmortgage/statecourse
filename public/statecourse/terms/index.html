Terms

These terms explain the basic responsibilities associated with a StateCourse LLC account and education services.

Accounts

Provide accurate information, protect your sign-in credentials, and use only your own account. Creating an account does not create a course enrollment or award licensing credit.

Courses and approvals

Course availability, regulatory status, study requirements, examination rules, completion periods, refunds, and certificates are governed by the disclosures and policies presented for the specific state and course. A product marked In Development is not an approved enrollment offer.

California refund and cancellation policy

For California statutory/pre-license courses, a student may cancel and receive a 100% refund of tuition paid through the later of seven business days after enrollment or completion of the first lesson assignment, provided the student has not attempted the final examination. After that period, a student who withdraws after completing 60% or less of the course is eligible for a pro rata refund of unearned tuition based on the percentage of the course not completed. No tuition refund is due after more than 60% of the course has been completed, after any final examination attempt, or after a course completion certificate has been issued, whichever occurs first.

For multi-course packages, refund eligibility and earned tuition are calculated separately for each course using the package allocation recorded at checkout. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 10 business days after StateCourse LLC approves the cancellation or withdrawal request. Requests may be submitted through the student's StateCourse LLC account or in writing to info@statecourse.com. The effective date is the date the request is received by StateCourse LLC. Policy version: CA-2026-08-20-v1.

Payments and records

When enrollment is available, the checkout terms, price, acknowledgments, and refund policy shown for that course apply to the transaction. Student and completion records remain tied to the specific course version.

Questions

For questions about these terms, email info@statecourse.com.
