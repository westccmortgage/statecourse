What California still requires

The examination opens when every requirement below is met. Each one comes from the Department's rules for correspondence courses, not from StateCourse.

    You are eligible to sit the examination

    Once you begin, the clock runs continuously and does not pause. Answers are saved as you make them, so closing the page does not lose your work — but it does not stop the time either.

    • The examination is administered under a proctor who has verified your photo identification.
    • It is closed book. No notes, no course materials, and no other person may assist you.
    • Questions and answers are never released, before or after the examination.
