Academic and regulatory standards

Course approval controls access and credit.

StateCourse does not accept payment, create a credit-bearing enrollment, or issue a completion certificate until the exact state and course version is approved and enabled.

Stripe handles payment only. The StateCourse compliance engine controls required study time, assessments, examination eligibility, completion, and certificate issuance.
