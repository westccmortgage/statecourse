Student Information

Privacy

StateCourse uses student information to provide licensing-path guidance, account access, course delivery, support, payment records, and regulated education records.

Information and purpose

Account information, course activity, assessment and completion records, support requests, and transaction references are used to operate StateCourse and preserve required student records. Payment-card details are handled through the payment provider and are not stored as raw card data by StateCourse.

Access and protection

Access to student information is limited according to role and operational need. StateCourse uses access controls, audit records, and service providers necessary to operate the platform.

Visits to this site

StateCourse counts visits to its public pages itself. No advertising or analytics company receives this information, and StateCourse does not sell or share it.

For each page opened we record the page address, the site that linked to it, any campaign tag in the link, the approximate location (country, state and city) reported by our hosting provider, the kind of device and browser, and whether the visitor appears to be a person or an automated program. We do not store your IP address or the full browser identification string. A one-way mark that changes every day lets us count visitors without following a browser from one day to the next.

If you choose Accept on the cookie notice, two cookies are set in your browser: one remembering your choice and one random number that lets us recognise the same browser on later visits, so we can see which sources bring visitors back and how many go on to open an account. If you choose Decline, only the cookie remembering that choice is set and your browser is not remembered. A browser that sends a Global Privacy Control signal is treated as having declined. Lessons, quizzes and examinations are not part of visit counting.
