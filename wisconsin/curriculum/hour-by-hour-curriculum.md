# Wisconsin Salesperson Pre-License v1.0 — Hour-by-Hour Curriculum

Regulatory status: **Development; not approved; no enrollment or credit.**

This file contains 72 auditable instructional units. Each unit has its own objectives, reading, state layer, applied work, knowledge check, and remediation.

## WI-01 — Real property and the real estate business (5 hours)

Authority: REEB 25.033(1)-(2)

## Hour 001 — Land, real estate, and real property

Module: WI-01 · Required engagement: 60 minutes · Content layer: NC-OWN, NC-INV

### Learning objectives

By the end of this hour, the student can:

1. explain land, real estate, and real property using accurate professional vocabulary;
2. apply land, real estate, and real property to a state-specific transaction fact pattern; and
3. identify the controlling source, required record, and point for professional referral.

### Instructional reading

**Concept and professional purpose.** Land, real estate, and real property is studied as part of Real property and the real estate business. The student begins by identifying the people, property interests, documents, money, deadlines, and public-protection purpose involved. The lesson distinguishes a fact from an assumption, a legal requirement from a provider practice, and a licensee's task from work that must be referred to an attorney, lender, appraiser, surveyor, inspector, tax professional, or other qualified specialist.

**Transaction application.** The student applies the concept to a realistic file. The file is read chronologically: source document, consumer communication, decision point, required disclosure or agreement, performance, and retained evidence. A compliant response identifies controlling authority, verifies material facts from an appropriate source, communicates within the licensee's role, documents the action, and escalates unresolved legal or technical issues. Marketing language, form selection, compensation, and technology do not override the state rule.

**State-law layer.** Wisconsin layer: apply REEB 25.033(1)-(2), current Wisconsin-approved forms, ch. 452, and the REEB rules identified in the controlled source register. Do not substitute a generic national form or another state's agency terminology. Source dates and form editions are checked at controlled release. If a cited source changes, the lesson is held from new enrollment until the course owner completes impact review and records a new content version.

**National-core relationship.** This unit may reuse the concepts in NC-OWN, NC-INV, but its state examples, terminology, forms, citations, assessment record, and approval mapping are versioned independently for Wisconsin.

### Applied activity

Analyze a Wisconsin transaction fact pattern involving **land, real estate, and real property**. Build a five-entry audit note: (1) known fact, (2) missing fact, (3) controlling source, (4) permitted licensee action, and (5) referral or follow-up. Explain why payment status, course progress, and regulatory credit are separate records.

### Knowledge check and remediation

1. Which source controls this issue in Wisconsin, and what is the effective-date check?
2. What fact must be verified before the licensee communicates or acts?
3. What evidence belongs in the transaction or course audit record?

If any response is incorrect, the LMS assigns the source excerpt, the worked scenario, and a new parallel check. The original answer is not exposed as a substitute for remediation. Hour 1 is complete only after the tracked instructional sequence and required check are complete.
