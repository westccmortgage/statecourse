# Wisconsin Salesperson Pre-License v1.0 — Syllabus

**Regulator:** Wisconsin Department of Safety and Professional Services / Real Estate Examining Board  
**Hours:** 72  
**Delivery:** Online distance education — proposed; approval pending  
**Status:** Development; not submitted or approved; enrollment disabled  
**Instructor:** Unassigned. Instructor must be Board-approved/accepted under Form #831 and satisfy one of the REEB 25 qualification paths.

## Outcomes

Students who complete the approved version will be able to explain the required real-estate concepts, apply Wisconsin law and forms to entry-level transaction scenarios, perform required calculations, recognize when referral is necessary, and document decisions consistent with consumer protection.

## Completion

Complete all approved content and course controls; no Board-mandated final-exam score or proctor rule was located in the controlling pre-license provisions, so StateCourse's readiness exam is a provider control subject to Board review.

## Assessment

Module knowledge checks and quizzes diagnose mastery and trigger remediation. Final structure: 1 secure form(s), 100 multiple-choice items per form, 75% passing standard. StateCourse proposed mastery control; not represented as a Wisconsin-mandated score.

## Integrity and access

The authenticated student must complete the state-configured identity, timing, module, assessment and final controls. Payment grants only the purchased course after a verified webhook; it never establishes completion or regulatory credit.
