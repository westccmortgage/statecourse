## Corrections and residual scope

- Removed operative-law claims for repealed 24.07(3), while preserving and explaining the stale course-outline cross-reference.
- Corrected inspection access/party-instruction exceptions, reliance conditions, lender side-agreement duty, and net-listing/tie-in scope.
- Corrected source chapter misidentifications and overbroad form-modification and trust-account claims.
- The final trust-scope review specifically distinguishes REEB 18.031(4), which concerns a licensee with an ownership interest in the rental property, from the separate paragraph (5)(b) owner-payable rental application deposit, rent, and security-deposit arrangement. The 48-hour clock runs from receipt by the firm or an associated licensee; the next-two-business-days exception concerns receipt on the day before a holiday or other bank-closure day.
- Corrected the additional 452.23 error: the statute concerns disclosures and inspections by licensees, not Department investigation power.
- Replaced the basement exercise's unjustified diagnosis: efflorescence and a dehumidifier do not by themselves prove seasonal flooding.
- Changed every identified dependent formative answer with those errors; retained item identity and 143-item structure. Newly correct answers remain authored at index 0 for the existing deterministic delivery shuffler.

Mathematical inputs are stipulated scenario inputs, not current market rates or statutory thresholds.
