## REEB 25.033 — Salesperson's pre-license program

> An education program for applicants for a real estate salesperson's license **shall consist of 72
> hours**. The education program shall contain all of the following topics:

Nineteen topics. **The rule allocates no hours among them.** It fixes the total at 72 and lists what
must be included, exactly as 18VAC135-20-400 does in Virginia — so this project's hour allocation is
a StateCourse proposal for Board review and must be described that way in every document.

**(1) Real property** — land, real estate and real property; the difference between real and personal
property; classification of fixtures; characteristics of real estate; types of home ownership; mobile
homes under s. 70.043 Stats.; ownership expenses; property features; investment considerations; tax
benefits for home ownership; homeowner's insurance.

> **The rule carries a Note: "Section 70.043, Stats., was repealed by 2023 Wisconsin Act 12."** The
> rule still lists the topic and the statute it referenced no longer exists. A course must teach
> mobile and manufactured home treatment without citing a repealed section as though it operated.

**(2) The real estate business** — real estate specialties; the real estate market; factors affecting
supply and demand; business cycles; real estate practice under ch. 452 Stats.

**(3) Real estate brokerage** — law of agency; creating and terminating agency; fiduciary duties;
responsibilities to third parties; broker's and salesperson's compensation; antitrust laws; the
difference between independent contractor and employee; firm-to-firm relationships;
licensee-to-licensee relationships; **agency, including duties owed to parties and clients under
s. 452.133, the requirement for an agency agreement and disclosure of agency under s. 452.135, and
revisions to common law duties and responsibilities under s. 452.139**; and **agency relationship,
including multiple representation with designated agency, multiple representation without designated
agency, and single agency**.

**(4) Listing agreements** — listing property; **listing agreements under ch. REEB 16**; special
listing provisions; terminating listings; obtaining listings; pricing the property; disclosures; home
warranties.

**(5) Interests in real estate** — government powers; estates in land; encumbrances; **water
rights**; forms of ownership; trusts; ownership by business organizations; cooperatives;
**time-shares under ss. 70.095, 707.02, 707.05, 707.40, 707.47 and 707.49**; **condominiums under
ss. 703.08, 703.21 and 703.33**; **homestead under ss. 706.01(7) and 766.605**.

**(6) Legal descriptions** — methods of describing real estate; land units and measurements.

**(7) Taxes and other liens** — liens; tax liens; general tax assessment, equalization, and tax
bills; mortgage liens; **construction liens**; judgments; estate and inheritance tax liens; other
liens; **taxes under ss. 74.15 and 74.47(1) and (2)**; **taxation and transfer fees under ss. 77.22

**(8) Real estate contracts** — contract law; elements of a valid contract; performance; discharge;
default or breach; **forms used in real estate under ch. REEB 16**, including listing agreements
under s. 240.10, offers to purchase, addenda, buyer agency agreements, counter-offers, **multiple

including the seller condition report, agency, and buyer and seller disclosure**, cancellation
agreement and mutual releases, options, bills of sale, and exchanges; and **agreement to arbitrate
real estate transaction disputes under s. 788.015**.

**(9) Title records and transfers of title** — **requirements of a valid conveyance under ss. 240.10,
706.02 and 706.03**; types of deeds; involuntary alienation; probate; transferring title by will;

**(10) Real estate finance and basic math** — buyer qualification; mortgage instruments; payment
plans; provisions for default, assignment, release and subject to; **land contracts**; secondary

**(11) Appraisal and market analysis** — basic principles of value; direct market comparison
approach; cost approach; income approach; appraisal process.

**(12) Fair housing laws** — equal opportunity in housing; federal fair housing law; blockbusting,
steering and redlining; **equal rights under s. 106.50**; organizations.

**(13) Ethical real estate practices** — **ch. REEB 24**, and ethical business conduct including

**(14) Consumer protection** — **property inspections under REEB 24.07(1)(a)**; **inquiry by listing

**(15) Leases** — leasehold estates; standard lease provisions; lease documents; legal principles of

**(16) Property Management** — functions of a property manager; management agreement; management

**(17) Land use control and development** — public controls; the comprehensive plan; zoning;

## § 452.133 — Duties of licensees; prohibitions

Read in full. This is the heart of Wisconsin brokerage and it is structured differently from every
other state in this project.

### (1) Duties to ALL PARTIES to a transaction

A firm providing brokerage services to a party owes that party:

**(a)** the duty to provide brokerage services **honestly and fairly**;
**(b)** the duty to provide brokerage services **with reasonable skill and care**;
**(c)** the duty to **timely disclose in writing all material adverse facts that the firm knows and
that the party does not know or cannot discover through reasonably vigilant observation**, unless
disclosure is prohibited by law;
**(d)** the duty to **keep confidential** any information given to the firm in confidence, or any
information the firm knows a reasonable person would want kept confidential, unless disclosure is
required by law or the person whose interests may be adversely affected specifically authorises it —
and **the firm shall continue to keep the information confidential after the transaction is complete
and after the firm is no longer providing brokerage services to the party**;
**(e)** the duty to **provide accurate information about market conditions** that affect the
transaction, within a reasonable time after a request, unless disclosure is prohibited by law;
**(f)** the duty to **safeguard trust funds and other property** held, as required by rules under
s. 452.13(5);
**(g)** when negotiating on behalf of a party, the duty to **present contract proposals in an
objective and unbiased manner and disclose the advantages and disadvantages of the proposals**.

**This is the rule that most distinguishes Wisconsin.** Seven duties are owed to *every party*,
client or not — including a written material-adverse-facts disclosure duty and a confidentiality duty
that survives the transaction. Most states owe a customer only honesty and a narrow disclosure.

### (2) Additional duties to CLIENTS

**(a)** the duty to **loyally represent the client's interests** by (1) placing the client's interests
ahead of the firm's and (2) placing them ahead of the interests of persons in the transaction who are
not the firm's clients, by not disclosing to them information or advice whose disclosure is contrary
to a client's interests, unless disclosure is required by law;
**(am)** the duty, **when requested by the client**, to provide information and advice on matters
material to the client's transaction **and within the scope of the knowledge, skills and training
required under ch. 452**;
**(b)** the duty to disclose to the client all information known by the firm that is material to the
transaction and not known by, or discoverable by, the client through reasonably vigilant observation
— except confidential information under (1)(d) and other information whose disclosure is prohibited
by law;
**(c)** the duty to fulfil any obligation required by the agency agreement, and any order of the
client within its scope, that is not inconsistent with another duty under ch. 452 or other law;
**(d)** the duty to **negotiate on behalf of the client**.

### (3) Prohibited conduct

**(a)** accepting **any fee or compensation** related to the transaction from anyone other than the
licensee's client or firm **without the prior written consent of all parties**;
**(b)** acting in a transaction **on the licensee's own behalf**, on behalf of the licensee's
immediate family where the firm is an individual, on behalf of the licensee's firm, or on behalf of
any organisation or business entity in which the licensee has an interest, **without the prior
written consent of all parties** — and the consent must be obtained **in the offer to purchase,
option, lease, or other transaction contract**;
**(c)** referring, recommending or suggesting the services of a person or entity from which the
licensee may receive compensation for a referral, or in which the licensee has an interest, **unless
the licensee has disclosed that fact in writing**;
**(d)** negotiating the sale, exchange, purchase or rental of **personal property** unless related to
the transaction.

### (4) Subagent's duties

A subagent owes all parties to whom it provides brokerage services the **sub. (1)** duties but
**not** the sub. (2) client duties, and may not place its own interests ahead of the principal firm's
clients, nor give advice or opinions contrary to those clients' interests unless required by law.

### Version warning

The section as read carries **effective-date notes for 2025 Wis. Act 69, effective 1 January 2027**,
amending **(3)(a)** and **(3)(c)**. Before that date (3)(a) reads "from any person other than the
licensee's client, principal firm, or firm", and (3)(c) cross-references s. 452.19(1) rather than
s. 452.19(1m)(a). **Any statement in the curriculum about either paragraph must be rechecked against
the operative version before the course is delivered on or after 1 January 2027**, under the
legal-update control.
