## Form A · Item 001

**Objective:** WI-01 — Reserved property rights

A deed conveys a parcel while expressly reserving a specified mineral interest to the grantor. Ignoring any unstated defect in the instrument, why is knowing the physical parcel alone insufficient to describe what the purchaser acquired?

A. The purchaser must also identify the legal rights conveyed and the interest reserved.
B. Every conveyance of the surface necessarily includes all reserved subsurface interests.
C. An express reservation changes the geographic location of the parcel.
D. A reservation means the purchaser receives only a residential lease.

**Key:** A

**Rationale:** Real property includes legal interests as well as the physical real estate. The instrument’s stated reservation distinguishes the physical parcel from the bundle of rights conveyed.

## Form A · Item 002

**Objective:** WI-01 — Cooperative ownership

An offering gives the purchaser shares in the corporation that owns an apartment building and a proprietary lease for one apartment. What ownership arrangement is described?

A. A condominium unit conveyed by an individual real-estate deed.
B. A fee-simple subdivision lot.
C. A tenancy in common in which no occupancy right is allocated.

**Key:** D

**Rationale:** A cooperative pairs ownership of shares with an occupancy right under a proprietary lease; it differs from receiving a deed to an individually owned condominium unit.

## Form A · Item 004

**Objective:** WI-01 — Immobility and location

Two otherwise comparable houses face different school-access routes and commute times. A purchaser values one location more even though the buildings could be reproduced at equal cost. Which characteristic most directly explains why reproducing the building does not reproduce the whole property?

A. Land is immobile, so the location and its surrounding influences cannot be moved with the building.
B. All parcels are physically identical when their lot areas match.
C. Construction cost fixes the price of every nearby sale.

**Key:** A

**Rationale:** Real estate includes an immobile location. Reproducing a structure does not reproduce access, surroundings, or the market’s response to that location.

## Form A · Item 005

**Objective:** WI-01 — Market absorption arithmetic

A market report shows 96 comparable active listings and an average of 16 sales per month over the stated observation period. Assuming that sales pace and no new listings, how many months would the current inventory represent?

A. Four months.
