# Washington Official Source Register

Last verified: **2026-08-17**. Official regulator, legislature/rule publisher, and current regulator forms control. Missing or ambiguous requirements remain filing questions; another state's rule is never substituted.

1. [Washington DOL — Get your broker license](https://dol.wa.gov/professional-licenses/real-estate-brokers/get-your-license-real-estate-brokers) — 60-hour Fundamentals plus 30-hour Practices; school submits completion to PSI; required exams must be completed within two years after education.
2. [Washington DOL — School approval](https://dol.wa.gov/professional-licenses/real-estate-educators/school-approval-real-estate-educators) — School application, student publication, administrator résumé, fee, and two-year cycle.
3. [Washington DOL — Instructor approval](https://dol.wa.gov/professional-licenses/real-estate-educators/apply-approval-real-estate-instructors) — Instructor application, qualifications, résumé, fee, and two-year cycle.
4. [Washington DOL — Course approval](https://dol.wa.gov/professional-licenses/real-estate-educators/get-your-course-approval-real-estate-educators) — Separate course application, outline, materials, examination/key, evaluation, and distance method approval or ARELLO certificate.
5. [Washington DOL — Curriculum](https://dol.wa.gov/professional-licenses/real-estate-educators/curriculum-real-estate-educators) — Current prescribed Fundamentals and Practices curricula.
6. [Washington DOL — Forms](https://dol.wa.gov/professional-licenses/real-estate-educators/forms-real-estate-educators) — School, instructor, pre/post course, and Distance Education Delivery Method applications.
7. [Washington DOL — Fees](https://dol.wa.gov/professional-licenses/real-estate-brokers/fees-real-estate) — Current school, instructor, course, and renewal fees.
8. [WAC 308-124H-820](https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=308-124H-820) — 50-minute clock hour; course/instructor/material/objective requirements; for 30+ hour courses, at least three exam questions per clock hour and a passing grade of at least 70%.
9. [WAC 308-124H-830 through -845](https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=308-124H-830) — Distance definition, interactivity, delivery-method application, inactivity shutdown, remediation, identity/clock-hour certification and ARELLO alternative.
10. [WAC 308-124H-855](https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=308-124H-855) — Required completion-certificate fields.
11. [WAC 308-124H-880 through -895](https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=308-124H-880) — School/administrator approval, application and responsibilities.
12. [WAC 308-124H-935](https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=308-124H-935) — Five-year student and required-publication retention.
13. [WAC 308-124H-945 through -960](https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=308-124H-945) — Instructor approval, two-year term and qualification pathways.
14. [WAC 308-124H-990](https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=308-124H-990) — Approval terms and fees.

## Verified point-in-time controls

- Fundamentals and Practices are separate mandated course records.
- Fundamentals requires 60 clock hours; Practices requires 30 clock hours.
- Each clock hour represents 50 minutes of actual instruction.
- The required final specifications are 180 questions for Fundamentals and 90 for Practices, each at a minimum 70% passing grade.
- The current public fee page lists $456 for Fundamentals and $15.20 per clock hour ($456) for Practices; school approval is $380 and instructor approval is $114.
- Course approvals run four years; school and instructor approvals run two years. Actual issued dates and numbers control the LMS gate.

## Release control

All sources are checked again on filing day, on regulator correction, before each controlled release, and before enrollment after a legal/form change. A snapshot, checksum, effective date, reviewer, and impact decision belong in the regulatory-document record.
