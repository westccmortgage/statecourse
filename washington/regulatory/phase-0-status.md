## Exact personal route

1. Obtain an official California DRE license history showing agency letterhead, active dates, license
   level, expiration, disciplinary history, method obtained, and creation date. A license copy or screenshot
   is insufficient.
2. Use SecureAccess Washington to request Washington reciprocity exam approval at the **Managing Broker**
   level. The approval window is six months. DOL makes the equivalency determination.
3. After approval, wait the DOL-published four business days before scheduling with PSI and take only the
   Washington-law portion. Published fee: **$210 per attempt**.
4. After passing, submit fingerprints using the DOL process for an applicant outside Washington.
5. Wait for DOL's notice that exam and background results are received.
6. Apply within one year of passing. Published original Managing Broker fee: **$330**. Fingerprint vendor
   fees are extra. If DOL rejects level equivalence, use the lesser Broker reciprocity route it identifies.

## Fixed education filing fees

School $380 + instructor $114 + Fundamentals $456 + Practices $456 = **$1,406**. This excludes business
registration/UBI, Workforce Board, ARELLO, fingerprint, exam, mailing and other ancillary costs. DOL's
published planning figure is 45 days for each original school/instructor/course review; it is not a promise
of approval or an aggregate project timeline.

## Firm/designated-broker distinction

The current school application does not state that an education provider must hold a firm license or that
its administrator must be a Washington Managing Broker. A brokerage is separate: a Washington firm needs
name approval, entity/UBI steps, a Washington Managing Broker with controlling interest, a designated-broker
endorsement, and a $304 firm application. Do not buy that license solely to file the school application.
