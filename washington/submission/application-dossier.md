# School, instructor and course application dossier

## School — RE-623-121 (R/12/25)

Prepare the legal school name, assumed name if any, physical and mailing addresses, phone/email, website,
16-digit UBI/Business ID/Location ID accepted by DOL, ownership form and ownership disclosures. Name one
administrator and document relevant employment/experience. Answer legal-history questions outside this
repository. Attach:

- required publication with admission, progress, attendance/completion, conduct, grievance, cancellation,
  refund and other current WAC 308-124H-907 policies;
- course descriptions satisfying WAC 308-124H-910 for both prescribed courses; and
- administrator resume.

Do not insert the statutory DOL approval statement until approval exists. Do not use a registered-agent
address as the operating school address unless operations actually occur there.

## Instructor — RE-623-125 (R/8/23)

Request Fundamentals and Practices. Attach a cover letter explaining qualification, a current resume,
official California DRE history, recent full-time experience evidence, and proof of a permissible
instructional-technique route. The application has two separate tests: teaching technique and subject
competency. Long licensure does not automatically satisfy both.

Provisional fact application: California broker since 2009 plausibly exceeds the form's three-active-years-
within-five-years competency option. Because the form does not say whether out-of-state licensure counts,
keep it as regulator confirmation, not a checked conclusion.

## Each course — RE-623-137 (R/10/25)

Create one inclusive PDF per course containing:

1. signed current form and payment;
2. course title, category and prescribed hours;
3. detailed outline with hours and objective mapping;
4. all instructional material exactly as delivered;
5. comprehensive final and answer key (Fundamentals at least 180 items; Practices at least 90; passing grade
   at least 70 percent); and
6. BPD-600-002B distance-method application or school-specific ARELLO certificate with clock-hour control.

The two current DOL curriculum PDFs are the outer specification. A thematic outline is not enough: every
objective needs a pinpoint to submitted material, instructional time and assessment evidence.

## Distance delivery — BPD-600-002B (R/9/21)

The final narrative must answer all eight numbered questions with real system evidence:

| Form subject | Evidence required | Current state |
|---|---|---|
| orientation | student workflow and support | draftable |
| technology / inactivity shutdown | exact hardware/software plus tested shutdown behavior | blocked |
| identity / dual certification | enrollment identity, integrity controls, school and student written attestations | blocked |
| instructor contact | approved instructor, hours, channels, response standard | blocked |
| interactive events | each event mapped to objective and feedback | partially present |
| content equivalence | content and 3,000/1,500-minute evidence | blocked by traceability |
| clock hours | auditable active-instruction methodology | blocked |
| mastery/progress/remediation | objective criteria, regular measurement, remediation and retest | partially present |

## Records and renewal

Design the system to retain required student records and each required-publication edition for five years,
including student contact information, registration/start/end data where applicable, course and exam results,
and evaluation. School and instructor approvals are two-year records. Non-CORE course approvals are four-year
records under the current fee page; verify again before renewal.
