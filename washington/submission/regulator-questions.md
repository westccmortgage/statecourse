# Washington prefiling questions

These are the only Phase 0 questions that need regulator answers before the filing route can be finalized.

## DOL Real Estate Education

1. The applicant has held only a California real estate broker license, continuously since 2009. For
   RE-623-125 (R/8/23), page 2, will an official California DRE license history plus evidence of at least
   three years active licensure within the five years before application satisfy the listed Fundamentals
   and Practices subject-competency option? If not, what exact additional evidence or qualification applies?
2. StateCourse LLC is a California LLC delivering courses only online from its California physical office,
   with no Washington classroom. RE-623-121 requests a 16-digit UBI/Business ID/Location ID. Must this LLC
   register as a Washington foreign LLC and obtain a Washington UBI before the school application? If a
   different DOR record is sufficient, identify it.
3. May the original school and instructor applications be reviewed concurrently, and may course packages
   be submitted while either is pending? If not, state the required sequence.
4. The reviewed rules/forms do not expressly require an independent proctor for the 180/90-item
   prelicensing comprehensive exams. Does DOL impose any separate proctoring or exam-security condition for
   self-paced distance delivery beyond the current forms and WAC 308-124H?

Request answers in writing and archive the complete message headers/body. Do not convert an unanswered
question into a requirement or an exemption.

## Workforce Board

prelicensing program for the public, delivered entirely online from California, with no Washington classroom.
Request a written determination whether Chapter 28C.10 RCW requires a Washington Private Vocational School
license, registration, surety, catalog/enrollment-agreement approval or other filing. Include the Board's
