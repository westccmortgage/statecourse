2. State the accounting duty and identify that it survives termination.

6. State the record a broker should be able to produce, and why.
7. State the risk management habits that follow from the statute.

## A. The transaction in order

### A.1 First contact

**Recognise that buyer agency may already exist.** **RCW 18.86.020(1):** a broker performing brokerage
services for a buyer **is** the buyer's agent unless the firm has appointed them to the seller, to both, or the
broker is the seller. **No signature is required for the relationship to exist.**

**Give the pamphlet and obtain the acknowledgment** — **RCW 18.86.030(1)(f)** — *"as soon as reasonably
practical but before the party signs a services agreement."*

**And to any party not represented by a broker**, before they sign an offer or as soon as reasonably practical.
**That duty is owed even though they are not your principal**, because RCW 18.86.030(1) runs **to all
parties.**

### A.2 Establishing the relationship

**The FIRM enters a services agreement with the principal**, before or as soon as reasonably practical after
services commence — **RCW 18.86.020(2)(a)** — containing **all five** items in **(2)(b)**: the term (with the
**60-day buyer default**), the appointed broker, **exclusive or nonexclusive with checkbox options for a
buyer**, the **separately initialed** limited dual agency consent with its acknowledgment, and the **second
consent** covering the designated and managing brokers.

**And the compensation terms RCW 18.86.080(7) requires** — the amount, consent to sharing, consent to
compensation by more than one party, and, for a buyer, **whether the broker will show properties where no one
is offering to compensate the firm.**

### A.3 Working the transaction

**Present everything written, both directions, timely, regardless.** *(1)(c)*
**Disclose known material facts not apparent or readily ascertainable.** *(1)(d)*
**Keep the principal informed, and do not disclose their confidential information** — the five-part test in
**RCW 18.86.010(9)**.
**Timely disclose any conflicts of interest.** *(RCW 18.86.040(1)(b) / .050(1)(b))*
**Route what is beyond you** — a statutory duty. *(.040(1)(c) / .050(1)(c))*

### A.4 Offer and agreement

**Disclose the role in writing** in a paragraph titled **"Agency Disclosure"** — **before the principal signs
an offer**, and in any event before mutual agreement — **including any compensation terms offered by a party
or firm to a firm representing another party.** *(1)(g)*

**Remember RCW 18.86.010(15):** *"a prospective transaction does not exist until a **written offer has been
signed by at least one party**."*

### A.5 Through closing

**Account in a timely manner for all money and property received.** *(1)(e)*
**Track and deliver.** **Confirm the closing statement against the agreement.** **Do not opine on legal effect
or tax consequences** — **RCW 18.86.110** leaves the practice-of-law boundary where the courts put it.

## B. Money — the duty, and where the mechanics live

### B.1 The statutory duty

**RCW 18.86.030(1)(e)** — owed to the principal **and to all parties**, **unwaivable**:

> *"To **account in a timely manner for all money and property received from or on behalf of either
> party**."*

**And RCW 18.86.070(2)** — after the relationship terminates, **two duties survive**, and this is one:

> *"(a) To **account for all moneys and property received during the relationship**; and (b) to **not disclose
> confidential information**."*

**A broker who cannot produce a clean accounting has breached a statutory duty**, whatever the mechanics turn
out to require — and the duty follows them past the end of the relationship.

### B.2 What the mechanics are, and where they are

**Which account, on what deadline, with what records, reconciled how often, retained how long** — all of it is
in **WAC 308-124 and the remainder of RCW 18.85**, **which this project did not read.**

**This course therefore states no deposit deadline, no account requirement, no reconciliation interval and no
retention period.** A broker learns them from the rule's own text and from the firm's designated broker,
**before** handling money — not after.

### B.3 What is true regardless of the mechanics

**Other people's money is not the firm's money.** Three things follow, and none of them depends on a rule this
project did not read:

- **Never mix it with the firm's or your own funds.**
- **Never use it**, for any purpose, for any period, however briefly and however certain you are of putting it
  back.
- **Never disburse it on your own judgement of who is entitled** where there is a dispute. **The broker holds;
  the parties or a court decide.**

**A broker who is unsure what to do with money in a dispute asks the designated broker and counsel — before
moving it.**

## C. Two situations that test the whole chapter

### C.1 Multiple offers

**Present them all.** **RCW 18.86.030(1)(c)**: all written offers, notices and communications, timely, **both
directions**, **regardless of whether the property is already under contract.** **The broker does not filter.**

**Do not disclose the terms of one offer to another buyer without the seller's authority** — the seller's
instructions govern, and **RCW 18.86.010(9)**'s five-part test tells you whether the information is
confidential in the first place.

**Treat every buyer the same way.** A process explained to one buyer and not another is a fair housing problem
waiting to be alleged, and it is also a failure of **duty (b) — honestly and in good faith, owed to all
parties.**

**Advise the seller to seek expert advice** where the choice among offers turns on legal or tax questions —
**a statutory duty under RCW 18.86.040(1)(c).**

### C.2 The in-house transaction

**RCW 18.86.060(5)** assigns three positions at once when two brokers in one firm represent the two sides:

- **Each appointed broker SOLELY represents their own party.**
- **The designated broker, and any managing broker supervising both, IS a limited dual agent** — by operation
  of the statute, not by anyone's choice.

**The consent that covers it is RCW 18.86.020(2)(b)(v)** — the **second** consent, distinct from (iv). A firm
that obtained only (iv) has consent to the wrong thing.

**And the supervisor's position is genuinely constrained.** A limited dual agent must **take no action adverse
or detrimental to either party.** Relaying one side's position to the other broker is exactly what that
forbids, and it is the most common way an in-house transaction goes wrong.

## D. The record

**Washington's record-keeping and retention rules were not read for this project, and no requirement from them
is stated here.** What follows is what the **statutory duties themselves** imply a broker should be able to
produce.

- **The pamphlet acknowledgment**, for every party it was owed to — including **unrepresented** parties.
  *(1)(f)*
- **The services agreement**, with all five RCW 18.86.020(2)(b) items and the **separately initialed**
  consents.
- **The Agency Disclosure**, in a paragraph bearing that title, dated before the principal signed an offer.
  *(1)(g)*
- **Every written offer, notice and communication**, and evidence of when it was presented. *(1)(c)*
- **What was disclosed, to whom, and when.** *(1)(d)*
- **A complete accounting** of every dollar and item received. *(1)(e)*
- **Conflict disclosures**, dated. *(.040(1)(b) / .050(1)(b))*

**Every one of those corresponds to a statutory duty, and the record is how the duty is shown to have been
performed.** A broker with no record has no answer.

## E. Risk management — the whole programme in one page

Ten habits, each traceable to a provision you have now read.

1. **Give the pamphlet and get the acknowledgment** — including to unrepresented parties. *(1)(f)*
2. **Get the services agreement in place**, with all five contents and both consents. *(RCW 18.86.020(2)(b))*
3. **Answer the compensation questions in the agreement**, including whether you will show properties where no
   one is offering to pay the firm. *(RCW 18.86.080(7))*
4. **Present everything written, both directions, timely — regardless.** *(1)(c)*
5. **Disclose what you know** that is material and not apparent — and remember **you have no duty to
   investigate what you did not agree to investigate**, and **an agreement to investigate creates the duty.**
   *(1)(d)*
6. **Account for every dollar**, and know it survives the relationship. *(1)(e), RCW 18.86.070(2))*
7. **Keep confidences** — the five-part test — **except under subpoena or court order, even after
   termination.** *(RCW 18.86.010(9), .040(1)(d), .050(1)(d), .060(2)(d))*
8. **Disclose conflicts timely**, and disclose your role under the prescribed heading before your principal
   signs an offer. *(1)(g))*
9. **Market publicly and concurrently to all brokers** unless health or safety reasonably requires otherwise.
   *(RCW 18.86.130)*
10. **Route what is beyond you** — a statutory duty, not a courtesy. *(.040(1)(c) / .050(1)(c))*

**And the one that holds the rest together — RCW 18.86.030(1)(b): deal honestly and in good faith.** Owed to
everyone. Not waivable. **Every violation in these two courses is, underneath, a failure of that sentence.**
