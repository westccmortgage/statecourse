# Washington Real Estate Practices 30h v1.0 — Unit-by-Unit Curriculum Blueprint

**Development draft — not approved and not a finished student textbook.**

This blueprint defines 30 distinct 50-minute instructional units. Topic-hour allocations are StateCourse's submission proposal mapped to the current DOL-prescribed Practices curriculum; DOL acceptance remains a release gate.

The comprehensive final is outside the 30 instructional clock hours. A production-ready secure bank does not yet exist.

## Required delivery pattern

- current-source lesson and Washington transaction example;
- interactive scenario with direct feedback;
- server-authoritative active time and inactivity shutdown;
- regular scored mastery check;
- individual remediation and alternate assessment;
- instructor access and identity/completion certification;
- immutable course-version audit record.
