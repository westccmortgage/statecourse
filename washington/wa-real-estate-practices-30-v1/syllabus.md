# Washington Real Estate Practices 30h v1.0 — Course Syllabus

**Regulator:** Washington State Department of Licensing  
**Hours:** 30  
**Status:** Development; not approved; enrollment disabled  
**Instructor:** Unassigned; state approval/qualification and course assignment required

## Learning outcomes

Students completing an approved release will explain the prescribed subjects, apply Washington law/forms to entry-level facts, perform required calculations, recognize professional-scope limits, and produce an auditable transaction response.

## Delivery and completion

Course-specific Distance Education Delivery Method Application, or an accepted ARELLO certificate for the same course and clock-hour controls. The exact course configuration controls active time, sequence, units, interactions, assessment, remediation, identity, inactivity, final eligibility, completion period, and proctoring. Payment never determines completion or credit.

## Assessment

Formative checks and module quizzes trigger objective-specific remediation. Draft final configuration: 1 form(s), 90 items/form, passing standard: 70%. Secure school examination; no separate proctor mandate identified in WAC 308-124H. Any item count not prescribed by the regulator is a StateCourse submission proposal.
