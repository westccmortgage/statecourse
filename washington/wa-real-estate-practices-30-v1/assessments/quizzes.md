# Washington Practices — Formative Assessment Development Blueprint

**CONFIDENTIAL development record — no production-ready quiz bank exists yet.**

Each of the 30 Washington practice units requires a versioned scored progress check tied to its measurable objective and transaction activity. Each module also requires a cumulative assessment. A repeated generic prompt is prohibited.

## Item-development requirements

- use the unit title, objective, Washington authority and applied activity from `curriculum/course-blueprint.mjs`;
- use document chronology, consumer communication, disclosure, calculation, source-selection and broker-escalation scenarios;
- provide direct feedback only after submission;
- record item/version, response, score, timestamp, attempt and remediation path;
- assign alternate instruction, a different Washington fact pattern and a parallel scored item after missed mastery;
- create instructor-support records for repeated objective failure;
- complete current-law/form, instructor/SME, editorial, accessibility and duplication review.

Quantitative mastery thresholds and attempt rules remain DOL-acceptance/configuration gates. These formative controls do not replace the separate 90-question comprehensive final.

Source: WAC 308-124H-835 and 308-124H-840(1), (2), (4)–(7).
