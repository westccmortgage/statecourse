# Washington commercial roadmap

Washington is commercially plausible, but Phase 0 does not support a revenue forecast. A historical DOL
meeting transcript containing license counts was located during research, but its official URL returned 404
during final live validation, so the counts and source were excluded rather than treated as current evidence.

Product order after approvals:

1. 90-hour broker prelicense bundle: 60-hour Fundamentals + 30-hour Practices.
2. First active renewal portfolio: 90 hours, including the prescribed law, advanced-practices and CE
   components in the then-current rules.
3. Recurring active renewal: 30-hour CE portfolio including current CORE and fair-housing requirements.
4. Open-curriculum electives only after the prescribed products are stable.

The personal Managing Broker license and a future brokerage firm may help brand credibility, but neither is
a substitute for school/course/instructor/distance approvals. Build and approve the prelicense product first;
obtain a current official applicant/license series and validate competitor pricing and acquisition costs
before pricing or forecasting.
