# Washington Fundamentals — Formative Assessment Development Blueprint

**CONFIDENTIAL development record — no production-ready quiz bank exists yet.**

Each of the 60 units requires a versioned scored progress check tied to its measurable objective. Each module also requires a cumulative assessment. A simple repeated stem is prohibited.

## Item-development requirements

- use the unit title, objective, authority and applied activity from `curriculum/course-blueprint.mjs`;
- include classification, calculation, source-selection, document-review and scenario items appropriate to the objective;
- provide direct feedback after submission, never before;
- record item/version, response, score, timestamp, attempt and remediation path;
- when mastery is missed, assign an alternate explanation, different example and parallel scored item;
- flag repeated objective failure for instructor support;
- complete legal/current-source, SME, editorial, accessibility and duplication review.

Quantitative mastery thresholds and attempt rules remain DOL-acceptance/configuration gates. These formative controls do not replace the separate 180-question comprehensive final.

Source: WAC 308-124H-835 and 308-124H-840(1), (2), (4)–(7).
