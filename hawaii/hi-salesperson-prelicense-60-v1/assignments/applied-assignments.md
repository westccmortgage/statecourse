# Hawaiʻi Salesperson Prelicensing — Applied Assignments

**Course ID:** HI-SALES-60-v1  
**Status:** Original development draft; instructor review required.  
**Student instruction:** Use the supplied course materials and official-source register. Do not give legal, tax, engineering, appraisal, or title opinions. Clearly flag facts that need verification.

Each assignment is designed for the corresponding four-hour unit. Students submit their own work. Instructors score each criterion 0–2: **0** missing/incorrect, **1** partly supported, **2** accurate and complete. A passing submission must show the reasoning and identify needed escalation; a correct conclusion without supporting facts is incomplete.

## Assignment 01 — Hawaiʻi land history and parcel research

A client asks whether a fictional parcel has “clear ownership” because a family has occupied it for generations. Prepare a one-page research plan that:
- distinguishes the historical land terms introduced in Unit 01;
- lists the records and agencies that would need to be consulted;
- separates known facts from assumptions;
- names at least three questions you cannot answer from the listing description.

**Submit:** annotated parcel-fact sheet, source checklist, and a 150-word client-safe explanation.  
**Rubric:** historical vocabulary; reliable research path; fact/assumption separation; professional boundary.

## Assignment 02 — Land-use and environmental due diligence

A fictional buyer is considering an agricultural-zone parcel with a shoreline component, an older cesspool, and a detached dwelling. Build a due-diligence matrix covering state/county roles, zoning and permitted use, shoreline/SMA issues, water/sewer, permits, flood/environmental questions, and specialist referrals.

**Submit:** matrix with issue, source/agency, status, next action, responsible specialist, and deadline.  
**Rubric:** correct jurisdiction routing; no unsupported entitlement claim; environmental and permit questions; clear contingencies/escalations.

## Assignment 03 — Licensing and permitted activity

For 12 short fact patterns, classify the actor's conduct as likely requiring a Hawaiʻi real estate license, potentially exempt, or requiring regulator/counsel confirmation. For each, cite the applicable course source and explain the limit of your conclusion.

**Submit:** classification table and a short memo on two difficult scenarios.  
**Rubric:** statutory/rule source; distinction between facts and conclusion; recognition of exceptions; appropriate escalation.

## Assignment 04 — Agency and disclosure timeline

A fictional buyer contacts a licensee at an open house, asks for offer advice, later asks the same licensee to represent them while the firm represents the seller, and discloses confidential financial information. Build a timeline showing role, duties, disclosures, consent, information handling, and supervisor involvement at each step.

**Submit:** one-page timeline plus the proposed neutral client communication.  
**Rubric:** client/customer distinction; informed consent; confidentiality; written-document and supervisory steps; no unauthorized legal advice.

## Assignment 05 — Ownership and property-interest comparison

Compare two fictional ownership records: one fee-simple parcel with a recorded easement and one condominium unit with common elements and association restrictions. Explain what each record establishes, what remains unknown, and which specialist or document would resolve each unknown.

**Submit:** comparison chart and a list of five verification questions.  
**Rubric:** accurate interests/limits; encumbrances; condominium/association questions; avoids guaranteeing title or use.

## Assignment 06 — Title, transfer, and encumbrances

Using a fictional preliminary title report and draft deed supplied by the instructor, create a transfer checklist. Identify vesting, legal description, easements, liens, restrictions, recording questions, and inconsistencies; do not decide legal sufficiency.

**Submit:** marked checklist, issue log, and escalation email to the supervising broker.  
**Rubric:** accurate document reading; issue prioritization; no title opinion; actionable escalation.

## Assignment 07 — Contract issue spotting

Review a fictional offer package containing an incomplete acceptance date, a financing contingency, an unclear included-appliance term, and inconsistent initials. Identify the issue, relevant document, practical consequence, and who should address it.

**Submit:** issue table and corrected transaction calendar; do not rewrite legal clauses.  
**Rubric:** identifies all material gaps; distinguishes business terms from legal interpretation; deadlines; proper referral.

## Assignment 08 — Hawaiʻi forms and deadline calendar

From an instructor-provided, fictional executed transaction package, extract parties, dates, required notices, deposits, contingency deadlines, and signatures. Build a calendar with reminders and backup owners.

**Submit:** deadline calendar and document index.  
**Rubric:** accurate extraction; dates calculated correctly; source field for every deadline; privacy and version control.

## Assignment 09 — Closing statement reconciliation

Reconcile a fictional settlement statement containing taxes, rent, association charges, escrow fees, loan payoff, commission, and buyer/seller credits. Show formula, day-count convention, rounding, and source for each line.

**Submit:** spreadsheet-style worksheet plus a short exception note for any item that cannot be verified.  
**Rubric:** correct arithmetic; assumptions disclosed; no unexplained plug; mismatch escalation.

## Assignment 10 — Material-fact investigation and disclosure

A fictional seller reports prior water intrusion, a repaired retaining wall, and a neighbor's unverified boundary complaint. Prepare a fact log and disclosure workflow that records what was said, what is documented, what should be investigated, and who must receive the information.

**Submit:** fact log, source list, and draft neutral communication to the supervising broker.  
**Rubric:** prompt, accurate recording; distinguishes verified/unverified; no minimization; appropriate disclosure/escalation.

## Assignment 11 — Comparative market analysis

Given six fictional comparable sales with differences in location, lot, condition, tenure, and timing, prepare a CMA. Explain adjustments as a broker's market analysis, disclose limitations, and avoid presenting it as an appraisal.

**Submit:** comparison grid, indicated range, and client explanation.  
**Rubric:** comparable selection; transparent reasoning; arithmetic; limitations and professional boundary.

## Assignment 12 — Financing and transaction math

Solve a set of fictional financing and closing calculations: loan-to-value, simple interest, points, commission, tax proration, square-foot conversion, and percentage change. Show units and intermediate steps.

**Submit:** calculation worksheet with a check method for each result.  
**Rubric:** correct formula; correct units; accurate arithmetic; independent reasonableness check.

## Assignment 13 — Leasing and property management

A fictional tenant reports an urgent repair, disputes a deposit deduction, and asks the licensee to change lease terms. Triage the file, identify immediate safety/communication steps, contractual and recordkeeping questions, and referrals.

**Submit:** response timeline, issue matrix, and draft acknowledgement that does not promise a legal outcome.  
**Rubric:** urgency; neutral communication; documentation; correct escalation; avoids unauthorized practice.

## Assignment 14 — Fair housing, ownership, and tax questions

Review fictional property marketing copy, a request to screen applicants based on a protected trait, and a question about tax consequences of a proposed ownership change. Correct the service and advertising response, identify a fair-housing concern, and refer tax questions.

**Submit:** revised neutral marketing copy, response script, and referral note.  
**Rubric:** equal service; non-discriminatory language; accurate referral; no tax advice.

## Assignment 15 — Integrated Hawaiʻi transaction file

Manage a fictional transaction from first inquiry through closing. Assemble the file index, agency/disclosure timeline, due-diligence checklist, deadline calendar, calculation worksheet, and unresolved-issue log. Explain the decisions and cite course sources.

**Submit:** complete file packet and a 5-minute recorded or written handoff to the supervising broker.  
**Rubric:** completeness; traceable sources; deadline and calculation accuracy; escalation; professional communication.
