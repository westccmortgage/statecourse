# Assessment and Remediation Plan

**Approval status:** Proposed provider academic policy; not a Virginia-mandated scoring rule.

## Assessment architecture

| Layer | Placement | Purpose | Mastery gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hourly engagement check | Every Hour 1–60 | Verify active engagement and immediate concept application | Correct response after feedback/remediation |
| Unit quiz | After Hours 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50, and 55 | Test cumulative five-hour unit mastery | 75%; alternate-item retry after remediation |
| Readiness check | Within Hour 60 | Diagnose final gaps; still instructional | All critical-compliance errors corrected |
| Monitored written final | After 3,000 instructional minutes | Verify independent course mastery | 75% (38 of 50) |
| Alternate final | After failed first final and assigned remediation | Verify remediated mastery | 75% (38 of 50) |

The 75% final passing score, item count, time limit, and retake structure are StateCourse proposals for Board review. The official sources reviewed do not state a required provider-selected passing percentage.

## Final-exam design

- 50 scored four-option multiple-choice items.
- 100-minute limit.
- Two secure forms, A and B.
- Each form contains exactly two items for each of the 25 subjects in 18VAC135-20-400.
- Form A uses bank positions 1–2 for each subject.
- Form B uses positions 3–4 for each subject.
- Position 5 for each subject is a validated reserve/replacement item.
- Forms A and B have no repeated item.
- Questions are original StateCourse items and are not represented as PSI or Virginia state-exam questions.
- No feedback, answer key, or topic label is visible during the final.
- A basic nonprogrammable calculator and blank proctor-controlled scratch paper may be allowed; course notes, web access, phones, and assistance are not.

## Hourly and unit remediation

1. Identify the missed concept and error type.
2. Reopen the precise explanation or official source.
3. Present a worked alternate example.
4. Require the learner to state the rule or calculation method.
5. Administer a new item with changed facts.
6. Record the result and elapsed active instruction.
7. Route repeated or critical misses to the certified instructor.

Critical subjects include fair housing, handling funds, agency/relationship disclosure, misrepresentation, supervision, consumer lending integrity, wire fraud, and unauthorized practice.

## Final-exam remediation

After a failed Form A:

1. No answer key or complete item replay is released.
2. The system reports performance by required-subject code, not secure item.
3. The certified instructor assigns targeted lessons, problems, and source-navigation tasks.
4. The learner must complete assigned remediation and a short mastery check.
5. The instructor authorizes Form B.
6. A second failure triggers individualized instructor review and a provider decision consistent with the Board-approved policy.

## Item validation and security

- Every item has a subject code, learning objective, cognitive level, correct answer, rationale, distractor rationale, source, author, reviewer, legal-review date, and status.
- Virginia-law items are revalidated against the current official source before every exam release.
- Item access is role-limited and logged.
- Student-facing exports never contain answers, rationales, source tags, or item-bank identifiers.
- Compromised items are retired and replaced with the same blueprint position.
- Statistical review tracks difficulty, discrimination, distractor performance, and unusual exposure.

## Completion rule

Course completion is issued only after:

- 60 separately completed 50-minute instructional hours;
- all hourly and unit gates;
- passed monitored final;
- resolved exam incidents;
- required notarized course-compliance affidavit for online delivery;
- complete student record and certificate data.
