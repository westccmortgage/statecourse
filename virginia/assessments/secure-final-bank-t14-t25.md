## T14 — Real estate financing

### T14-01

A $360,000 loan is made on a $450,000 value. What is LTV?

A. 70%

B. 80%

C. 90%

D. 125%

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** $360,000 ÷ $450,000 = 80%.

### T14-02

Two points on a $275,000 loan equal what?

A. $2,750

B. $5,500

C. $27,500

D. $550

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** One point is 1% of the loan amount; two points equal $5,500.

### T14-03

Which statement about preapproval is correct?

A. It guarantees funding

B. It generally reflects lender review but remains subject to conditions and property/final verification

C. It is a deed

D. It eliminates appraisal

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** Preapproval is not final funding and may be conditioned on property and updated borrower information.

### T14-04

Which item can affect cash to close besides down payment?

A. Prepaids, closing costs, deposits, credits, and reserves

B. Only the street number

C. Only appraised condition

D. Only listing days

**Correct answer:** A

**Rationale:** Cash to close reconciles multiple transaction and lender items.

### T14-05

Who communicates the official credit decision and underwriting conditions?

A. The lender/authorized mortgage professional

B. The listing photographer

C. The property manager

D. The county assessor

**Correct answer:** A

**Rationale:** Credit decisions and official conditions belong to the lender process.
