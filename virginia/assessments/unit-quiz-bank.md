# Unit Quiz Bank — Quizzes 01–11

**Security:** Instructor/LMS use. These items are separate from the final-exam bank.  
**Policy:** Each five-item quiz requires 75%; because a five-item set cannot express exactly 75%, the LMS requires 4 of 5 correct (80%) for the fixed set. A larger randomized pool may later implement a precise 75% threshold. Misses trigger alternate remediation before progression.

## Unit Quiz 01 — Hours 01–05: Economy, Market, Property, and Estates

### U01-01

Why can a strong national housing report fail to describe a local Virginia submarket?

A. Real estate demand and supply are location- and segment-specific

B. National reports are illegal

C. Virginia has no real estate

D. Prices never vary

**Correct answer:** A

**Rationale:** Market area and property segment must be defined before applying broad data.

### U01-02

There are 24 active listings and 8 average monthly closed sales. Estimated months of inventory?

A. 2

B. 3

C. 8

D. 32

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** 24 ÷ 8 = 3 months.

### U01-03

A seller wants to keep a built-in bookcase. Best risk control?

A. Remove it after closing

B. State the exclusion clearly in the contract before acceptance

C. Say nothing

D. Ask the lender

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** Written allocation reduces fixture dispute.

### U01-04

O grants A a life estate and keeps the future interest. What is O’s future interest?

A. Remainder

B. Reversion

C. Lease

D. Lien

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** The estate returns to the grantor, creating a reversion.

### U01-05

Which is a freehold estate?

A. Fee simple

B. Tenancy for years

C. Periodic tenancy

D. Tenancy at sufferance

**Correct answer:** A

**Rationale:** Fee simple is a freehold ownership estate.
