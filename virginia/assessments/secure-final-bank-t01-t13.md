## T01 — Economy and social impact of real estate

### T01-01

Which characteristic most directly causes real estate markets to be local?

A. Land is immobile

B. Buildings are insured

C. Deeds are recorded

D. Loans are amortized

**Correct answer:** A

**Rationale:** Land cannot be moved to another demand center, so location-specific conditions shape its market.

### T01-02

A sharp demand increase occurs while new construction will take two years. What is the most likely short-run effect, all else equal?

A. Lower prices and higher vacancy

B. Upward price pressure and lower vacancy

C. No change because supply is fixed

D. Immediate equilibrium from new supply

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** Slow supply response can produce upward price/rent pressure and reduced vacancy before new inventory arrives.

### T01-03

Which function develops a supported opinion of value for an identified use?

A. Property management

B. Brokerage

C. Appraisal

D. Title recording

**Correct answer:** C

**Rationale:** Appraisal develops an opinion of value under assignment conditions; brokerage and management have different roles.

### T01-04

Which is an example of real estate’s social impact?

A. A household’s access to housing and community services

B. The color of a loan file

C. A deed’s font

D. A broker’s office furniture

**Correct answer:** A

**Rationale:** Housing location and availability affect households, communities, access, and public services.

### T01-05

Why does real estate development commonly require substantial financing?

A. Land is always tax exempt

B. Projects have long, capital-intensive acquisition and construction periods

C. Every project is federally guaranteed

D. Deeds cannot transfer without a loan

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** Development often requires large expenditures before stabilized income or sale proceeds occur.

## T02 — Real estate market and analysis

### T02-01

Months of inventory is commonly estimated by dividing active listings by what?

A. Average monthly closed sales

B. Assessed value

C. Total mortgage debt

D. Average commission

**Correct answer:** A

**Rationale:** Active inventory divided by an average monthly sales pace estimates months of inventory.

### T02-02

Why might median sale price be preferred to average in a small market with one extreme luxury sale?

A. Median uses no sale data

B. Median is less affected by an extreme value

C. Median always equals market value

D. Average is prohibited

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** The median is less sensitive to extreme observations than the arithmetic average.

### T02-03

What does an active listing most directly show?

A. A proven arm’s-length value

B. A seller’s asking position and current competition

C. A recorded transfer

D. A lender’s approval

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** An asking price is market competition but does not show an accepted and completed transaction.

### T02-04

Before selecting comparables, an analyst should first define what?

A. The desired conclusion

B. The relevant market, property segment, period, and intended use

C. The commission split

D. The buyer’s protected class

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** Comparable selection must follow a defined analytical problem, not a desired price.

### T02-05

Demand rises and supply also rises by an equal market-relevant amount. What should an analyst conclude without more evidence?

A. Price must double

B. Price must fall

C. No guaranteed price direction can be stated

D. Vacancy must be zero

**Correct answer:** C

**Rationale:** The relative magnitude, timing, elasticity, segmentation, and other factors are needed.

## T03 — Property rights

### T03-01

Which fact most directly resolves whether a chandelier transfers with a home?

A. Its purchase price

B. A clear written inclusion or exclusion in the contract

C. The seller’s private intention after closing

D. Its color

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** A clear written agreement reduces fixture uncertainty more directly than a single physical factor.

### T03-02

O grants a life estate to A, remainder to B. Who has the future interest if the grant is valid as stated?

A. A

B. B

C. O’s tenant

D. The lender automatically

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** A has the present life estate; B holds the remainder.

### T03-03

An easement appurtenant benefits what?

A. A particular dominant parcel

B. Only the government

C. A mortgage lender’s note

D. Any tenant worldwide

**Correct answer:** A

**Rationale:** An appurtenant easement benefits a dominant estate and burdens a servient estate.

### T03-04

Which co-ownership form does not create automatic survivorship merely by that form?

A. Tenancy in common

B. Properly created joint tenancy with survivorship

C. Tenancy by the entirety with survivorship

D. Life estate

**Correct answer:** A

**Rationale:** A tenant in common’s interest does not automatically pass to the other co-tenant.

### T03-05

A zoning variance is best described as what?

A. A transfer of title

B. Limited relief from a zoning requirement under governing standards

C. A private deed restriction

D. A mortgage release

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** A variance grants relief from a requirement; it is not a deed, lien release, or private covenant.

## T04 — Contracts

### T04-01

Which is generally required for an enforceable contract?

A. Mutual assent

B. A broker as a party

C. A cash purchase

D. A recorded deed before offer

**Correct answer:** A

**Rationale:** Offer and acceptance producing mutual assent are core formation elements.

### T04-02

A seller changes the price in a buyer’s offer and signs it. The seller has generally made what?

A. An acceptance

B. A counteroffer

C. A deed

D. A title policy

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** Changing a material term rejects the original offer and proposes new terms.

### T04-03

Which contract classification means one protected party may elect to avoid the contract?

A. Void

B. Voidable

C. Executed

D. Recorded

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** A voidable contract remains effective unless the protected party avoids it.

### T04-04

What document changes a term in an already formed contract?

A. Amendment

B. Appraisal

C. Deed of trust

D. Title commitment

**Correct answer:** A

**Rationale:** An amendment changes an existing agreement; an addendum commonly adds terms to the contract package.

### T04-05

Why must a financing contingency be read in full?

A. All contingencies have identical deadlines

B. Its trigger, deadline, notice, waiver, and remedy depend on its language

C. It replaces lender underwriting

D. It guarantees approval

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** The precise clause allocates rights and obligations; generic labels are insufficient.

## T05 — Deeds

### T05-01

Which event is central to making a deed operative between grantor and grantee?

A. Delivery and acceptance

B. MLS entry

C. Loan preapproval

D. Property inspection

**Correct answer:** A

**Rationale:** A deed requires delivery and acceptance in addition to proper execution and other legal elements.

### T05-02

A quitclaim deed generally conveys what?

A. Guaranteed marketable title

B. Whatever interest the grantor has, if any, without the same warranties

C. A lender’s insurance policy

D. Only a leasehold

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** Quitclaim language transfers the grantor’s existing interest, if any, without broad title covenants.

### T05-03

A special warranty deed typically limits the grantor’s covenants to claims arising when?

A. At any time in history

B. Through the grantor

C. Only after the grantee sells

D. Only from tax liens

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** Special warranty protection is generally limited to claims arising by, through, or under the grantor.

### T05-04

What is a principal effect of proper recordation?

A. It replaces delivery

B. It provides public/constructive notice and affects priority

C. It guarantees condition

D. It creates loan approval

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** Recording supports notice and priority but does not replace all validity requirements.

### T05-05

Which item is essential to identify the real estate conveyed?

A. Adequate legal description

B. Buyer’s credit score

C. Appraiser’s license number

D. Broker’s commission percentage

**Correct answer:** A

**Rationale:** The deed must adequately identify the property interest conveyed.

## T06 — Mortgages and deeds of trust

### T06-01

Which document contains the borrower’s promise to repay?

A. Promissory note

B. Deed

C. Title policy

D. Survey

**Correct answer:** A

**Rationale:** The note is the debt instrument; the mortgage or deed of trust secures it.

### T06-02

In a deed-of-trust structure, the lender/beneficiary’s security is held under an instrument involving which additional party?

A. Trustee

B. Tenant

C. Appraiser

D. Assessor

**Correct answer:** A

**Rationale:** A trustee is named in the deed-of-trust structure for the beneficiary/lender.

### T06-03

A buyer takes title subject to an existing loan but does not receive lender-approved personal liability. This is best described as what?

A. Assumption

B. Subject-to transfer

C. Novation

D. Payoff

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** Subject-to leaves the lien in place without, by itself, creating the buyer’s personal liability to the lender.

### T06-04

After a secured debt is paid, what separate public-record step may still be required?

A. Satisfaction or release of the lien

B. New appraisal

C. Lease renewal

D. Variance

**Correct answer:** A

**Rationale:** Payment and public-record release are related but distinct steps.

### T06-05

Why should a salesperson not promise a reinstatement amount?

A. Only cash purchases have amounts

B. The authorized servicer/creditor must calculate current default, fees, interest, and through-date

C. Reinstatement is always free

D. A deed replaces the loan

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** The current amount depends on the account, documents, law, and creditor/servicer calculation.

C. Only sellers qualify

### T07-05

Seller financing creates which need?

## T08 — Leases

A lease with a fixed beginning and ending date creates which tenancy?

B. Periodic tenancy

A tenant transfers the entire remaining leasehold interest. This is generally what?

A. Sublease

B. Assignment

D. Life estate

**Rationale:** An assignment transfers the tenant’s whole remaining interest; a sublease retains a reversionary interest.

### T08-03

Which lease term most directly controls whether a tenant may transfer occupancy rights?

A. Assignment and subletting clause

B. Granting clause in a deed

C. Appraisal certification

D. Title exception only

**Correct answer:** A

**Rationale:** The lease and applicable law govern consent and transfer.

### T08-04

A tenant remains after lawful termination without landlord consent. This is commonly called what?

A. Tenancy at sufferance

B. Joint tenancy

C. Tenancy by entirety

D. Fee simple defeasible

**Correct answer:** A

**Rationale:** Holdover possession without consent is traditionally tenancy at sufferance.

### T08-05

For a Virginia residential notice, the safest source is what?

A. A remembered generic deadline

B. Current Virginia statute, lease, approved form, and broker/legal procedure

C. A neighboring state’s website

D. A social media post

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** Grounds, content, service, cure, and timing require current Virginia authority and actual documents.

What is a title-policy exception?

A. Yes

C. Only lender policies do

D. Only if recorded

## T13 — Taxes in real estate

### T13-01

A locality charges $1.00 per $100 on a $350,000 assessment. What is estimated annual tax?

A. $350

B. $3,500

C. $35,000

D. $350,000

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** $350,000 ÷ 100 × $1.00 = $3,500.

### T13-02

What is a special assessment?

A. A charge for a specific public improvement or service under applicable law

B. A broker commission

C. A title policy

D. A mortgage payment

**Correct answer:** A

**Rationale:** Special assessments fund specified improvements/services rather than ordinary ad valorem tax.

### T13-03

Taxes are $3,650 per year and the stated convention uses 365 days. What is the daily rate?

A. $1

B. $10

C. $100

D. $365

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** $3,650 ÷ 365 = $10 per day.

### T13-04

Why is gain not simply sale price minus original down payment?

A. Basis, costs, improvements, depreciation, debt, and tax rules must be considered

B. Down payment is always basis

C. Loans are income

D. Tax law ignores cost

**Correct answer:** A

**Rationale:** Tax gain uses adjusted-basis concepts and transaction facts, not only initial cash.

### T13-05

What should a salesperson do when a seller asks whether a sale is tax-free?

A. Guarantee the answer if owned two years

B. Identify the issue and refer to a qualified tax professional

C. Use the property tax bill

D. Ask the appraiser to decide

**Correct answer:** B

**Rationale:** Income-tax eligibility and consequences require current law and qualified advice.
