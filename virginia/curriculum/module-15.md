## Hour 57 — National principles cumulative review

## 57.1 Property and rights

**Land, real estate, real property** — the third adds the legal interests, and it is what is actually
sold. The **bundle of rights** is separable; a seller may convey some sticks and keep others.

**Physical characteristics** — immobility, durability, uniqueness, scarcity. **Economic** —
improvements, permanence of investment, area preference.

**Fixtures** — MARIA: method of annexation, adaptation, relationship of the parties, intention,
agreement. **Intention governs**, judged objectively; the others are evidence of it. **Trade
fixtures** stay the tenant's and must be removed before the term ends or pass by **accession**.
**Severance** runs the other way; **emblements** protect the tenant's annual crop.

**Estates** — freehold and leasehold. **Fee simple absolute**; **defeasible fees** with a
**possibility of reverter** (determinable, automatic, "so long as") or a **right of re-entry**
(condition subsequent, must be exercised, "but if"). **Life estate** with a **reversion** to the
grantor or a **remainder** to a third party; **pur autre vie** is the inheritable one; the life tenant
may not commit **waste**.

**Concurrent ownership** — **severalty** (one owner); **tenancy in common** (undivided fractional
shares, no survivorship, freely transferable, partition available); **joint tenancy** (four unities:
time, title, interest, possession — with **survivorship**, destroyed as to a share that is conveyed);
**tenancy by the entirety** (spouses, survivorship, neither may convey alone). **Virginia is not a
community property state.**

**Encumbrances** — **easements** appurtenant (dominant and servient tenements, runs with the land) and
in gross (benefits a person or entity); created by grant, reservation, implication, necessity,
prescription, or condemnation; terminated by merger, release, abandonment, end of necessity, or
express terms. A **licence** is personal and revocable. An **encroachment** is found by survey.
**Restrictive covenants** are private, can be stricter than zoning, and are privately enforced.

**Government powers — PETE**: police power (zoning, codes, no compensation), eminent domain (taking,
just compensation, executed by condemnation), taxation, escheat. **Variance** relieves a dimensional
requirement for a hardship from the property; **special use permit** authorises a listed conditional
use; **nonconforming use** was lawful when established; **rezoning** amends the ordinance.

## 57.2 Description and transfer

**Metes and bounds** — point of beginning, calls of bearing and distance, closes; **monuments control
over courses and distances**. **Lot and block** references a recorded plat. **Rectangular survey** is
not used in Virginia; a section is 640 acres.

**Deed elements** — competent grantor, identifiable grantee, recital of consideration, granting
clause, adequate legal description, grantor's signature, **delivery and acceptance**. Acknowledgment
is generally needed to **record**, not to be valid between the parties. **The grantee does not sign.**

**Deed types** — general warranty (warrants against all defects, whenever arising), special warranty
(only during the grantor's ownership), quitclaim (conveys whatever the grantor has, warrants
nothing — a **curative** instrument).

**Notice** — **actual** (what is known), **constructive** (what is recorded, charged to everyone),
**inquiry** (what visible facts required one to investigate; **possession is notice**). Recording
gives constructive notice; **title passes on delivery**, not on recording.

**Chain of title**; **gap**; **cloud**; **marketable title**; **quiet title** action.

**Title insurance** — backward-looking indemnity for defects existing at policy date and not
excepted; **owner's** and **lender's** policies are different, and the lender's protects the lender
only. **Schedule B-I requirements** are actions; **B-II exceptions** are risks the insurer declines;
**exclusions** are printed categories. **No physical condition is covered.**

## 57.3 Contracts

Four elements: **mutual assent, competent parties, lawful objective, consideration** — plus the
**statute of frauds** writing for an interest in land.

**Valid, void, voidable, unenforceable.** **Bilateral or unilateral.** **Executory or executed** — and
the legal meaning of executed is **performed**, not signed.

**Offer** ends by acceptance, rejection, **counteroffer** (a rejection plus a new offer — the original
is dead), revocation before acceptance, lapse, death or incapacity, or destruction of the property.
Acceptance must be **unqualified and communicated**; modern forms require **delivery**.

**Remedies** — damages, liquidated damages, rescission, **specific performance** (available because
land is unique). **Assignment** transfers rights; **novation** substitutes a party and **releases**
the original.

**Contingencies** are **satisfied, waived, or failed with a stated consequence** — and a period
allowed to lapse without notice may be deemed satisfied or waived.

## 57.4 Liens and finance

**Voluntary or involuntary; statutory or equitable; general or specific.** **Tax liens hold statutory
priority regardless of recording date.** **Mechanics' liens relate back.** **Subordination** changes
position by agreement. Otherwise: **first to record, first in right.** On foreclosure, **junior liens
are extinguished as to the property; senior liens survive.**

**Note** is the debt and is not recorded; **security instrument** is the collateral and is recorded.
**Virginia uses the deed of trust** — grantor, trustee, beneficiary — not the two-party mortgage.
Clauses: **acceleration, alienation (due-on-sale), prepayment, defeasance, subordination, escrow.**

**Assumption** — buyer becomes liable; the seller is released only in writing. **Subject to** — buyer
does not assume; the seller stays liable.

**Programme categories** — conventional (conforming or not; **PMI protects the lender**), FHA
(**insured**, owner-occupant, MIP, minimum property standards), VA (**guaranteed**, eligible veteran,
funding fee, certificate of eligibility), USDA (rural area and income limits). **Eligibility is the
lender's determination, never the licensee's.**

## 57.5 Settlement, appraisal, and the numbers

**Debit** is a charge; **credit** is in a party's favour. **Arrears → debit seller, credit buyer.**
**Advance → debit buyer, credit seller.** **Rent → debit seller, credit buyer** for the unearned
portion. **Security deposits transfer in full and are never prorated.**

**Value, price, cost** are three different things. **Market value** requires an open market, prudent
and knowledgeable parties, **no duress**, reasonable exposure, and typical terms.

**Principles** — substitution, highest and best use (**legally permissible, physically possible,
financially feasible, maximally productive**), anticipation, contribution, conformity, progression and
regression, increasing and diminishing returns, competition, change, plottage.

**Sales comparison** — **adjust the comparable, never the subject; comparable better, subtract.**
**Cost** — land + cost new − depreciation, where depreciation is **physical**, **functional**, or
**external** (always incurable).
**Income** — **V = I ÷ R**; NOI **excludes debt service, depreciation, and capital expenditures** but
**includes replacement reserves**; **GRM uses gross rent and therefore ignores vacancy and every
expense.**

### Hour 57 engagement check

**Question.** Answer without looking back. (a) A deed is signed, acknowledged, and locked in a drawer.
Did title pass? (b) A comparable is superior to the subject — adjust which, and in which direction?
(c) Which liens survive a foreclosure sale? (d) What does NOI exclude and what does it include?

**Expected answer.** (a) **No** — **delivery** with present intent to pass title is missing;
acknowledgment makes the deed recordable but transfers nothing. (b) **Adjust the comparable, downward**
— comparable better, subtract. (c) **Senior liens survive**, and the purchaser takes subject to them;
**junior liens are extinguished as to the property**; **real property tax liens hold statutory
priority** and survive regardless of date. (d) NOI **excludes** debt service, depreciation, and capital
expenditures, and **includes** replacement reserves.

## Hour 58 — Virginia law and regulations cumulative review

## 58.1 The article, in the order it operates

**§ 54.1-2144 — common law abrogated.** Answer from the article, not from national agency doctrine.

**§ 54.1-2136 — preconditions.** Before any relationship: **(i)** the type proposed and **(ii)** the
compensation and whether shared.

**§ 54.1-2137 — the agreement.** In writing, before providing services. **Definite termination date or
90 days.** Fee and how and when paid. Services. Other agreed terms. Dual-representation disclosures
where applicable. After it ends, **only two duties survive: account, and keep confidential.**

**§ 54.1-2132 A 1 — brokerage agreement with a buyer *before showing property*.**

**§ 54.1-2131 A / § 54.1-2132 A — the duties.** Perform per the agreement; promote the client's
interests; **confidentiality**; **ordinary care**; **timely accounting**; disclose material facts of
**actual knowledge**; comply with the article and fair housing.

**§ 54.1-2131 B — toward the other side.** Honesty; no knowingly false information; **written**
disclosure of material adverse facts about the **physical condition** actually known; **defective
drywall** under § 36-156.1. And the statutory exclusions: **not** matters outside the boundaries or as
to adjacent properties, **not** governmental land use regulations, **not** highways or public streets.

**§ 54.1-2132 B — the buyer's licensee** discloses to the seller whether the buyer intends to occupy
as a **principal residence**.

**§§ 54.1-2131 C, 54.1-2132 C — ministerial acts** neither breach the agreement nor create a
relationship.

**§ 54.1-2138 — disclosure of relationship.** Trigger: a **substantive discussion about a specific
property** with someone neither client nor represented. Timing: **in writing, earliest practical time,
no later than when specific real estate assistance is first provided.** Form: **conspicuous — bold,
all capitals, underlined, or boxed.** Leasing: in the **application or the lease, whichever first**;
**not for terms under two months.** Change of relationship: written, to everyone already involved.
Retention: **three years** for disclosures relating to fully executed purchase contracts.

**§ 54.1-2138.1 — limited service agent.** Written agreement; disclose the status; **list services
provided**; **list standard-agent duties not provided**; conspicuous; statutory consent language. A
licensee helping the other side **without additional compensation** is liable only for **gross
negligence or wilful misconduct** and creates no relationship; **for a fee**, still no relationship,
but the **ordinary standard of care** applies.

**§ 54.1-2138.2 — disclose an ownership interest** in specific real property.

**§ 54.1-2139 / § 54.1-2139.01 / § 54.1-2139.1 — dual, commercial dual, designated.** Designated is
**not** dual **provided no designated agent represents more than one client** — but the **supervising
broker is dual**. Designated agents disclose confidences **only to their own broker**.

**§ 54.1-2140 / § 54.1-2141 — compensation and common source information companies create no
relationship.**

## 58.2 Licensing, supervision, and records

**§ 54.1-2104 / -2105 / -2105.1 / -2105.2** — the Board, its powers, and cease and desist orders for
unlicensed activity. **§ 54.1-2103** exemptions. **§ 54.1-2106.1** licences required.
**§ 54.1-2110** resident broker's place of business.

**§ 54.1-2110.1 — supervision.** Every place of business, branch office, **and team**. Availability to
review and approve documents. **Written policies in eight areas** — escrow, fair housing, advertising
and marketing, negotiating and drafting, unlicensed assistants, agency and independent-contractor
relationships, distributing new or amended law, and disclosing physical-condition information.
Competent services. Current licences. Teams and entities. **Supervising broker named in brokerage
agreements.** **Records three years.** **Over 50 miles → quarterly written certification.**

**18VAC135-20-165** — timely supervision; only licensees do licensed activity (with -335 A); adequate
supervision of unlicensed persons (with -335 B); diligence in responding to cooperating brokers.

**18VAC135-20-181** — federally insured escrow accounts, in the firm's licensed name, **labelled
escrow**, **principal broker responsible with signatory authority**, others with authority may be,
**balance sufficient at all times**; all transaction funds in, **including rents and rental security
deposits**.

**18VAC135-20-185** — financial records readily accessible; **brokerage agreements and dual and
designated consents three years from execution**.

**18VAC135-20-190** — **in the firm's name**, under the broker's direct supervision, firm's licensed
name clearly and legibly displayed; **advertising disclosure with the firm's name and office contact
information as the firm's written policy specifies**; property information **consistent with condition
and current contract status**; **updated in a timely manner**; **timely written request** where a third
party controls it.

**18VAC135-20-220 A** — disclosure of relationship, and dual and designated **written consent from all
clients**, timed to the presentation of the offer.

## 58.3 Conduct and discipline

**18VAC135-20-155** — the Board's disciplinary power, including for violations of **Chapter 10 of
Title 55.1**, and **B**: conduct in a **personal capacity** is reachable.

**18VAC135-20-260** — prohibited acts, including **two 30-day self-reporting clocks**: convictions and
out-of-state disciplinary action.

**18VAC135-20-270** — conflict of interest: active with another broker without written consent; acting
for more than one client under §§ 54.1-2139, 54.1-2139.01 or 54.1-2139.1 **without first obtaining
written consent of all clients**.

**18VAC135-20-300** — misrepresentation or omission: **bait and switch**; failure by a standard agent
to disclose known material adverse physical-condition facts; **failure to tender promptly every
written offer, counteroffer and rejection**; failure to disclose material facts to the client where
that is a failure of ordinary care; failure to give required written notices.

**18VAC135-20-330** — the broker is disciplined for a salesperson's violation **only** if the broker
**knew or should have known** and **failed to take reasonable action to remedy**. Not vicarious
liability.

**§§ 54.1-2112–54.1-2120** — the **Transaction Recovery Fund**; **§ 54.1-2119** revokes the licence on
payment from the fund.

## 58.4 Disclosure, fair housing, and common interest communities

**§ 55.1-703 — buyer beware.** The owner furnishes the **Real Estate Board's** statement, whose content
is that the **owner makes no representations**, and which advises the purchaser to exercise whatever
due diligence they deem necessary — naming home inspection (§ 54.1-500), mould assessment following
EPA guidance, energy analysis (§ 54.1-1144), survey, and contacting the locality.

**§ 36-96.3 — Virginia's protected classes:** race, colour, religion, national origin, sex,
**elderliness**, **source of funds**, familial status, **sexual orientation**, **gender identity**,
**military status**, and disability. Words or symbols associated with a religion, national origin, sex
or race are **prima facie evidence** and a disclaimer does not cure it; **reference alone to a place of
worship is not**. **B (i)** modification — tenant pays, restoration may be required where reasonable.
**B (ii)** accommodation — a change to a rule.

**§ 36-96.2 A — the owner exemption does not inure to the benefit of any Real Estate Board licensee,
regardless of capacity.** Every exemption is expressed "except for subdivision A 3", so **advertising
is never exempt**.

**§ 55.1-2308** — five contract disclosures for a resale in a common interest community; omission
makes cancellation before settlement the purchaser's **sole remedy**.
**§ 55.1-2309** — the **seller or seller's agent** obtains and provides the resale certificate; **not
waivable by agreement**; **14 days** from written request, then **deemed unavailable**.
**§ 55.1-2312** — cancel within the agreed period, or **three days from ratification** if delivery
preceded it, **three days from receipt** if it followed, and **any time before settlement** if nothing
was delivered; **written notice**, **purchaser bears the burden of proving delivery**; **without
penalty**, deposit returned promptly.
**§ 55.1-2314** — a non-compliant certificate relieves the purchaser of delinquent assessments and
prior violations; preparer liable to the seller up to **$1,000**.

### Hour 58 engagement check — the compliance circuit

**Question.** For each, name the governing section and the answer. (a) A listing agreement states no
end date; when does it end? (b) When must a buyer brokerage agreement be signed? (c) A licensee has a
substantive discussion about a specific property with an unrepresented buyer; when is written
disclosure due? (d) The firm has both sides through two licensees; what is the supervising broker's
status and what is required? (e) A salesperson is convicted of a felony; what must happen and when?
(f) A licensee sells their own house; can they use the owner exemption from fair housing?

**Expected answer.** (a) **§ 54.1-2137 C 1** — **90 days after its date.** (b) **§ 54.1-2132 A 1** —
**before showing property** to the prospective buyer. (c) **§ 54.1-2138 A** — in writing at the
earliest practical time and **no later than when specific real estate assistance is first provided**,
conspicuously; and **18VAC135-20-220 A 2** restates it. (d) **§ 54.1-2139.1** — designated agency, and
the **supervising broker is a dual agent or representative**; **written consent from all clients** is
required, timed by **18VAC135-20-220 A 3**, and its absence is a **conflict of interest under
18VAC135-20-270 2**. (e) **18VAC135-20-260 4 and 5** — the conviction is a prohibited act, and the
licensee must **inform the Board in writing within 30 days**. (f) **No** — **§ 36-96.2 A** provides
that the exemption does not apply to or inure to the benefit of any Real Estate Board licensee,
**regardless of whether the licensee is acting in a personal or professional capacity**.

## 59.1 The capstone

*Facts.* Marguerite owns a 1971 townhouse in a property owners' association in a Virginia locality.
She is selling and buying a condominium in the same locality. Both firms in the purchase are the same
firm. Marguerite's townhouse has an active roof leak she has mentioned to her licensee, and a

condominium Marguerite is buying has a special assessment discussed in the association's March
minutes.

The capstone requires the student to produce a complete transaction file and a compliance narrative.

## 59.4 Both sides in one firm

Two licensees of the same firm on the two sides of Marguerite's **purchase** makes it **designated
agency under § 54.1-2139.1**, in which the **supervising broker is a dual agent or representative**.
**Written consent from all clients** is required, timed by **18VAC135-20-220 A 3** — from the buyer no
later than when the offer is presented to the licensee who will present it, from the seller no later
than when it is presented to the seller. Without it, **18VAC135-20-270 2** makes it a conflict of
interest. Designated agents may disclose client confidences **only to their own broker**. Consents are
retained **three years from execution** under **18VAC135-20-185 C 1**.

## 59.5 The five deliberate traps

**One — the encroachment.** Students disclose it as a physical-condition fact. § 54.1-2131 B expressly
excludes **matters outside the boundaries of the land or relating to adjacent properties** from the
physical-condition duty. It is still a **material fact** owed to the **client** under A 6, and the
correct handling is a survey, counsel, and honesty — not silence and not a licensee's opinion about the
boundary.

**Two — the roof leak.** Squarely inside § 54.1-2131 B: material, adverse, physical condition, actually
known. **In writing.** A student who relies on § 55.1-703 to carry it has confused the **owner's**
buyer-beware statement with the **licensee's** duty.

**Three — the voucher and the veteran.** **Source of funds** and **military status** are Virginia
protected classes. Any suggestion that a VA offer is "harder" or that a voucher co-applicant is a
problem is where this file goes wrong.

**Four — the special assessment in the March minutes.** Nothing requires the buyer to be given the
minutes automatically; the buyer must **obtain and read them**, and the resale certificate and the
cancellation window in § 55.1-2312 are the mechanism. A licensee who does not diary that window has
lost the buyer's protection.

**Five — 1971.** Pre-1978, so **target housing**, so the federal lead-based paint package. Students
forget it on resale transactions constantly.

## 59.6 Remediation

Any capstone element missed opens targeted remediation: the governing section, a short alternate

to the new facts, and answer an alternate item correctly before the element is marked complete.
Elements missed twice are added to the instructor remediation queue and are addressed in a

### Hour 59 engagement check

**Question.** Marguerite tells her licensee, "don't put the roof in writing — the buyer's inspection
will find it, and § 55.1-703 says I make no representations anyway." Respond.

**Expected answer.** The instruction cannot be followed. **§ 55.1-703 governs the owner**, and it is
correct that the owner's statutory statement makes no representations. **It does not govern the
licensee.** **§ 54.1-2131 B** requires a licensee engaged by a seller to disclose to prospective
buyers **all material adverse facts pertaining to the physical condition of the property that are
actually known by the licensee**, **in writing** — and an active roof leak the licensee has been told
about is exactly that. Failing to do so is also **misrepresentation or omission under
18VAC135-20-300 2**. A client's instruction cannot authorise a violation, the statute provides that
**no cause of action arises against a licensee for revealing information as required**, and I would
confirm the position to Marguerite in writing and notify my principal broker.

## 60.1 The distinctions most often missed

**Delivery vs. recording** — title passes on delivery; recording gives notice.
**Adjust comparable, not subject** — comparable better, subtract.
**Arrears vs. advance** — arrears: debit seller, credit buyer. Advance: the reverse. **Deposits never
prorate.**
**NOI excludes** debt service, depreciation and capital expenditures; **includes** reserves.
**Assessed value is not market value.**
**Variance is dimensional; special use permit is a listed conditional use; rezoning is legislative.**
**Fee determinable is automatic; condition subsequent must be exercised.**
**Joint tenancy conveyance destroys survivorship as to that share.**
**Counteroffer kills the original offer.**
**Executed means performed**, not signed.
**Lender's title policy protects the lender.** **PMI protects the lender.**
**Junior liens die at foreclosure; senior liens and tax liens survive.**
**Ordinary care, not fiduciary** — § 54.1-2144 abrogated the common law.
**Actual knowledge, not a duty to investigate** — §§ 54.1-2131 A 6 and B, 54.1-2132 A 7.
**Buyer agreement before showing** — § 54.1-2132 A 1.
**90 days if no termination date** — § 54.1-2137 C 1.
**Virginia's disclosure statute is buyer-beware** — § 55.1-703.
**The owner exemption never helps a licensee** — § 36-96.2 A.
**Designated agency: the supervising broker is the dual agent** — § 54.1-2139.1.
**The broker is liable only on knew-or-should-have-known plus failure to remedy** — 18VAC135-20-330.
**Every written offer is presented** — 18VAC135-20-300 3.

## 60.3 Academic integrity and the final examination

**The final assessment is monitored, is administered after the sixtieth instructional hour, and its
time is not counted in the 3,000 instructional minutes.** Its conditions — permitted materials, time
limit, passing standard, retake policy, and identity verification — are set out in
`virginia/compliance/monitored-final-exam-sop.md` and are given to the student before the
examination.

Integrity rules: the work must be the enrolled student's own; the identity verification exists to
protect every honest student's certificate; and any assistance, sharing of items, or substitution
invalidates the attempt and is reportable. A certificate of completion attests to instruction the
student actually received.

**Two things this course must never be misunderstood as saying.** The course carries **no Virginia
pre-license credit** and is **not approved**; approval is a decision of the Real Estate Board and, until
it is recorded, no completion of this course qualifies anyone for anything. And the course does not
license anyone: licensure requires application to the Board, examination, and the Board's action.

### Hour 60 engagement check

**Question.** State, in one sentence each and without notes: (a) the standard of care a Virginia
licensee owes a client; (b) the knowledge standard for disclosing material facts; (c) when a brokerage
agreement with no termination date ends; (d) whether Virginia's residential property disclosure statute
requires the owner to describe the property's condition; (e) whether a licensee selling their own home
may rely on the fair housing owner exemption.

**Expected answer.** (a) **Ordinary care** — § 54.1-2131 A 4 and § 54.1-2132 A 5; the common law of
agency is abrogated to the extent inconsistent under § 54.1-2144, so it is not a fiduciary standard.
(b) **Actual knowledge** — §§ 54.1-2131 A 6 and B and 54.1-2132 A 7; there is no duty to investigate.
(c) **90 days after the date of the agreement** — § 54.1-2137 C 1. (d) **No** — § 55.1-703 is a
buyer-beware statute under which the owner furnishes a Real Estate Board statement saying the owner
makes **no representations** and advising the purchaser to exercise due diligence. (e) **No** —
§ 36-96.2 A provides the exemption does not apply to or inure to the benefit of any Real Estate Board
licensee, **regardless of whether the licensee is acting in a personal or professional capacity**.

## Instructor source note for Module 15

Every Virginia citation in this module restates text read directly from law.lis.virginia.gov on
2026-08-20 and recorded in `virginia/regulatory/primary-text-readings.md`. No new authority is
introduced in Hours 57 through 60; the module is review, and a review hour that introduces an unread
rule is a defect.

**Hours 57 and 58 are deliberately dense and are written as retrieval material**, not as first
instruction. Every proposition in them appears in an earlier hour with its explanation, and the
cross-references are exact so a student can return to the source hour rather than re-reading the
summary.

**The capstone in Hour 59 contains five deliberate traps**, listed at 59.5, and the marking scheme in
59.2 and 59.3 is the assessment instrument. The encroachment trap in particular tests the statutory
boundary in § 54.1-2131 B — that matters relating to adjacent properties sit **outside** the
physical-condition duty while remaining material facts owed to the client under A 6 — which is the
distinction this course has taught most carefully and which national material does not draw.

**Hour 60.3 states the course's regulatory status in the terms every document in this package uses.**
The final assessment's conditions, permitted materials, time limit, passing standard, retake policy and
identity verification are held in `virginia/compliance/monitored-final-exam-sop.md` and are **not**
restated here, so that there is one controlling statement of them rather than two that can drift apart.
