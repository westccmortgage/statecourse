# Certified Instructor Guide

**Use:** Attachment 2 instructor material and operational guide.  
**Prerequisite:** The assigned instructor must hold current Virginia Real Estate Board prelicense instructor certification before delivery.

## Instructor responsibilities

- Teach and supervise the approved curriculum without unapproved material changes.
- Keep Virginia-specific content current on its effective date.
- Distinguish binding law/regulation from official guidance, contract terms, broker policy, and StateCourse policy.
- Preserve the full 50 minutes of instruction in every class hour.
- Review flagged remediation, answer content questions, and document resolution.
- Protect exam security and approve final-exam remediation/retake.
- Escalate legal, tax, lending, appraisal, engineering, environmental, fair-housing, or compliance questions outside teaching authority.

## Source hierarchy

1. Code of Virginia
2. Virginia Administrative Code
3. Current Virginia DPOR/Real Estate Board forms and official instructions
4. Applicable official federal statutes/regulations/agencies
5. Current course-approved proprietary materials
6. Secondary educational references, used only when consistent with controlling sources

Never teach a commercial test-prep page as regulatory authority.

## Standard hour delivery

1. Confirm current lesson version and source-review status.
2. Open objectives and retrieval prompt.
3. Deliver concept segment A.
4. Deliver concept segment B with examples and misconceptions.
5. Facilitate the applied activity.
6. Administer the scored engagement check.
7. Provide immediate feedback without exposing secure final items.
8. Assign alternate remediation when needed.
9. Confirm both 3,000 active seconds and mastery gate.
10. Record instructor intervention or exception.

## Virginia-law teaching method

For each Virginia rule:

- identify the official source and effective date;
- quote or paraphrase accurately without exceeding necessary source text;
- explain the operational consequence;
- apply it to a new fact pattern;
- state what remains a legal interpretation requiring counsel;
- tag the lesson and form affected by any future change.

## Discussion boundaries

An instructor may explain course principles and direct students to official law. The instructor does not:

- create an attorney-client relationship;
- advise a student or consumer how to act in a live dispute;
- select tax treatment or entity/vesting structure;
- promise loan eligibility, value, title quality, zoning approval, or legal outcome;
- provide secure exam answers.

## Remediation

A remediation record identifies:

- student and hour/quiz;
- learning objective and error type;
- original result;
- alternate explanation/activity;
- alternate item result;
- instructor note and date;
- clearance or further action.

Critical-compliance misses require instructor review even if the automated retry is correct.

## Final exam

The instructor confirms:

- all 60 hours and unit gates;
- identity/proctor controls;
- assigned secure form;
- accepted incident status;
- score;
- subject diagnostic;
- remediation completion;
- retake authorization if needed;
- final pass and course completion.

## Course revision control

The instructor reports suspected outdated content immediately. Content is quarantined when a change may affect accuracy. Legal-update-only changes follow the update SOP; changes to approved topics or time allocation are evaluated with DPOR before publication.
