## StateCourse proposed academic policies

The following are provider choices, **not representations of Virginia-mandated thresholds**:

- Formative knowledge checks: 80% mastery target, unlimited guided retries after feedback.
- Unit quizzes: 75% required before progression; alternate-item retry after remediation.
- Monitored final written assessment: 80 scored multiple-choice items, 120-minute limit, 75% passing score.
- Final retake: one alternate-form retake after assigned remediation; additional attempts require instructor review.
- Final exam time is excluded from the 3,000 instructional minutes.
- Course clock advances only during verified active instructional engagement.

These policies remain subject to Board review and may be adjusted before submission without being described as state requirements.

## Online engagement control

Each numbered class hour is a separate LMS gate:

1. Minimum 50 active instructional minutes.
2. Clock pauses when the course is backgrounded, the network session is lost, or the idle threshold is reached.
3. At least two meaningful learner interactions and one scored check occur in the hour.
4. The learner cannot skip forward or accelerate timed media.
5. The server records start, pause, resume, interaction, answer, completion, user, device/session, and accumulated active time.
6. A completed hour cannot be awarded until both time and engagement gates pass.
7. Exceptions are reviewed and documented by an administrator.
