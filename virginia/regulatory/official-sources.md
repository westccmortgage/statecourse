# Virginia 60-Hour Pre-License Course — Official Source Register

**Course:** Principles and Practices of Real Estate (Salesperson)  
**Control date:** August 16, 2026  
**Source policy:** Virginia statutes, Virginia Administrative Code, Virginia DPOR/Real Estate Board forms and instructions control. Federal material uses official federal sources. Commercial exam-prep sources do not establish compliance requirements.

## Controlling Virginia course-approval sources

1. [Virginia DPOR — Real Estate Board Pre-License Education](https://www.dpor.virginia.gov/Boards/Real-Estate/Pre-License)  
   Confirms 60 class/clock hours of a Board-approved course and passage of the state and national examination portions.

2. [18VAC135-20-10 — Definitions](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title18/agency135/chapter20/section10/)  
   Defines a “class hour or clock hour” as 50 minutes of instruction.

3. [18VAC135-20-361 — Certification of instructors](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title18/agency135/chapter20/section361/)  
   Controls qualification and Board certification of prelicense instructors.

4. [18VAC135-20-362 — Approval of courses](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title18/agency135/chapter20/section362/)  
   Requires Board acceptance before offering the course, a certified prelicense instructor, a monitored final written assessment, an active-engagement mechanism for each class hour, the course application materials, five-year records, completion documentation, electronic reporting within five business days, and timely legal updates.

5. [18VAC135-20-400 — Course content](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title18/agency135/chapter20/section400/)  
   Lists the 25 subjects that must be included in a course of at least 60 class hours.

6. [DPOR Form A490-0214PRE (rev. 04/01/2026) — Prelicense Education Course Approval Application](https://www.dpor.virginia.gov/sites/default/files/boards/Real_Estate/A490-0214PRE.pdf)  
   Controls the current electronic submission format, detailed Timed Course Outline, seven attachments, online-course affidavit/testing notices, monitored final-exam description, completion-certificate fields, and course-timer evidence.

7. [DPOR Form A490-0211SCHL (rev. 04/01/2026) — Proprietary School Certification Application](https://www.dpor.virginia.gov/sites/default/files/boards/Real_Estate/A490-0211SCHL.pdf)  
   Current school application. It lists a $265 fee, Virginia SCC authorization/registration requirements, and evidence of financial responsibility with at least $2,000 net worth.

8. [Virginia Real Estate Education Committee](https://www.dpor.virginia.gov/Boards/Real-Estate/Education_Committee)  
   Current application deadlines and Education Committee/Board meeting schedule. Dates must be rechecked immediately before submission.

9. [Code of Virginia § 54.1-2105.02](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title54.1/chapter21/section54.1-2105.02/)  
   Authorizes Board regulation of real estate education providers, instructors, curricula, and financial responsibility.

## Change-control rule

Before submission and before every production release:

1. Recheck every controlling Virginia source.
2. Record the review date and reviewer.
3. Diff current law/regulation against the course source notes.
4. Update affected lessons on the law’s effective date.
5. Treat changes to approved topic allocation or time allocation as potentially substantial and consult DPOR before release.
