# Student Guide — Virginia 60-Hour Principles and Practices Course

**Draft:** This guide becomes student-facing only after Virginia Real Estate Board course approval.

## What this course does

The course provides 60 Virginia class hours of prelicense real estate instruction. Under current Virginia regulation, one class/clock hour equals 50 minutes of instruction, so the course contains at least 3,000 active instructional minutes.

Completing the course does not itself issue a real estate license. A licensing applicant must satisfy the Virginia Real Estate Board’s current education, examination, application, background, and other requirements.

## Course structure

- 60 separately timed instructional hours
- Applied activity and scored engagement check in every hour
- Unit quiz after each five-hour unit through Hour 55
- Readiness and remediation in Hour 60
- Separate monitored written final examination
- Online course-compliance affidavit when required
- Certificate after every completion condition is satisfied

## Timer rules

The clock counts active instruction only. It pauses when you are idle, leave the course in the background, lose the session, skip required content, or fail an engagement gate. Two simultaneous sessions do not double-count. You must complete at least 3,000 counted seconds in each hour.

## Assessment policy

- Unit quiz passing score: 75%
- Final: 50 questions, 100 minutes, monitored
- Final passing score: 75% (38 correct)
- Failed first final requires assigned remediation before alternate Form B
- These are StateCourse academic policies approved as part of the course package, not the Virginia state licensing examination

## Academic integrity

You must personally complete all instruction and assessments. Do not share accounts, obtain answers, copy/record secure exam items, use another person, or use unapproved resources. Violations may invalidate completion and may be reported as permitted or required.

## Technical requirements

- Current supported browser and operating system
- Stable internet
- Working camera/microphone for monitored final
- Ability to receive secure course communications
- Private exam environment
- Device that permits the approved monitoring process

Complete the system test before beginning. Report errors promptly; do not create duplicate accounts or sessions.
