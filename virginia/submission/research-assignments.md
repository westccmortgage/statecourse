# Research Assignments and Materials

**Purpose:** Attachment 3 description and copies of required research assignments.  
**Timing:** Assignment work occurs inside the specifically identified class hours and does not add unsupported credit.

## Assignment 1 — Local Market Evidence File

**Hours:** 2–3  
**Task:** Define a Virginia market segment, collect current active/pending/closed/expired data from an authorized source, calculate inventory/absorption, and write limitations.  
**Deliverable:** Two-page evidence table and conclusion with source/date.  
**Rubric:** Market definition 25%; data classification 25%; calculation 25%; limitations 25%.

## Assignment 2 — Land, Plat, and Title Source Navigation

**Hours:** 9–13  
**Task:** Using instructor-provided redacted/teaching records, trace the legal description, plat reference, chain of title, easement, and lien/release issue.  
**Deliverable:** Chain diagram, issue log, and professional-referral list.  
**Rubric:** Record sequence 30%; issue identification 30%; correct role/referral 20%; documentation 20%.

## Assignment 3 — Virginia Brokerage Relationship Matrix

**Hours:** 20–25  
**Task:** Navigate §§ 54.1-2131 through 54.1-2139.1 and current Board regulations for a seller/buyer/firm case.  
**Deliverable:** Party/relationship diagram and cited action matrix.  
**Rubric:** Relationship accuracy 30%; timing/documents 30%; duties/confidentiality 25%; citations 15%.

## Assignment 4 — Virginia Fair Housing Audit

**Hours:** 26–28 and 54  
**Task:** Audit advertising, lead response, source-of-funds criteria, and a disability request against current Virginia DPOR guidance and law.  
**Deliverable:** Corrected materials, response procedure, and 30-day audit design.  
**Rubric:** Protected-class accuracy 25%; prohibited-practice analysis 25%; corrective action 25%; objective controls 25%.

## Assignment 5 — Common-Interest Property Due Diligence

**Hours:** 29–30  
**Task:** Review a teaching declaration, rules, budget, reserve summary, minutes, insurance page, and resale-disclosure index.  
**Deliverable:** Buyer issue and deadline checklist.  
**Rubric:** Document identification 25%; financial risk 25%; restrictions 20%; deadlines/evidence 20%; referral limits 10%.

## Assignment 6 — Finance and Closing Worksheet

**Hours:** 34–41  
**Task:** Analyze a teaching note/deed-of-trust structure, compare loan categories, calculate LTV/points/cash to close, and reconcile settlement debits/credits.  
**Deliverable:** Calculation worksheet and milestone map.  
**Rubric:** Document roles 20%; calculations 35%; contingency/process 25%; closing reconciliation 20%.

## Assignment 7 — Capstone Transaction File

**Hours:** 51–60  
**Task:** Complete the integrated 25-topic transaction simulation.  
**Deliverable:** Issue/evidence/deadline/action table, source citations, calculations, and remediation record.  
**Rubric:** Required-topic coverage 25%; legal/source control 25%; transaction execution 25%; calculations/evidence 15%; professional boundaries 10%.

## Research-material controls

- Teaching records are redacted or fictional.
- Live consumer information is prohibited.
- Source/date is required for external facts.
- Commercial materials do not establish Virginia compliance.
- Copyrighted materials are linked or used under permission; they are not copied into the package without rights.
