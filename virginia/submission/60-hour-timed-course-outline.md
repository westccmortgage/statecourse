# Comprehensive Timed Course Outline (TCO)

**Course title:** Virginia Principles and Practices of Real Estate — Salesperson Pre-License  
**Delivery:** Online, self-directed, student-paced  
**Submission schedule:** 15 Course Days × 4 class hours per Course Day = 60 class hours  
**Instructional time:** 50 minutes per class hour; 3,000 minutes total  
**Final assessment:** Monitored written final administered after the 60 instructional hours; final-exam time is not counted in the 3,000 minutes.  
**Approval status:** Proposed for Virginia Real Estate Board submission; not Board-approved until written approval is issued.

For online delivery, a “Course Day” is a four-hour content bundle used to satisfy the TCO structure. Students may complete bundles on different calendar days within the enrollment period. Every numbered hour remains separately timed and gated.

## Standard 50-minute hour design

- **0–5 minutes:** retrieval prompt, objectives, and source orientation
- **5–20 minutes:** direct instruction segment A
- **20–35 minutes:** direct instruction segment B with examples
- **35–45 minutes:** applied scenario, calculation, document/source activity, or guided practice
- **45–50 minutes:** scored engagement check and feedback

The timer records active instructional time only and pauses for inactivity, loss of foreground focus, or disconnected sessions. Progression requires both time completion and the hour’s engagement check.

## Coverage audit

Every required subject T01–T25 appears in at least one substantive instructional hour and is reinforced in Hours 59–60. Topic tags are not substitutes for content: the curriculum and lecture notes must contain the instruction described in this TCO.

## Assessment placement

- Hourly scored engagement check: every hour
- Unit quiz: after Hours 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50, and 55
- Comprehensive readiness check: Hour 60
- Monitored written final assessment: after Hour 60, outside instructional time

## Change control

Once approved, content topics and time allocations in this TCO are controlled. Legal updates effective after approval are incorporated under 18VAC135-20-362(G). Any other material change is evaluated with DPOR before release to determine whether revised approval is required.
