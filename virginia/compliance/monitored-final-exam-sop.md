# Monitored Written Final Examination SOP

**Regulatory basis:** 18VAC135-20-362(A) requires a monitored final written assessment. DPOR Form A490-0214PRE requires Attachment 5 describing administration.

## Roles

- **Certified instructor:** owns academic integrity, remediation, and result approval.
- **Approved proctor/monitor:** verifies identity, environment, rules, and incidents; has no financial or personal conflict.
- **Administrator:** schedules, secures form assignment, preserves records, and does not disclose answer content.
- **Student:** follows identity, environment, conduct, and affidavit requirements.

## Pre-exam controls

1. Verify 3,000 active instructional minutes and all progression gates.
2. Verify student legal name against enrollment record.
3. Schedule the monitored session and assign Form A.
4. Send permitted-material and environment rules.
5. Confirm technology and accessibility accommodations without exposing item content.
6. Lock course answer feedback during the exam window.

## Identity and environment check

- Government-issued photo identification reviewed through the approved privacy-controlled process.
- Student name matched to course record.
- One student in a private, adequately lit environment.
- Work surface and permitted materials checked.
- Additional monitors, phones, notes, recording devices, messaging, and web resources removed or disabled as policy requires.
- Screen, camera, audio, and session monitoring initiated with required notice/consent.
- Identity check outcome and proctor identity recorded.

## Exam delivery

- Form contains 50 scored multiple-choice questions.
- Time limit: 100 minutes.
- Questions presented without answer feedback.
- Navigation/review is allowed within the session unless security design says otherwise.
- No copying, printing, screenshots, external communication, or assistance.
- Temporary connection loss pauses access and triggers re-authentication under the incident rule.
- Proctor may warn, document, pause, or terminate based on the approved incident matrix.

## Incident matrix

| Event | Response |
|---|---|
| Brief connection loss with continuous monitoring recovery | Pause, re-authenticate, document, resume with remaining time |
| Unverified person enters or communication occurs | Pause, investigate, document; invalidate if integrity cannot be established |
| Prohibited resource/device | Stop access, preserve evidence, refer to instructor/administrator |
| Medical or accessibility event | Protect student safety, pause/document, reschedule or resume under approved accommodation |
| Platform failure | Preserve logs, do not score incomplete form, assign a secure replacement session |
| Suspected item capture or distribution | Terminate if necessary, secure evidence, retire exposed items, escalate |

## Scoring and result

- Passing score: 75%, requiring at least 38 correct out of 50.
- Score computed only after session integrity is accepted.
- Student receives pass/fail and subject-level diagnostic information.
- Answer key and individual secure items are not released.
- Failed Form A triggers documented remediation before Form B.
- Result, form, timestamps, proctor, incident status, and authorization are retained in the student record.

## Post-exam records

- Identity verification outcome
- Proctor/monitor identity
- Form and item version
- Start/end/pause timestamps
- Session and monitoring audit
- Permitted-material acknowledgment
- Incident report/evidence
- Score and subject diagnostics
- Remediation and retake authorization
- Final course result
