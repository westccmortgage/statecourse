# Student Records and Completion Reporting SOP

**Regulatory basis:** 18VAC135-20-362(D)–(F).

## Required student record

For each student, maintain:

1. Student name and address
2. Course name and clock hours attended/completed
3. Course syllabus or outline version
4. Instructor name
5. Successful completion date
6. Board course code
7. Enrollment and identity records
8. Timer/active-engagement audit
9. Hour checks, unit quizzes, final form/result, and remediation
10. Online affidavit when applicable
11. Completion certificate
12. Completion-transmission record

Items 1–6 reflect the express regulatory record list. Items 7–12 are StateCourse controls supporting verification and audit.

## Retention

- Retain all student and class records for at least five years.
- Apply any longer period required by other controlling law, contract, litigation hold, or approved school policy.
- Preserve the exact course and source version applicable to the student.
- Records remain available for authorized Board inspection during normal business hours.

## Data quality

- Legal name and address are validated before final exam.
- Automated edits retain old value, new value, date, user, and reason.
- Completion date cannot precede the final accepted result.
- Course code/approval number is populated only from the issued Board approval.
- Manual time or score adjustments require documented approver and evidence.

## Completion certificate

Issue only after the complete-course rule passes. The certificate contains all current required fields and the exact approved statement.

## Electronic reporting

- Transmit course completion data in the Board-approved format/method within five business days of individual course completion.
- Validate mandatory fields before transmission.
- Retain submission timestamp, payload identifier, response, rejection, correction, and final acceptance.
- Failed transmissions create an urgent queue; correction does not overwrite the failed audit record.

## Access and security

- Role-based least-privilege access
- Encryption in transit and at rest
- Multifactor authentication for staff
- Logged export, edit, and deletion
- Secure backup and recovery testing
- Documented incident response
- Privacy retention/deletion only when consistent with mandatory record retention
