# Virginia Legal and Regulatory Update SOP

**Regulatory basis:** 18VAC135-20-362(G).

## Source watch

Monitor:

- Virginia Legislative Information System — Title 54.1 Chapter 21 and course-related titles
- Virginia Administrative Code — 18VAC135-20
- Virginia DPOR Real Estate Board forms, guidance, Education Committee information, and notices
- Virginia Fair Housing Office
- Official federal CFPB, HUD, EPA, FEMA, and other sources used by the course

## Change workflow

1. Log source, citation, publication date, effective date, and reviewer.
2. Determine affected required topic, lesson, activity, quiz, final item, form, SOP, certificate, and LMS behavior.
3. Classify:
   - editorial/no legal effect;
   - legal update effective on a stated date;
   - possible substantial course change affecting approved content/topic/time.
4. Draft and independently review the change.
5. Obtain certified-instructor and compliance/legal review as appropriate.
6. Contact DPOR before release if the change may require revised approval.
7. Schedule publication no later than the law/regulation effective date.
8. Retire affected exam items and replace them before use.
9. Record course version and student cohorts affected.
10. Verify production content and archive evidence.

## Emergency quarantine

Immediately block a lesson, form, or exam item when credible evidence suggests it is materially wrong or unlawfully outdated. Route students to instructor support and do not award completion through inaccurate content.

## Release record

- Change ID
- Source and effective date
- Old/new summary
- Affected assets
- Approval/reviewer
- DPOR contact/decision if any
- Test results
- Release date/version
- Student/cohort communication
